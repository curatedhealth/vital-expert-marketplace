# Output format: mechanism statement (draft for the person)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the chain in one line ("<component> is expected to lead users to <action>, expected to change <change>, expected to move <outcome>"), for whom, not for whom, the biggest gap, and who chooses (named, or "owner not named").
2. **Chain:** table Link · What the person stated · Source term · Measure named (or [GAP]) · Rows (each [PAPER] row with its own DOI; abstract-only and [WF] rows labeled).
3. **For whom / not for whom:** one line each, with the source (the person's fact or a precedent row).
4. **Evidence or precedent (only if asked):** one line per paper or product, stating only what its record or a passage read in the same direction says, with DOI, NCT id or De Novo number.
5. **Gaps:** links with no measure, missing exclusion, model sources not named (DiGAV § 2 asks for them, TM149).
6. **Record (last line of the answer body):** "Mechanism statement draft. AI-assisted. Each link is a hypothesis until tested (TM8, DOI 10.1136/bmj.n2061)." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
7. **Calls made (after the record line):** list.
