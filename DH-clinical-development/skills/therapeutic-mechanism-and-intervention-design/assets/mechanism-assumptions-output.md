# Output format: mechanism assumptions and stopping rules (draft for the person)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the assumptions the person marks fatal, each with its signal, the milestone (the person's, or open) and who stops the version (named, or "owner not named"); the one decision the person needs to make now.
2. **Register:** table Link · Assumption · Signal and measure · Milestone (person's, set in advance, TM57, ORBIT, DOI 10.1037/hea0000161) · Gate and action the source names · Stopper · Rows (each [PAPER] row with its own DOI; abstract-only and [WF] rows labeled).
3. **No measure yet:** assumptions marked [GAP], routed to mechanism validation monitoring A1. Engagement assumptions from A3 and untested mitigations from A4 route to mechanism validation monitoring A3 and A4.
4. **Precedent signals (only if useful):** one line each, record facts (TM138, TM143, TM144).
5. **Record (last line of the answer body):** "Assumption register draft. AI-assisted. No milestone set by the model." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
6. **Calls made (after the record line):** list.
