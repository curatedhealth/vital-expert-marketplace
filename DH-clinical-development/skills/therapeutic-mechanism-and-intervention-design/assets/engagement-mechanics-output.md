# Output format: engagement mechanics (draft for the person)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** mechanics tied to a link (count), mechanics not tied (names), whether effective engagement is defined (the person's definition, or "not given"; SE86, Michie 2017, DOI 10.2196/jmir.7126), the flags that need the owner the person names for consumer-protection law (or "owner not named"), and who decides what ships.
2. **Mechanics:** table Mechanic · Link and component served · Usage measure · Intended use (person's, or "usage, not adherence", SE92, Sieverink 2017, DOI 10.2196/jmir.8578) · Flags (row and status) · Rows (each [PAPER] row with its own DOI; [WF] rows labeled in every cell, and SE109 to SE113 also "staff report, not a rule" in every cell). For a subscription mechanic, the Flags cell carries ROSCA (SE114, statute) and the Negative Option Rule status (SE115, SE116, Federal Register).
3. **Engagement is not the outcome:** one line naming the proximal link each metric sits on (TM87, JITAI, DOI 10.1007/s12160-016-9830-8).
4. **Hazards carried to A4:** mechanics that could carry a risk to health, with the record that shows the pattern (SE16), unrated.
5. **Mismatches in the person's map:** each mechanic whose usage measure differs from the dose its link states (for example, a daily streak on a link of 3 sessions a week), as a fact from the map, not a flag.
6. **Open items:** effective engagement definition, intended-use thresholds, flags to settle, rewards of value routed to `dh-go-to-market` channel-and-partner-design.
7. **Owner question (only if the person named no owner):** write "owner not named" and ask, before the record line: "Who owns which mechanics ship and the flags for consumer-protection law, and who clears this draft? A name or role in your words."
8. **Record (last line of the answer body):** "Engagement mechanics draft. AI-assisted. No benchmark or target set by the model; no mechanic called safe, effective or compliant." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
9. **Calls made (after the record line):** list.
