# Disclosure rules: registration, results, reporting, authorship

Interpretive reference. Every rule cites a row in `clinical-study-and-rwe-research.md` (CV rows, read 19 October 2026). 42 CFR Part 11 was read from the annual edition revised as of 1 October 2025; later amendments are not reflected (research "Not sourced"). Regulation (EU) No 536/2014 was read as the original Official Journal text.

## Which regime applies (the person decides; the skill lays out the test)

| Regime | Applies when | Row | Who decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICMJE registration | Any clinical trial (prospective assignment to a health-related intervention, devices and behavioral treatments included) whose results go to an ICMJE journal | CV133, CV132 | The person, with the journal |
| US 42 CFR Part 11 | An "applicable device clinical trial": a prospective controlled study of health outcomes of a 510(k), PMA or HDE device, not a small feasibility study; or an applicable drug clinical trial (not phase 1) | CV142, CV143 | The owner the person names for registration, or owner not named. The skill never states that a trial is or is not an applicable clinical trial. |
| EU CTR Article 37 | A clinical trial under Regulation (EU) No 536/2014 | CV150 to CV154 | The owner the person names for registration, or owner not named. Whether a device study falls under the CTR is not in the rows `[GAP]`. |
| WHO joint statement | The sponsor or funder signed the 18 May 2017 joint statement (a pledge, not law) | CV140 | The person |

## Registration

- ICMJE: registered at or before first patient consent for enrollment; the date registration materials were first submitted counts (CV132). Accepted registries: WHO ICTRP primary registers with the 24-item data set, or ClinicalTrials.gov (CV134). WHO Trial Registration Data Set version 1.3.1 has 24 items and includes an IPD sharing statement (CV138). WHO ICTRP itself is not a registry (CV139).
- US: an applicable clinical trial registers no later than 21 calendar days after the first participant is enrolled (CV141). ICMJE is stricter than US law (research "Changes and conflicts").
- Updates (US, trials initiated on or after 18 January 2017): registration information at least every 12 months; overall recruitment status within 30 days of a change (CV149).
- Data sharing plan in the registration for trials enrolling from 1 January 2019 (ICMJE, CV135).
- Non-interventional RWD studies: FDA recommends posting the protocol publicly, for example on ClinicalTrials.gov or ENCePP (CV77); EU applicants register non-interventional studies in the HMA-EMA Catalogue of RWD studies (CV108).

## Results

| Clock | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| US results | No later than 1 year after the primary completion date (date the final participant was examined or treated for final primary outcome data collection, whether completed or terminated) | CV144, CV145 |
| US delayed results | With a certification for a product not yet approved or cleared: 30 days after approval or clearance for a studied use, or after an application is withdrawn for not less than 210 days; in all cases no later than 2 years after the certification | CV146 |
| US extension | Requested before the deadline for good cause; more than one is allowed | CV147 |
| US content | All-cause mortality table; non-serious adverse events above 5 percent in any arm; protocol and SAP with approved amendments | CV148 |
| EU end of trial | Last visit of the last subject, or a later point defined in the protocol; notify each Member State within 15 days | CV150, CV151 |
| EU results | Within one year of the end of the trial in all Member States, regardless of outcome: results summary (Annex IV) with a lay summary (Annex V); later only for scientific reasons stated in the protocol | CV152 |
| EU clinical study report | Within 30 days after a marketing authorization decision or withdrawal, where the trial supports one | CV153 |
| EU halts and interim | A halt not resumed within two years becomes the end date; a protocol-specified interim analysis triggers a summary within one year of that date | CV154 |
| WHO pledge | Summary results 12 months from primary completion; journal publication indicative 24 months from study completion | CV140 |
| ICMJE | Registry posting as a brief structured abstract or tables is not prior publication | CV137 |

Gaps: the content of Annex IV and Annex V, the EU pediatric timeline, the EU CTR date of application and transition end, and any 42 CFR Part 11 change after 1 October 2025 are not sourced `[GAP]`. The day-counting convention for "1 year" is not in the rows `[GAP]`; the scripts add one calendar year and say so.

## Reporting checklists (cite the [PAPER] row and DOI)

| Study | Checklist | Rows and DOI |
|---|---|---|
| Randomized trial | CONSORT 2025: 30 items plus flow diagram, supersedes CONSORT 2010; open science items 2 to 5 (registration, protocol and SAP access, data sharing, funding and conflicts); outcome specification item 14; changes after start item 10; intervention as delivered and fidelity item 24a; new items on patient involvement, harms, analysis populations and missing data | CV110 to CV114, DOI 10.1136/bmj-2024-081123 |
| Trial protocol | SPIRIT 2025: 34 items, supersedes SPIRIT 2013; dissemination plans (item 8); address every item before ethics submission | CV115 to CV117, DOI 10.1136/bmj-2024-081477 |
| Trial of an AI intervention | CONSORT-AI: 14 items added to CONSORT 2010 (algorithm version, input data, human-AI interaction, output, performance errors, code access). It extends the superseded parent; item numbers refer to CONSORT 2010 | CV118 to CV121, DOI 10.1136/bmj.m3164 |
| Protocol of an AI trial | SPIRIT-AI: 15 items added to SPIRIT 2013, including a plan for performance errors | CV122, DOI 10.1136/bmj.m3210 |

## Authorship, AI and data sharing (ICMJE Recommendations, updated January 2026, CV129)

- Authorship needs all four criteria: substantial contribution; drafting or critical revision; final approval; accountability (CV130). Who meets them is the person's and the authors' decision.
- AI and AI-assisted tools are not listed or cited as authors (CV131). AI use must be disclosed (CV131), so a manuscript drafted with this skill discloses it; where in the manuscript ICMJE wants the disclosure, and its wording, are not quoted in the rows `[GAP]`.
- Data sharing statements are required in manuscripts from 1 July 2018 and must say whether IPD will be shared ("undecided" is not acceptable), what, related documents, when and for how long, and access criteria (CV135, CV136).
