ILLUSTRATIVE PROTOCOL EXCERPT (written for testing; not a real trial)

SleepWell-RCT Protocol, version 1.2, 2 September 2026
Sponsor: Nocturne Digital Health Inc.

1. Background. Insomnia affects many adults. Digital CBT-I apps have shown benefit in earlier trials.
2. Objective. To show that the Nocturne app improves insomnia compared with sleep hygiene education.
3. Design. Two-arm, parallel-group randomized trial, 1:1, superiority.
4. Setting. Fully remote in the United States. Participants may complete the week-8 assessment either by video call with a study clinician or by themselves in the app, as they prefer.
5. Participants. Adults 18 to 65 with chronic insomnia (ISI 15 or more at screening). Participants use their own smartphone and may pair their own wrist wearable.
6. Interventions. Nocturne app (version to be confirmed) for 8 weeks versus a sleep hygiene education app.
7. Outcomes. Primary: Insomnia Severity Index. Secondary: total sleep time from the participant's wearable; PHQ-9.
8. Sample size. 300 participants (150 per arm).
9. Randomization. Computer-generated sequence by the CRO statistician.
10. Data. App data flow to the sponsor's cloud and are exported weekly to the CRO's EDC.
11. Statistics. Primary analysis: ANCOVA on ISI at week 8, adjusted for baseline. Missing data will be handled by multiple imputation.
12. Consent. Electronic consent in the app.
13. Ethics. Central IRB approval will be sought.
References: SPIRIT 2013 statement; ICH E6(R2).
