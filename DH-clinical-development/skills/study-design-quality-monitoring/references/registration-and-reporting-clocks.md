# Registration and reporting clocks

Interpretive reference for A2 and B1. Each clock applies only where its instrument applies; whether it does is the call of the owner the person names for regulatory status, or owner not named. Clocks are stated as facts, never as pressure.

## Registration

| Rule | Applies to | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| Register at or before the first patient's consent; the date registration materials were first submitted counts. | Trials reported in ICMJE journals (a condition of consideration for publication) | CV132 |
| Acceptable registries: a WHO ICTRP primary register with the 24-item data set, or ClinicalTrials.gov. | ICMJE | CV134, CV138 |
| Register no later than 21 calendar days after the first participant is enrolled. | US applicable clinical trials | CV141 |
| Applicable device clinical trial: prospective controlled study of health outcomes for 510(k), PMA or HDE devices, excluding small feasibility studies. Applicable drug trial: controlled, other than phase 1. | US definitions | CV142, CV143 |
| Data sharing plan in the registration for trials enrolling from 1 January 2019. | ICMJE | CV135 |
| ICMJE is stricter than US law (before consent versus 21 days after enrollment). | Both | research file "Changes and conflicts" |

## Updates

| Rule | Applies to | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| Registration information updated at least every 12 months; overall recruitment status within 30 days of a change. Rules differ for trials initiated before and from 18 January 2017. | US applicable clinical trials initiated on or after 18 January 2017 | CV149 |

## Results

| Rule | Applies to | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| Results no later than 1 year after the primary completion date (the date the final participant was examined or treated for the primary outcome). | US applicable clinical trials | CV144, CV145 |
| With a certification for an unapproved product: 30 days after approval or clearance, or after withdrawal for 210 days; in all cases no later than 2 years after certification. Extensions for good cause can be requested, more than once. | US applicable clinical trials | CV146, CV147 |
| Results include all-cause mortality, non-serious adverse events above 5 percent in any arm, and the protocol and SAP with approved amendments. | US applicable clinical trials | CV148 |
| End-of-trial notice within 15 days per Member State; results summary with a lay summary within one year of the end of the trial in all Member States; clinical study report within 30 days after a marketing authorization decision. | EU CTR trials (medicinal products) | CV150, CV151, CV152, CV153 |
| A halt not resumed within two years becomes the end date; a protocol-specified interim analysis triggers a results summary within one year. | EU CTR trials | CV154 |
| 12 months from primary completion for summary results and 24 months for publication. A funder pledge, not law. | WHO joint statement signatories | CV140 |
| Posting results in a qualifying registry as a brief structured abstract or tables is not prior publication. | ICMJE | CV137 |

## Changes and amendments

| Rule | Rows |
|---|---|
| The protocol states plans for communicating important modifications (SPIRIT item 31). | SP-31 |
| The trial report states important changes after the trial started, including non-prespecified outcomes or analyses, with reasons (CONSORT 2025 item 10). | CV114 |
| For RWE studies supporting FDA drug decisions, protocol revisions are date-stamped with the rationale for each change. | CV75 |
| For device RWE, finalize protocol and analysis plan before reviewing outcome data and say in the submission whether that was done. | CV94 |
| Reviews: changes to protocol methods are declared transparently. | ES-41 |
