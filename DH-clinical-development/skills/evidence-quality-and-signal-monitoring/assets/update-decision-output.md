# Output format: update decision

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Answer:** the three questions (relevant, good enough, anything new) with their facts, the options open for this purpose, and the one decision the person must make. Any recommendation labeled "proposed".
2. **Options:** Option · Fits when (row) · What it needs. List every option open for the purpose from tasks/update-decision.md Step 2, including living review (ES-104), and say why any is closed. Cite rows individually (for a JCA rerun: ES-155, ES-161, ES-163, ES-169, ES-170), never as a range.
3. **Pass rule:** the person's words and each element against the facts, or "pass rule not given [GAP]".
4. **Open items and owners:** Item · Owner as named, or "owner not named".
5. **AI use** (mandatory if the evidence feeds an EU JCA).
6. **Calls made:** list.
7. **Record:** "AI-assisted. Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft." This line appears in every answer, refusals included; never drop the verify.build_record clause.
8. One line offering the full version.
