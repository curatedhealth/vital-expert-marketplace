# Output format: execution quality review

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Verdict per site against your rules:** pass, miss or not measurable, with the number (for example "Munich: retention 76.9 percent against your 85 percent: miss").
2. **Stall point per missing site:** the first metric that missed.
3. **First hypothesis to test:** one per stall point, labeled hypothesis, with what would confirm it.
4. **Owner:** the person's words, or "owner not named".
5. **Decision needed:** one line.
5a. **Report note:** one line: exceeded ranges and remedial actions are summarized in the clinical trial report (CVB4); important deviations need the investigator's explanation and recurrence measure (CVB1).
6. **Record:** "Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. Rules and owners are yours."
