# Output format: site execution metrics

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Where execution differs most:** one sentence naming the site, the metric and the number (for example "Boston: completeness 77.0 percent against 88.2 percent trial-wide; arm B 71.3 percent").
2. **Table by site:** Site · Enrolled (of target) · Retention % · Important deviations per participant · Completeness % · Arm gap.
3. **Definitions:** one line with the four formulas and the as-of date.
4. **Measure next:** at most three measurements.
5. **Data gaps:** columns missing from the export.
6. **Record:** "Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. Numbers are yours; no verdict without your rules."
