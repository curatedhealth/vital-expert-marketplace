# Review rules by body

Interpretive reference. Every rule cites its row in `EVIDENCE_SYNTHESIS_METHODS.md` (ES rows), read on 19 October 2026 (section 15 rows, ES-171 to ES-234, on 20 October 2026). Where bodies disagree, the table shows each; the person names the target and the rule set follows it. Where a cell says `[GAP]`, the research did not read that body's rule.

## 1. Question and protocol

| Element | Rule | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| PICO | Population, intervention, comparison, outcome; not every element needs equal precision. | ES-36 |

| EU JCA PICO | One PICO is one population, one intervention, one comparator (which can hold more than one treatment) and at least one outcome; scoping aims for the fewest PICOs. The search must cover all PICOs in the assessment scope. | ES-167, ES-159 |

PRESS 2015 elements (abstract only, ES-63): translation of the research question; Boolean and proximity operators; subject headings; text word search; spelling, syntax and line numbers; limits and filters. Best timing: after the MEDLINE strategy is drafted, before translation to other databases (ES-65). The 2015 update found no new elements, and structured PRESS can find search errors and improve term selection (ES-194, abstract only, doi 10.1016/j.jclinepi.2016.01.021). IQWiG uses it because strategies are error-prone (ES-233); PRISMA-S names it for search peer review (ES-234 [PAPER] doi 10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z). These are restatements: no source read lists the checklist questions under each element, and the PRESS full text was not reachable on 20 October 2026 `[GAP]`.

## 3. Reporting the search (PRISMA 2020 and PRISMA-S)

- PRISMA 2020 replaces PRISMA 2009 (ES-02); 27 items in seven sections (ES-03); it is a reporting guide, not an appraisal tool (ES-04).
- Item 6: every source with the date it was last searched (ES-06). Item 7: full strategies with filters and limits (ES-07).
- PRISMA-S, 16 items (ES-25 [PAPER] doi 10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z): each database with its platform (ES-26); registries; citation searching and contacts (ES-27); strategies copied exactly as run (ES-28); limits stated or justified (ES-29); last search date per strategy and search peer review (ES-30); records per source and the deduplication process and software (ES-31). No single database is enough (ES-32).

Records, reports and studies: a record is a title or abstract; records of the same report are duplicates; records of merely similar reports are unique (ES-22). The study, not the report, is the unit (ES-56). Deduplication has no single best method; software plus visual inspection is common (ES-54, ES-55).

## 6. When the review is for an EU JCA: the short list

1. Scope: all PICOs in the assessment scope (ES-159, ES-167).
2. Databases: MEDLINE and CENTRAL at least (ES-151, ES-155); device registries list (ES-161); device sources including the CER and registries (ES-155).
3. Cut-off at most 3 months before submission (ES-152, ES-156), including searches for covariates and indirect-comparison networks (ES-160, ES-168).
4. Strategies in Appendix D.2; PRISMA flowchart; exclusion list with reasons in Appendix D.2.2; RIS files (ES-152, ES-162).
5. Risk of bias: RoB 1 for RCT outcomes, ROBINS-I for comparative non-RCTs, per outcome in scope (ES-163, ES-164). RoB 1 seven domains and Low, High, Unclear criteria: `appraisal-and-certainty.md` section 2a (ES-171 to ES-193).
6. Synthesis: exchangeability; random effects usually, fixed effect only with rigorous justification (ES-165).
7. AI-assisted steps disclosed, a named human responsible for each (ES-169, ES-170).
8. Scope of devices in JCA: class IIb and III with an expert panel opinion, class D IVDs (ES-146); selection criteria include incorporation of AI (ES-147). Whether a product is selected is a fact the owner the person names confirms (or "owner not named"); dh-regulatory-and-quality owns device status.
9. Diagnostic-accuracy search filters: no JCA rule read [GAP]. NICE PMG36 says do not use them (ES-130); if used in a JCA plan, cite it as NICE's rule. Diagnostic-accuracy risk of bias (for example QUADAS-2): not in the sourced rows [GAP]; read the HTACG validity guidance and the QUADAS-2 paper before naming a tool.

## 7. When the review is for NICE

PMG36 (last updated 13 October 2026, ES-126): protocol-driven systematic review (ES-127, ES-128), more than one reviewer on all records and a log of exclusions (ES-129), no diagnostic filters (ES-130), validated risk-of-bias tool for non-randomized studies (ES-131), pooling only with enough comparable valid data and with heterogeneity reported (ES-132). PMG48 HealthTech: existing reviews may be used or updated (ES-133), unpublished evidence with enough detail (ES-134), rapid methods if agreed (ES-135), and the Evidence Standards Framework for digital health technologies (ES-136).
