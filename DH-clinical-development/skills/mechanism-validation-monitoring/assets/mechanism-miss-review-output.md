# Output format: mechanism miss review (draft for the person)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** each missed link (A2), use measure outside the person's threshold (A3) or crossed signal (A4) with its category, the owner (named, or "owner not named"), the person's choice (component change or narrowed claim) or the open options, the version that carries it or the date the person names to look again; for a crossed safety signal, that reportability is the call of the owner the person names for vigilance reporting (dh-regulatory-and-quality audit-capa-and-complaints); the one decision the person needs to make.
2. **Misses:** table Link · What missed (value vs threshold, source) · Category (design, evidence, population) · Owner · Responses the sources name · Person's choice · Version · Rows (each [PAPER] row with its own DOI; abstract-only and [WF] rows labeled).
3. **Claims beside misses:** each claimed effect verbatim beside its link result; "draft for the owner" facts only; wording stops for dh-regulatory-and-quality regulatory-submission-and-dossier.
4. **Routing:** component change to therapeutic mechanism and intervention design A2; change record to dh-regulatory-and-quality document-and-change-control; re-check on the named version.
5. **Record (last line of the answer body):** "Miss review draft. AI-assisted. No claim rewritten; no owner assigned by the model." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
6. **Calls made (after the record line):** list.
