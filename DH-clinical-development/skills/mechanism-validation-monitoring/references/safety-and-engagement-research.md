## Changes and conflicts

1. **2026 classifications shift safety language.** 880.5735 (24 July 2026) requires showing the device "does not adversely impact" health and that patient risk is "minimal" (SE7), and adds psychiatric exclusion statements (SE8). Older software-therapy special controls (882.5801) require only hazard analysis and warnings (SE1). 882.5804 (26 June 2026) requires an adverse-event discussion in labeling (SE3).
2. **Adverse event wording differs.** 882.5803 says "capture all adverse events"; 876.5960, 890.5800 and 886.5500 say "evaluate all adverse events"; 882.5804 says "relevant adverse events"; 880.5735 says "all device-related adverse events observed". A skill should quote the exact regulation for the product code.
3. **eCFR URL form.** research_tm cites 890.5800 under "subchapter-J"; 21 CFR parts 800 to 898 are in subchapter H. This file uses the short form https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-890.5800.
4. **Engagement-linked risks are recognized by FDA.** EndeavorRx's risk table lists screen addiction and decreased sleep quality (SE16), and 882.5705 requires measuring increased sleepiness (SE4). Engagement mechanics can therefore carry risks to health that go into the risk file, not only into growth metrics.
5. **"Engagement" is not a regulatory endpoint.** DHAC 2025 states the engagement-outcome relationship is not well understood (SE26); Perski (SE82) and Sieverink (SE91, SE92) show usage-outcome correlations are often unjustified or confounded. DiGAV requires usage reporting (SE70 to SE73) without making usage a benefit.
6. **Expected side-effects vs serious incidents.** MDR Art. 87(1)(a) excludes from individual reporting only side-effects that are documented and quantified (SE59); unquantified ones are not excluded.
7. **US Negative Option Rule.** The 2024 "click to cancel" amendments are vacated; the pre-2024 prenotification rule (16 CFR 425) is back in force from 12 February 2026 and a new ANPRM is open (SE115, SE116). ROSCA's simple-cancellation duty is statutory and unaffected (SE114). Older sources describing click-to-cancel as in force are out of date.
8. **DSA Art. 25 reach.** Art. 25 applies to online platforms only (SE123), excludes micro and small enterprises (SE124), and defers to the UCPD and GDPR (SE120). For a therapy app sold to consumers, the UCPD (SE126 to SE130; research_cma) is the main EU rule on manipulative design; the DSA matters mainly for app stores (SE67).
9. **DiGAV dates.** Usage AbEM data first due 15 April 2027 (research_ma1); PRO and satisfaction data first due 15 April 2028 (SE75); indication-specific questionnaires from 1 January 2028 (SE76). The DiGAV was read live on 20 October 2026 (version of 27 January 2026), whereas research_tm used a 19 October 2026 copy; the texts used here match.
10. **AI Act Art. 5 and the 2026 omnibus.** Art. 5 quotes are from the 2024 Official Journal text; whether Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 amended Art. 5 was not checked in this pass (research_reg2 EU-17).
11. **MHRA DMHT guidance versions.** Version 1.1 (13 February 2025), 1.2 (3 July 2025) and 1.3 (29 July 2026); version 1.3 changed only the wording for function 11 (SE30).
12. **MHRA AE report cites a secondary study.** The 26 percent figure (SE34) is MHRA's citation of Gomez-Bergin et al. 2023; the study itself was not read.
