Research pass of 20 October 2026 (DH-01.01 Clinical Product Development, row prefix TM). Primary sources only: methods papers read in full text at Europe PMC or PMC (labeled [PAPER] with DOI), PubMed abstracts where full text was not reachable (labeled "abstract only"), the EQUATOR Network's official TIDieR checklist file, eCFR (21 CFR, up to date as of 16 October 2026), FDA De Novo decision summaries and the FDA De Novo database, and the DiGAV as published on gesetze-im-internet.de. ## Method

- Full texts: Europe PMC REST `fullTextXML` (Skivington 2021, Michie 2011, Nahum-Shani 2018, Moore 2015, Carroll 2007, Pinnock 2017, Moberg 2018, Guastaferro and Collins 2021, Campbell 2018); NCBI efetch (db=pmc) for NIH author manuscripts that Europe PMC does not serve as XML (Czajkowski 2015 ORBIT, Collins 2016 MOST). PMC web pages and Europe PMC PDF rendering returned bot challenges, so efetch was used instead.
- Publisher pages bmj.com and link.springer.com returned Cloudflare challenges to curl. TIDieR items were therefore taken from the official checklist Word file on equator-network.org (the PDF version extracts as garbled text). BCT Taxonomy v1 and Bellg 2004 are abstract-only (PubMed). GRADE EtD Part 1 (Alonso-Coello 2016) was read only through WebFetch; its criteria table was not in the fetched text, so the criteria come from Moberg 2018 (GRADE Working Group, full text).
- Regulations: eCFR versioner API, title 21 up to date as of 16 October 2026; eCFR search for "digital therapy" and "behavioral therapy" in title 21 to find every software-therapy classification.
- FDA: De Novo decision summaries DEN160018 and DEN200026 (accessdata.fda.gov PDFs, `pdftotext -layout`) and the De Novo database records for decision dates.
- DiGAV: gesetze-im-internet.de reset the connection on every attempt on 20 October 2026. The text used is the copy downloaded from the same URL on 19 October 2026 by the promotion-and-claims pass (research_cma/raw/digav.html), copied to raw/digav_copy_20261019.html. Rows using it say "Read on 19 October 2026 (copy)".
- Raw files in `raw/`. Quote check: `qcheck_tm.py` (whitespace, ligature and quote-mark normalization; " ... " splits a quote into fragments that are checked separately).

## Changes and conflicts

1. **reSET module counts.** The device description says 61 lessons, 31 core and 30 supplemental; the trial description in the same decision summary says participants were asked to complete "all 32 core modules". The summary does not reconcile the difference (trial used a desktop version; the device is a mobile app).
2. **MOST terminology.** Collins, Kugler and Gwadz 2016 say "optimization criterion"; Guastaferro and Collins 2021 say "optimization objective" and add EASE. Same concept; skills should accept both and prefer the newer term.
3. **Fidelity components disagree across frameworks.** Moore 2015 states there is no consensus on subdividing implementation (fidelity, dose, reach). Carroll 2007 treats dose as part of adherence and quality of delivery and responsiveness as moderators, while the literature it reviews treats all five as fidelity elements. Bellg 2004 uses five different areas (design, training, delivery, receipt, enactment). A skill should name which framework it uses.
4. **Fidelity vs adaptation.** Skivington 2021 and StaRI allow adaptation if core functions or components are kept; Carroll 2007 records that some argue the case for local adaptation "may well have been exaggerated". Specify core components and permitted variation before deployment (TM11, TM117).
5. **Regulation dates.** 882.5803 was codified in 2024 (89 FR 71156, 3 September 2024) although EndeavorRx was granted 15 June 2020; 882.5801 was codified 27 December 2017 after reSET's grant on 14 September 2017. The reSET summary lists the De Novo "date" as 16 May 2016, which is the receipt date, not the decision date.
6. **2026 additions.** Three software-therapy classifications appeared in the CFR in 2026: 886.5500 amblyopia (22 April 2026), 882.5804 fibromyalgia (26 June 2026), 880.5735 diabetes (24 July 2026). Older reference lists that name only 882.5801 and 882.5803 are out of date.
7. **Mechanism wording differs by regulation.** 882.5801, 876.5960, 880.5735 and 890.5800 require the "model of (behavioral) therapy" to be validated "as implemented by the device"; 882.5803, 882.5705 and 886.5500 instead require clinical performance with validated measures and full software description, without a "model of therapy" requirement.
8. **DiGAV read date.** gesetze-im-internet.de reset every connection on 20 October 2026; the DiGAV text is the 19 October 2026 download (version as last amended 27 January 2026).
9. **BCW wording.** Michie 2011 (TM44) contains the sentence "All, apart from reflective motivation, are necessary for a given behaviour"; it is not used as a row because its meaning is unclear without the book.
