# Clinical study design, real-world evidence and evidence communication for digital health products: sourced reference

Research pass of 19 October 2026. Primary sources only: ICH guideline texts (database.ich.org) and EMA's Step 5 publications of them, FDA guidance documents and their FDA landing pages (fda.gov), the European Commission recommendation paper on decentralised elements (health.ec.europa.eu), EMA scientific guideline and program pages (ema.europa.eu), EU legislation from the EU Publications Office cellar, 42 CFR Part 11 from the govinfo.gov annual CFR edition, the ICMJE Recommendations PDF (icmje.org), WHO ICTRP pages (who.int), and peer-reviewed reporting-guideline and framework papers read in full text through the Europe PMC REST service (rows labeled [PAPER] with DOI). No row in this file is marked [WF]; [WF] would mean the page was read through a fetch tool that returns a model-written reading rather than the raw text. Not owner-reviewed.

Evidence class: P (published primary source or published research) for every row, read on 19 October 2026. FDA guidance is nonbinding ("should" means recommended), except where it restates a statute or regulation; ICH guidelines bind only as adopted by each region; 42 CFR Part 11 and Regulation (EU) No 536/2014 are law.

Scope note for skill builders: these sources set required elements, definitions, reporting items and legal deadlines. They do not set accuracy thresholds for digital endpoints, sample sizes or effect sizes; those remain study-specific decisions.

## Changes and conflicts

- **ICH E6(R3) is in force in pieces.** Principles and Annex 1: ICH Step 4 on 6 January 2025, EU effective 23 July 2025, FDA final September 2025. Annex 2 (decentralized, pragmatic, RWD): ICH Step 4 on 3 June 2026, EU effective 15 January 2027. Until then, Annex 2 is adopted but not yet effective in the EU. EMA has also published a consolidated E6(R3) (principles, Annex 1 and Annex 2), legal effective date 15 January 2027.
- **EU DCT recommendation paper version.** The URL path still says 2023-03, but the file now holds Version 02 (1 October 2025, effective 29 October 2025), which supersedes the December 2022 version. Changes listed: a cross-border statement on the front page and an updated national provisions appendix.
- **FDA CDRH RWE guidance.** The 31 August 2017 final guidance is superseded by the final of 18 December 2025 (draft 19 December 2023). Cite the 2025 version.
- **FDA DHT guidance references a draft DCT guidance.** The DHT guidance (December 2023) footnotes "Decentralized Clinical Trials for Drugs, Biological Products, and Devices (May 2023)" as draft. That draft was finalized as "Conducting Clinical Trials With Decentralized Elements" (September 2024).
- **FDA DHT guidance cites ICH E6(R2).** Its other-considerations footnote cites E6(R2) (March 2018). FDA has since issued E6(R3) as final (September 2025).
- **RWD reliability wording differs by source.** FDA EHR/claims guidance (drugs): reliability = accuracy, completeness, traceability. ICH E6(R3) Annex 2: reliability = accuracy, completeness, provenance, traceability. FDA CDRH device guidance: reliability = accrual, quality, integrity; relevance = availability, timeliness, generalizability. A skill should name which framework it applies.
- **Verification and validation terms differ.** FDA DHT guidance: two steps (verification of the measured parameter; validation of the clinical event or characteristic in the population) and says these are not the QSR terms. V3 (a peer-reviewed framework, not FDA policy): three steps (verification, analytical validation, clinical validation). They map roughly but are not identical, and V3 is not an FDA requirement.
- **AI extensions sit on superseded parents.** CONSORT-AI extends CONSORT 2010 and SPIRIT-AI extends SPIRIT 2013. Both parents are now superseded by the 2025 statements, so AI item numbers refer to the old checklists.
- **Results-reporting clocks differ.** US (42 CFR 11.44): 1 year after primary completion date, with certified delays and good-cause extensions. EU (CTR Art 37(4)): 1 year after end of trial in all Member States (end of trial = last visit of last subject unless the protocol says otherwise), plus a lay summary. WHO joint statement: 12 months from primary completion for summary results, 24 months for publication (a funder pledge, not law). ICMJE: registration at or before first patient consent; US law: within 21 days after first enrollment. The ICMJE rule is stricter.
- **WHO TRDS item count.** WHO's page (version 1.3.1) and ICMJE both say 24 items.
