## Method

- Downloaded with curl through the configured proxy, TLS verification on. PDFs converted with `pdftotext -layout`; HTML converted to text with the shared `h2t.py` script; Europe PMC full-text XML stripped of tags. Quotes were copied from those text files.
- training.cochrane.org/handbook now redirects to cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current; chapters were read there. Chapter 4 carries version 6.5.1 (2025); the other chapters carry version 6.5 (2024).
- EU legal texts were taken from the Publications Office cellar (`publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX>`): 32021R2282, 32024R1381, 32025R2086. HTA Coordination Group guidance PDFs were taken from their health.ec.europa.eu publication pages, which state the publication date.

PRESS 2015, PRISMA-ScR and the 2024 rapid-review update were read as Europe PMC abstracts or through the fetch tool only.
- Raw files in `raw/`: `cochrane_ch01`, `ch02`, `ch04`, `ch04_techsupp`, `ch05`, `ch08`, `ch10`, `ch14`, `ch25`, `chii`, `chiv`; `grade`; `epmc_PMC8005924` (PRISMA 2020), `epmc_PMC8005925` (PRISMA 2020 E&E), `epmc_PMC7839230` (PRISMA-S), `epmc_PMC5833365` (AMSTAR 2), `epmc_PMC5062054` (ROBINS-I), `epmc_PMC7557165`, `epmc_PMC12013547`, `epmc_PMC11874353` (rapid reviews); `epmc_abs_*` (abstracts); `prisma_*`; `rob2_page`, `robinsi_page`, `robinsi_v2_page`; `nice_pmg36_*`, `nice_pmg48` (copied from the earlier market-access pass, same source URL); `iqwig_gm_v8`; `eu_32021R2282`, `eu_32024R1381`, `eu_32025R2086`; `jca_*`; `ec_jca_md`, `ec_jca_mp`; `cope_retraction_WF_notes.md`.

## Changes and conflicts

- **RoB 1 versus RoB 2.** Cochrane Handbook chapter 8 presents RoB 2 as the recommended tool for randomized trials, while the HTACG validity guidance (adopted 19 September 2024) asks JCA assessors to use RoB 1 for RCTs, calling it "currently ... preferable" for practical reasons. A dossier for the EU JCA should be ready to present RoB 1, while Cochrane-style reviews use RoB 2.
- **ROBINS-I 2016 versus V2.** ROBINS-I V2 is a draft and only covers cohort studies; it renumbers domains and drops the deviations domain. The 2016 version remains the published tool and the one HTACG guidance cites. riskofbias.info gives two dates for the V2 revision (home page "30 November 2025"; V2 page "20 November 2025").
- **Starting certainty for non-randomized studies.** Cochrane chapter 14 text says NRSI start low, while its Figure 14.2.a lists "studies evaluated with ROBINS-I" with randomized trials at high certainty; the same chapter adds that sound NRSI are generally graded low even with ROBINS-I. Skills should state which convention they apply.
- **Search currency.** Cochrane MECIR C37 allows up to 12 months (preferably six) between last search and publication; the EU JCA requires a cut-off of at most 3 months before dossier submission. IQWiG v8.0 replaced the search update with an up-to-dateness check of ongoing studies for drug assessments.
- **Minimum databases.** EU JCA sets a legal floor of MEDLINE plus CENTRAL; Cochrane names CENTRAL, MEDLINE and Embase as the core three; the 2021 rapid-review guidance limits to the same three; the 2024 rapid-review update [WF] asks for at least two databases.
- **Registries for devices versus medicines.** The device dossier guidance (November 2025) adds WHO ICTRP to the minimum registry list; the medicines guidance (November 2024) does not list it. IQWiG treats ICTRP as standard for non-drug interventions.
- **Screening rigor.** Cochrane MECIR allows single-person title/abstract screening but mandates two people at full text; IQWiG uses two people at all steps; NICE PMG36 asks for more than one reviewer on all records; the rapid-review guidance accepts dual screening of only 20% of abstracts.
- **Rapid-review guidance versions.** The 2021 interim guidance (26 recommendations) has been refined by the 2024 BMJ update (24 recommendations, [WF]); skills should cite the 2024 version where they can verify its wording.
- **Cochrane Handbook versioning.** Chapter 4 is version 6.5.1 (2025); other chapters read are version 6.5 (2024). The ROBINS-I chapter (25) predates ROBINS-I V2.
- **IQWiG versioning.** Version 8.0 (19 December 2025) renumbered information retrieval from chapter 8 to chapter 9; older citations to "chapter 8" refer to version 7.0.
- **NICE PMG36** was updated 13 October 2026 (funding variation); the evidence chapter text was read on that version.
