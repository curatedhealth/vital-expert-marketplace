# Output format: decentralized trial setup

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Framework line:** which rules apply to this trial (for example "Drug trial, EU and US: E6(R3) principles and Annex 1 apply; Annex 2 takes EU effect on 15 January 2027 (CV2)" or "Device investigation: E6(R3) does not name devices (CV4); ISO 14155 [GAP]").
2. **Answer:** the direct answer to the question, with the row behind it.
3. **What the protocol or plan must state:** at most five lines, each with its row.
4. **Open items that block the next document:** each with owner (person's words or "owner not named").
5. **Gaps:** rules not sourced and documents not seen.
6. **Record:** "Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. Visit choices and owners are yours."
