# Output format: data integrity review

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Readiness against your rule:** one sentence, quoting the person's rule (for example "Against your rule 'all primary-endpoint controls met before first participant': not met; 3 controls open").
2. **Open items that block it:** Control · What is missing · Row · Owner (person's words or "owner not named").
3. **First to close:** the item others depend on, and why.
4. **Not assessable:** controls without a rule or without evidence.
5. **Record:** "Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. Rules and owners are yours."
