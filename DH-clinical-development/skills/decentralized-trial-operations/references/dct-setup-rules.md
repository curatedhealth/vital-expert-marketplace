# Decentralized trial setup: the rules and where they come from

Interpretive reference for task A1. Every rule cites its row in `clinical-study-and-rwe-research.md` (CV) or `gcp-quality-rows.md` (CVB). Read the row before quoting it to the person. Where a row is guidance, say "recommends"; where it is law, say "requires".

## 1. Which framework applies

| Question | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Does ICH E6(R3) cover this trial? | E6(R3) applies to interventional trials of investigational products; its footnote names drugs, medicines, medicinal products, vaccines and biological products. Devices are not named. | CV4 |
| Is E6(R3) in force? | Principles and Annex 1: ICH Step 4 on 6 January 2025, EU effect 23 July 2025, FDA final guidance September 2025. Annex 2 (decentralized, pragmatic, RWD): ICH Step 4 on 3 June 2026, CHMP adoption 25 June 2026, EU effect 15 January 2027. | CV1, CV2, CV3 |
| Has FDA adopted Annex 2? | Not checked in the research pass. | `[GAP]` (research "Not sourced") |
| Device investigation | The device GCP standard (ISO 14155) and EU MDR investigation rules were not read. Say so; do not borrow E6(R3) as if it bound a device study. | `[GAP]` |
| EU Clinical Trials Regulation | Regulation (EU) No 536/2014 is the regulation on clinical trials on medicinal products for human use. Whether a device investigation falls under it is not answered by the rows read. | CVB7 |
| US expectations | FDA requirements are the same with or without decentralized elements. | CV66 |
| EU expectations | The EU recommendation paper (Version 02, 1 October 2025, in effect 29 October 2025, not legally binding) says data from decentralized trials supporting marketing authorization meet the same expectations as on-site trials. | CV95, CV98 |

## 2. What counts as a decentralized element

- Trial activities outside the investigator's location, including remote interactions and DHT use for visits, procedures and data collection (Annex 2). CV19
- A DCT in FDA's words: a trial with activities at locations other than traditional sites. CV66
- A decentralized element identified as critical-to-quality (ICH E8) needs a risk-proportionate approach (EU paper). CV96; proportionality principle CV7.

## 3. Visit modality

- The protocol states which visits are at sites, which are remote and which are left to the participant's choice; free choice can bias results (for example, sicker participants may prefer remote visits). CV67
- Study records capture visit type (telehealth or in person), location, date and data originator. CV69
- Non-inferiority: comparator effect sizes measured at sites may not hold when assessed remotely; FDA recommends consulting the review division before a non-inferiority DCT. CV72. The consultation itself stops for the owner the person names for regulatory contact, or owner not named.

## 4. Consent

- Remote consent may be considered; in person or remote, the investigator confirms the participant's identity. CV16
- EU paper: remote consent conversations in real time with audio and video, identity checks both ways; on-site offered unless justified. CV100
- EU paper: the participant can download a protected copy of the e-signed form; any modification invalidates the signatures. CV101
- FDA DHT guidance: consent explains who may access DHT data and that terms of service may allow sharing outside the trial. CV61

## 5. Who does what

- Local health care providers may perform tasks within their normal clinical practice that need no detailed protocol knowledge; protocol-specific tasks go to trained trial personnel. CV70
- Representatives of intended participants and health care professionals should be involved in designing systems where relevant (Annex 1); early patient involvement can check DHT suitability, digital literacy and technology access (Annex 2). CV10, CV25, CV30

## 6. Monitoring approach

- The monitoring plan considers decentralized settings and may mix on-site, remote and centralized monitoring. CV17
- FDA recommends risk-based monitoring and centralized monitoring for missing, inconsistent and outlying data and potential deviations. CV71
- Contingency plan for malfunction of a digital tool or a disrupted decentralized visit, for critical-to-quality elements (EU paper). CV99

## 7. Submission and registration items

| Item | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| EU clinical trial application | Summarize planned decentralized elements in the cover letter. The national provisions appendix of Version 02 was updated; its content per Member State was not read. | CV97, CV95; national content `[GAP]` |
| Registration for journal publication | ICMJE: register at or before first patient consent; the date materials were first submitted counts. | CV132 |
| US applicable clinical trials | Register no later than 21 calendar days after the first participant is enrolled. Applicable device clinical trials are defined in 42 CFR 11.10. | CV141, CV142 |
| Acceptable registries | WHO ICTRP primary register with the 24-item data set, or ClinicalTrials.gov. | CV134, CV138 |

ICMJE is stricter than US law on timing (registration before first consent, not within 21 days after first enrollment). Say both when both apply.

## 8. DHT choice at setup (detail in `remote-capture-rules.md`)

- If participants may bring their own device, sponsor-provided DHTs and connectivity are offered so no one is excluded for lacking them. CV53
- Use the same data collection method in all arms. CV58

## What the sources do not set

No source here sets how many visits may be remote, a minimum share of in-person visits, a usability pass rate or a validation threshold. Those are the person's decisions, recorded in their words.
