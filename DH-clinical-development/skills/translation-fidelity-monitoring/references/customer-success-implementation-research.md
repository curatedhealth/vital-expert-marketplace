## Changes and conflicts

- **CFIR 2009 vs 2022.** 2009 had 5 domains with 37 constructs listed by domain (8+4+12+5+8). 2022 has 48 constructs and 19 subconstructs. Implementation Climate and Readiness for Implementation were removed and repositioned in the Outcomes Addendum. Skills should use the 2022 names (for example "Innovation" rather than "Intervention characteristics") and state which version they apply.
- **RE-AIM "E".** The 1999 abstract says "efficacy"; re-aim.org now says "Effectiveness (or Efficacy)" and adds negative effects, quality of life and economic outcomes.
- **Overlapping terms.** Proctor's sustainability maps to RE-AIM maintenance. Proctor's penetration is "similar to reach" (Proctor's own crosswalk). Adoption is defined in both Proctor (intention, decision or action to try) and RE-AIM (number, proportion and representativeness of settings and staff). A skill should pick one definition per metric and name it.
- **SAFER Guides count.** 42 CFR 495.24 still says "all nine SAFER Guides". The 2025 ONC set has eight guides, and ONC says the 2016 versions still apply for the CMS measures. The eCFR text read names reporting "In 2022"; later-year requirements were not confirmed in this pass.
- **FDA HF guidance date.** The FDA download now carries "Document issued on August 3, 2026" (originally 3 February 2016). The training passages quoted are from the 2026 file. A comparison with the 2016 text was not done.
- **DTAC home and status [WF].** The transform.england.nhs.uk DTAC page says its content stopped updating on 31 March 2026 and that links redirect to digital.nhs.uk from April 2026. Updated form from 24 February 2026; the previous form is not to be used from 6 April 2026.
- **DCB0129 and DCB0160 under review [WF].** NHS England states a strategic review is underway. No completion date or replacement was given. DCB0160 current release Amd 25/2018.
- **ACCESS targets are period-specific.** Thresholds (50 percent OAT, PHQ-9 5-point, GAD-7 4-point) apply to the Effective Period 5 July 2026 to 31 December 2027. CMS says it may update measures and targets.
- **Breach notice timing differs by regime.** HIPAA business associate: without unreasonable delay, at most 60 calendar days. GDPR processor: without undue delay, no fixed limit. A contract may set shorter limits; those are set by the person.
