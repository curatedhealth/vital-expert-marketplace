# Output format: the full report (Outcome list) and the direct answer

The session ends with the full report, delivered in one go (references/facilitation.md). During the session, use the turn template only.

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

150 to 300 words, hard cap 300: the answer first (for a statement check: the rewritten statement and every defect, including a missing context or party); the rule it rests on in one plain line, no clause numbers; at most three gaps; one record line. End with one offer: continue the session, or write up the list now with what is known.

## Full report (Outcome list)

Written for someone who was not in the conversation. It opens with the decision so it reads with Decision Record sections 1 and 2. Outcomes are grouped by value driver, never ranked.

### Summary

Three lines, counts taken from sections 2 and 3 (check them against the tables): how many outcomes, for which parties, under which drivers; how many have a measurable objective; the main gap.

### 1. The decision

One sentence, owner, goal (from the frame). "Not framed" if absent.

### 2. Outcomes, by value driver

For each driver:

**[Driver]** ([dimension])

| # | Outcome | For whom | Level | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [direction] [object] [context] | [party] | strategic / category / mission (proposed until accepted) | person (O) or outcome library (V) | accepted by [name] / proposed |

Outcomes that contribute to no driver are listed last, flagged.

### 3. Objectives and measures

| Outcome | Measure (unit) | Baseline (source, date) | Target (date) | Leads or lags | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Outcomes not yet held to a measure: one line listing them.

### 4. Restated solutions (options for the Intelligence Core)

"[What the person said]" restated as "[outcome]". The solution is carried as an option, not chosen.

### 5. Not yet outcomes

Statements that could not be put in the standard form, in the person's words.

### 6. Ready for scoring

The list as it will go on the score sheet, numbered, with the scorers named if known. Only outcomes accepted by the person and linked to a driver go here; hold the others back by name with the reason ("not yet accepted", "no driver"). "Scores are given in *Outcome Prioritization*, privately, before discussion."

### 7. Gaps

Every gap, most blocking first, merged where they close the same way.

### 8. Record

Draft prepared with an AI assistant on DATE. Cleared by NAME, or "uncleared draft". Library calls made: yes or no (connector unavailable).
