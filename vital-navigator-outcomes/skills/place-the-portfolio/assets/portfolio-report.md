# Output format: the full report (Portfolio view) and the direct answer

The session ends with the full report, delivered in one go (references/facilitation.md). During the session, use the turn template only.

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

150 to 300 words, hard cap 300: where the option lands and why (value, oversight needed, capacity), first; one line on who scored capacity; at most three gaps; one record line. End with one offer.

## Full report (Portfolio view)

Supports Decision Record section 6 (route). Hard cap 1,000 words including tables.

### Summary

Three lines: what goes where; what capacity says about the portfolio; the owner's result, if chosen, and where its option sits.

### 1. The decision and the options

One sentence, owner; the options placed; the baselines named as reference lines, not placed.

### 2. Capacity and consequence

Oversight the organization can carry today: group average, range, scorers; disagreement if any. Options the owner marked too costly to delegate, with the owner's reason.

### 3. Placement

| Option | Value (outcome fit) | Oversight needed | Fit | Bucket |
|---|---|---|---|---|

The grid as text (two rows by two columns), and "Support, do not automate" beneath with each option's underlying bucket. Value split and its source. One line per bucket on what it means for the options in it. Options close to a line named.

### 4. Lenses

Cost against benefit; capability against capacity (augmentation or automation play); productivity against differentiation; internal against external. Short groupings with buckets beside; at most two patterns named.

### 5. Views recorded

Any owner or scorer disagreement with a placement, as a view; the computed bucket stands.

### 6. Gaps and record

Every gap, most blocking first (a missing baseline is not a placement gap). Never write "start", "can start", "fund" or "first investment" about a bucket. "A decision aid, not a measurement." Draft prepared with an AI assistant on DATE; cleared by NAME or "uncleared draft: name the clearer"; placement stored on a Navigator brief: yes or no.
