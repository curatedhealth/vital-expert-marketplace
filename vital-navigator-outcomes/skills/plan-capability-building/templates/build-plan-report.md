# Output format: the full report (Capability build plan) and the direct answer

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

150 to 300 words, hard cap 300: the answer first; at most three gaps; one record line; one offer as a statement.

## Full report

Feeds the route and the assumptions and risks in the decision record. Hard cap 1,000 words. Asks nothing except a pending owner choice.

### Summary

Three lines: which gaps are planned, by whom; what waits on them; what is deferred.

### 1. The register it plans from

Top gaps with weighted gap and the outcomes behind them (from *Strategic Readiness*). "Maturity assessment not in use yet."

### 2. Build plan

| Capability | Action (their words) | Owner (role) | Aims for (required, 1 to 10; "8.5 (default)" when the person stated none) | Check by |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### 3. What waits

| Capability | Options waiting | Delivery steps waiting |
|---|---|---|

Flags: options waiting on an unplanned gap.

### 4. Deferred

Capability, reason (owner's words).

### 5. Gaps and record

Every gap, most blocking first. Draft prepared with an AI assistant on DATE; cleared by NAME or "uncleared draft: name the clearer".
