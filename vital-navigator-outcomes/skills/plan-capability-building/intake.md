## C4 Execution intake (8 November 2026)

Send this as one message for a C4 capability build plan. Anything not supplied becomes [GAP].

**A. The register.** The strategic gap register rows (capability, current 1 to 10, required 1 to 10, weighted gap), each score supplied by a person, with its source. No AI-made maturity percentages. Without the register: run Strategic Readiness first, or carry the gap.

**B. What waits.** For each gap: the options or delivery steps that need it closed, the date each is meant to scale, and whether you accept that scale stays conditional until the capability is accepted.

**C. The build.** For each top gap: the action in your words, the approach (build, borrow, transfer, partner), the owner (a person or role, never the assistant), the check date, and what would count as accepted: an observation, a work sample, a paired review, an independent re-performance. Attendance and contract completion are not acceptance.

**D. Transfer or handover.** If work is borrowed or transferred: the deliverables, who receives it, and the evidence the receiver can do it unaided. A vendor appears only if you name it and say where the name came from.

**E. Oversight.** If AI-delegated work waits on this gap: who oversees it, hours per week available, evidence of competence (observed, not training completion), and who has authority to stop or escalate.

**F. Prerequisites.** Which other capabilities must be accepted first, with dates. Cycles and missing prerequisites are refused.

**G. Mandatory constraints.** MLR and other mandatory reviews stay in force; no plan waives them, and training is not a waiver.

Never invent figures, names, vendors, quotes or study titles. Never choose the action, owner or date. "Just fill it in" is refused and left as [GAP].
