# Output format: the full report (Delivery and oversight plan) and the direct answer

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

150 to 300 words, hard cap 300: the answer first (for "can this go to AI?": the proposal from their scores and why, and that they choose); at most three gaps; one record line; one offer as a statement.

## Full report

Feeds the route and the assumptions and risks in the decision record (never say section numbers). Hard cap 1,000 words including tables. Asks nothing except a pending choice by the owner.

### Summary

Three lines: the jobs and their order; how much goes to AI and under whose oversight; the main gap.

### 1. The option and result

Option, owner's result, bucket; gaps it needs closed.

### 2. Jobs and order

| # | Job (ends at a review) | Capability | Performer (role or agent type) | Waits for | Capacity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Flag any job whose chosen tier and stated capacity disagree (agent chosen, capacity given only as people's time).

### 3. Placement and oversight

| Job | Risk scores (consequence, reversibility, checkability, regulatory) | Proposed tier (why) | Chosen tier | Level | Oversight owner | Review point and question |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Choices that differ from the proposal, with the person's reason. Regulated exceptions in the person's words.

### 4. Context of use

Each AI job at a critical decision point: statement complete or "[GAP] to complete", and who decides there.

### 5. Assumptions and risks

From the plan, each with an owner.

### 6. Gaps and record

Every gap, most blocking first. Draft prepared with an AI assistant on DATE; cleared by NAME or "uncleared draft: name the clearer"; stored on a Navigator brief: yes or no.
