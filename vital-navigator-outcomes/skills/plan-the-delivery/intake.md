## C4 Execution intake (8 November 2026)

Send this as one message for a C4 delivery plan. Anything not supplied becomes [GAP]; the checker treats a missing value as unknown, never as feasible.

**A. Work packages.** For each step: id, name, the output it ends with, the review gate, what it depends on (step ids), constraints on who may act (regulated, privacy, MLR).

**B. Capacity, per resource.** Resource name (a role or a pool, matching the steps), what the person can supply (FTE, or hours per week) with where that figure comes from (rota, HR plan, owner statement), and a window for each step (YYYY-MM or YYYY-MM-DD) if dates exist. One unit per resource: we do not convert hours to FTE for you. Missing availability stays unknown.

**C. People.** Who owns each step and who oversees each delegated step. Humans only; roles are fine, names are fine, "the assistant" and "the team" are not. Who chose them (the owner, not the assistant).

**D. Risk and tier.** The four 1 to 10 risk scores from the people in the room (consequence, reversibility, checkability, regulatory). The tier you choose, and your reason when it differs from the proposal. For irreversible work given to an agent: who can stop it, how, and what happens next (the fallback).

**E. Premortem.** The failure prompt used, each person's independent contribution (written before anyone read the others), whether the round-robin happened, and anything an AI suggested, labeled as an additional hypothesis. No statistic about premortem effectiveness unless you hold the primary paper.

**F. Upstream.** The option and the owner's result, the bucket, the gaps it needs closed, any trial design. Missing: [GAP].

Stop and ask once if the decision owner is missing. Never invent a figure, a name, a quote, a peer comparison or a study title. Never score, choose, weight or sign for the person; "just fill it in" is refused and left as [GAP].
