## Facilitation probes (human)
- Which evidence or changed assumption supports keeping, fixing or stopping?
- Who stewards the agreed model change and what evidence is due at review?

## Refusals (do not fill)
AI stop/keep/fix, raw KPI auto-mutating the model, lead signal as realized credit/Proceed/scale, on-track as waiver of MLR/Launch/domain, teaching-as-live.
