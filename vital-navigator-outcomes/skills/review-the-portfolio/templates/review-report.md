# Output format: the full report (Portfolio review) and the direct answer

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

150 to 300 words, hard cap 300: the evidence first; at most three gaps; one record line; one offer as a statement. A request to decide is a refusal with the review sheet.

## Full report

Hard cap 1,000 words. Asks nothing except a pending owner or steward decision.

### Summary

Three lines: what is stopped, kept, fixed; what changes in the model and by whose decision; what scales.

### 1. Activities

| Activity | Outcome | Evidence | Decision (by) | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Flags: traces to no outcome; keep and watch.

### 2. Learning

Re-score candidates (outcome, evidence, steward, decision and reason, or pending with review-by date). Readiness re-run (factors and scores, no index; capability gaps re-scored or pending). Direction change (proposal, two approvers, or none).

### 3. Scaling

| Activity | What is scaled | Owner | Order |
|---|---|---|---|

Record decisions as made; never grade them ("the right resolution"). Evidence dates are taken as given. Hand-ons to Delivery and Oversight Planning are offered ("can go"), not required.

### 4. Gaps and record

Every gap, most blocking first. Draft prepared with an AI assistant on DATE; cleared by NAME or "uncleared draft: name the clearer"; recorded on a Navigator brief: yes or no.
