## Method notes (routing)

1. **Family test first.** If the ask needs Launch Pass/Conditional/Hold/Escalate, READY chairs, or DD valuation grades, stop and name the sibling — do not partially perform their job.
2. **Entry shapes.** Top-down (goal clear), middle-out (capability without stake), bottom-up (tool looking for a problem). Middle/bottom always pass through frame + size before options.
3. **One question per turn.** Classification is a human-confirmed gate when ambiguous.
4. **GenAI vertical.** Tool-named asks ("we want GPT agents") are reframed as investment decisions; the tool becomes an option, not the decision.
