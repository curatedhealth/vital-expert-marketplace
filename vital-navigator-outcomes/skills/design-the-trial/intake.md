## C4 Execution intake (8 November 2026)

Send this as one message for a C4 trial design. Anything not supplied becomes [GAP].

**A. Scope first.** Is this an internal, nonclinical process question? Say the scope type. If anyone is unsure whether it touches clinical, regulatory, privacy or GxP duties, who assessed it, the reference or date, and the outcome. If the answer is clinical, stop: this skill redirects to the qualified clinical workflow and does not draft the design. Also: data classes involved (personal, health, none).

**B. The decision.** What next step could change because of this trial, and what is the uncertainty? A trial that informs no decision is a demonstration.

**C. Hypothesis and measure.** What you expect to see, the one measure, today's level with its source, the direction, the target and by when. The target is set by a human; say who set it and when. If it ever changes after results, there must be a revision reference.

**D. Stop and control.** The stop rule, who holds stop authority (a person), how they stop it (the accessible mechanism), and the fallback. If an agent performs any part of the trial: its mode (agent or agent with review). Reversibility score 1 to 10 for what the trial touches.

**E. Cost and worth.** Value and cost-to-test as low, likely, high, with unit and basis. A single point is refused.

**F. Premortem.** Failure prompt, independent human contributions, round-robin done or not, AI suggestions labeled as additional hypotheses. A premortem is not evidence that the trial would work.

**G. Oversight.** Who oversees the trial, with how much time. Local target reach never approves scale or certifies ROI; the owner decides next steps.

Never invent figures, names, quotes, peer comps or study titles. Never score, choose, weight or sign. "Just fill it in" is refused and left as [GAP].
