# Output format: the full report (Trial design) and the direct answer

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

150 to 300 words, hard cap 300: what the design has and lacks, first (target, baseline, stop rule); the cost-against-value reading if both ranges exist; at most three gaps; one record line; one offer as a statement.

## Full report

Feeds the route and the assumptions and risks in the decision record. Hard cap 1,000 words. Asks nothing except a pending owner choice (the target).

### Summary

Three lines: what the trial tests and for whom; how success and stopping are defined; worth against cost.
