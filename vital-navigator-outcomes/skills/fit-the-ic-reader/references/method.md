## C6 validator-aligned method (C6 Phase 1, 8 November 2026)

Each named section below matches a content check in `scripts/reader_fit_check.py`. The check fails on the packet itself with no confess flag; flags are secondary (see `gap-rules.md`). Rule ids and dispositions: `diligence/V_C6_RULE_CODE_CONTENT_MATRIX_2026-11-08.md`.

### Reader must-do (V-C6-READER-01)
`one_pager.must_do` is one plain sentence naming what the reader must do (endorse, mandate, supply, hold). It is separate from `asks`: asks list requests and owners; `must_do` is the reader's job. A paper that only describes does not pass. The assistant does not invent a board mandate. LA-C6-004 is nonmandatory guidance, so the one-sentence form is a house rule.

### Fit of summary to decision (V-C6-READER-02, deferred)
The script cannot tell whether brevity hid a limit. By hand: every material limit, dissent and source in the working file is either on the one-pager or named as available on request. Flag `brevity_hides_limits` records a confession, not a check. Tracked as D-C6-READER-02.

### Reader Hold is a register
An IC or board Hold is the reader's register entry. It is not a Navigator close vocabulary and not a Launch Hold.

### One-pager contract
decision, options (at least two, including do nothing where applicable), value at stake with an evidence class, what it turns on, integrity posture (verified or not tool-checked), and asks of type Endorse, Mandate, Supply or Hold with request and who.
