# Outcomes: private score sheet

Score alone, before any discussion. Return to the facilitator only. All scores are revealed together; the decision owner's are shown after the group's.

**Decision:** [decision statement]

**Your name:**

For each outcome, two whole numbers from 1 to 10. This is your judgment; there are no right answers.

- **Importance:** 1 = not important, 10 = critically important, for this decision and the people the outcome is for.
- **Satisfaction with today:** 1 = not satisfied at all, 10 = fully satisfied with how well this is achieved today, by any means.

These are the ODI / JTBD ranking **axes**. The facilitator computes opportunity as **Importance + max(0, Importance − Satisfaction)** (Strategyn opportunity algorithm — not Importance × Satisfaction as a product). Room scores are labeled workshop judgments unless field-verified.
