# Audience calibration, directions and dimensions

## Who is in the room sets the level

Owner ruling, 19 October 2026 (method.md O9). Say which level you chose and why; the person may change it.

| Audience | Language to use | Outcome level offered | Measure level offered |
|---|---|---|---|
| CEO, C-suite | Enterprise results, years | Strategic and category | Headline metrics |
| Head of function, VP | Function results, months | Category | Category measures |
| Head of department, director | Department delivery, weeks | Category and mission | Category measures and measures |

Mixed room: start at the most senior person's level; others drill down. Within an investment decision, stop at mission level. Job and atomic outcomes belong to execution design.

The library today: 15 strategic outcomes, 60 category outcomes and about 187 mission outcomes, all active; a function pack exists only for Medical Affairs (LANE, 19 October 2026).

## The six directions of change

| Direction | Use when |
|---|---|
| Minimize | Less is better and the floor is zero (time, errors, risk) |
| Reduce | Less is better, without implying zero |
| Eliminate | The thing should not happen at all |
| Maximize | More is better with no natural ceiling |
| Increase | More is better, by a stated amount |
| Maintain | The level is right and must hold under change |

## Value dimensions

The outcome takes the dimension of the value driver it contributes to (method.md O6). Say it in words:

| Code | Say | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| $V | Financial | revenue, cost, margin, return on investment |
| OV | Operational | efficiency, speed, throughput |
| CV | Clinical or scientific | evidence quality, patient outcomes |
| RV | Risk | exposure, compliance, safety |
| KV | Capability | skills, knowledge, organizational capacity |
| SV | Strategic | market position, competitive advantage |

## Parties

Typical parties: patient, caregiver, prescriber or clinician, payer or assessor, regulator, an internal role (medical writer, field medical lead, brand team). An internal team is a valid party when the outcome is about their work experience; it is not the party just because it does the work.
