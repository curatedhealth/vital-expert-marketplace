# Task: No fabrication

Input: draft text or exhibit claims; list of supplied sources/notes
Atom: compose-draft
Tool: none (Verify is handoff-verify, after this)
Template: templates/repair-note.md
Output: repaired draft + repair note of every cut
Check: no remaining assertive figure without a source line
Human: named owner accepts cuts (human only on release)

## What counts as invented
Study/trial names; sales/share/NPV/IRR; peer comps; competitor actions; quotes; causes; “first/largest/only”; dates presented as current without a source.

## Guard
1. List what was supplied (one line each). If nothing was supplied and facts are needed, ask before drafting.
2. Draft or read sentence by sentence against the material.
3. Where the request wants something the material lacks: `[GAP: not supplied — <what> — <what would close it>]`.
4. Refuse “just put a typical number”.

## Repair
Classify each factual claim: supported; altered; not found; conflicting. Keep / restore / gap / ask. Never delete silently — every change is in the repair note.
