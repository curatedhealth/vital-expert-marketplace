### Grounding fields (V-C6-GROUND-01)
Every claim with `action: keep` and non-empty `numbers` carries at least one non-empty `methods` (how the figure was derived), `data_assumptions` (population, period, scope, version) or `assumption_note` (what is assumed and why it is tolerable). An owner estimate is kept as an owner estimate with an `assumption_note`, not dressed up as a measurement. A claim with none of the three becomes `gap` or `remove`. LA-C6-006 grounds the practice of documenting data, assumptions and methods.

### Source and relative context
`keep` also needs `source_id`; relative figures need `absolute_present`; `remove` needs a `repair_note`. Money fields never take ODI opportunity scores; cashable keeps need a `realization_action`.

### Claim, evidence, warrant (V-C6-GROUND-02, deferred)
Write each claim with its grounds and the warrant that links them, plus a qualifier and the strongest counter-evidence supplied. The script does not check warrants (D-C6-GROUND-02); a citation is not a warrant and provenance is not truth. LA-C6-007 is an educational account, not evidence that the method improves decisions.

### No fact creation (V-C6-GROUND-03)
No invented figures or sources, no ODI-to-money, no Option A conversion bridge, no rewrite of a C5 measured figure or Owner L2 recognition.
