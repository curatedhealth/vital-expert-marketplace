### Reader need first (V-C6-SUB-01, deferred)
Before cutting, write down who reads this and what they need from it. The script has no field for the need, so this is a by-hand check (D-C6-SUB-01). Cut what the reader does not need; keep what they need even if it is long.

### Active language without invented people (V-C6-SUB-02)
Active voice should say who acts, using only names the source gave. A rewrite that introduces a person name absent from `before` fails unless the name is listed in `attributed_names` with its source. Use the role the source used when no name was given. The check is a `[CHECK]` heuristic: titled names and two capitalized words; single-word names are not caught, so read the rewrite.

### Semantic preservation (V-C6-SUB-03)
Cutting must not add facts or remove limits. New numbers in `after` fail unless listed in `new_numbers_allowed` with a source. If `before` carries a qualifier or dissent marker (however, unless, caveat, limitation, preliminary, not tool-checked, ...), `after` keeps one. Material limits move, they do not vanish. LA-C6-005: clarity cannot alter facts or caveats; no arbitrary word cap.

### Shorter and so-what
