# Output format: the full report (Capability and gap register) and the direct answer

The session ends with the full report, delivered in one go (references/facilitation.md). During the session, use the turn template only.

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

150 to 300 words, hard cap 300: the biggest gaps first and the outcomes behind them; one line on the scorers (how many; indicative or not); disagreement in one line; at most three gaps; one record line. End with one offer: continue the session, or write up the register now.

## Full report (Capability and gap register)

Drafts the capability and gap section of the decision record (never say section numbers to the reader). Hard cap 1,000 words including tables. Say each fact once; any explanation of why the room split is marked H unless the scorer said it.

### Summary

Three lines: the top gaps and the outcomes they hold back; how far the room agrees; the main thing not yet measurable.

### 1. The decision and the outcomes

One sentence, owner; the attested top outcomes with their scores (or "provisional: priorities not attested").

### 2. Capability and gap register

| Rank | Capability | Serves | Current | Required | Gap | Weight from outcomes | Weighted gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Group averages, computed, scored in the room. Scorers named; "indicative" if fewer than three. One line per top gap on why it leads. Capabilities serving no scored outcome listed last, flagged.

### 3. The owner's view

After the group's: the owner's ranking of the top gaps and where it differs. Not reconciled.

### 4. Not yet measurable

Data readiness, change capacity, risk appetite: the owner's view (O) or a gap each.

### 5. Disagreement

| Capability | Criterion | Range | Low end (who) | High end (who) | View recorded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 6. Carried to option comparison

The top gaps each option must address; restated tools carried as options.

### 7. Gaps

Every gap, most blocking first, merged where they close the same way.

### 8. Record

Draft prepared with an AI assistant on DATE. Cleared by NAME, or "uncleared draft: name the clearer". Computed locally with the Navigator's own gap formula; library calls: yes or no.
