# Output format: the full report (Options and result) and the direct answer

The session ends with the full report, delivered in one go (references/facilitation.md). During the session, use the turn template only.

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

150 to 300 words, hard cap 300: how the options compare against the two reference lines, first; the trade-off in one line per option; one line on the scorers (how many; indicative or not); sensitivity in one line; at most three gaps; one record line. End with one offer. Never a recommendation.

## Full report (Options considered; result and route)

Drafts the options and recommendation sections of the decision record (never say section numbers to the reader). Hard cap 1,000 words including tables.

### Summary

Three lines: how the options compare with doing nothing and doing it internally; the main trade-off; the owner's result, or "pending".

### 1. The decision, outcomes and gaps

One sentence, owner; top outcomes; top capability gaps.

### 2. Options considered

| Option | Serves | Closes or needs | AI involvement (proposed by) |
|---|---|---|---|

Baselines listed first as reference lines.

### 3. Scores against the reference lines

| Rank | Option | Outcome fit | Cost | Time | Reversibility | Risk | Governance burden | Composite | vs do nothing | vs do it internally |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Reference rows for the two baselines below. Group averages, computed, scored in the room; scorers named; "indicative" under three; weights equal, or the change and reason with the unadjusted ranking. One line per option on the trade-off.

### 4. Does it hold?

What was tested and what changed (leader under each scorer left out and each weight ±20%). Ties named.

### 5. The owner's view

After the group's; where it differs. Not reconciled.

### 6. Result and route

"Result: [Proceed / Investigate first / Handle it internally], for [option], chosen by [owner], [date]. Reason: [owner's words]. Route: [next step, who leads]." Or "Result pending: [owner]" with the question.

### 7. Disagreement

| Option | Criterion | Range | Low end (who) | High end (who) | View recorded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 8. Gaps and record

Every gap, most blocking first. Draft prepared with an AI assistant on DATE; cleared by NAME or "uncleared draft: name the clearer"; scores stored on a Navigator brief: yes or no.
