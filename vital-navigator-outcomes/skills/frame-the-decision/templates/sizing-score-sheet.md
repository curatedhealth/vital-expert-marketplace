# Decision sizing: private score sheet

Send one copy to each scorer. Ask them to score alone, before any discussion, and to return it to the facilitator only. Scores are revealed together.

**Decision:** [the accepted decision statement]

**Your name:**

Give a whole number from 1 to 10 for each line. There are no right answers; this is your judgment.

| Criterion | 1 means | 10 means | Your score |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stakes | A wrong call is cheap | A wrong call is costly for the organization | |
| Reversibility | Very hard to reverse | Easy to reverse | |
| Commitment horizon | A few weeks | Several years | |
| Functions involved | One function | Many functions | |

Optional: one line on why, for any score you feel strongly about.

Your scores are used to size the decision and choose how deep the work should go. They are not a vote on whether to invest.
