# Governance

Source: the stop rules bound to the Digital Health Access & Reimbursement Lead on the VITAL platform, rewritten for pricing and value communication work on the buyer's assistant, plus the sourced review rule in PC-US-33 (FDA recommends review by qualified medical or scientific, legal and regulatory personnel before a CFL communication is released).

## May proceed

Money flow maps on the person's description, comparator price tables from registered records, value-based anchors and budget arithmetic on the person's numbers, and value message drafts, all as advisory drafts.

## The person decides

- The value unit, the corridor and both ends, the price, the comparators kept, the willingness-to-pay threshold, the audiences and the outcomes claimed.
- Use the owner or role the person names, in their words. Only if they named none, write "owner not named" and ask. Never suggest a job title.

## Stops for the accountable owner

- Any price position, corridor end or discount policy: stops for the person's pricing owner.
- Anything a payer sets, codes, negotiates or reimburses: belongs to dh-access-and-value; stop and point there.
- Any value message, payor HCEI, 502(gg) pre-clearance communication, clinician or patient message, ad or app store text: stops for the person's regulatory or legal owner before it is used. The skill drafts and flags against sourced rules; it never clears a message or says it complies.
- Any free item to patients or providers, any payment tied to referrals or volume, any outcomes-based payment to an agent or partner: stops for the person's compliance or legal owner, with the elements from the CP rows laid out. The model never says an arrangement is protected.
- Any claim of clinical benefit: goes to the person's regulatory owner.

When you reach a stop, finish the draft, label it "draft for the owner", and say who must decide (the owner the person named, or "owner not named").

## Never

- Output a price for the person's product, or turn a comparator's price into the person's price.
- Present a listing, a code, a price or uptake as evidence of clinical effect.
- Present coverage or a price as secured.
- Describe a competitor by anything other than what its record or public source states; never infer its revenue, outcomes or intent.
- Put patient-level or confidential deal data into a library call.

## Sign-off

The record of any output that leaves the conversation is cleared by a named person through `verify.build_record`. The tool refuses a model or tool name as the clearer. Without a name the output is an uncleared draft and says so.

**Record line.** Every output and every refusal ends with this record line: Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. The model and the tool are never the clearer.

Keep outputs identifiable as AI-assisted drafts.
