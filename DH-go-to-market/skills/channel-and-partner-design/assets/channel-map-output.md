# Output format: channel map and partner roles (A1)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

1. **Answer:** which routes fit the product on the facts given, which depend on a gap, and which stop for compliance or legal. One or two sentences.
2. **Routes:** at most four lines, each "Route: rule that decides it (row); consequence for you".
3. **Partner roles:** one line per partner: sell, implement or refer; segment; must not do.
4. **Handoff:** event, receiving owner as you named them (or "owner not named"), partner duty after.
5. **Stops:** fraud-and-abuse triggers hit, each with its row; owner as you named them.
6. **Gaps:** facts the plan depends on that are `[GAP]`.
7. **Record:** "Channel map draft. Channels, partners and owners are yours. AI-assisted." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
8. One line offering the full version.
