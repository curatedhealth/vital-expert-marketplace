# Register starter rules (unsourced, yours to adopt)

The earlier version of this competency in the VITAL register (CF-12.02.02.2.1 and CF-12.02.02.2.2, old skill files read on 19 October 2026) carried one-line methods and "do not" rules. No primary source stands behind them: the research pass found no primary or peer-reviewed definition of customer health, renewal rate, churn, net revenue retention or expansion (`customer-success-research.md`, "Not sourced"). They are kept here because they are sensible defaults a person may choose. Evidence class V (asserted by a document).

How to use them: offer the relevant rule by its id, labeled "register starter rule, yours to adopt". Apply it only after the person says yes, and then call it their rule. Never apply one silently, and never present one as a standard.

## Account growth and renewal (DH-02.02.2.1)

| ID | Old task | Rule as the register wrote it |
|---|---|---|
| SR1 | Renewal horizon | Set the horizon (the register's example: 120 days) and the date of the list. |
| SR2 | Renewal horizon | Show each account with its renewal date and current unit. |
| SR3 | Renewal horizon | Mark accounts with an open implementation bar as not ready to expand. |
| SR4 | Renewal horizon | Do not propose expansion to an account that missed go-live. |
| SR5 | Growth offer | For each ready account, name the additional module, site or population. |
| SR6 | Growth offer | Tie the offer to a result the account has already seen, or mark the result `[GAP]`. |
| SR7 | Growth offer | Name the internal owner of the conversation. |
| SR8 | Growth offer | Do not offer a module the account has not used the current one of. |
| SR9 | Renewal risk note | State the reason: value unseen, champion gone, price, implementation open. |
| SR10 | Renewal risk note | State the action before the renewal date, and its owner. |
| SR11 | Renewal risk note | Do not mark an account healthy because the contract has not expired. |

## Expansion and renewal monitoring (DH-02.02.2.2)

| ID | Old task | Rule as the register wrote it |
|---|---|---|
| SR12 | Renewal rate definition | The cohort is the accounts with a renewal date in the period. |
| SR13 | Renewal rate definition | Renewed means a signed continuation of the same unit or a written successor. |
| SR14 | Renewal rate definition | Exclude pilots that were never annual contracts. |
| SR15 | Renewal rate definition | Do not count a verbal intent as renewed. |
| SR16 | Cohort score | List each account as renewed, lost or open, and keep the contract id. |
| SR17 | Cohort score | Separate a loss on price from a loss on an open implementation. |
| SR18 | Cohort score | Do not drop open accounts from the denominator. |
| SR19 | Expansion result | Match offers to signed expansions. |
| SR20 | Expansion result | Name offers that were refused and the reason given. |
| SR21 | Expansion result | Assign an owner where the reason was an unseen result. |
| SR22 | Expansion result | Do not count a meeting as an expansion. |

Two of these touch sourced rows and should be stated together when offered: SR3 and SR4 sit beside the published point that implementation outcomes are preconditions for clinical or service outcomes (CS32 [PAPER], Proctor 2011, DOI 10.1007/s10488-010-0319-7), and SR6 sits beside the usage report against an expected usage profile agreed at commissioning in the NICE framework (CS61). The rows support the reasoning; they do not make the rule a standard.
