# Discount safe harbor rows added in this build (FA1 to FA12)

Added on 19 October 2026 for DH-02.02.2.1, from the raw download that the channels research pass already saved (`research_cmb/raw/ecfr_1001_952.txt`, eCFR versioner API, 42 CFR 1001.952 at the 1 October 2026 point in time). No new source was fetched. The research file `channels-partners-launch-research.md` quotes the statutory discount exception (CP-US-05) but not the regulatory discount safe harbor in paragraph (h), which is the rule a renewal or expansion price reduction is tested against. Quotes were checked by script against the normalized raw text (see the build notes). Evidence class P. Not owner-reviewed. These rows lay out elements; they are not legal advice and never show that an arrangement is protected (CP-US-07).

## Not added

- The warranties safe harbor (paragraph (g)) and the full personal services text beyond CP-US-09 and CP-US-10 were not quoted. A warranty offered at renewal is outside the discount definition (FA12); whether it fits paragraph (g) is `[GAP]` and goes to the person's compliance owner.
- Whether a given product or service is one "for which payment may be made in whole or in part under Medicare, Medicaid or other Federal health care programs" is a fact about the customer's billing that the rows do not settle. The person's compliance owner decides it.
