# Output format: implementation outcomes

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the two or three figures that stand out, with numbers, sites and the as-of date.
2. **Per-site table (compact):** Site · Patient penetration · Provider penetration · Usage vs agreed profile · Go-live slippage. Pooled figure and site range in one line below.
3. **Fidelity to plan:** one line per site that is off plan, with the late core milestones.
4. **Against your targets:** met or missed; "no target set" otherwise.
5. **What it means for you:** two lines from the rows (implementation outcomes are not clinical outcomes, CS32).
6. **Data checks and gaps.**
7. **Record:** "Outcome read on your data. Cleared by NAME; record built with verify.build_record." Until a named person clears it: "Outcome read on your data. Uncleared draft." AI-assisted. Refusals carry the same line.
8. **Papers:** one line, each [PAPER] paper used once with its DOI (not counted toward the 450 words); omit if none was used.
9. One line offering the full version.
