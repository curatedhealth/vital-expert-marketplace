# Output format: partner action review (B1)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

1. **Action by partner:** Partner · Hold / Continue / Change terms / For your decision · the rule and number behind it · owner you named · date you set (or "date not set").
2. **The decision needed:** one or two sentences.
3. **Stops:** claim breaches, fraud-and-abuse triggers in any change of terms (elements laid out in one line each, never "met"), anything sent to a partner.
4. **Hypotheses to test first:** at most three, each with what would confirm it.
5. **Record:** "Action review draft. Rules, actions, owners and dates are yours. AI-assisted." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
6. One line offering the full version.
