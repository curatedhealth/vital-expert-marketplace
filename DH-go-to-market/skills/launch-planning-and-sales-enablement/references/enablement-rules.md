# Enablement and field-activity rules

What the sourced rows say about the activities a launch puts in the field: training, samples and demonstration units, gifts and hospitality, consultants and speakers, patient support tools, partner and reseller payments, and referral arrangements. Interpretation of CP rows (`channels-partners-launch-research.md`) and PC rows (`promotion-and-claims-research.md`), read 19 October 2026. Rows marked [INDUSTRY] are codes, not law. An OIG advisory opinion binds only its requestor (CP-US-43).

The model lays out the elements a row sets. It never says an arrangement is protected, compliant or safe: every item in section 2 stops for the person's compliance or legal owner.

## 1. Training the field and customers (may proceed as drafts)

| Activity | What the rows say | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| Product training for HCPs, including software platforms | Allowed in settings suited to transmitting information, with qualified faculty [INDUSTRY]; MedTech Europe adds appropriate venues and qualified trainers [INDUSTRY] | PC-US-91, PC-EU-18 |
| Answers on unapproved uses | Come from authorized personnel, are truthful and identified as unapproved [INDUSTRY]; FDA's SIUU guidance sets separation from promotion (not yet for current implementation) | PC-US-92, PC-US-50 to PC-US-56 |
| What reps may say | Only claims on the approved list (task A2). A claim added while writing the leave-behind goes back to A2. | old task rule; PC-US-33 |
| Coverage, coding and economic information to customers | May be given if accurate and objective, but not as an inducement, for example free services that remove an HCP overhead cost [INDUSTRY]; HCEI policy covers payors, not individual prescribers | PC-US-93, PC-US-35 |

A briefing is a delivered session with a named briefer and a date. A file placed in a shared folder is not enablement (old task rule).

## 2. Activities that stop for the compliance or legal owner

| Activity | Elements the rows set | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| Anything of value to induce referrals, orders or recommendations of federally payable items | Felony; one-purpose test; receiving side also liable; claims from kickbacks are false claims; civil penalties and exclusion | CP-US-01 to CP-US-08 |
| Reseller, implementation partner or contracted clinician paid as an agent | Personal services safe harbor elements: signed writing covering all services, term of at least one year, compensation method set in advance at fair market value and not tied to referral volume or value, services commercially reasonable | CP-US-09, CP-US-10 |
| Outcomes-based payment to an agent | Legitimate outcome measures on clinical evidence, written terms, monitoring; device makers, device distributors and several others are excluded from this protection | CP-US-11 to CP-US-14 |
| Care coordination arrangement in a value-based enterprise | In-kind only, not for marketing or recruitment; signed writing; recipient pays at least 15 percent; device makers only as limited technology participants without exclusivity or minimum purchase; records 6 years | CP-US-15 to CP-US-22 |
| Downside-risk arrangements | Device makers and distributors excluded | CP-US-23, CP-US-24 |
| Patient engagement tool given to patients | Device maker only if the tool is digital health technology and no Open Payments ownership reporting; in-kind, recommended by the patient's licensed professional, not for marketing or recruitment, not dependent on insurance type; annual cap $500 in the regulation, $623 for calendar year 2026 per OIG | CP-US-25 to CP-US-28 |
| Free item or loaned device to a Medicare or Medicaid beneficiary | Beneficiary inducement penalties; exceptions for promoting access to care with low risk, or equal terms to the public, or financial need; AO 19-02 shows OIG treating a limited loaner phone as remuneration and the safeguards it relied on | CP-US-34 to CP-US-42 |
| Donated cybersecurity or EHR technology | Separate safe harbors with their own conditions (EHR: interoperable, recipient pays 15 percent) | CP-US-29 to CP-US-31 |
| Paid placement in referral software | AO 26-15 (unfavorable): fees not uniform, cost-based and volume-neutral | CP-US-33 |
| Consultants and speakers | Legitimate need identified in advance, qualification-based selection, written agreement, fair market value not tied to business volume, sales staff not controlling selection [INDUSTRY] | PC-US-89, PC-US-90, PC-EU-16, PC-EU-17 |
| Evaluation units, samples, demonstration products | Reasonable quantities, documented no-charge status, title retained and removal for multi-use units, demonstration units not for patient care [INDUSTRY] | PC-US-94, PC-US-95, PC-EU-13 to PC-EU-15 |
| Gifts and promotional benefits in Germany | Banned except listed cases; HCPs may not accept prohibited gifts; hospitality only at professional scientific events within reasonable limits | PC-DE-06, PC-DE-07 |
| DiGA prescription steering in Germany | Contract physicians may not make deals with DiGA makers about allocating or transmitting prescriptions; no deals with drug or aid makers restricting patient choice | CP-DE-06, CP-DE-07 |
| HIPAA marketing using a provider's patient list | Needs patient authorization; treatment and operations exceptions are lost if the covered entity is paid for the communication | PC-US-85 to PC-US-87 |

## 3. What the rows do not cover

Stark Law (physician self-referral) was downloaded but not used; inflation-adjusted penalty amounts were not read; employer and app store channels have no sourced rule (research file, Not sourced). These are `[GAP]` for this skill.
