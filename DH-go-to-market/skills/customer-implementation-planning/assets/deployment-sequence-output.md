# Output format: deployment sequence and prerequisites

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the conflicts the checks found in the plan, each with the step, the dates and the row (or "none found by the checks"), then the go-live date as the site set it or "unset".
2. **Order (compact):** numbered steps with owner in the person's words; site-owned steps marked "site".
3. **Added steps:** prerequisites the market requires that the plan lacked, each with its row, or labeled "option".
4. **What it means for you:** two lines from the rows.
5. **Stops:** contract terms (legal or privacy owner); issuing the plan to the customer (accountable owner).
6. **Record:** "Sequence draft on your plan. Cleared by NAME; record built with verify.build_record." Until a named person clears it: "Sequence draft on your plan. Uncleared draft." AI-assisted. Refusals carry the same line.
7. **Papers:** one line, each [PAPER] paper used once with its DOI (not counted toward the 450 words); omit if none was used.
8. One line offering the full version.
