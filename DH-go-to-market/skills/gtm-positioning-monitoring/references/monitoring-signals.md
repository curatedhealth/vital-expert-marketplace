# Monitoring signals: what can show that positioning needs review

Interpretive reference for tasks A1, A2 and B1. Every rule cites a PC row (`promotion-and-claims-sources.md`) or CP row (`channels-partners-launch-sources.md`), read 19 October 2026, not owner-reviewed, or a live connector behavior recorded in `tools.md`. A signal is a reason to look, never a verdict on the positioning or on a team.

## Signal 1. The line drifts in market (A1)

The line is present, altered or absent in the artifacts customers see. The register's own method: score only the copy in market, never a draft; a correction in a slide customers will not see is not a correction. What marketing says is part of the intended use (PC-US-17, PC-EU-03, PC-UK-01), and the UK guidance names app store description and category, landing page and social media as promotional material (PC-UK-01). Those channels belong in the check set.

## Signal 2. Wording in market crosses a rule (A1)

Flag words from `positioning-rules.md`: device-indicating words (PC-UK-04), approval wording (CP-US-74, PC-EU-11, PC-UK-08), certainty and no-side-effect wording (PC-DE-04; PC-UK-13 [INDUSTRY]), wellness exit wording (PC-US-63), testimonials (PC-UK-03, PC-DE-11). Disclaimers do not cure a medical claim made elsewhere (PC-UK-02); a disclosure cannot contradict a claim (PC-US-74 [WF]). Automated ad assets are still the advertiser's responsibility (PC-UK-15 [INDUSTRY]).

## Signal 3. Peers' records change (A2)

New authorizations, listings, studies, filings or recalls for named peers can change what "different from peers" means. The library's change log began 12 October 2026 and filters by event date, not load date; "no change recorded" is a statement about the log, not about the company (`tools.md`, diligence `get_deltas`). Market Access deltas are not available through the commercial tool (`tools.md`). A peer is described only by what its record states.

## Signal 6. Enforcement shows how a claim is read (context only)

FTC actions on melanoma apps (PC-US-76 [WF]) and Lumosity (PC-US-77 [WF]); ASA rulings on HealthTracker Apps (PC-UK-14 [INDUSTRY]) and Impulse Brain Training (PC-UK-16 [INDUSTRY]). These are examples of how regulators read wording; they are not findings about the person's product.

## What is not a signal

- Internal agreement that the line is right (the register's method: do not score positioning from internal agreement alone).
- A peer's absence from a screen (`gap-rules.md`).
- Sales or uptake figures as proof that a line is accurate: use is not evidence of effect.
