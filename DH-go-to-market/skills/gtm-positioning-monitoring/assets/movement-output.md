# Output format: peer and market movement

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** whether anything moved since DATE that touches the positioning, in one or two sentences.
2. **Changes:** What changed · Date · Source · Part of the positioning it could touch (hypothesis).
3. **No change recorded:** names, with the log caveat (log began DATE; last sweep DATE; about the log, not the company).
4. **What the library cannot see:** one line from the perimeter.
5. **Calls made:** tool and arguments, one line each.
6. **Record:** "Movement read." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
7. One line offering the full version.
