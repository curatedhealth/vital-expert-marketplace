# Output format: positioning review and correction (review draft)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Verdict per artifact:** Artifact · Meets your rule / Misses / Not measurable · Evidence (line status, quoted sentence).
2. **Line or claim list:** for each miss, the reading (hypothesis) and what would confirm it.
3. **Corrections:** Artifact · What changes (proposed unless yours) · Owner you named · Date you set.
4. **Stops:** claims to the claims audit and the regulatory or legal owner; withdrawal or replacement to the artifact's owner.
5. **Record:** "Review draft. Rules, owners and dates are yours." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
6. One line offering the full version.
