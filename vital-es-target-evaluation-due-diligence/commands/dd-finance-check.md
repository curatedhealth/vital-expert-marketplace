---
command: /dd-finance-check
args: [company or asset, buyer_mode (bd|investor), mode (company|asset|investor), markets (US|EU|both)]
---
Run intake for analyze-financial-statements-and-tax and build the financial register in the chosen mode
(company: full; asset: counterparty ability to perform and inherited license obligations only; investor:
weighted toward runway, dilution and the financing path). Quality of earnings, the net debt definition,
accounting opinions and tax opinions are human only; stop and name the accountant or tax specialist.
Any market other than US or EU is a [GAP].
