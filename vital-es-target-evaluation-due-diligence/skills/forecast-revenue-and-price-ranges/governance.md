# Governance

## Human gates (present options, sources and consequences; name who decides; stop)
| Gate | What is presented | Deciding role |
|---|---|---|
| the forecast the deal team adopts | the low, mid and high ranges by country, the sensitivity table, the probability of success beside them | deal lead (BD head or investment lead) |
| net price assumption ranges | list prices with sources, the confidential-discount evidence, the assumption as written | the named deal-team member who sets them, confirmed by the deal lead |
| which analogs count for the uptake path | the candidate analogs, their dates and the comparability basis | deal lead, with the commercial reviewer (named) |
| erosion parameters (price and volume decline, years to the floor) | the parameters and their sources or the statement that none exist | deal lead, with the commercial reviewer |
| which patents count toward the exclusivity end date | both effective dates from the exclusivity register | deal lead, with the IP reviewer |
| whether a Market Access pack output is accepted once Released | the pack records and their state | deal lead, with the Market Access pack reviewer |
| release of the register outside the buyer team | the signed register | BD head or investment lead |

## Review
A named reviewer with commercial or forecasting experience signs the register. The register is UNSIGNED until a person with a name and a date signs it.

## Flags
- [CHECK]: a rule, default or status not traced to a dated primary text opened in the build (no default uptake curve, net ratio or erosion curve)
- [GAP]: a fact the documents do not give
- UNSIGNED: no named reviewer
- ADOPTED FORECAST: HUMAN ONLY (shown on every register)
- UNRISKED AND UNDISCOUNTED: shown on every register, with the probability of success beside the ranges

## Conflicts of interest
A seller's or vendor's forecast is a claim, not an analog. Practitioner and vendor sources (for example a consultancy blog on analog benchmarking, VP-R-053) are labelled and are not standards. Where a cited study has industry-affiliated authors or funders, the reference says so.

## Confidentiality
Data-room material, the net price assumptions and the bid view stay inside the buyer's environment. Do not paste them into public tools.
