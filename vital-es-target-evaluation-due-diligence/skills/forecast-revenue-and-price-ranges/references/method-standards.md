# Method standards (what the register is judged against)

Status rule as in the other skills of this plugin: VERIFIED-REOPENED = the primary or official text was opened, the wording appears in it, and the passage is registered in `verbatim-passages.json` (the "Passage ids" column); SUMMARY-ONLY = abstract or secondary report only, never used for a figure or rule; [CHECK] = not verified, with the reason. Access date for every row: 7 November 2026.

No published standard prescribes deal-level revenue forecasting arithmetic, net-to-list ratios, uptake curves or loss-of-exclusivity erosion curves for a buyer or investor. The skill therefore applies a transparent arithmetic (patients x share x adherence x price), takes prices from dated public sources, treats net price as a team assumption, and labels its own design choices ENGINEERING RULE with a reason in `grounding-register.md`. The only method source that structures an eligible-population by treatment-share calculation is the payer-side budget impact guidance; it is context, not a seller standard.

## Judgement rules
- Ranges first. Name the rule id (FC-...) beside every figure.
- A list price is a dated public record or a labelled team assumption; net is always a labelled assumption with a setter's name.
- Registered erosion evidence is context (US generic prices, European biosimilar prices); it says nothing on brand volume after small-molecule generic entry, EU small-molecule erosion or US biosimilar erosion. Those are [GAP] unless the team supplies a sourced parameter.
- Forecast error literature is context; the register shows no error band and applies no correction.
- Another market, or a price field a source does not publish, is [GAP], not interpolated.
