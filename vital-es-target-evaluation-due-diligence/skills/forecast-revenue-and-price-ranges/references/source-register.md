# Source register: where each input may come from

Access date for every page: 7 November 2026. Public list prices are dated records; a price that is not published here is a [GAP].

| Input | Source (country) | What it publishes | Update | Limit stated by the source or the build | Passage ids |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| list price | CMS ASP pricing files (US) | quarterly Part B payment limit files by effective year | quarterly | a payment limit may be absent; absence says nothing on coverage | VP-R-030, VP-R-031 |
| acquisition cost | CMS NADAC (US) | average survey invoice prices from retail community pharmacies | weekly | retail community pharmacies only; the page was read in a browser | VP-R-032, VP-R-041 |
| list price | NHSBSA Drug Tariff (UK) | drug and appliance prices paid to pharmacy contractors | monthly | tariff prices, not a manufacturer net price | VP-R-033, VP-R-034 |
| list price | AIFA transparency lists (IT) | monthly lists of medicines for regions | monthly | Italian text; list not net | VP-R-035 |
| list price | Spanish nomenclator (ES) | products in the NHS pharmaceutical benefit | page-stated | the page states it is not necessarily exhaustive, complete, exact or up to date | VP-R-036, VP-R-037 |
| list price | BDPM (FR) | public medicines database, downloadable | monthly | a price field was not checked in this build; [GAP] until the team confirms the field | VP-R-038 |
| list and negotiated price | GKV-Spitzenverband overview (DE) | overview of reimbursement amounts under paragraph 130b | page-stated | non-binding information, not an official publication; negotiations are confidential by statute | VP-R-039, VP-R-040 |
| net | none public | confidential arrangements | n/a | net is a deal-team assumption | VP-R-021, VP-R-022, VP-R-023, VP-R-024 |

Gaps recorded: no registered source for brand volume erosion after small-molecule generic entry, for EU small-molecule erosion or for US biosimilar erosion; no default uptake curve or time to peak; no country-level net-to-list ratios; no German commercial price file (Lauer-Taxe).
