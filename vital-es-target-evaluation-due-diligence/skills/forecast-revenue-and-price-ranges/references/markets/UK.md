# United Kingdom (GBP)

Country by country, own currency; there is no EU price (owner ruling R3). Access date 7 November 2026.

**Where inputs come from.** NHSBSA Drug Tariff, monthly (VP-R-033, VP-R-034). Net: assumption (VP-R-021).

**Limits.** The tariff reimburses pharmacy contractors; hospital and negotiated prices are not covered.

**Erosion.** Registered evidence is context only (references/method-standards.md, R3 and R4). A sourced erosion parameter set (price decline, volume decline, years to the floor) must come from the deal team; otherwise revenue after the exclusivity end year is shown unadjusted with a [CHECK] and a GAP.

**Market Access cross-check.** Shown side by side when the pack is Released (references/market-access-interface.md).
