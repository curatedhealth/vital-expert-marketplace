# Governance

## Human gates (present options, sources and consequences; name who decides; stop)
| Gate | What is presented | Deciding role |
|---|---|---|
| Choice of the benchmark stratum | the strata that fit, with n per transition and any LOW_N flag | BD head or investment lead, advised by the development reviewer |
| Every adjustment (reason and bounds) | the adjustment, its reason, its register row or failure-mode reference, its bounds, the sensitivity without it | BD head or investment lead |
| The final probability and timeline number | the benchmark first, then the adjusted ranges and drivers | the named owner of the number (BD head or investment lead): value, owner and date recorded |
| Decision on bid, price, milestones or contingent terms | the implications by buyer mode, as options | BD head or investment lead |
| Release of the estimate outside the buyer team | the signed estimate | BD head or investment lead |

## Review and release
A named development or regulatory reviewer signs the estimate. The estimate is UNSIGNED until a named owner records the final number. Nothing from this skill goes outside the buyer team without a named human release.

## Flags
- [CHECK]: a figure or rule not traced to a registered verbatim passage from a dated primary document
- [GAP]: a fact missing (for example the EU approval benchmark)
- UNSIGNED: no named owner for the final number
- ENGINEERING RULE: a script constant or design rule that rests on no external source, with its reason in references/grounding-register.md

## Conflicts of interest
If a cited benchmark or analysis has industry-affiliated authors, the reference says so (the BIO benchmark is published by an industry association with commercial data providers; the Wong analysis authors are academics at a university laboratory).

## Confidentiality
Data-room material and the buyer's valuation stay inside the buyer's environment.
