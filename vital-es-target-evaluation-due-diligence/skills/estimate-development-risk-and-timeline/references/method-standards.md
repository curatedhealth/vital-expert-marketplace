# Method standards (what the estimate is judged against)

Status rule as in skill A (upgrade lane, 7 November 2026): VERIFIED-REOPENED = the primary or official text was opened, the wording appears in it, and the passage is registered in `verbatim-passages.json` (the "Passage ids" column); SUMMARY-ONLY = abstract, summary or secondary report only, never used for a figure, rule or quoted wording; [CHECK] = not verified, with the reason. Pages are PDF page indices of the file opened.

No published standard prescribes how to adjust a phase-transition benchmark for the features of one asset, or how to turn a benchmark into a range for a deal. The skill therefore applies one published benchmark stratum unadjusted, requires every adjustment to carry a reason and a reference, and labels its own design choices ENGINEERING RULE with a reason in `grounding-register.md`. No peer-reviewed method paper for benchmark adjustment was located and opened in this build.

## Judgement rules
- State the benchmark first, with figure and page. Then adjustments, each with reason and reference.
- A benchmark describes past programs; it does not measure how far a deal model overstates success.
- Benchmark phase durations come from successful transitions and exclude sponsor decision time (B1, VP-B-003 states they are averages over successful transitions).
- Another market, or a modality or indication cell the source does not report, is [GAP], not interpolated.
- Biomarker stratum: use it only when patients are selected by a biomarker; a program that evaluates a new biomarker is not that stratum (B2, VP-B-061 and VP-B-065). Outside oncology the biomarker and orphan strata rest on few data points (VP-B-064, VP-B-066): state it.
- US and EU review timing are separate sources and different statistics (B10 medians and percentages; B11 averages). Never add, average, subtract or compare them; the script keeps them in separate keys.
- Benchmark combination: no source describes how to combine two factors (for example oncology and biomarker); BIO names the top contributing factors only (VP-B-068). Any combination stays an adjustment with a reason and a register or failure-mode reference (ENGINEERING RULE, stated gap).
- EU approval probability stays [GAP]: B6 and B7 give opinion outcomes among applications, with withdrawals and a different denominator from the FDA "approval after submission" figure; a design decision for the owner.
- Skill A's references (M1 to M14) are the findings the adjustments cite; their statuses are those of skill A.
