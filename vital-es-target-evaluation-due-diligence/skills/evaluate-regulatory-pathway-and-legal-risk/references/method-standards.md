# Method standards (what the pathway map and risk register are judged against)

**Status rule (grounding lane, 7 November 2026).** VERIFIED-REOPENED = the primary or official text was opened in the build, the wording appears in it, and the passage is registered in `verbatim-passages.json` (exact text, section, page, URL, access date); the passage ids are in the "Passage ids" column and a row without a registered passage cannot carry this status. SUMMARY-ONLY = abstract, summary page, preview, secondary report or a text that could not be re-opened here, never used for a figure, rule or quoted wording. [CHECK] = not verified, with the reason. PRACTITIONER and TRADE PRESS rows are labeled so in the row and are not standards.

No published standard prescribes how to choose a regulatory route or how to run regulatory due diligence. The skill lists routes with their published criteria and leaves the choice to a person. Every criterion and clock in `references/rules/pathway-rules.json` carries passage ids. ## Open points
- R2 and R3 are drafts; the draft status is recorded (D4). A person checks whether they were finalized before the buyer relies on them.
- R8b and R10: later revisions were not looked up in this lane.
- Public inspection data are incomplete by design (R14): absence of an inspection in the database is not absence of an inspection.
- No EU surrogate-endpoint table exists in the references; EU endpoint precedent comes from EPARs and qualification opinions the team supplies.
