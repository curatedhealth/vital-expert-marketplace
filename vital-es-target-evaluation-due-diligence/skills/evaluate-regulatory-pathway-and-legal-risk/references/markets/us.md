# US route table (reference)

Routes, criteria and clocks are held in rules/pathway-rules.json (the script reads that file; this note explains it). Status per row in method-standards.md.

| Route key | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|
| us_standard | R1 | standard review: 10 months (of receipt, as R1 words it) |
| us_priority_review | R1, R5 | serious condition and, if approved, a significant improvement in safety or effectiveness; 6 months of receipt. For new molecular entities under the Program the clock is described in R1 section IV (not extracted): [CHECK] |
| us_fast_track | R1 | serious or life-threatening condition and potential to address an unmet medical need |
| us_breakthrough | R1 | serious or life-threatening condition and preliminary clinical evidence of substantial improvement on a clinically significant endpoint |
| us_accelerated_approval | R1, R3 (DRAFT), R2 (DRAFT) | surrogate or earlier clinical endpoint reasonably likely to predict benefit; confirmatory trial expectations; check the FDA surrogate table (R4) for precedent, case by case |

Not covered in this build: RMAT and other designations, the statutory text of section 506 beyond the quotations in R1, PDUFA goal dates beyond R1. Mark any such need [CHECK].
