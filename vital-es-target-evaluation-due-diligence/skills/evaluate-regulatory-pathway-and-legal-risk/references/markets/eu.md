# EU route table (reference)

EU pharma reform is not yet law (R18, secondary). Current rules are used; reform changes are flagged by the reform_note on every EU route.

| Route key | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|
| eu_standard | R7 | up to 210 days, not counting clock stops |
| eu_accelerated_assessment | R7 | 150 days if the CHMP agrees; applicant justification; major interest for public health and therapeutic innovation |
| eu_prime | R6 | medicines under development not yet authorised; unmet medical need; potential to address it to a significant extent; support scheme, not an approval route |
| eu_conditional_ma | R8, R11 (partial), R9 | four criteria; valid one year, renewable annually; specific obligations; R12: Art 14(7) of Reg 726/2004 is shown as deleted in the 28/01/2022 consolidation, so rely on R8 and R11 |
| eu_exceptional_circumstances | R10 | any one of three grounds; annual reassessment; normally no full dossier |

No EU surrogate-endpoint table exists in these references. Another market is a [GAP].
