# Governance

## Human gates (present options, sources and consequences; name who decides; stop)
| Gate | What is presented | Deciding role |
|---|---|---|
| route choice in each market | routes, criteria, precedent and obligations | regulatory reviewer (named) with the BD head or investment lead |
| any legal opinion (compliance position, exposure from a dispute, adequacy of a representation, effect of a warning letter) | the documents and the questions | counsel (named) |
| materiality of a gap or risk in a deal term | the gap, the risk and the consequence | BD head or investment lead, with counsel |
| whether a draft guidance is treated as the working expectation | the draft status, the text and the consequence | regulatory reviewer (named) |
| release of the map outside the buyer team | the signed map and register | BD head or investment lead |

## Review
A named regulatory reviewer signs the pathway map; a named counsel signs the risk register's legal rows. The output stays UNSIGNED until then.

## Flags
- [CHECK]: a rule, clock or status not traced to a dated primary text opened in the build
- [GAP]: a fact the documents do not give
- DRAFT: a guidance still in draft when the build read it
- UNSIGNED: no named reviewer
- HUMAN ONLY: route choice and legal opinion (shown on every output)

## Conflicts of interest
If a cited analysis has industry-affiliated authors, or a law-firm note is the only source, the reference says so.

## Confidentiality
Regulator correspondence, inspection reports and deal documents stay inside the buyer's environment.
