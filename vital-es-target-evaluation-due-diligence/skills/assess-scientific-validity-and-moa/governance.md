# Governance

## Human gates (present options, sources and consequences; name who decides; stop)
| Gate | What is presented | Deciding role |
|---|---|---|
| The scientific go/no-go: whether the evidence chain is strong enough to proceed | the register: evidence chain grade, weakest points, class flags, questions | named scientist (scientific lead with disease and target expertise) |
| How much weight a single human cohort, a single species or a single laboratory's result carries | the evidence rows and what each rests on | named scientist |
| Whether a class failure or class warning applies to this asset's specifics (selectivity, dose, population) | the class precedent rows and the differentiation basis | named scientist, with the regulatory reviewer for safety warnings |
| Release of the register outside the buyer team | the signed register | BD head or investment lead |

## Review and release
A named scientist signs the register before it goes to the clinical data skill. The register is UNSIGNED until a person with a name and a date signs it.

## Flags
- [CHECK]: a rule, finding or status not traced to a registered verbatim passage from a dated primary document, or contested
- [GAP]: a fact the documents do not give
- UNVERIFIED / abstract-only: a source read only in part (status SUMMARY-ONLY); never used for a figure, rule or quoted wording
- UNSIGNED: no named reviewer
- SCIENTIFIC GO/NO-GO: HUMAN ONLY (shown on every register)
- ENGINEERING RULE: a design rule or constant that rests on no external source, with its reason in references/grounding-register.md

## Conflicts of interest
Where a cited analysis is by authors employed by, or funded by, a company with an interest in its message (for example a funder of target-genetics work, or a company reporting its own preclinical audit), the reference says so. The seller's own data are claims to check, not independent evidence.

## Confidentiality
Seller data, unpublished study reports and the buyer's views stay inside the buyer's environment. Do not paste them into public tools.
