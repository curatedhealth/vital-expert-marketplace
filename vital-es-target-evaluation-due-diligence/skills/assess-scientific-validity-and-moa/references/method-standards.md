# Method standards (what the mechanism register is judged against)

Status rule (upgrade lane, 7 November 2026): VERIFIED-REOPENED = the primary or official text was opened, the wording appears in it, and the passage is registered in `verbatim-passages.json` (the "Passage ids" column); SUMMARY-ONLY = abstract or secondary report only, never used for a figure, rule or quoted wording; [CHECK] = not verified or contested, with the reason. Passages from four sources (Perel, Seok, BEST, and the two Wallach passages of skill A) were confirmed in the page text in the browser pane because the build tool could not fetch them; the register marks them.

No published standard exists for judging the scientific validity of a mechanism of action as a whole, and none was located for what an "independent reproduction" must meet; the skill does not name one. It anchors each judgement on the sources below (reporting checklist, risk-of-bias tool for animal studies, biomarker definitions, analyses of reproducibility and translation) and labels its own design choices ENGINEERING RULE with a reason in `grounding-register.md`. No figure from these sources is applied to an asset as a probability.

## Notes
- F1: the 2.6-fold figure is a population-level relative figure for a studied set of mechanisms. It is not the probability that this asset succeeds and is never used as one. Two authors' employers are investors that fund target-genetics work; the reference says so.
- F2 to F4: abstract-only; full texts are not open access (Europe PMC reports no open full text); none of their numbers is repeated in any rule, example or output. **Nelson 2015 (F2) is no longer relied on**: the genetic-support context rests on F1 (which restates the earlier estimate, VP-F-002) and F15 (an independent reanalysis), both open access, with the authors' disclosed industry ties.
- F6, F7, F14 measure reproducibility on selected sets of papers; they do not give a general rate for other disease areas.
