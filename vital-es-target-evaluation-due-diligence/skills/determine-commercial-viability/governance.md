# Governance

## Human gates (present options, sources and consequences; name who decides; stop)
| Gate | What is presented | Deciding role |
|---|---|---|
| the commercial decision: GO, CONDITIONAL GO or NO-GO | all three options with conditions, evidence for and against | decision owner (BD head or investment lead) |
| which analogs count and which are excluded | the candidate analogs and their criteria | decision owner, with the commercial reviewer (named) |
| the buyer's thresholds (peak-sales hurdle, required exclusivity after launch, minimum differentiation claims) | the inputs the thresholds apply to | decision owner |
| which patents count toward the exclusivity end | both effective dates | decision owner, with the IP reviewer |
| whether a price, coverage or access assumption from the Market Access pack is accepted once Released | the pack records and their state | decision owner, with the Market Access pack reviewer |
| release of the register outside the buyer team | the signed register | BD head or investment lead |

## Review
A named reviewer with commercial experience signs the register. The register is UNSIGNED until a person with a name and a date signs it.

## Flags
- [CHECK]: a rule, band or status not traced to a dated primary text opened in the build
- [GAP]: a fact the documents do not give
- UNSIGNED: no named reviewer
- COMMERCIAL DECISION: HUMAN ONLY (shown on every register)
- ACCESS INPUTS: GAP until the Market Access pack is Released

## Conflicts of interest
If a cited analysis has industry-affiliated authors or funders (the AMNOG withdrawal study has authors employed by or paid by a pharmaceutical company), the reference says so. A seller's sales claim is a claim, not an analog.

## Confidentiality
Data-room material, the buyer's hurdle and the bid view stay inside the buyer's environment. Do not paste them into public tools.
