# Failure modes for the viability register

Every register is screened against every mode. Status as in method-standards.md.
Where no published failure analysis was found the row says so and the mode rests on the detection question and the sourced requirement.

| Id | Mode | Detection question | Source and finding | Passage ids | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MH-01 | Peak sales or market size overestimated | Is peak sales shown only as a range from dated analogs, with n, sensitivity to each analog and the statement that it is not a forecast? Are analogs chosen for comparability and not for their size? | Cha et al. supplement: 54 of 260 drugs had consensus forecasts more than 160% above actual revenues (VP-H-010, context). Kossmeier et al. (Austria, 102 drugs): median overestimation 33%, 55.9% of forecasts more than 100% too high or 50% too low, larger error in saturated markets (VP-H-001 to VP-H-005); scope one country, no band applied | VP-H-010, VP-H-001 to VP-H-005 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| MH-02 | Reimbursement or access assumptions are not granted | Does any price, coverage or access assumption come from a Released Market Access pack with records? Is the range labelled as unadjusted when it does not? | Staab et al. (Germany): 21 of 22 withdrawn medicines had a "no additional benefit" appraisal (VP-H-020; authors disclose industry ties). Leong and Macaulay (ATMPs only; conference abstract): 7 of 32 withdrawn, 6 for commercial reasons, 4 with negative HTA or reimbursement issues (VP-H-021, VP-H-022) | VP-H-020, VP-H-021, VP-H-022 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| MH-03 | Differentiation rests on a cross-trial comparison | Does every differentiation claim that the viability conditions count come from skill G's "supported" claims (an A or F row, head-to-head or indirect comparison)? | FDA 2006 guidance on the adverse reactions section (registered in skill G): rates from one drug's trials cannot be directly compared to rates from another's (VP-G-040); no official efficacy statement was found | VP-G-040 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| MH-04 | Exclusivity ends before the plan assumes | Is the effective exclusivity end read from the signed exclusivity register of skill C, with both the all-patents and the composition-only dates, against the exclusivity the buyer wants after the expected launch? | Directive 2001/83/EC Art 10(1): generic not placed on the market until ten years from the initial authorisation, extended to a maximum of eleven (VP-H-050; VP-C-071, VP-C-072 in skill C); the effective dates come from skill C | VP-H-050, VP-C-070 to VP-C-073 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |

## Notes
- No real deal or company is named in these rows; the sources are a journal supplement, a government-agency guidance and a directive.
- A mode is added only with a source opened in the build.
