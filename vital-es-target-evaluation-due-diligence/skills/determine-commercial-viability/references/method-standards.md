## Judgement rules
- Peak sales is shown only as a range from at least three dated, sourced analogs with n and leave-one-out sensitivity; it is not a forecast.
- Forecast error (H1b) is context: no error band from another market or study is applied without a stated assumption; the Cha band stays disabled (H1a).
- Analog selection criteria and option dimensions are practitioner rows (H3, H4b) and are not standards; no source gives a number of analogs or a threshold.
- Price, coverage and access come from the Market Access pack; until it is Released they are a [GAP] and the range is labeled unadjusted.
- Access-failure evidence (H2, H5a) carries its scope (Germany; ATMPs only).
- The commercial decision is human only.
