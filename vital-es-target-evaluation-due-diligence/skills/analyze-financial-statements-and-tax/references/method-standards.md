## Judgement rules
- The runway flags a scenario that runs out of cash inside 12 months of the period end (Item 303) and inside one year beyond the financial-statement date (AS 2415); the register does not say which a filing applied.
- A redacted license term (Item 601(b)(10)(iv)) is recorded as GAP with a request for the unredacted agreement; it is never read as zero.
- Section 382, the 80 percent limit and the IRS rate source are flags and questions for a tax specialist; no limitation amount is computed.
- IPR&D treatment and contingencies are specialist flags: the deal structure drives IPR&D treatment (E9); contingency recognition is the accountant's.
- **ASC 205-40 / `FIN-ASC205-40-ISSUE` stays DISABLED (E3).** Refuse enable: SUMMARY-ONLY, `passage_ids` empty, disabled_message `[CHECK]`. E3 PARTIAL ASU documentation thickens the GAP narrative only — it never flips the rule to VERIFIED-REOPENED.
- QoE, net debt, accounting opinions, and tax opinions are HUMAN ONLY (E13 / governance.md). No AI QoE or net-debt arithmetic.
