# Intake

Ask for every missing item in one message. Do not start on assumptions.

| # | Input | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buyer mode (bd or investor), unit (company, or asset with its counterparty) and the mode to run (company, asset or investor). Markets in scope (US, EU or both). Another market is a [GAP]. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 2 | Filings: the latest annual report (10-K or EU annual report), the latest interim report (10-Q), registration statements, current reports with license or financing exhibits, and the period-end date of each. The date each was filed or issued. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 3 | Cash position items: cash and equivalents, marketable securities, restricted cash, debt with maturity dates, and any dated scheduled payments. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 4 | Burn basis: which of trailing operating cash flow, company guidance, management budget or analyst assumption the buyer wants used, with its source. If none is given, record a GAP; do not choose one silently. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 5 | Financing history: each round or facility with date, type, amount, shares issued, price, warrants and convertibles. Share counts at the start of the period you want. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 6 | Upstream license agreements and amendments (the filed text, not a summary): licensor, field, milestones, royalties, sublicense income share, change-of-control, assignment, diligence and termination terms. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 7 | Commitments: purchase, manufacturing, lease, clinical and other obligations from the commitments note; guarantees and off-balance-sheet arrangements. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 8 | Tax: whether net operating losses are reported, ownership-shift information for the last three years (percentage points moved among 5-percent shareholders), and the shift the proposed transaction would cause. The tax filings if the buyer has them. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 9 | The seller's financial claims (decks, data-room statements) to cross-check. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 10 | Names of the accountant and the tax specialist who will review, and of counsel for the license questions. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |

Rules: if 1 to 4 are missing, ask. Asset mode needs the counterparty's filings and the licenses (items 2, 3, 4, 6, 7). A date or figure you were not given is a [GAP] with the consequence stated, never an estimate.
