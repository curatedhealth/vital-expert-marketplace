# Governance

## Human gates (present options, sources and consequences; name who decides; stop)
| Gate | What is presented | Deciding role |
|---|---|---|
| quality of earnings, net debt definition, accounting and tax opinions | questions, cross-checks and gaps; never a certification | accountant (named) for accounting; tax specialist (named) for tax |
| which burn basis counts for the runway | the runway on each burn basis | accountant (named) |
| materiality of an inherited licensor obligation or of a tax limit in a deal term | the clause or limit and the consequence | BD head or investment lead, with counsel or the tax specialist |
| whether the seller's inconsistent claim is a misstatement or a difference in assumptions | the claim and the filing side by side | accountant (named) |
| release of the register outside the buyer team | the signed register | BD head or investment lead |

## Review
A named accountant reviews the runway, financing and commitments; a named tax specialist reviews the tax flags; counsel reviews the license obligations. The register is UNSIGNED until a person with a name and a date signs it.

## Flags
- [CHECK]: a rule, period or status not traced to a dated primary text opened in the build
- [GAP]: a fact the documents do not give
- UNSIGNED: no named reviewer
- HUMAN ONLY: shown on the four reserved fields of every register

## Conflicts of interest
If a cited analysis has industry-affiliated authors, or the source of a figure is the seller, the reference says so. A seller's statement is a claim to cross-check, not a source.

## Confidentiality
Data-room material, license terms and the buyer's modelling stay inside the buyer's environment. Do not paste them into public tools. Read filings through the public pages; no scripted bulk access.
