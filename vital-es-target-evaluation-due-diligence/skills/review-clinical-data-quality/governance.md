# Governance

## Human gates (present options, sources and consequences; name who decides; stop)
| Gate | What is presented | Deciding role |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-off of the register before skill B runs | the register, the screen, the gaps with options to close each | clinical development reviewer (named) |
| Every failure mode marked "triggered" before it appears in a summary | the mode, the register row, the passage it rests on | clinical development reviewer |
| Any "high" risk-of-bias judgement that would change a deal term | the domain judgements with quoted basis | clinical development reviewer, with the BD or investment lead for the deal term |
| Release of the register outside the buyer team | the signed register | BD head or investment lead |

## Review and release
A named clinical development reviewer signs the register (name and date on the register; UNSIGNED until then). Nothing from this skill goes to the seller, a regulator or an investor committee without a named human release by the BD head or investment lead.

## Flags
- [CHECK]: a rule or figure not traced to a registered verbatim passage from a dated primary document
- [GAP]: a fact missing; never filled
- UNVERIFIED / abstract-only: a source read only in part (status SUMMARY-ONLY in the references); never used for a figure, rule or quoted wording
- NOT_REGULATORY_RECORD: the row rests on company-controlled material only

## Conflicts of interest
If a cited analysis has industry-affiliated authors, or the trial sponsor is the seller, the row says so. Industry authorship is stated in the failure-mode and method rows where it applies.

## Confidentiality
Data-room material stays inside the buyer's environment. Do not paste it into public tools. A non-disclosure agreement may bar use of some connectors on confidential names; check before any lookup.
