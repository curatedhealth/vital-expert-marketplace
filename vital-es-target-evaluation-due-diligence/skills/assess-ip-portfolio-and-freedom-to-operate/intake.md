# Intake

Ask for every missing item in one message. Do not start on assumptions.

| # | Input | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buyer mode (bd or investor), unit (asset, or company with the list of pipeline assets; one run per asset, then a roll-up), and markets in scope (US, EU, or both). Another market is a [GAP]. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 2 | Asset facts: active substance, type (small molecule or biologic), indications, and for each market whether the product is approved, with the first approval date. Whether the product is designated a qualified infectious disease product (qidp; GAIN extension, 21 U.S.C. 355f). Whether the active moiety was ever approved before (needed for new-chemical-entity status). | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 3 | The seller's patent list: number, family, jurisdiction, claim types (composition, method of use, formulation, process), filing, grant and expiry dates, and any patent term extension or SPC filed or granted. Orange Book or Purple Book listings, if any. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 4 | Regulatory milestones for the term calculations: for the US, the date the IND exemption became effective, the date the marketing application was first submitted, and the approval date; for the EU, the first marketing authorisation date in the Union and the basic patent filing date. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 5 | Designations and paediatric status: orphan designation (US, EU), paediatric exclusivity granted or written request, qualified infectious disease product, new clinical investigations supporting approval. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 6 | Ownership and license documents: assignments and the chain of title for each family; every in-license (upstream licensor), with royalty, milestone, change-of-control, assignment, sublicense and field restrictions. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 7 | Live disputes and challenges: paragraph IV notices, inter partes or post-grant reviews, oppositions, court actions (US, EU including the Unified Patent Court), settlements. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 8 | Name of the patent counsel who will give the freedom-to-operate opinion. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |

Rules: if 1 to 3 are missing, ask. A date you were not given is a [GAP] with the consequence stated (which period cannot be computed), never an estimate. The company unit runs one register per asset; the roll-up lists the assets side by side and never merges dates across assets.
