# Poor: Orange Book listing treated as validity or FTO

Anti-pattern. Register text: "Patent US9,999,999 is listed in the Orange Book, therefore it is valid and the asset is clear to operate in the US."

Refuse / correct:
- Orange Book rows are **listings and exclusivity postings**, not validity or infringement findings (FDA Patents & Exclusivity FAQ Q19: FDA's listing role is **ministerial** — VP-C-142; S22; IP-05).
- Patents and exclusivity are distinct and may or may not run concurrently (VP-C-140).
- Exclusivity notice is the Orange Book posting, not an award letter (VP-C-141).
- Form FDA 3542 is required for listing (VP-C-143); listing still ≠ FTO.
- FTO / validity / infringement remain HUMAN ONLY (counsel). Register may record listing status, dispute-list status, and open questions only.
