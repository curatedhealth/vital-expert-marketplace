# Failure modes for the exclusivity register

Every register is screened against every mode. Status as in method-standards.md.
No documented deal case was found for IP-02, IP-03 or IP-04 in the build searches (three queries, 6 November 2026,
recorded in docs/due_diligence/BUILD_LOG.md); where that is so the row says so and the mode rests on the
detection question, not on a published analysis.

| Id | Mode | Detection question | Source and finding | Passage ids | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IP-01 | Patent invalid for lack of written description | Do the claims cover a genus or a result broader than the specification describes (for example antibody or small-molecule classes claimed by function)? Does counsel's file show prosecution amendments after the filing date that widen scope? | Ariad Pharmaceuticals, Inc., et al. v. Eli Lilly and Company. 2008-1248, Fed. Cir. en banc, 2010): section 112 first paragraph "contains a written description requirement separate from enablement"; the test is whether the disclosure "reasonably conveys to those skilled in the art that the inventor had possession of the claimed subject matter as of the filing date" (S13a, S13b) | VP-C-110, VP-C-111, VP-C-112 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| IP-02 | Inherited licensor obligations | Does any in-license contain a royalty, milestone, change-of-control, assignment, sublicense or field clause that passes to or is triggered by the buyer? Is the licensor's consent needed? | No published deal-harm case opened. PRACTITIONER Fenwick (2025) names Field/Territory, exclusivity, and sublicense-consent as diligence focus (VP-C-161, S17). Detection still rests on the register questions; not a legal opinion | VP-C-161 | PARTIAL (PRACTITIONER; no deal-case primary) |
| IP-03 | Exclusivity or patent-term error | Were the exclusivity and extension dates recomputed from the milestone dates and rules in references/rules, or only copied from the seller's list? Is any extension application late, or on the wrong patent? | No documented deal case found for the mode. The periods and conditions themselves are registered (S2 to S11), so the detection question rests on those passages; a practitioner blog on a district court decision about the 60-day PTE deadline was read in a summary only (S18, SUMMARY-ONLY) | VP-C-010 to VP-C-017, VP-C-060 to VP-C-063 | [CHECK] |
| IP-04 | Chain-of-title gap | Does an assignment from every inventor to the owner of record exist for every family, dated before or at filing? Does any employee or university agreement use language of future assignment only? | Stanford v. Roche, 563 U.S. 776 (2011): the Bayh-Dole Act does not automatically vest title in contractors (VP-C-120); an earlier promise to assign ("agree to assign") lost to a later present assignment ("do hereby assign") (VP-C-121, VP-C-122). Practitioner anchor: an IP map from inventor to owner with a supporting document for each link (VP-C-130, S16) | VP-C-120, VP-C-121, VP-C-122, VP-C-130 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| IP-05 | Orange Book listing treated as validity or FTO | Does any register row, memo or warrant treat an Orange Book (or Purple Book) listing as proof the patent is valid, enforceable, or that the asset is free to operate? | FDA Patents & Exclusivity FAQ: patents and exclusivity are distinct; FDA's listing role is ministerial (S22, VP-C-140 to VP-C-142). Listing ≠ validity ≠ FTO | VP-C-140 to VP-C-142 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| IP-06 | Non-grounds listed as EPO opposition grounds / outcome predicted | Does the register or memo treat clarity, entitlement, or lack of unity alone as Art 100 opposition grounds? Does any row predict opposition or UPC revocation outcome or state EU FTO? | EPC Art 100 exhaustive grounds (a)–(c) only (S27, VP-C-181). Art 99 is a 9-month status/date lever (S26, VP-C-180). UPC competence is forum fact (S28, VP-C-182). FTO / validity / infringement remain HUMAN ONLY | VP-C-180, VP-C-181, VP-C-182 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| IP-07 | SPC manufacturing waiver treated as shorter exclusivity or as FTO | Does any row shorten Art 13 SPC duration because of Reg 2019/933, or treat the export/stockpiling derogation as clearance to operate? | Reg 2019/933 replaces Art 5 with a conditional derogation for making for export and near-expiry EU stockpiling (S7c, VP-C-187); import for mere repackaging/re-export/storing is excluded (VP-C-188). Art 13 duration arithmetic is unchanged (S7/S7b). Applicability and competitive effect are HUMAN ONLY (counsel) — never AI FTO | VP-C-187, VP-C-188 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| IP-08 | Bolar studies treated as blanket FTO / commercial clearance | Does any row treat Directive 2001/83 Art 10(6) studies/trials safe harbour as freedom to manufacture, stockpile, or launch? | Art 10(6): necessary studies/trials for Art 10(1)–(4) applications and consequential practical requirements are not contrary to patents/SPCs (S10b, VP-C-190). National implementations differ. Commercial manufacture/launch remain counsel questions — never AI FTO | VP-C-190 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| IP-09 | SPC Art 3(a) eligibility predicted by AI | Does any row grant/deny an SPC or state the product is/is not protected by the basic patent under Art 3(a)? | Medeva C-322/10: Art 3(a) precludes SPC for actives not specified in the claim wording (S30, VP-C-191). Later cases refine functional claiming. Register may pose the counsel question only — never predict grant/validity/FTO | VP-C-191 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |

Further modes are added only with a published analysis behind them.
