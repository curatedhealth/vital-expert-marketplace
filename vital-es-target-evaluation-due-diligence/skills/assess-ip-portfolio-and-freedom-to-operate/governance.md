# Governance

## Human gates (present options, sources and consequences; name who decides; stop)
| Gate | What is presented | Deciding role |
|---|---|---|
| the freedom-to-operate opinion | the landscape, the questions, the dates; never a statement that the asset is free to operate | patent counsel (named) |
| which patents count toward the effective exclusivity date | the all-patents date and the composition-only date | the named IP reviewer with the BD head or investment lead |
| validity, enforceability and infringement of any patent | the documents and the questions | patent counsel (named) |
| materiality of an ownership gap or an inherited licensor obligation in a deal term | the gap, the clause, the consequence | BD head or investment lead, with counsel |
| release of the register outside the buyer team | the signed register | BD head or investment lead |

## Review
A named patent counsel (or a named IP reviewer) signs the register before it goes to the regulatory skill. Dates computed by the script are marked UNSIGNED until a person confirms the inputs.

## Flags
- [CHECK]: a rule, period or status not traced to a dated primary text opened in the build
- [GAP]: a fact the documents do not give
- UNSIGNED: no named reviewer
- FTO-OPINION: HUMAN ONLY (shown on every register)

## Conflicts of interest
If a cited analysis has industry-affiliated authors or a law firm is the source, the reference says so.

## Confidentiality
Data-room material, license terms and counsel's advice stay inside the buyer's environment. Do not paste them into public tools.
