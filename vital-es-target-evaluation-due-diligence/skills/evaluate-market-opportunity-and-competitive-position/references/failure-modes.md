# Failure modes for the market register

Every register is screened against every mode. Status as in method-standards.md.
Where no published failure analysis was found the row says so and the mode rests on the detection question and the sourced requirement.

| Id | Mode | Detection question | Source and finding | Passage ids | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MG-01 | Market size or peak sales overestimated | Does every population figure carry source, year and the market's own geography and case definition? Is the addressable figure shown as a range from sourced shares rather than a single number? | Cha, Rifai and Sarraf 2013, supplement (G1): of 260 drugs with a pre-launch consensus forecast, 54 had forecasts "more than 160% higher than actual revenues"; forecasts for companies outside the large pharmaceutical companies "tend to be systematically overestimated, particularly in the peri-launch years"; follow-on drugs showed "no meaningful improvement in forecast accuracy". Sell-side consensus for marketed drugs, not buyer forecasts; the authors are at a consulting firm and declare no competing financial interests | VP-G-060 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| MG-02 | Competitor timing wrong or stale | Does each pipeline entry carry a dated source and a timing basis? Is any timing resting on a registry record alone? | Fleminger and Goldacre 2018: 16.2% of dual-registered trials were discrepant on completion status; completion status on registries is commonly inaccurate (VP-G-050, VP-G-051). BMJ Open 2017: 125 trials (31%) had an incorrect recruitment status or an update delay of more than one year (VP-G-052). Preprint and conference-abstract context (G12) is SUMMARY-ONLY | VP-G-050, VP-G-051, VP-G-052 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| MG-03 | Differentiation claimed on a cross-trial comparison | Does each differentiation claim point to a row of the clinical evidence register (A) or mechanism register (F)? Is the comparison head-to-head or indirect, or is it two separate trials side by side? | FDA 2006 guidance on the adverse reactions section: rates in one drug's trials cannot be directly compared to rates in another drug's trials (VP-G-040); NICE DSU TSD 18: when connected evidence exists only anchored population adjustment may be used; unanchored methods should not be used when anchored methods can be applied (VP-G-070, VP-G-071) | VP-G-040, VP-G-070, VP-G-071 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| MG-04 | Population definition or geography mismatched | Is the case definition the one the target product profile claims? Is the figure from the market's own geography and year? Does the orphan arithmetic use the same thresholds the regulator uses in that market? | Statutory definitions differ by market: US fewer than 200,000 persons (VP-G-020); EU not more than five in 10 thousand (VP-G-021); the EMA COMP separates complete and partial prevalence (VP-G-010) | VP-G-010, VP-G-020, VP-G-021 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |

## Notes
- MG-01 names no real deal; the sources are a journal supplement and statutes.
- A mode is added only with a source opened in the build; a mode without a published analysis would carry the detection question alone and say so.
