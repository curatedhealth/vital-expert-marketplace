No published standard exists for market sizing, competitive positioning or a competitor-pipeline method in drug due diligence. The skill applies reporting expectations from the sources below to population figures and registry data and labels its own design choices ENGINEERING RULE. Price, coverage and access are never stated here: they belong to the Market Access pack and are a [GAP] until it is Released.

## Judgement rules
- Every population figure carries source, year and geography; the prevalence measure (complete or partial) and any extrapolation are stated (G8 by analogy, G7 reporting).
- The orphan arithmetic uses each market's own threshold (G2, G3) and is never a designation.
- A registry-only competitor status is cross-checked against the other registry (G5b) and flagged otherwise; sponsor statements are claims.
- A differentiation claim on a raw cross-trial comparison is not counted (G6 adverse-reaction rates; G9 TSD 18: unanchored population adjustment only when no connected network — VP-G-070/071). Counting an anchored MAIC/STC claim still requires the TSD justification evidence (effect-modifier imbalance) — otherwise GAP / not counted.
- The FDA TPP guidance is a draft (G4); differentiation is read against the TPP the seller supplies.
- Price, coverage and access: GAP until the Market Access pack is Released.
