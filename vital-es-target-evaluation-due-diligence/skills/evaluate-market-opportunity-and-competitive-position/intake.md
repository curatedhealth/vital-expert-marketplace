# Intake

Ask for every missing item in one message. Do not start on assumptions.

| # | Input | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buyer mode (bd or investor), unit (asset, or company with the list of pipeline assets; one run per asset, then a roll-up), markets in scope (US, EU, or both, and the countries DE, FR, IT, ES, UK when the revenue skill will follow; another market is a [GAP]), and the reference date for all sources. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 2 | The asset and indication: active substance or code, modality, the specific patient population the target product profile claims, and the target product profile itself if the seller has one (route, schedule, efficacy, safety, convenience attributes). | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 3 | Population sources the buyer already trusts: registry reports, epidemiology papers, claims studies, with the year and the geography of each figure and the case definition used. Any share diagnosed and any share treated, each with source, year and geography. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 4 | For the EU: the total population used as the denominator, with its source and year. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 5 | Standard of care and unmet need: guidelines, reviews and the seller's account, with sources. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 6 | Competing marketed products and competing pipeline the buyer knows of, with sources and dates. Any competitor signals from the Market Access pack. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 7 | The clinical evidence register (skill A) and mechanism register (skill F) for the asset, with their row ids, so that differentiation claims can point to them. If either is missing, say so: differentiation claims then stay "not counted". | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 8 | The buyer's limits, if any: maximum age of a population figure in years, maximum age of a pipeline source in days. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 9 | The state of the Market Access pack and the name of the person who owns price, coverage and access assumptions. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |
| 10 | The name of the reviewer who will sign the register. | ask in the one message; do not start on an assumption; record a [GAP] if it cannot be supplied |

Rules: if 1 and 2 are missing, ask. A figure you were not given is a [GAP] with the consequence stated, never an estimate. The company unit runs one register per asset; the roll-up lists assets side by side and never merges populations across assets or indications.
