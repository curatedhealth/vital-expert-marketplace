# Governance

## Human gates (present options, sources and consequences; name who decides; stop)
| Gate | What is presented | Deciding role |
|---|---|---|
| which case definition and which population count for the decision | the candidate definitions with sources and years | reviewer with market and clinical knowledge (named) |
| whether a registry-only competitor timing is accepted or the sponsor is asked | the registry entry and its cross-check | reviewer with market and clinical knowledge (named) |
| whether a differentiation claim that is "not counted" is worth a study or a request to the seller | the claim, the comparison type and the gap | BD head or investment lead |
| price, coverage and access assumptions | nothing: owned by the Market Access pack; a GAP until Released | Market Access pack reviewer |
| release of the register outside the buyer team | the signed register | BD head or investment lead |

## Review
A named reviewer with market and clinical knowledge signs the register before it goes to the viability skill. The register is UNSIGNED until a person with a name and a date signs it.

## Flags
- [CHECK]: a rule, status or figure not traced to a dated primary text opened in the build
- [GAP]: a fact the documents do not give
- UNSIGNED: no named reviewer
- ACCESS INPUTS: GAP until the Market Access pack is Released (shown on every register)

## Conflicts of interest
If a cited analysis has industry-affiliated authors, or the source of a figure is the seller or a competitor, the reference says so. The seller's market statements are claims to check, not sources.

## Confidentiality
Data-room material and the buyer's views stay inside the buyer's environment. Do not paste them into public tools. Live registry and literature lookups use only the asset and indication, never confidential terms.
