### Optimism bias: the published bounds (UK public-sector guidance)

The Green Book 2026 says that without a body's own historical evidence, practitioners can use the generic values in the supplementary guidance (SP-171). That guidance is still on gov.uk, not withdrawn, page first published 21 April 2013; the PDF dates from 2003 and rests on a 2002 Mott MacDonald review (SP-170). Quote it with that age stated. Table 1, upper and lower bound in percent (SP-172):

| Project type | Works duration, upper | Works duration, lower | Capital expenditure, upper | Capital expenditure, lower |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard buildings | 4 | 1 | 24 | 2 |
| Non-standard buildings | 39 | 2 | 51 | 4 |
| Standard civil engineering | 20 | 1 | 44 | 3 |
| Non-standard civil engineering | 25 | 3 | 66 | 6 |
| Equipment/development | 54 | 10 | 200 | 10 |
| Outsourcing | n/a | n/a | 41 (operating expenditure) | 0 (operating expenditure) |

- Software, systems and ICT development fall in "Equipment/development" (SP-173), so a digital health build starts from 54% (duration) and 200% (capital expenditure).
- The upper bounds are the average historic bias at outline business case stage for traditionally procured projects; earlier stages may need more. They are "indicative starting values", not extremes (SP-174).
- Method: start at the upper bound and reduce it by a mitigation factor between 0.0 and 1.0 for each contributory factor the project manages, aiming for the lower bound by contract award (SP-175).
- No sound bounds exist for operating costs (except outsourcing) or for benefits shortfall: use sensitivity analysis and switching values instead (SP-176), which is what `options_check.py rank` swap points and `analytics.risk-tornado` do on the person's inputs.
- Wording: "UK public-sector guidance (HM Treasury optimism bias supplementary guidance, 2003, Table 1, SP-172): equipment and development projects, which include software (SP-173), start at 54% on duration and 200% on capital expenditure, reduced only for factors you manage (SP-175). The uplift you apply, and the project type, are yours." The applied percentage and the mitigation factors are the person's; never apply an uplift to their numbers unless they choose it.

### What the status blocks

When a candidate's status changes who the counterparty is, say which option it blocks and why, as a fact. For an in-license: the option cannot be scored on counterparty-dependent criteria until the person confirms the legal name of the entity that holds the authorizations and would grant the license; the listing holder is not proof of it. For an acquisition: the target as named may now be a subsidiary of another company, so the deal is with the parent; route deal work to `investment-memo-and-decision-record` (this pack). For a co-development with a company that stopped SEC reporting: no 8-K Item 1.01 will disclose the agreement (DL-019 to DL-021).

Wording for a candidate: "EndeavorRx: De Novo granted 15 June 2020 (FDA De Novo DEN200026, library, retrieved 19 October 2026); now listed by Akili Interactive Labs c/o Virtual Therapeutics (FDA registration and listing database, read 20 October 2026, SP-168).
