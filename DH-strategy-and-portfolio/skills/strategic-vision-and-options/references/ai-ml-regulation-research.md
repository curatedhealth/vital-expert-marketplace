# AI/ML-enabled medical device software: regulation reference

Research pass of 19 October 2026. Primary sources only (statute, Official Journal, regulator pages and guidance, joint regulator documents, IMDRF documents). Rows marked [WF] were read through WebFetch (a model-summarized page read), not from a downloaded raw file, and their quotes could not be script-checked. Nothing in this file has been reviewed by the owner. Evidence class for every row: P (published primary source), read on the date shown.

Scope: skills "AI/ML pathway and change control plan" and "AI/ML readiness monitoring".

## Method

- Downloaded with curl through the configured proxy into `raw/`; PDFs converted with `pdftotext -layout`, HTML stripped to text. Quotes are copied from the raw text.
- EUR-Lex HTML/PDF endpoints returned an empty 202 challenge page. The Official Journal PDFs of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 were therefore fetched from the EU Publications Office Cellar (publications.europa.eu), which serves the same OJ manifestation. The omnibus was located with the Cellar SPARQL endpoint (CELEX 32026R1744).
- GOV.UK pages were read through the GOV.UK Content API (www.gov.uk/api/content/...), which returns the page body and publication metadata.
- imdrf.org refused every curl connection (HTTP/2 stream reset, then empty reply over HTTP/1.1) and WebFetch was blocked by robots.txt for the N67 and N88 PDFs. One IMDRF PDF (N93 draft) was readable through WebFetch only; those rows are [WF].
- The FDA AI-enabled device count was measured by downloading the list's CSV export and counting data rows (script in this pass); the page itself states no exact total.
- Quote check: every non-[WF] quote was script-checked against its raw text after normalizing whitespace, ligatures, curly quotes and PDF line numbers (see end of file).

## Changes and conflicts

1. FDA PCCP guidance: the brief says December 2024. The current version was issued on 18 August 2025 and records 4 December 2024 as the original issue (US-01). Cite the August 2025 version.
2. The FDA PCCP guidance still cites QSR sections (21 CFR 820.30, 820.90, 820.100). The January 2025 lifecycle draft notes that the amended Part 820 (QMSR) takes effect on 2 February 2026. Quality-system citations in both documents predate the QMSR.
3. FDA lifecycle guidance: still draft on 19 October 2026 (US-21). It is on CDRH's FY 2027 A-list as a final guidance topic (US-22), so finalization is planned but has not happened.
4. AI Act, Article 6(1) / Annex I (medical devices): 2 August 2027 in the 2024 text (EU-14), now 2 August 2028 under Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (EU-19). This is adopted and in the Official Journal, not a proposal.
5. AI Act, Annex III high-risk: 2 August 2026 in the 2024 text, now 2 December 2027 (EU-19).
6. MDCG 2025-6 (June 2025) still states 2 August 2027 for Annex I systems (EU-32). On dates it is superseded by the omnibus. Its classification and pre-determined-change content is not affected by the date change.
7. Article 4 AI literacy: the 2024 wording was "ensure, to their best extent, a sufficient level"; it is now "support the development", with no guaranteed level (EU-15 vs EU-20). The duty itself has applied since 2 February 2025.
8. Article 72(3): implementing-act template by 2 February 2026 (original), now guidance plus template by 2 September 2027 (EU-35).
9. FDA AI device count: the hub says "over 1,600 as of September 2026" (US-34). The measured CSV count is 1,614, with the latest decision on 29 June 2026 (US-35). The list lags authorizations by design (US-26).
10. UK: the 2022 roadmap planned PCCPs in secondary legislation (UK-02). By 6 October 2026 the stated next step is draft guidance for comment (UK-04). The roadmap page was last updated 14 June 2023, so its plan may be stale.
