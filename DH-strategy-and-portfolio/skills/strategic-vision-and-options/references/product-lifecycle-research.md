## Changes and conflicts

- **QMSR terminology vs older guidance.** The 2020 multiple function guidance still cites 21 CFR 820.30(g) and 820.180 (PL25 footnote); the PDURS draft cites 820.30(g). Both predate the 2 February 2026 QMSR. FDA's added note (PL12) says the "design controls" and "design validation" elements now sit in ISO 13485 Clause 7.3. Read 820.30(g) references as ISO 13485 7.3.7.
- **Design review independence.** QS regulation 820.30(e) required an individual without direct responsibility for the stage; the QMSR does not (research_q QS23; PL1, PL2). IMDRF N41 still recommends independent review of clinical evidence for higher-risk SaMD categories (PL66, PL67). A skill should treat independence as a risk-proportionate choice to justify, not a QMSR rule.
- **General Wellness.** 6 January 2026 version supersedes 27 September 2019 (PL46) and adds the non-invasive physiologic-output policy and disqualifier list (PL49 to PL51). Older wellness analyses predate this.
- **PDURS.** Draft only (September 2023). Not for implementation; content may change at finalization.
- **ISO 14155.** 2020 edition withdrawn 23 March 2026; 2026 edition current [WF]. The iso.org page for the 2026 edition shows stage 90.92 "to be revised" and a new ISO/AWI 14155 project, as reported by the summarizer; not verified from a raw file.
- **DiGA class IIb.** Eligible since 26 March 2024 (DigiG) but excluded from trial listing (PL78, PL79). Earlier DiGA material describing "class I and IIa only" is out of date.
- **DiGA data security dates.** BSI requirements from 1 January 2025 and BfArM privacy criteria from 1 August 2024 replace the earlier self-declaration under Anlage 1 for those areas (PL87).
- **DTAC.** New form from 24 February 2026; old form not to be used from 6 April 2026 [WF] (PL99).
- **IEC 62304 vs 82304-1 gate.** IEC 62304 excludes device validation and final release (PL105); IEC 82304-1 covers product validation for standalone health software (PL103). In the EU neither is harmonised under the MDR (research_q EU30).
- **IEC 62304 page.** The webstore page shows the base 2006 edition with "Under development" in its lifecycle tab; research_q ST7 and ST8 give the consolidated edition 1.1 (2015) and edition 2.0 forecast.
