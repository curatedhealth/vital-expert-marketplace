## Method

Pages and PDFs were downloaded with curl through the configured proxy on 19 October 2026 and converted to text with `pdftotext -layout` or a small HTML-to-text script. HAS (has-sante.fr) served an incomplete certificate chain; the missing intermediate (Sectigo Public Server Authentication CA OV R36) was fetched from the certificate's AIA URL into `research_ma3/ca/has-ca.pem` and combined with the proxy CA bundle, with TLS verification kept on throughout. HAS also returns intermittent bot-protection 403s, so some pages needed retries; its search pages could not be used. Legifrance (Cloudflare challenge), federalregister.gov (access-request page) and transform.england.nhs.uk (proxy 502) could not be fetched with curl. French code articles are therefore quoted as HAS reproduces them in its own guides, US Federal Register notices were taken from the official govinfo.gov copies, and the DTAC page was read with WebFetch [WF]. Facts are paraphrased in plain American English; quotes stay in the source language. No number in this file is inferred; each comes from the quoted passage.

## Changes and conflicts

1. **"Evidence submission template" does not exist in the current NICE HealthTech process.** PMG48 (paragraph 1.3.4) states that companies do not make evidence submissions; NICE issues requests for information and evidence requests. The task brief's assumption is outdated for guidance started after 14 July 2025.
2. **PMG39 (early value assessment interim statement, 15 December 2022) versus PMG48.** PMG48 now describes "early use" assessments. PMG39 is still online, but PMG48 is the current manual. EVA terminology should be treated as legacy.
3. **DTAC is not stated as a NICE prerequisite.** The ESF says NHS use "may" need DTAC compliance, and PMG48 does not mention DTAC. DTAC is a commissioner/provider assurance tool. The transform.england.nhs.uk page also says its content stopped updating on 31 March 2026 [WF], so DTAC's home may have moved.
4. **BfArM receipt confirmation: 14 days versus 21 days.** DiGAV § 16 Abs. 1 says 21 days for confirming receipt of complete documents. Leitfaden v3.6 section 5.1 says the applicant gets an answer within 14 days. Cite the statute (21 days) as the legal maximum and the Leitfaden (14 days) as BfArM practice.
5. **Leitfaden v3.6 is older than the DiGAV text in force.** The guide is dated 10 December 2025; the second DiGAV amendment took effect 1 February 2026. The guide's fee table cites "DiGAV, Stand: 20.12.2022", but the amounts match current §§ 25 and 26. Expect a Leitfaden update.
6. **HAS LPPR guide file name versus content.** The URL still reads "guide_fabricant_2016_01_11", but the PDF itself is the version of 13 March 2026. Cite it by its internal version date.
7. **HAS pages and guides mix timeline dates.** The PECAN guide is dated 15 September 2026 and the PECAN and LATM pages were updated 22 September 2026. The DM deposit page (updated 17 October 2023) links to March 2026 documents.
8. **US RAPID pathway is proposed, not final.** Its comment period closed 13 October 2026; no final notice was checked.
9. **The DiGA statistics are a snapshot** (chart dated 9 September 2026, which BfArM says it updates monthly). Withdrawals (131) far exceed refusals (29), so a refusal-only rate understates failed applications.
