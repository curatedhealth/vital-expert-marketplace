## Changes and conflicts

- Patient engagement cap: 42 CFR 1001.952(hh)(5) states $500; OIG's inflation page sets $623 for calendar year 2026. Use the OIG figure for the current year and recheck after 30 September each year.
- DMHT coverage: CY 2025 rule limited G0552 to 21 CFR 882.5801 devices (effective 1 January 2025). CY 2026 final rule (published 5 November 2025) added 882.5803 ADHD devices and declined the gastrointestinal, fibromyalgia and sleep classifications. Later correcting documents to the CY 2026 rule (2025-21458, 2026-04797) were listed in the Federal Register but not reviewed.
- Remote monitoring: before CY 2026 RPM device supply required 16 days of data; the CY 2026 code set adds 2 to 15 day codes. CMS valued 99445 and 99454 on OPPS cost data, and the RUC will resurvey after a year of data, so values may change.
- EUDAMED: the MDR text says obligations start 6 months after the Art 34(3) notice; the notice was published 27 November 2025 and the Commission states mandatory use from 28 May 2026. The 12-month backfill deadline in Art 123(3)(e) therefore runs to about 27 November 2026 by arithmetic; the Commission page linked here does not state that date, so confirm against its gradual roll-out Q&A. Vigilance and clinical investigation modules are not yet mandatory.
- DTAC: the old form was retired with full transition by 6 April 2026 (NHS Innovation Service news, published 4 March 2026). The developers' guidance page describing DTAC may predate the refresh.
- The US Code text is the 2023 govinfo edition; amendments after it are not reflected. eCFR sections are as of 1 October 2026.
- AKS fine: the statute text read gives $100,000 and 10 years (amended by Pub. L. 115-123 in 2018). Older secondary material citing $25,000 and 5 years is out of date.
