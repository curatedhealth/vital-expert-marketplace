## Changes and conflicts

1. **HSR form: eCFR versus FTC.** eCFR parts 801 and 803 show their latest amendment dated 10 February 2025 (measured from the eCFR versions API on 20 October 2026, file `raw/ecfr_803_hist.json`), so eCFR still prints the updated rules. The FTC states the updated form was vacated on 12 February 2026 and that it accepts the prior form (DL-051). For filing practice the FTC page governs; the appeal (Fifth Circuit No. 26-40094) was pending on the pages read.
2. **HSR 2026 thresholds: two dates.** The notice says thresholds take effect 30 days after publication (16 January 2026) and its DATES line reads 17 February 2026 (DL-044). Use 17 February 2026 as stated; transactions closing before then used 2025 figures (research not done on the 2025 notice, FR Doc 2025-01518).
3. **EU FDI screening: two regimes in sequence.** Regulation (EU) 2019/452 applies until 17 January 2028; Regulation (EU) 2026/1386 applies from that date, with some articles from 16 July 2026 (DL-075). The new regulation's mandatory scope excludes health and medicines; those appear only as risk factors (DL-076, DL-078). National laws may still cover health.
4. **Swiss IPG: adopted but not in force.** Referendum period expired 17 April 2026; SECO plans entry into force in 2027; the Federal Council fixes the date (DL-081, DL-082). Health thresholds (CHF 100 million turnover) apply only to foreign state-controlled acquirers (DL-083, DL-084).
5. **UK accounts change from 1 April 2028.** Today small and micro companies may file without a P&L (DL-040); from 1 April 2028 they must deliver it but may opt out of publication (DL-042). The opt-out mechanism is "to be confirmed" by Companies House. Size thresholds changed for periods from 6 April 2025 (DL-041).
6. **CFIUS one-million threshold versus DOJ bulk thresholds.** CFIUS treats health data as sensitive at more than one million individuals (genetic results at any count) (DL-056 to DL-058). The DOJ rule sets far lower bulk thresholds: 10,000 for health data, 100 for genomic data, and counts de-identified data (DL-066, DL-067). A target can be below CFIUS's test and still inside the DOJ rule.
7. **Breach notice timing differs by regime** (already flagged in research_cs): HIPAA covered entity 60 days to individuals; business associate 60 days to the covered entity (research_cmb CP-US-49); FTC HBNR 60 days (research_cma PC-US-83); GDPR processor "without undue delay" (research_cmb CP-EU-08).
8. **Evidence frameworks disagree on what counts.** The clinical robustness score counts only FDA filings and registered trials and ignores publications (DL-116, DL-118); the critique argues for accreditations and peer-reviewed studies (DL-119); Evidence DEFINED grades the evidence itself with GRADE and sets adoption levels (DL-107 to DL-112). None of the three was designed for investors, and none has published interrater reliability (DL-113).
9. **Delisted, deregistered and dissolved are three different states.** Form 25 ends the exchange listing and 12(b) registration only (DL-144); Form 15 suspends SEC reporting but can lapse if holder counts rise (DL-149); neither says the company was dissolved. Dissolution is a registry fact (Companies House "dissolved", DL-160 to DL-163). A diligence skill should not equate an EDGAR filing gap with a company's end.
10. **Absence of evidence in German notices.** Insolvency notices are deleted six months after proceedings end (DL-166), so a clean search today is not proof of no past insolvency.
