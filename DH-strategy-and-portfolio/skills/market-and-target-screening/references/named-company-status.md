# Named companies: status as the filings and registers state it (read 20 October 2026)

Source: rows SP-148 to SP-169 of `references/strategy-and-portfolio-research.md` (section 8, read 20 October 2026) and the "what it does not mean" rows DL-133 to DL-149 of `references/diligence-research.md`. Shared by the diligence skills of the dh-strategy-and-portfolio pack. Every SEC row here is [WF]: sec.gov was read through a summarizing fetch, so quotes are close but not script-checked. FDA listing rows come from openFDA's registration and listing data (dataset last updated 5 October 2026).

## How to use this file

1. **Check here first.** Before writing "Status: [GAP]: not read" for a named company, look for it below. Where a row covers it, state the status from the filing, with the source in plain words and the date, then the row id: "Pear 8-K, 7 April 2023 (SP-156 [WF])". Never a row id alone.
2. **Keep "[GAP]: not read" for the rest.** A company not in this file, or a register this file does not cover (Companies House, German or Israeli registers, court dockets), stays "[GAP]: not read" with the filings to open (status step in the skill's status reference).
3. **Say what each record does not mean** (last column). A Form 25 or a Form 15 is not dissolution.
4. **State the product's holder separately from the company that was authorized.** The 510(k) and De Novo pages show the applicant at clearance; the current FDA listing holder is a different record. Write both, each with its source.
5. **Dates.** These are reads of 20 October 2026. Anything filed after that date is not covered; say "as read on 20 October 2026".
6. **Inference rows** are labeled "(inference)" every time they are cited.

What the listing does not mean: the listing holder is who lists the device with FDA now. It is not the sale record and does not show a transfer of the clearance or the price; Pear's buyers are named only in court sale orders, not read (SP-161; research file "Not sourced"). FDA databases search by applicant, so a parent or predecessor name can hide a later owner (DL-133 (inference)): a library or screen row that shows "Pear Therapeutics, Inc." for Somryst shows the applicant as stored, not today's holder.
