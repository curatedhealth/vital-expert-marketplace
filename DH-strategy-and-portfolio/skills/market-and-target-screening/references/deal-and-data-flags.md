# Deal and data rules to flag (facts for the owner the person names for legal questions)

These rows are facts to flag beside a candidate, each with its row id and date. Whether a deal is caught, reportable, notifiable or compliant is for the owner the person names for legal or compliance questions. The model never says a deal is clear, never says a rule does not apply, and never estimates a filing outcome. Rows are in `diligence-research.md` (read 20 October 2026); "(inference)" marks the research reader's own reading.

## How to flag

For each candidate on a short list, write a flag only where the person's facts or the record touch a trigger in the table below, in this form: "Flag for the owner you name for legal questions: RULE (row, instrument and date). Trigger in the facts: WHAT (source). Not assessed here." Where the facts needed to know (data volume, investor nationality, deal size) are not given, write "not given" and list the question for the owner. Never compute whether a threshold is met from a figure the person did not give. Where the person gives a figure, show the threshold and the figure side by side, with no word in prose on whether it is under or over, for the owner the person names for legal questions.

## What stops

Any statement that a deal is or is not reportable, notifiable, caught or compliant; any filing strategy; any wording for a regulator or counterparty. Draft the fact list for the owner, labeled "draft for the owner", and stop.
