# Strategy and portfolio: sourced reference (row prefix SP)

Research pass of 20 October 2026 for the dh-strategy-and-portfolio skill pack (digital health strategy, product portfolio governance, horizon scanning and technology evaluation, innovation experimentation, business development and licensing). Primary sources only: statutes and regulations, regulators and official bodies (EMA, FDA, MHRA, NHS, European Commission, WHO, OECD, HM Treasury, UK project delivery bodies, US DOJ and FTC, SEC rules via eCFR), ISO abstract pages for paywalled standards, and peer-reviewed papers read at full text (labeled [PAPER] with DOI). [WF] means the row was read through WebFetch (a summarizing fetch), so its quote could not be script-checked against a raw download; treat [WF] quotes as close but not guaranteed verbatim. Not owner-reviewed. Row numbers have gaps where rows were cut in editing; IDs were not reused.

## Method

- Downloads with curl through the configured proxy on 20 October 2026. EU acts came from publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX> as XHTML (EUR-Lex itself blocks automated download). WHO IRIS PDFs came from the IRIS DSpace REST API (bitstream content links). eCFR text came from the eCFR versioner API (version of 1 October 2026). Peer-reviewed papers came from the Europe PMC full-text XML service or the publisher's open-access PDF.
- PDFs were converted with `pdftotext -layout` (and a non-layout pass for two-column documents); HTML with a tag-stripping script (`h2t.py`).
- iso.org and the OECD site returned 403 to curl; ISO pages were read with WebFetch [WF]. The Oslo Manual 2018 PDF was read from a copy hosted by the Hungarian National Research, Development and Innovation Office (nkfih.gov.hu); the file is the OECD/Eurostat publication itself (cover, OECD disclaimer and DOI 10.1787/9789264304604-en present), but the host is not the OECD.
- ISPOR MCDA task force reports (Value in Health) returned 403 to curl and were read with WebFetch [WF]. Only Report 2 could be read; Report 1 failed to fetch.
- Raw files: `research_sp/raw/`. Quotes were checked by script (`qcheck.py`) against the raw text after removing whitespace and normalizing quotes, dashes, ligatures and line-end hyphens.
- Read date for every row: 20 October 2026.

## Changes and conflicts

- TTBER: Regulation 316/2014 and the 2014 Guidelines expired on 30 April 2026. Regulation (EU) 2026/877 applies from 1 May 2026 to 30 April 2038, with a transitional period to 30 April 2027 for agreements that met the old rules (SP-134 to SP-141). Any skill text citing 316/2014 as current is out of date. The 20% and 30% thresholds are unchanged (2014: "on the relevant market(s)"; 2026: "on any relevant market"); the 2026 review clarified how they apply to technology markets.
- WHO Global Strategy on Digital Health: titled 2020-2025 but extended to 2027 by WHA78 (27 May 2025); a 2028-2033 strategy goes to the 80th World Health Assembly in 2027 (SP-066). Not yet adopted.
- WHO CDI: the 2018 first edition ("Classification of digital health interventions v1.0") is superseded by the 2023 second edition with a new title (SP-054 to SP-058).
- ISO innovation standards: ISO 56000:2020 was withdrawn on 23 January 2025 (replaced by ISO 56000:2025, not read); ISO 56002:2019 is under revision; ISO 56001:2024 is the certifiable requirements standard (SP-044 to SP-047). [WF] reads.
- UK Green Book: the 2026 edition (5 February 2026) withdrew earlier editions and dropped "evaluation" from the subtitle; evaluation now sits in the Magenta Book and delivery in the Teal Book (SP-088 to SP-098).
- UK benefits assurance guidance: the file at the 2021 URL is now v2.1 dated August 2026 and is credited to NISTA, not the Infrastructure and Projects Authority (SP-099).
- TRL: EU and NASA scales share nine levels but differ at levels 7 to 9 (operational environment vs space and flight). Use the EU wording for health products (SP-028 to SP-035). The Horizon Europe 2026-2027 annex keeps the same wording as 2023-2025.
- EHDS: general application 26 March 2027, but most operational duties phase in 2029, 2031 and later (SP-068 to SP-073).
- FDA EFS guidance (October 2013) predates the QMSR (effective 2 February 2026); FDA added a note that QMSR terms differ (SP-118).
- FDA TAP: still a pilot, but expanded to all OHTs from 1 July 2026 (SP-122). Enrollment counts change; do not reuse the 159 figure without date.
- MHRA IDAP: the pilot is closed to new applicants; the future IDAP is not yet defined on the page read (SP-124).
- Clinical trial success rates differ by method (path-by-path vs phase-by-phase) and dataset; do not mix figures from different methods in one portfolio model (SP-082 to SP-085).
- Somryst: the 510(k) record still names Pear Therapeutics as applicant (SP-165), while FDA's registration and listing data show Nox Medical as the listing owner from 18 December 2024 (SP-167). EndeavorRx's De Novo names Akili Interactive Labs; its listing is now held "c/o Virtual Therapeutics" (SP-166, SP-168). The 510(k) and De Novo pages show the applicant at clearance; for the current holder, read the registration and listing record.
- Akili: the EDGAR registrant Akili, Inc. was formerly the SPAC Social Capital Suvretta Holdings Corp. I; Akili Interactive Labs, Inc. is its subsidiary, not a former name (SP-148).
- Green Book optimism bias guidance: the gov.uk page dates from 21 April 2013, but the content is a 2003 document built on 2002 data. It is not withdrawn and the 2026 Green Book still cites it (SP-170, SP-171).
