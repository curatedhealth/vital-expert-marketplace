# Strategy and Portfolio: capability map

Framework position: **DH-01.05 Digital Health Strategy & Vision**, **DH-01.08 Innovation & Emerging Technologies**, the **DH-01.03.3 Governance & Portfolio** heading of DH-01.03 Cross-Functional Leadership, and the **DH-DIL** diligence suite, now one pack (`dh-strategy-and-portfolio`). This skill replaces the old `diligence-capability-map`, the three old core menus (DH-01.05, DH-01.08, DH-01.03) and their heading menus, and the old "DH Diligence" core menu and job menus. It does one job: show the person what the pack covers and send them to the one competency skill that fits the request.

## What this skill does and does not do

It reads the request, matches it to a competency, names that skill (and the task where it helps), and says in one line why. If two skills fit, it names both and the order. It does not answer the strategy, portfolio or diligence question itself, call a connector, or state any rule, record, readiness level, benchmark, success rate, score or threshold. Those live in the competency skills, each with its own sourced references.

People decide in every skill of this pack: vision, options chosen, priorities, kill or continue, weights, scores, thresholds, horizons, owners and investment decisions are the person's. A development success rate or any other benchmark is context with its counting method, never the person's probability or target. No skill supplies a market size, valuation, return or investment advice.

If the named skill is not available in this assistant, say so and name the pack to install (`dh-strategy-and-portfolio`). Do not do the competency's work from this map.

## Two portfolios, never mixed

"Portfolio" always says which:

- **Product portfolio**: the company's own products and programs (what to fund, continue, stop or delay, and whether the forum followed its rule). Skills: `product-portfolio-prioritization`, `product-portfolio-review`.
- **Investment portfolio**: companies held or targeted as investments (holdings, funding records, side-by-side comparison of companies). Skills: `investment-portfolio-monitoring`, `funding-and-portfolio-comparison`.

If a request says "portfolio" and does not show which, ask one question before routing: "Do you mean your own products and programs, or companies you hold or are looking to invest in?" When asking, name both routes per side (product: `product-portfolio-review`, `product-portfolio-prioritization`; investment: `investment-portfolio-monitoring`, `funding-and-portfolio-comparison`) and say the two are never combined in one answer. Never route on the bare word.

## The framework tree

Each heading has a skill that does the work (design, evaluation, assembly) and one that checks it over time. The monitoring skill always works from the measures, rules and dates the person sets; it never sets its own and never scores a person.

**DH-01.05 and DH-01.08: Strategy** (where to play and how to test it)

- `strategic-vision-and-options` (DH-01.05.1.1; folds DH-01.08.2.1). Shape the opportunity and the vision, lay out build, partner, license or acquire options with public-record facts and the licensing rules legal must check, design option tests (prototypes, experiments, early regulated studies, sandboxes), and record the person's vision and choice with their scores ranked.
- `strategy-execution-monitoring` (DH-01.05.4; folds DH-01.05.1.2 and DH-01.08.2.2). Turn the chosen strategy into objectives, a benefits register and milestones with pass rules (A1), read realization, benefits and option tests against the person's own baselines, targets and rules (A2), and review each failed element with its owner (B1). UK Green Book terms are labeled as UK public-sector guidance when used for a company.

**DH-01.08 and DH-01.05.3: Horizon and technology**

- `horizon-scanning-and-technology-evaluation` (DH-01.08.4.1; folds DH-01.08.1.1 and DH-01.05.3.1). Scan for sourced, dated signals kept apart from trends, evaluate an emerging technology with a proposed readiness level in the EU wording, and build scenarios and implications on the person's drivers.
- `foresight-signal-monitoring` (DH-01.08.4.2; folds DH-01.08.1.2 and DH-01.05.3.2). Check logged signals against the person's quality checks (A1), resolve past foresight calls and technology selections on dated records by the person's own rules (A2), and review each miss with its owner (B1).

**DH-01.03.3: Product portfolio** (the company's own products and programs)

- `product-portfolio-prioritization` (DH-01.03.3.1). List what asks the forum to continue, stop or delay, write the rule before the rank, rank only what the person scored, and record the forum's decision.
- `product-portfolio-review` (DH-01.03.3.2). Check each decision record against the pass rule, score the forum against its own rule and capacity, read continue items against the person's benefits plan, and draft the repair.

**DH-DIL: Diligence** (companies, targets and the investment portfolio)

- DH-DIL.1 Market and pipeline (folds DH-01.05.2 Market Intelligence)
  - `market-and-target-screening` (DH-DIL.1.1). Screen a market and map its ecosystem: an ecosystem map of buyers, payers, peers and rules from records (A3, folds DH-01.05.2.1), the companies the library holds for a segment, what each register shows about a named company and whether it still exists, and a short list on the person's criteria and scores.
  - `pipeline-and-market-monitoring` (DH-DIL.1.2). Dated reruns of a pipeline and a market: what changed since a date, screen and rule movement, a market intelligence quality check against the person's rule (A3, folds DH-01.05.2.2), and a pipeline review with owners.
- DH-DIL.2 Company and evidence
  - `company-and-evidence-assessment` (DH-DIL.2.1). One company from public records: profile, evidence maturity with a proposed framework level, and an assessment record on the person's criteria.
  - `evidence-maturity-monitoring` (DH-DIL.2.2). A company's evidence over time: measures, movement between dated runs, review.
- DH-DIL.3 Funding and investment portfolio
  - `funding-and-portfolio-comparison` (DH-DIL.3.1). Public funding records and 2 to 12 companies side by side, then ranking only what the person scored.
  - `investment-portfolio-monitoring` (DH-DIL.3.2; renamed from `portfolio-monitoring`). Holdings or a committed shortlist against the person's watch rules.
- DH-DIL.4 Investment memo and thesis
  - `investment-memo-and-decision-record` (DH-DIL.4.1). A sourced fact base, the deal rules a memo must flag (including, for a license, EU TTBER 2026/877 and the DOJ/FTC IP licensing guidelines, for the legal owner), and a decision record under the person's headings.
  - `thesis-and-memo-monitoring` (DH-DIL.4.2). Whether the thesis and the memo's facts still hold.

Tie-breakers:

1. Portfolio: own products and programs go to the product portfolio pair; companies held or targeted go to the diligence skills by name. If unclear, ask the one question above.
2. Strategy before portfolio: if there is no strategy or option set yet, start with `strategic-vision-and-options`; ranking already-scoped products and programs is `product-portfolio-prioritization`.
3. Options and deals: the strategic choice among build, partner, license or acquire is `strategic-vision-and-options`; diligence on a named target is `company-and-evidence-assessment` (A1 profile, A2 evidence maturity; company status only from its own filings or register entry, else '[GAP]: not read'; for companies the pack's research rows already cover, such as Big Health, Pear or Akili, the skill states status from those rows), and the memo with its deal and licensing flags is `investment-memo-and-decision-record`. In both, whether a license is exempt or a deal is reportable is for the owner the person names for legal questions.
4. Signals and markets: technology and market signals over a horizon are `horizon-scanning-and-technology-evaluation`; the buyers, payers, peers and rules around one market are `market-and-target-screening` A3.
5. Design and assessment come before monitoring. If nothing has been set, scanned, ranked, screened or written yet, the monitoring skill has nothing to check; send the person to its partner first.
6. If the request is vague ("help with our digital health strategy"), ask one question: is this about our options, delivering the strategy, what is coming, our own products, or companies and deals? Then route.

## Out of scope for this pack

## Routing examples

- "We're a mid-size pharma company deciding whether to build, partner on or acquire a digital therapeutic for insomnia." Route to `strategic-vision-and-options` (A1 for the facts, B1 for the choice); for a named target, `company-and-evidence-assessment` next.
- "Map the insomnia DTx ecosystem in the US and Germany: who buys, who pays, who the peers are." Route to `market-and-target-screening` A3; no market size is given.
- "Our portfolio board meets next quarter; which programs should we fund?" Own products: `product-portfolio-prioritization`. The person scores; the skill ranks only those scores.
- "Did last year's portfolio board follow its own rule on the programs it continued?" Route to `product-portfolio-review`.
- "Anything new on our holdings Akili and Pear since January?" Investment portfolio: `investment-portfolio-monitoring`.
- "What is moving in AI triage, and what TRL is ambient scribing at?" Route to `horizon-scanning-and-technology-evaluation` (A1, then A2); the level is proposed in the EU wording.
- "Our 2025 horizon scan said wearables would be reimbursed by now. How good were our calls?" Route to `foresight-signal-monitoring`.
- "Are we delivering our digital health strategy against the objectives we set?" Route to `strategy-execution-monitoring`.
- "We're in-licensing an AI algorithm from a German company. Is it block-exempted?" Route to `investment-memo-and-decision-record` A2 for the licensing facts, flagged for the legal owner; whether it is exempt is that owner's call.
