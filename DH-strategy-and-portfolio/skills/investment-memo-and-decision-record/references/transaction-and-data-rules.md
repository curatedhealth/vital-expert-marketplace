# Transaction and health-data rules a memo flags

Source: `diligence-research.md` (DL rows, read 20 October 2026) and `channels-partners-launch-sources.md` (CP rows, read 19 October 2026). Interpretive reference for A2. The skill states each rule, its figure, its instrument and date, and how the person's deal facts sit beside it. Whether a deal is reportable, covered, notifiable, prohibited or compliant is for the owner the person names for legal or compliance, or owner not named. Never write that a deal is clear.

If the value is between USD 133.9 million and USD 535.5 million, the size-of-person test also applies (DL-046, DL-047); if the person gave no party sizes, write "party sizes not given". ## 2. CFIUS and sensitive personal data (31 CFR part 800)

| Rule | Figure or fact | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Sensitive personal data: health condition data and health or life insurance application data are listed categories | DL-057 |
| Scale test: more than one million individuals in the prior 12 months, or a business targeting national-security agencies, or a demonstrated objective to reach that scale | DL-056 |
| Genetic test results: sensitive at any count | DL-058 |
| The one-million test can be met across categories combined | DL-059 |
| Public-record data and most employee data are excluded | DL-060 |
| A US business that maintains or collects sensitive personal data of US citizens is a TID US business | DL-061 |
| A non-controlling foreign investment in a TID US business is covered if it gives a board or observer seat, access to material nonpublic technical information, or involvement in decisions on sensitive personal data | DL-062 |
| Mandatory declaration: a foreign person acquires a substantial interest in a TID US business and a single foreign government holds a substantial interest in that person | DL-063 |
| Substantial interest: 25 percent voting by the investor; 49 percent by a foreign government in the investor | DL-064 |
| Foreign person includes any entity controlled by a foreign national, government or entity | DL-065 |

Not sourced: CFIUS filing fees, timelines, the excepted foreign states list and statistics (`[GAP]`).

## 3. DOJ bulk sensitive data rule (28 CFR part 202)

| Rule | Figure or fact | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Bulk thresholds in the preceding 12 months | Human genomic data: more than 100 US persons; other human 'omic data: more than 1,000; personal health data: more than 10,000 | DL-066 |
| De-identified data counts | Counted whether or not anonymized, pseudonymized, de-identified or encrypted; HIPAA de-identification does not take a dataset out of scope (inference) | DL-067 |
| Personal health data | Past, present or future health, care and payment, including basic measurements such as vital signs | DL-068 |
| Countries of concern | China, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Venezuela | DL-069 |
| 'Omic prohibition | No covered data transaction giving a country of concern or covered person access to bulk human 'omic data or biospecimens | DL-070 |
| Covered person | An entity 50 percent or more owned by a country of concern, or organized or based there | DL-071 |

The CFIUS test and the DOJ thresholds differ: a target can be below CFIUS's one-million test and still inside the DOJ rule (research file, changes and conflicts item 6).

## 8. What the person must give for A2

Stated transaction value and currency; expected closing date; structure (control or minority; voting percentages); each investor's country and any government ownership and its percentage; board or observer rights; counts of US individuals whose health, genetic or 'omic data the target holds, and whether any country of concern or covered person gets access; EU member states and Swiss presence of the target; Swiss turnover if a Swiss target; whether the target is a HIPAA covered entity, a business associate or neither. Anything not given is "not given" and is asked for; nothing is assumed.

For a deal that includes a technology license, the license facts the person must give are in `licensing-deal-rules.md` section 4 (territory, rights, exclusivity, whether their legal owner treats the parties as competitors, markets and shares as that owner defines them, in force on 30 April 2026, research and development restrictions, pools, SEC registrant). The skill never defines a market or supplies a share.
