# Strategy & Portfolio (DH)

This pack replaces four packs: `dh-digital-health-strategy` (DH-01.05 Digital Health Strategy & Vision), `dh-innovation` (DH-01.08 Innovation & Emerging Technologies, except DH-01.08.3 partnerships, which route to `dh-go-to-market` channel-and-partner-design), `dh-cross-functional-leadership` (DH-01.03.3 Governance & Portfolio only; DH-01.03.1 stakeholder alignment, DH-01.03.2 talent and DH-01.03.4 matrix leadership were dropped) and `dh-diligence` (the DH-DIL diligence suite).

"Portfolio" always says which: product portfolio (the company's own products and programs) or investment portfolio (companies held). The two are never mixed; start with `strategy-and-portfolio-capability-map` if unsure.

## Map

- `strategy-and-portfolio-capability-map`: The map of the VITAL Strategy and Portfolio pack for pharma, medtech and digital health strategy, portfolio and business development teams, and investors.

## DH-01.05 Digital Health Strategy & Vision and DH-01.08.2 Prototype & Experimentation (was dh-digital-health-strategy and dh-innovation)

- `strategic-vision-and-options`: Shape a digital health strategy and its options for a pharma, medtech or digital health company: name the opportunity (WHO intervention axes, Oslo type, proposed EU TRL), lay out build, partner, license or acquire options with public-record facts on named candidates and the licensing rules legal must check (EU TTBER 2026/877, DOJ/FTC), design option tests (prototypes, experiments, FDA early feasibility, TAP, MHRA IDAP, AI Airlock), and record the person's vision and choice with their scores ranked and stress-tested.
- `strategy-execution-monitoring`: Track whether a digital health strategy is being delivered for a pharma, medtech or digital health company: turn the chosen strategy into objectives, a benefits register, milestones with dependencies and pass rules; read realization against the person's own baselines, targets and rules (benefits, milestones, option tests, partners' public records); and review each failed element with the owner and recheck date the person sets.

## DH-01.08 Innovation & Emerging Technologies and DH-01.05.3 Technology Foresight (was dh-innovation and dh-digital-health-strategy)

- `horizon-scanning-and-technology-evaluation`: Scan the horizon for digital health, AI and medtech developments and evaluate emerging technology for a pharma, medtech or digital health strategy team: a log of sourced, dated records (FDA and EU clearances, decisions per device type, publications, company changes, dated rule changes such as the AI Act and EHDS) kept apart from leads and trends; a technology fact sheet with a proposed TRL in the EU wording; scenarios, implications and disruption on the person's drivers, with their scored options ranked.
- `foresight-signal-monitoring`: Check whether a strategy team's horizon signals, foresight calls and technology selections in digital health, AI and medtech hold up: test each logged signal against the person's quality checks and reread it on the record (FDA and EU registers, publications, company filings), separate leads and press items from signals, resolve past calls and selections by the person's own rules on dated records, count hits and misses with the denominator stated, and review against the person's pass rule with the miss owners they name.

## DH-01.03.3 Governance & Portfolio: the product portfolio, own products and programs (was dh-cross-functional-leadership)

- `product-portfolio-prioritization`: Prioritize a company's own product portfolio (digital health, SaMD, DTx, pharma or medtech programs): list the items asking a governance forum to continue, stop or delay, with their sourced facts and every gap; write the person's prioritization rule before anything is ranked; rank only what the person scored (MCDA or RICE), test where the ranking flips; and record the forum's decision with decider, date and the capacity it uses.
- `product-portfolio-review`: Review how a company's product portfolio forum decided and whether its decisions hold: check each decision record against the person's pass rule (rule, rank, decision, person), score the last forum by reading each decision against the confirmed rule (match or drift, continue items past the named capacity), read continue items against the person's own benefits plan, and draft the repair: whether the rule or the decision is corrected, the owner, and the forum that must show it.

## DH-DIL Diligence, with DH-01.05.2 Market Intelligence folded in: companies, targets and the investment portfolio (was dh-diligence)

- `market-and-target-screening`: Screen and map a digital health market (DTx, SaMD, DiGA, AI tools): an ecosystem map of buyers, payers, peers and rules from records; the companies the library holds for a segment and jurisdiction; what each register and filing shows about a named company and whether it still exists (FDA, DiGA directory, SEC filings, Companies House); and a short list ranked only on the person's criteria and scores, with deal and data rules flagged for the owner they name.
- `pipeline-and-market-monitoring`: Monitor a diligence pipeline and a digital health market (DTx, SaMD, DiGA) by dated reruns, never watches: what changed in named companies' records since a date, each one's status from its own filings, what moved in a rerun screen and the library's perimeter, which dated rule changes touch the pipeline (HSR, EU and Swiss screening, AI Act, DiGA directory), whether a market intelligence map still meets the person's rule (source, date, coverage, moved rules), and each entry reviewed against the person's rules with owner and reread date.
- `company-and-evidence-assessment`: Assess one digital health company (DTx, DiGA, SaMD) for diligence from public records: build its profile (authorizations, registered studies, SEC and Form D filings, what each record does not show), read its evidence maturity (the library score kept apart from a proposed NICE ESF, Evidence DEFINED or V3 level), and write an assessment record against the investor's own criteria.
- `evidence-maturity-monitoring`: Watch a digital health company's evidence over time for diligence or an investment portfolio (DTx, DiGA, SaMD): set the measures that count as evidence movement, compare two dated evidence maturity runs and sort each difference into a record change (new trial, results posted, new authorization) or an artifact (rubric version, newly loaded source, different company resolved), and review it against the investor's own rules with owners and reread dates.
- `funding-and-portfolio-comparison`: Read the public funding record of digital health companies (DTx, DiGA, SaMD) and put 2 to 12 of them side by side from their records: SEC filings by type (Form D, 8-K, 10-K, S-1), NIH grants, EU awards, authorizations, studies, safety events, the library's evidence maturity rubric, and each company's status (bankrupt, delisted, deregistered, acquired) from its filings or register; then rank only what the person scored and record their decision.
- `investment-portfolio-monitoring`: Watch an investment portfolio (companies held) or a shortlist of digital health companies against the person's own watch rules: what each public signal can and cannot tell (8-K items, Form D amendments, FDA records, HHS breach portal, UK filings), what changed in the library's record since a date, each holding's status (bankrupt, delisted, deregistered, acquired) from its filings or register, a diff against an earlier saved run, and each flag with owner and reread date.
- `investment-memo-and-decision-record`: Assemble an investment memo on a digital health company from the public record: a sourced fact base (authorizations, studies, filings, Form D, safety events), the deal rules a memo must flag (HSR, CFIUS, DOJ bulk data, EU and Swiss screening, HIPAA, GDPR; for a license, EU TTBER 2026/877 and DOJ/FTC IP licensing guidelines, flagged for the legal owner), and a decision record under the person's headings, thesis, scores and decision.
- `thesis-and-memo-monitoring`: Watch an investment thesis and the facts in a memo on a digital health company (DTx, SaMD, DiGA) after the decision: set which assumptions to re-check and the public signal behind each, read what changed in the record since the memo date (authorizations, studies, safety events, SEC filings, evidence maturity) and in the deal rules (HSR, EU and Swiss screening, Companies House), and review each assumption against the person's own triggers with owners and reread dates.
