# DH Regulatory Strategy and Submissions

Install **DH Regulatory Strategy and Submissions** from `curatedhealth/vital-expert-plugins`, then paste the Digital Health Library key. The key is not in this pack.

## Install

1. In Cowork, open Customize, then Plugins, then Add marketplace.
2. Enter `curatedhealth/vital-expert-plugins`.
3. Install **DH Regulatory Strategy and Submissions**.
4. When Cowork asks for the Digital Health Library key, paste the `vlib_` key from your purchase.
5. Leave the plugin switched on.

The connector is `https://mcp.vital.expert/api/mcp/library`. You do not add a second connector.

## Skills

- `ai-ml-device-pathway-and-change-control`. Map the regulatory route for an AI-enabled medical device software product in the US, EU and UK, draft the structure of its predetermined change control plan (PCCP), and lay out which submission carries what and in which order.
- `ai-ml-readiness-monitoring`. Check whether an AI-enabled medical device software product is ready to file, and whether it stays inside its authorization afterwards: a completeness check of the submission or technical file against sourced US, EU and UK requirements for the person's jurisdictions and dates (AI Act obligations at their amended dates), tracking of model changes against the authorized PCCP, performance monitoring expectations, safety events for the product and comparable devices, and a review against the person's own rules.
- `multi-region-regulatory-alignment`. Plan how one software medical device reaches several regulators: how the same software is classified in the US, EU, Great Britain, Switzerland, Canada, Australia, Brazil and Japan, which reliance or recognition routes exist and whether they are law or still a draft, how MDSAP is used, which evidence (IMDRF documents, foreign clinical data, one technical file) can be reused, and in what order to file.
- `cross-region-compliance-monitoring`. Keep a software medical device compliant in every region it is already in: where it is authorized according to the registers, which registration, renewal and acceptance dates are running (FDA annual registration, Health Canada annual statement, EUDAMED device registration, CE acceptance end dates in Great Britain, Swiss registration), what the regulators changed or have in draft, and whether the multi-region plan is holding.
- `regulatory-pathway-and-classification`. Lay out how a medical device software product (SaMD, digital therapeutic, AI-enabled software, clinical decision support) is likely classified in the US, EU, UK and Switzerland, which routes comparable devices took, and which regulatory pathway and sequence to pursue.
- `regulatory-pathway-monitoring`. Check whether a medical device software product's regulatory strategy is working: what changed on the FDA and EUDAMED registers for the product and its comparables, how open 510(k), De Novo or PMA reviews sit against the regulation clock, MDUFA goals and FDA's actual performance, which EUDAMED, FDA, GB and Swiss registration duties apply, what public recalls, MAUDE reports and MHRA alerts show for comparables, and where the pathway has stalled.
- `regulatory-submission-and-dossier`. Prepare a software medical device submission: map what the dossier must contain per route (FDA 510(k) or De Novo eSTAR sections, the device-software documentation levels, a PCCP section, EU MDR Annex II and III technical documentation, UK position), build the clinical evaluation structure under MDR Article 61, Annex XIV and MDCG 2020-1 with literature found and labeled, and plan dossier assembly and agency interaction (Pre-Sub, SIR, review clocks, notified body).
- `submission-quality-and-outcome-monitoring`. Check and follow a software medical device submission: test a draft dossier for missing sections against the sourced content map for its route (FDA eSTAR, device software documentation, PCCP, EU MDR Annex II and III), track a filed submission's clocks in FDA days and sponsor days against MDUFA goals and FDA's reported performance, follow hold letters and response routes, see how comparable devices in the product code were decided, and review submission performance against the team's own rules.
- `regulatory-strategy-capability-map`. The map of the VITAL Regulatory Strategy & Submissions pack for digital health products (SaMD, AI/ML devices, DTx, DiGA-type apps).
- `digital-health-expert`. Find and download a VITAL Digital Health expert (a ready-made role such as the SaMD Regulatory Affairs Director, the Quality & Software Lifecycle Director or the Regulatory Submission Manager) to run on the person's own assistant.

The library tools in this pack search devices, open one authorization, compare authorizations, read market status, classify a product, read pathway precedents, find United States predicates, watch a register, read what that watch recorded, search safety events, list the loaded registers, retrieve stored guidance, and generate a pathway report. `get_deltas` is the same call as reading register changes.

Finding an expert is free. Downloading one expert is one counted call on the same key.

## The key

The key is the purchase. It carries a monthly call cap. Menu calls such as listing jobs are not counted. A successful tool call spends one unit. Verify can spend several units on one task. When the cap is reached, or the purchase ends, the tools stop. The installed plugin remains.
The skills run only while the Digital Health Library connector is connected. Turning the plugin off removes the skills and the connector together.
