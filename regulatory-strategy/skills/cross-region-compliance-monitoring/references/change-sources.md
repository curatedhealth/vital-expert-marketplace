## 3. How to word a change

- Name the instrument, its date and its status word: "Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (adopted, in force 27 July 2026) moves AI Act obligations for MDR devices to 2 August 2028."
- A proposal or draft never changes an obligation in the obligations table until it is adopted.
- A superseded document is named with its replacement.
- An agenda item is a plan: "listed for finalization in FY 2027", never "will be final".
