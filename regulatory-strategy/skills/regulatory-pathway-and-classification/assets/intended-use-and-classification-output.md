# Output format: intended use and classification (argument draft)

Short mode is 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column and the calls list. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question, full-mode sections. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Offer the full version in one line. A refusal or stop answer follows the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** one or two sentences: where the facts point, per jurisdiction, and the one fact that decides the close call.
1a. **Intended use (draft for the owner):** only when the person asks for it or none exists; the five parts in two or three sentences, inside the 450 words.
2. **Classes the facts point to:** Jurisdiction · Rule (row) · Points to · What would change it.
3. **Is it a device:** one line per jurisdiction where this is in doubt (CDS criteria, wellness exclusion).
4. **Decisions for the owner:** at most three (for example the final class, the intended use wording, a claim to drop).
5. **Gaps:** one line, including one clause of N12 reasoning (situation by significance, JX-01 to JX-03) with "N81 (2025) not read (RP IM-04)".
6. **Record:** "Argument draft for the regulatory owner (NAME, or owner not named). Uncleared." or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Intended use statement (draft for the owner)

Five parts: medical purpose; condition and seriousness; intended user; setting; output type. Where the person's material says two things, both versions and the class each points to.

### Classification arguments

| Jurisdiction | Rule | Facts that meet it | Points to | Alternative reading and what decides it | Row |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

IMDRF N12 reasoning (significance by situation) and the MDCG mapping, with the note on N81.

### Record check

What `classify_product` returned for any reference, labeled "from record", and that it is an input, not a decision.

### AI-specific points (if applicable)

AI Act high-risk status from the MDR class and its date with the amending act; PCCP availability per route.

### What moves the class

The facts and claims the owner controls that move the product up or down.

### Gaps

Rules not in the research rows; superseded versions flagged.

### Record

Argument draft. The class, the intended use as filed and any wording for a regulator stop for the regulatory owner. AI-assisted. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
