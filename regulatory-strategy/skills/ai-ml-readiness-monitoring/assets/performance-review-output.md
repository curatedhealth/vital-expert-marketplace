# Output format: performance review (review draft)

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

**Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.** Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Inline row brackets count. Also cut asides (stored names, why a change matters), conditional clocks for markets the person did not name, and repeated owner-decision sentences (say it once). The calls list is not counted.

1. **Verdict by area:** Area · On track / At risk / Missed · Stall point · Owner you named.
2. **The decision needed:** one or two sentences: which miss matters most and what choice the person faces.
3. **Hypotheses to test first:** at most three, each with what would confirm it.
4. **Where the response sits:** one line per missed area (pathway map, change control plan, submission plan, quality pack, data science pack).
5. **Record:** "Review draft. Rules and owners are yours. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary
Which areas are on track, which are not, and the one decision the review asks for.

|---|---|---|---|---|

### Results

|---|---|---|---|---|

### Stall points
| Area | First missed step | What it means |
|---|---|---|

### Hypotheses
| Area | Hypothesis | What would confirm it | Row |
|---|---|---|---|
Labeled as hypotheses; none is a finding.

### Response routing
| Area | Stall point | Responding work | Owner you named |
|---|---|---|---|

### Gaps
Rules that could not be measured, and why.

### Record
Review draft. Filing, regulator-facing wording and reportability stop for the accountable owner. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared. AI-assisted draft.
