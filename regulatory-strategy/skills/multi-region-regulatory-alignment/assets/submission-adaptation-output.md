# Output format: regional submission adaptation

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

Short mode: 450 words maximum. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose (keep them in the table), secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question, full-mode sections. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Do not send a short-mode answer over 450 words. Offer the full version in one line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Ranking:** the sequences in order with value estimates, and "scores and weights are yours".
2. **Close call:** the weight change that would swap the top two.
3. **Sequence:** step by step, with what each step unlocks (in-force routes only) and the shared prerequisite.
4. **Top adaptations:** at most five, each with its region and row.
5. **Decisions for the owner:** the owner or role the person named per region, in their words, or "owner not named" with a question. Never a suggested job title.
6. **Record:** "Decision draft, uncleared" or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary
Two or three sentences: the leading sequence, why, and how robust.

### Dependency graph
| Held | Unlocks (region) | Legal status | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
Draft routes listed separately: "would shorten the plan if adopted; not available today".

### Options and facts
| Sequence | Criterion 1 fact | Criterion 2 fact | ... |
|---|---|---|---|

### Anchors, weights and scores (the person's)
| Criterion | 0 means | 4 means | Weight | Scores per sequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Ranking and robustness
Value estimate and contributions from `analytics.mcda`; swap points worked by hand.

### Adaptation per region
| Region | Classification argument | Dossier format | Evidence conditions | Certificates | Registration | Owner | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Stops
What waits on the regulatory owner, per region.

### Record
Decision draft. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared. AI-assisted.
