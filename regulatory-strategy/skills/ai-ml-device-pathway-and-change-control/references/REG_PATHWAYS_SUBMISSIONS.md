# Regulatory pathways and submissions for software as a medical device: sourced reference

Research pass of 19 October 2026. Primary sources only: statutes and regulations (eCFR, govinfo Federal Register, Publications Office of the EU, legislation.gov.uk), regulators and their guidance (fda.gov, health.ec.europa.eu including MDCG documents, gov.uk/MHRA, swissmedic.ch). Rows marked [WF] would have been read through a fetch tool that returns a model-written reading of the page rather than raw text; no row in this pass carries [WF], because the one site that needed it (imdrf.org) refused both curl and the fetch tool. All quotes are copied from raw downloads saved under `research_reg1/raw/`. Not owner-reviewed.

Evidence class for every row: P (published primary source), read on 19 October 2026. Where a figure comes from a contractor study commissioned and published by the European Commission, the row says so.

## Method

- Downloaded with curl through the configured proxy, TLS verification on. PDFs converted with `pdftotext -layout` (Federal Register fee tables also with `pdftotext -raw`, saved as `*_raw.txt`); HTML converted with a short Python script (`bin/h2t.py`). Quotes copied from those text files.
- EUR-Lex returned an empty bot-challenge response, so EU legal texts were taken from the Publications Office cellar (`publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/...`), which serves the same consolidated and Official Journal texts. A SPARQL query to the Publications Office listed all consolidated versions of the MDR; the newest, 02017R0745-20260719 (consolidated 19 July 2026), is the main MDR source.
- imdrf.org dropped every connection from the proxy, and the fetch tool reported that imdrf.org disallows robots. IMDRF content is therefore only cited where a regulator restates it (FDA, MDCG). See "Not sourced".
- Quote check: every quote below was checked by script (`bin/check_quotes.py`) against the raw text files, with whitespace, ligatures and quote marks normalized. Quotes containing " … " are checked fragment by fragment. Result is reported at the end of this file.
- Raw files: `ecfr_807`, `ecfr_860`, `ecfr_814`, `fda_mma_policy`, `fda_cds`, `fda_premarket_sw`, `fda_qsub`, `fda_bdp_page`, `fda_bdp_guid`, `fda_estar_page`, `fda_estar510_guid`, `fda_estardn_guid`, `fda_mdufa_page`, `mdufa_reports`, `mdufa5_commitment`, `mdufa_perf_2026aug`, `fr_fy2026_fees(_raw)`, `fr_fy2027_fees(_raw)`, `fda_databases`, `fda_510k_db`, `fda_class_db`, `openfda_510k`, `fda_samd_page`, `02017R0745-20260719`, `32023R0607`, `32024R1860`, `mdr_oj`, `mdcg_index`, `mdcg_2019_11r1`, `mdcg_2020_1`, `mdcg_2020_13`, `eudamed_overview`, `nb_topic`, `nb_survey`, `uk_regulating`, `uk_ce_timelines`, `uk_indef_consult`, `uk_samd_ai`, `uk_apps_pub`, `uk_sw_apps_guid`, `uk_si_2024_1368`, `uk_pms_news`, `uk_ai_govresp`, `ch_legal`, `ch_faq`, `ch_transition`, `ch_sw_infosheet`.

## Changes and conflicts

- FDA CDS guidance: the task names the 2022 final guidance. FDA has replaced it twice: on 6 January 2026 and again on 29 January 2026 (US-19). The current text adds an enforcement-discretion policy for software that gives one clinically appropriate recommendation (US-23). Any skill content built on the 2022 text should be rechecked against the 2026 version.
- MDUFA De Novo goal: the commitment letter sets 70% within 150 FDA days, with conditional step-ups. The August 2026 performance report shows the goal as 80% for FY 2026 and 90% for FY 2027 (US-60), implying the step-up conditions were met. The FY 2026 cohort is still open (only 3 decisions as of 30 June 2026), so its 100% figure is not meaningful yet.
- Two review clocks: regulations set 120 days for De Novo (US-11) and 180 days for PMA (US-14); MDUFA performance goals use "FDA days" (excluding time with the sponsor) and a different De Novo target of 150 FDA days. Total calendar time is much longer (about 141 days for 510(k), about 250 to 280 days for De Novo; US-58, US-61).
- User fees: the task asks for FY 2026. FY 2026 ended on 30 September 2026; FY 2027 rates (US-55) now apply. FY 2026 figures are kept for reference.
- MDR text: the latest consolidation is 19 July 2026 (EU-26). Swissmedic's legal framework page still links the 9 July 2024 consolidation (stale; not a substantive conflict for Rule 11, which is unchanged).
- MDCG 2019-11 was revised in June 2025 (rev.1). Earlier copies (October 2019) are superseded.
- MDR transition (EU-21 to EU-24): dates as amended by Regulation (EU) 2023/607. The conditions (application by 26 May 2024, written agreement by 26 September 2024) have passed; a legacy software product that missed them cannot use the extension.
- EU vs GB software classification: the EU applies Rule 11 (most decision software at least class IIa). GB still applies MDD-derived rules under the UK MDR 2002, where much stand-alone software is class I (UK-14). A product can be class I in GB and class IIa or higher in the EU and Switzerland.
- UK CE recognition: 30 June 2028 and 30 June 2030 are the dates in force. MHRA has consulted on indefinite recognition; the outcome is not published. Treat the dates as current law, and indefinite recognition as a proposal.
- NB survey (EU-40 to EU-44): a study by contractors (Austrian National Public Health Institute, Areté, Civic Consulting) commissioned by the Commission and published on its site. The study notes its dashboard may differ from the slides.
- IMDRF: MDCG 2019-11 rev.1 cites a 2025 IMDRF document (N81) on characterizing medical device software. Whether it supersedes N12 could not be checked because imdrf.org could not be read.
