# Output format: performance review

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column. Cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections. Offer the full version in one line. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

Short mode uses only the numbered items below. Rule tables, agency context and full-mode sections are not added in short mode.

1. **Answer:** verdict per submission (on track, at risk, missed, or "no rules set") with the number behind it.
2. **Stall point and owner:** one line per miss, owner as named or "owner not named".
3. **Hypotheses:** at most two per stall point, labeled "hypothesis", each with its test.
4. **Route:** one line per stall point naming the responding sibling task.
5. **Decision needed:** the one decision for the regulatory owner. Agency figures go in full mode only.
6. **Record:** "Uncleared draft" or the clearer's name.
7. One line offering the full version.

## Full mode

### Summary
Verdicts, stall points, decisions needed.

### Rules (the person's words)
| Submission | Rule | Number | Owner | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Results
| Submission | Rule | Result | Pass, miss or not measurable | Basis and row |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Stall points
First stage where a rule missed, per submission.

### Hypotheses to test
| Stall point | Hypothesis (labeled) | What would confirm it | Row |
|---|---|---|---|

### Routing
| Stall point | Responding work | Owner (person's) |
|---|---|---|

### Context (not targets)
Agency figures used, with basis and cohort year.

### Record
Advisory draft. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared. AI-assisted.
