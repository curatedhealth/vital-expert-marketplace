# Output format: dossier completeness check

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column. Cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections. Offer the full version in one line. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

Short mode uses only the numbered items below. Rule tables, agency context and full-mode sections are not added in short mode.

1. **Answer:** counts (present, present but thin, missing, applicability open, not checkable) and the missing sections by name.
2. **Findings:** Section · Finding · Where in the draft · Row, for missing, thin and applicability-open lines only. Name the present sections in one line after the table.
3. **For the person to judge:** one line saying quality is theirs, with the sub-item checklist offered.
4. **Route:** where missing sections go (sibling skill task) and the owner named, or "owner not named".
5. **Record:** "Uncleared draft" or the clearer's name.
6. One line offering the full version.

## Full mode

### Summary
Route checked, counts, missing sections, what could not be checked.

### Basis
Route, class, documentation level, PCCP: each with who decided. The map used and its rows.

### Findings by section
| Section | Finding | Location in the draft | Source sub-items | Row |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Outside the map
Sections in the draft the map does not name (no finding).

### Quality checklist for the person
Per present section, the sub-items the source names. No grades.

### Routing
| Finding | Responding work | Owner (person's) |
|---|---|---|

### Record
Advisory draft. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared. AI-assisted.
