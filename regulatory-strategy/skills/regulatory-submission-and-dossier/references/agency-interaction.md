# Agency interaction: the sourced clocks and channels

Row prefixes as in `content-maps.md`. Read 19 October 2026. Every clock here is a fact about the agency's process. None is a promise about a product's outcome.

## FDA: before the submission

| Channel | What it is for | Clock | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-Submission (Q-Sub) | Feedback on specific questions to guide development or the application | Acceptance or technical screening within 15 days; written feedback within 70 days, or at least 5 days before a meeting (meetings typically day 70 to 75) | RP US-30 to RP US-33 |
| Pre-Sub goal and actual | MDUFA V goal: 90% within goal, up to 4,300 Pre-Subs a year | Actual: 98.84%, 98.66%, 97.49% within goal (FY 2023 to FY 2025); average 61.89 FDA days to written feedback in FY 2025 | RP US-35, S-47 |
| Meeting minutes | The applicant drafts minutes and sends them within 15 days of the meeting | 15 calendar days; FY 2025: 380 of 2,338 meetings had minutes past 15 days | S-30, S-37, S-48 |
| Study risk determination; informational meeting | Formal letter; meeting | 90 days each | RP US-34 |
| Breakthrough designation request | Sent as a Q-Sub; device must meet the statutory criteria | Decision within 60 calendar days | RP US-40, RP US-42 |
| 513(g) request | Classification information | Fee USD 7,820 (FY 2026 rate; FY 2027 rates now apply and the 513(g) FY 2027 rate is not in the rows read: `[GAP]`) | RP US-53, RP US-55 |

Breakthrough designations: 1,320 granted as of 30 June 2026 (RP US-43). This is context for the person, not a measure of their chance.

## FDA: during review

| Event | 510(k) | De Novo | PMA | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acceptance or screening | Accepted or not within 15 calendar days; eSTAR technical screening anticipated within 15 days | eSTAR technical screening (De Novo clock `[GAP]` in the rows read) | Acceptance for filing review within 15, filing status within 45 calendar days | S-15, S-23, S-77 |
| First substantive interaction | Within 60 calendar days for 95% | `[GAP]` (no De Novo SI goal in the rows read) | Within 90 calendar days of filing for 95% | S-24, S-31 |
| Decision goal (FDA days) | 90 FDA days for 95% | 150 FDA days, 70% rising to 80% (FY 2026) and 90% (FY 2027) | 180 FDA days for 90% (320 with an advisory committee) | RP US-46 to RP US-48, RP US-60 |
| Escalation | Written feedback on all issues if no decision by 100 FDA days | Discussion on request after 180 FDA days | Feedback if no decision 20 days past goal | S-27, S-32 |
| Regulatory clock (separate from MDUFA) | Submit at least 90 days before commercial distribution; no marketing until the SE order (RP US-07) | 120 days after acceptance | 180 days after filing, extendable up to 180 days by a major amendment | RP US-04, RP US-11, RP US-14, RP US-15 |

- FDA days count only time under review at FDA; an Additional Information or Major Deficiency letter stops the clock (S-25, S-26). Total calendar time is much longer than FDA days (RP US-58, RP US-61).
- Answering a deficiency: a Submission Issue Request (SIR, S-33) gets feedback on the planned approach in 21 days if sent within 60 days of the letter, 70 days if later, as resources permit (S-34). The formal response is still due on the letter's timeline (S-35). A SIR is not for NSE, withdrawal or deletion letters (S-36).
- Response deadlines: a De Novo is withdrawn if an additional-information request is not fully answered within 180 days (RP US-13). A 510(k) that fails eSTAR technical screening is withdrawn if no replacement comes within 180 days (S-16). The deadline for answering a 510(k) Additional Information letter is `[GAP]` in the files read.

## FDA: fees (the person's budget, not a recommendation)

FY 2027 rates apply from 1 October 2026: 510(k) USD 28,653 (small business USD 7,163); De Novo USD 191,020 (USD 47,755); PMA USD 636,732 (USD 159,183); establishment registration USD 13,785 (RP US-55). FY 2026 rates are kept for reference only (RP US-49 to RP US-54).

## EU: notified body

- The manufacturer chooses any notified body designated for the device type and may not apply to two in parallel for the same procedure (RP EU-12). The official list is in the Single Market Compliance Space (RP EU-45).
- Context from the 20th notified body survey, which is a contractor study commissioned by the Commission (data to 28 February 2026, published 2 July 2026; RP EU-40). Label it so whenever it is used:
  - 65% of applications reach a signed written agreement within 2 months (S-71).
  - From written agreement to a new MDR certificate: 62% of notified bodies report 13 to 18 months for a QMS certificate; for QMS plus product, 51% report 13 to 18 months and 31% report 19 to 24 months (RP EU-44, basis S-74).
  - Main refusal reasons: outside the body's designation scope (30%) and incomplete applications (25%); 989 MDR applications refused since designation (S-72, RP EU-42).
  - Only 14 of 52 notified bodies report that more than half of submissions are complete (S-73).
  - No software-specific breakdown exists in the survey sections read (RP "Not sourced").
- Expert panel consultation on the clinical development strategy is available for class III and certain class IIb devices (RP EU-19).
- EUDAMED: actor registration, UDI/device registration, notified bodies and certificates, and market surveillance modules are mandatory since 28 May 2026 (RP EU-36).

## UK and Switzerland

MHRA registration is required in Great Britain (RP UK-01); MHRA interaction channels (pre-submission meetings and their clocks) are `[GAP]` in the files read. Switzerland: `[GAP]` beyond RP CH-01 to RP CH-08.

## How to use these clocks

- Put the clocks in the plan as facts with their basis (FDA days or calendar days; goal or actual; which fiscal year cohort).
- The FY 2026 MDUFA cohort is still open; its figures are not comparable yet (RP "Changes and conflicts").
- Never turn a goal or an average into a promised date for the person's product.
