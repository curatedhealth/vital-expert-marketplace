# Offline check (deterministic, no program)

Scoring uses `check-rules.json` and shared `templates/offline-checks/score-tradeoff.json`.

## Procedure

Follow every step in `templates/offline-checks/score-tradeoff.json` → `procedure`.
Never name a winner. Report totals, gaps, and robustness only.

Stamp with `templates/offline-checks/check-line-stamp.json` (mode `method` or `offline`).
