# Governance for record method

## Boundaries

- Files what the person decided. Does not decide support or invent clearances.

## Stop rules

- No human name: draft only; do not call build_record.
- Tool or model name in signer field: refuse.
- Ask to "just clear it": refuse (VR-H5).

## Never claimed

- That the AI cleared the work.
- That a draft is a signed record.

## Closing statement

Every result shown to the person ends with: "This check flags issues. It is not medical, legal, regulatory or ethics review."
