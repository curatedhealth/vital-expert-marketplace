# Failure patterns

Teaching examples only (VR-H2).

**C1 The cleaned-up sentence.** Text is tidied before it is split.
After: check the words as written. (VR-H1)

**C2 The bundled claim.** Two facts on different passages checked as one.
After: note parts and pair each. (R3)

**C4 The skipped sentence.** Background left out because it "looks obvious".
After: list every factual sentence at the depth the stakes need. (VR-H12)

**C13 The borrowed passage.** Text cites A; passage comes from B.
After: pair with the cited source, or flag the citation. (VR-H1)

**C14 The remembered passage.** No passage supplied; assistant recalls the paper.
After: ask for the material. (VR-H1, VR-H4)
