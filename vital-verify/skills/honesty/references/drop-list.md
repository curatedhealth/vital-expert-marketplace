# Honesty reference

This skill is a writing constraint on top of support-decision. The inspiration is the rule that a specific claim without a source is worse than no claim. Verify already has the decision. Honesty says what the writer deletes.

## What stays a Verify object

| Anti-pattern | Verify object |
|---|---|
| A figure with no source | No `supported` or `partial` finding whose passage states the figure. Omit it. |
| A fabricated quotation or testimonial | No passage contains the quotation. Omit the person and the quote. |
| A compliance or performance boast | Status `unsupported` or `unverifiable`. Cut the sentence. |
| "Experts say" | Not a source. Source grading needs a publisher. |
| A plausible filler date or founder | `unverifiable`. The sentence stays out. |

## What this skill does not import

It does not ban punctuation, gradients, or layout. It does not replace the Verify card with a pass/fail design checklist. The card remains: counts by status, the open items, and one recommendation.

## Scores

Honesty does not change the faithfulness formula. A dropped sentence is not left in the answer and then marked supported.
