# Failure patterns

**H1 Softened guess.** Unsupported 62% becomes "around sixty percent".
After: remove; do not soften. (R2)

**H2 Fake citation.** Deleted sentence gets a new footnote invented by the assistant.
After: no citation for what left. (VR-H1)

**H3 Experts say kept.** Phrase survives because it sounds careful.
After: delete unless a named source and passage exist. (R3)

**H4 Recheck to bless.** Call check_claim on a passage the assistant wrote.
After: refuse; only supplied passages. (VR-H1, VR-H4)
