# Currency check examples

## Literature

A DOI resolves and the literature composite reports a retraction. The claim is "the trial found a benefit," not "the paper was retracted."

Tier becomes `inadmissible`. The finding is `unverifiable` unless another admissible source is supplied. The retraction notice itself may be cited if the claim is that the paper was retracted.

## URL only

`qualify_source` graded an FDA press URL silver. `check_currency` on that URL alone returns notices `["not checked"]`.

Print the silver grade. Print the source score with the currency axis omitted. Do not write "currency: clear."
