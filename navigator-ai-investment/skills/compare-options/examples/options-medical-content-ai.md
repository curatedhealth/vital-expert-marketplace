## The full report

**Summary.** Neither AI option scores above doing nothing on the equal-weight composite: doing nothing is cheap, fast and reversible and delivers almost none of the outcomes. The copilot sits just above doing it internally (+0.29); the medical information assistant sits below both, paying most in risk and governance burden for the highest outcome fit (8). Result: Investigate first, the copilot, chosen by the CMO.

## Why this is good

Doing nothing was scored, not assumed, and its lead on the composite was explained rather than hidden or re-weighted away. Each option's trade-off is one line. The owner chose the result, in her words; the assistant did not.
