# A Build the shortlist

Framework task: 3.2 Option Comparison, Part A.

## What this task delivers

Two to five options plus the two reference lines (doing nothing, doing it internally), each worded as a way of acting, each linked to the top outcomes it serves and the capability gaps it closes or needs closed first, with its proposed level of AI involvement marked open.

Why it matters: a comparison is only as good as its alternatives. Without doing nothing and doing it internally on the list, every AI option looks better than it is (method.md I2, I7).

## Workflow

### Step 1. Start with the two reference lines

Put "Do nothing" and "Do it internally" on the list and say so; they are the baselines. Then ask: "Besides doing nothing and doing it internally, which options are on the table?" Record each in the person's words (O). Options carried from *Outcome Discovery* (restated solutions) come in here; say which.

### Step 2. Offer library candidates (connector)

`opportunity_recommend` with stage `options` and words from the top outcomes as the hint. Offer up to three rows by display name as "candidates the library holds"; the person adds or ignores them. Never add a vendor or product yourself.

### Step 3. Word each option as a way of acting

"[What changes] for [which outcome], [how much AI involvement, if known]": for example "AI drafts routine label updates; writers review and approve". A named vendor stays in the person's words and class O.

### Step 4. Link outcomes and gaps

Two turns: "Which top outcomes does each option serve?", then "Which capability gaps does each option need closed first?" An option that serves no top outcome is flagged. An option that depends on a gap nobody closes is flagged: it may belong in "Build the basis first" at placement.

### Step 5. Mark the AI involvement level as open

If the person proposes a level (assist, augment, automate, orchestrate, redesign), record it as "proposed by [name]"; the process owner sets it. Where an outcome is very high in compliance sensitivity, note that assist is the most allowed; high allows augment at most.

## Boundaries

- Two to five options besides the baselines [H, skill practice]; more goes to a first cut by the owner.
- No vendors, prices or benchmarks from you.

## When you are unsure

If the person offers only one option, the comparison is still real: that option against doing nothing and doing it internally.
