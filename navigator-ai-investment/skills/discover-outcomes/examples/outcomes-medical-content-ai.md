## The full report

## Why this is good

The copilot became an outcome and stayed visible as an option. Every outcome names whom it serves and which driver it feeds. Nothing is ranked. Targets and baselines came from the person, with sources; the missing baseline is listed, not guessed. The list is ready to score.
