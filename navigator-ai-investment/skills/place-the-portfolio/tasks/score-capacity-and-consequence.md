# A Score capacity and consequence

Framework task: 3.3 Portfolio Placement, Part A.

## What this task delivers

The oversight the organization can carry today, scored privately by named people (1 to 10) and averaged; and, for each option, whether an error would be too costly to hand to AI at all (yes or no, the owner's answer, read from the context-of-use statement where one exists).

Why it matters: the same option is "invest now" for an organization with review capacity to spare and "build the basis first" for one whose reviewers are already stretched. Capacity is the organization's, so it must be scored by its people, not assumed (method.md I8; owner ruling R2).

## Workflow

### Step 1. Score capacity privately

One question with the sheet: "Who scores capacity?" The sheet asks "How much oversight can the organization carry today?"; scores come back privately, never typed into the shared chat. Each scorer gives one whole number: 1 = very little (reviewers and governance already stretched), 10 = a great deal (spare review and governance capacity for new AI work). Same scorers as the option comparison where possible. Revealed together; average computed; a spread of 3 or more flagged with both ends.

Capacity is about the people and governance that would oversee AI work in this decision's area, today, not after hiring.

### Step 2. Ask about consequence

One question to the owner: "For which options would an error be too costly to hand to AI at all?" Where a context-of-use statement exists, read its consequence, patient risk and regulatory impact and say what it shows; the owner still answers. Record yes or no per option, in the owner's words. An option marked yes becomes "Support, do not automate": AI assists a person who decides.

## Boundaries

- Never score capacity or decide consequence.
- Capacity is not a maturity level and not the governance burden of any one option.

## When you are unsure

If people want to score capacity separately for regulated and non-regulated work, allow it: run placement once per area and say so.
