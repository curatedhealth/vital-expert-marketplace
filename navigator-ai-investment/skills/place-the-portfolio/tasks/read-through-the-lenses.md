# C Read through the lenses

Framework task: 3.3 Portfolio Placement, Part C.

## What this task delivers

The same options grouped four other ways, from tags and scores people gave: cost against benefit (cost and outcome fit scores), capability against capacity (augmentation or automation play), productivity against differentiation, internal against external.

Why it matters: a portfolio heavy in automation plays with nothing that differentiates, or all external with no internal build, is a pattern the owner should see. Lenses show patterns; they never change a placement or the ranking (method.md I9).

## Workflow

1. **Ask for the tags** in one question (session question 5): augmentation (AI extends what people can do: a capability play) or automation (AI takes over work: a capacity play); productivity or differentiation; internal or external. Tags already held in the organization's records are used if the person supplies them [the owner's database holds play tags, not yet read by a lane].
2. **Show each lens** as a short grouping, with the bucket beside each option so the reader sees both.
3. **Name at most two patterns**, in plain words ("both high-value options are external automation plays; nothing builds internal capability"). Patterns are observations, not recommendations.

## Boundaries

- Never infer a tag; untagged shows as "not tagged".
- A lens never reorders or re-buckets.

## When you are unsure

If the person does not want to tag, skip the lenses and say the portfolio view is placement only.
