# Audience calibration and the value themes

## Who is in the room sets the level

Owner ruling, 19 October 2026 (method.md M10). Say which level you chose and why; the person may change it.

| Audience | Language to use | Value driver level offered | Catalogue level |
|---|---|---|---|
| CEO, C-suite | Enterprise goals, portfolio, risk to the organization, investors and society | Value themes | 1 |
| Head of function, VP | Function goals, budget, cross-function trade-offs | Value levers | 2 [CHECK with owner] |
| Head of department, director | Department goals, delivery, team capacity | Value levers and drivers | 2 to 3 |

Mixed room: start at the most senior person's level. Others drill down from there.

Within Direction, stop at level 3. Finer value (sub-drivers, metrics) belongs to outcomes and their measures.

## Value dimensions

Every value theme and lever has one primary value dimension (FW-VD §6). Say it in words:

| Code | Say | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| $V | Financial | revenue, cost, margin, return on investment |
| OV | Operational | efficiency, speed, throughput |
| CV | Clinical or scientific | evidence quality, patient outcomes |
| RV | Risk | exposure, compliance, safety |
| KV | Capability | skills, knowledge, organizational capacity |
| SV | Strategic | market position, competitive advantage |

Level 3 drivers carry no dimension of their own in the catalogue. Show the dimension of their parent lever and say "inherited from [lever]".
