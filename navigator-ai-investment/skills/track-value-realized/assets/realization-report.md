# Output format: the full report (Value realization) and the direct answer

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

150 to 300 words, hard cap 300: whether the outcomes were achieved, whether the value counts, and where it falls against the range, first; at most three gaps; one record line; one offer as a statement.

## Full report

Hard cap 1,000 words. Asks nothing.

### Summary

Three lines: outcomes achieved or not; realized value counted or not, and its place against the range; what else changed.

### 1. The decision and the declared range

Decision, owner, range (low, likely, high, unit, basis), declared on DATE.

### 2. Outcomes

| Outcome | Target (set before) | Measured result | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### 3. Realized value

Measured figure (source, date, unit, period); counted or not counted (unmet realizes zero); place against the range: below, inside or above. "Placement describes; the owner decides what it means."

### 4. What else changed

In the person's words, with who stated it.

### 5. Gaps and record

Every gap, most blocking first. Draft prepared with an AI assistant on DATE; cleared by NAME or "uncleared draft: name the clearer"; placed on a Navigator brief: yes or no.
