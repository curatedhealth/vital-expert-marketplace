## The full report

**Summary.** The largest outcome-weighted gap is answering medical information questions at speed (58.5), held back for the CMO's top outcome. Content data and tagging (47.8) and medical content review at volume (45.5) follow closely; the room split on both. Data readiness and change capacity have no instrument yet; risk appetite is stated as low for unreviewed clinician-facing content.

**1. The decision and the outcomes.** The CMO decides whether, and where, AI should change how medical content is produced. Attested outcomes: faster accurate answers (13.0), faster integration of new data (11.3), writer time on label updates (9.5, field test pending).

## Why this is good

Teams and tools were restated as capabilities. The ranking follows what the outcomes need, not the biggest raw gap. The owner's different view stands beside the group's. The unmeasurable factors are named, and the one the owner stated is recorded as hers.
