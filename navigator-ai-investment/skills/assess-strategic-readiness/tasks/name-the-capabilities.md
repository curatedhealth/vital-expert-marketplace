# A Name the capabilities

Framework task: 3.1 Strategic Readiness, Part A.

## What this task delivers

A short list of capabilities, each worded as something the organization must be able to do, each linked to the top outcomes it serves. It is the list people will score.

Why it matters: capability comes before performer. If the list starts from teams, systems or AI tools, the room scores what it already has and the gap the outcomes need goes unseen (method.md I1, I4).

## Workflow

### Step 1. Ask what the outcomes depend on

Ask: "Which capabilities do these outcomes depend on?" Record each in the person's words (O), with the outcomes it serves.

Restate as a capability when the answer names:

- **a team or role** ("the MLR team"): "review medical content for compliance at the volume needed";
- **a tool or AI** ("an AI writing assistant"): "draft routine scientific content from approved sources"; keep the tool as an option;
- **a project** ("the content hub project"): ask what it would let the organization do.

### Step 2. Offer the library (connector)

`catalogue_search` on the capability pillar with words from the top outcomes; `chain_trace` from mission-level outcomes for capabilities already linked. Offer up to five by display name, labeled "the library holds". The person keeps, drops or rewords. Without the connector, work from the person's words; say "library not checked" in the record line only.

### Step 3. Link each capability to the outcomes it serves

One line each: "*[capability]* serves *[outcome 1]*, *[outcome 2]*". The link weight is 1 unless the person says one outcome depends on it more. A capability that serves none of the top outcomes is flagged: keep it only if the person says why.

### Step 4. Keep it scoreable

About 4 to 10 capabilities [H, skill practice, CHECK with owner]. Merge duplicates; split a capability joined by "and".

## Boundaries

- No people, teams, vendors or agents as capabilities.
- No maturity levels; no scoring here.

## When you are unsure

If the person names only what they lack in systems, ask "what would that system let you do that you can't today?" The answer is the capability.
