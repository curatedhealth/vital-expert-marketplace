# C Read the gap register

Framework task: 3.1 Strategic Readiness, Part C.

## What this task delivers

The capability and gap register for Decision Record section 4: capabilities ranked by outcome-weighted gap, why each sits where it does, where the room disagrees, the owner's view, and the readiness factors that have no instrument yet.

Why it matters: the options compared next must close the gaps that hold back the top outcomes, or say why they need not. A register that only lists gaps, without what they hold back, cannot do that (method.md I2, I5, I6).

## Workflow

### Step 1. Show the register

| Rank | Capability | Current | Required | Gap | Weight from outcomes | Weighted gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Group averages, computed. Name the scorers; "indicative" under three.

### Step 2. Say why the top gaps lead

One line each for the top three: a large gap on capabilities that serve important outcomes; or a modest gap on the capability the most important outcome depends on; or a large gap on a capability that serves less important outcomes (lower than its raw gap suggests). Name the outcomes behind the weight.

### Step 3. Disagreement as views

Spread of 3 or more on Current or Required: name who is at each end, ask the minority holder for one line (session question 4), record it. Disagreement on Current usually means people see different parts of today.

### Step 4. The owner's view, after

The owner's ranking of the top gaps, and where it differs. Not reconciled.

### Step 5. Name what cannot be measured yet

Data readiness, change capacity, risk appetite: no instrument exists. Ask once (session question 5); record any answer as owner-stated (O), otherwise as a gap. Do not estimate.

### Step 6. Hand on

Carry the top gaps to *Option Comparison*: each option will be asked which gaps it closes or needs closed first.

## Boundaries

- No maturity levels, no "you are at level 2".
- No naming of who will close a gap.
- Never present the weighted gap as value or cost.

## When you are unsure

If every gap is small, say the room sees itself as ready and ask whether the outcomes' required levels were scored against the top outcomes or against today's work.
