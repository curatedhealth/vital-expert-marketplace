# Output format: the full report (Value at stake) and the direct answer

The session ends with the full report, delivered in one go (references/facilitation.md). During the session, use the turn template only.

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

Aim for 150 to 300 words: the answer first; the range or score reading it rests on; what is sourced and what is owner-stated; at most three gaps; one record line. End with one offer: continue the session, or write up the report now with what is known.

## Full report (Value at stake)

Written for someone who was not in the conversation. If a decision frame exists in the conversation, the report opens with it (decision, owner, goal) so that sections 1 and 2 of the Decision Record read together.

### Summary

Three lines: what the decision is worth as a range and on what basis; which drivers carry it; the main thing that would change the range.

### 1. The decision (from the frame)

One sentence, owner, goal. "Not framed" if absent, with the decision in the person's words.

### 2. Value drivers

| Driver | Level | Dimension | Chosen by |
|---|---|---|---|

Scores (if scored):

| Driver | Size of the prize | Confidence | Time to value (10 = slow) | Breadth | Composite (computed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Each cell: group view and range. Scorers named; missing scorers named. Disagreements to discuss.

### 3. Value at stake

| | Low | Likely | High | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [driver or total] | | | | |

Basis: how the figures were reached, with evidence class and source for each figure. Width read against confidence. "Estimated value at stake; not realized until the outcomes are measured as achieved."

**Cost of a wrong call:** in the owner's words, with any figure and its class.

### 4. Assumptions

| Assumption | Owner | What would break it |
|---|---|---|

### 5. Outcomes the value depends on

"*[Outcome]* contributes to *[driver]*", one line each; source: person or library. Drivers with no outcome flagged.

### 6. Gaps

Every gap, most blocking first, with what would close it.

### 7. Record

Draft prepared with an AI assistant on DATE. Cleared by NAME, or "uncleared draft". Value range stored on the Navigator brief: yes (brief reference) or no (connector unavailable).
