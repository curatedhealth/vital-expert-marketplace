# Output format: the full report (Outcome priorities) and the direct answer

The session ends with the full report, delivered in one go (references/facilitation.md). During the session, use the turn template only.

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

150 to 300 words, hard cap 300, tables included (a table or bullets, not both): what the scores say, first (the top outcomes and why they are there); one line on the scorers (how many, and whether that makes the result indicative); disagreement and what the sensitivity check tested and found, in one or two lines; the field-test rule if it applies; at most three gaps; one record line. End with one offer: continue the session, or write up the report now with what is known.

## Full report (Outcome priorities)

Written for someone who was not in the conversation. It drafts Decision Record section 3 and contributes to section 8. Hard cap 1,000 words including tables; rough budget: summary 60, decision 40, priorities table and reasons 250, does it hold 120, owner's view 60, field test 120, Gate 2 50, disagreement 150, carried forward 60, gaps and record 90.

### Summary

Three lines: the top outcomes and why (underserved, important); whether the room agreed and the ranking holds; field test or waiver, and Gate 2 status.

### 1. The decision

One sentence, owner, goal (from the frame).

### 2. Outcome priorities

| Rank | Outcome | For whom | Driver | Importance | Satisfaction today | Opportunity | Band | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Figures are group averages, computed. Source: "scored in the room" or "field-verified ([who], [how many], [when])". Scorers named; missing scorers named; "indicative" if fewer than three.

One line per top outcome on why it is there (underserved / important, fairly served / overserved).

### 3. Does it hold? (about 120 words)

Sensitivity: what was tested (each scorer left out) and what changed: the first-ranked outcome (a flip), the top set, any lower swap (reported only). Ties named. Disagreement is shown once, in section 7.

### 4. The owner's view

The owner's own ranking of the top set, shown after the group's, and where it differs. Not reconciled.

### 5. Field test

The rule read against the decision (which conditions hold, which do not). Then: the plan (outcomes, party, how many, by when, who), the results if returned, or "Proceeding without a field test: [owner's reason], [owner], [date]". Or "Not required: [why]".

### 6. Gate 2

"Priorities attested by [name, role], [date]", or "Pending attestation by [owner]: [the question to put]". Evidence: scorers, field test or waiver. Dissent entries: n.

### 7. Disagreement and dissent (one table, no repetition elsewhere)

| Outcome | Criterion | Range | Low end (who) | High end (who) | View recorded |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Room-versus-field disagreements included.

### 8. Carried to the Intelligence Core

The attested top outcomes; options carried from *Outcome Discovery*; for each top outcome, the open question of what level of AI involvement fits it, to be set by the process owner.

### 9. Gaps

Every gap, most blocking first, merged where they close the same way.

### 10. Record

Draft prepared with an AI assistant on DATE. Cleared by NAME, or "uncleared draft". Scores stored on the Navigator brief: yes (brief reference) or no (connector unavailable; computed locally with the same formula).
