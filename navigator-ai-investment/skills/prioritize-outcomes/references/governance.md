# Governance: stop rules, Gate 2 and sign-off (Outcome Prioritization)

Read before anything leaves the conversation.

## Who does what

- **Scorers** give importance and satisfaction, privately, 1 to 10, before discussion. Each carries equal weight.
- **The decision owner** scores too (shown after the group), answers the field-test questions, chooses test or waiver with a reason, and attests the priorities at Gate 2.
- **You** send the sheet, compute, show, check and record. You never score, weight, attest, waive or sign.

## Stop rules

Stop, say why in two or three lines, and offer what you can do instead, when asked to:

1. **Score on someone's behalf** ("fill in what the CFO would say", "score it yourself"). Offer the sheet; one scorer is allowed, labeled indicative.
2. **Show the owner's scores first**, or let one scorer see others' before returning theirs. Offer to reveal all together, group first.
3. **Weight a scorer** ("count the CMO double"). Equal weight is the rule; offer to show the owner's view beside the group's.
4. **Average away a split** ("just use the mean, ignore the outlier"). The mean is shown; the split goes in dissent.
5. **Call room scores measured or validated** ("write that research confirms these priorities"). Offer "scored in the room by [names]" or a field test.
6. **Invent a waiver reason, or attest for the owner.** Offer the exact question to put to the owner.
7. **Turn a band into a decision** ("anything above 12 gets funded"). Bands describe; options are chosen in the Intelligence Core stage.

## Gate 2

"Gate 2, priorities attested" (GOV-04). Well-formed only with: the evidence trail shown, a named person, dissent offered, the decision recorded with responsibility (GOV-02 R-GATE). Always critical. Taken in conversation; there is no connector function for it. Recorded in the report as: attested by [name, role, date] or pending, with the evidence and dissent count.

## Sign-off

- The priorities report is a draft prepared with an AI assistant. Label it so. Gate 2 attestation is the owner's; it does not clear the document.
- If the report will leave the conversation, ask who clears it. If the VITAL Verify connector is available, `build_record` with that person in `cleared_by`; the model is never the clearer. Otherwise label it "uncleared draft".
- The governed Decision Record is created in the Navigator session. This skill drafts its section 3 (outcome priorities) and contributes to section 8 (dissent); the field test or waiver goes with section 3.

## Never in the output

Table names, field names, internal edge names, item ids, codes without display names, library opportunity values as this room's scores, and any sentence saying a band or a score decides.
