# Output format: the decision record and the direct answer

## Direct answer (narrow questions only)

150 to 300 words, hard cap 300: ready for signature or not, and why (from the check), first; at most three gaps; one record line; one offer as a statement. If you give a count of blockers, it matches the list. Nothing you call in order may reappear as a gap. A person the question does not name (for example, which person is the owner) is a gap, never assumed. Blockers are the check's gaps; its annex gaps are notes, not blockers.

## The decision record (full report)

The client's record of one decision, in eight sections and an annex. Hard cap 1,000 words for the body; the annex may list sources. Asks nothing except a pending owner choice. Each fact keeps its evidence class (M, O, H, V). Never show keys or section codes beyond the section titles below. In body text, refer to another section by its title, never by its number. The record line reports what the check found; never call an item in order when the annex lists it as a gap.

### Status

"Prepared for signature by [owner] and [second signer] in the Navigator app" or "Blocked: [reasons]". Never "signed".

### 1. Decision

The decision, owner, goal, size (from the Decision Contract).

### 2. Value at stake

Range (low, likely, high, unit, basis) and the cost of a wrong decision. "Estimated; not realized until measured."

### 3. Outcome priorities

Top outcomes with opportunity scores; where the scores came from (room or field); Gate 2 attested by [name, date].

### 4. Capability and gap register

Top gaps with the outcomes they hold back.

### 5. Options considered

The options and both reference lines (doing nothing, doing it internally), with the trade-off per option.

### 6. Recommendation and route

"Result: [Proceed / Investigate first / Handle it internally], for [option], chosen by [owner], [date]. Reason: [owner's words]." Route: next steps, placement, delivery plan and trial, each with its lead.

### 7. Assumptions and risks

From every report, each with an owner.

### 8. Dissent

Each dissent with its holder's name, or "No dissent, attested by [name]". Scores released by [name] for each stage.

### Annex

Sources of each section (which report, date); gaps, most blocking first; prepared with an AI assistant on DATE; cleared by NAME or "uncleared draft: name the clearer".
