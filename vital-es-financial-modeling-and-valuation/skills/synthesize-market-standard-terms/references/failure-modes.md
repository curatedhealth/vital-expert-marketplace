# Failure modes for the register

The detection questions are written for this skill (ENGINEERING RULE in grounding-register.md; no published detection checklist exists). The documented column says what the cited analysis is about, so that a reader can judge the fit.

| Id | How it enters | Detection question | Documented for | Passage ids | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FV-02 | Peak sales or revenue above the analog range; forecast optimism | Is peak revenue within the analog range, and is the context that published forecasts have run high stated without a correction being applied? | sales forecasts in reimbursement dossiers (Austria) and consensus forecasts | VP-H-001, VP-H-002, VP-H-010 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| FV-06 | Comparables selected on outcome or not comparable | Does every comparable have a stated basis for comparability on the team's criteria, and does the list include terminated or failed deals as well as completed ones? | market approach: comparability duty and scarcity of comparable deals | VP-V-020, VP-V-021, VP-V-035 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |
| FV-07 | A fairness or solvency view implied | Does any sentence say a price is fair, a target under- or overvalued, or a company solvent? | fairness opinions are an advisor's regulated act with required disclosures | VP-V-040, VP-V-041, VP-V-050, VP-V-051 | VERIFIED (re-opened) |

No mode is added without a published analysis behind it. A mode marked [CHECK] stays a detection question only.
