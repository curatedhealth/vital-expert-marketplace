# Governance

## Human gates (present options, sources and consequences; name who decides; stop)
| Gate | What is presented | Deciding role |
|---|---|---|
| the discount-rate range and the rate, if any, the team uses | the range, the method and evidence documented, the effect of each rate on the result | deal lead, with the valuation reviewer (named) |
| the cost structure per scenario | the lines, their sources or owners and the effect on the present value | finance lead (named) with the deal lead |
| the terminal assumption | the annual change, its source and the share of value it carries | deal lead, with the valuation reviewer |
| which scenarios enter the register | the revenue register's low, mid and high and any named scenario | deal lead |
| the adopted valuation | the ranges, sensitivity and open items of this register | deal lead (BD head or investment lead) |

## Review
A named reviewer with valuation or corporate-finance experience signs the register. It is UNSIGNED until a person with a name and a date signs it.

## Flags
- [CHECK]: a rule, default or threshold not traced to a dated primary text opened in the build
- [GAP]: a fact the inputs do not give
- UNSIGNED: no named reviewer
- ADOPTED VALUATION: HUMAN ONLY (shown on every register); no fairness or solvency view is ever produced

## Conflicts of interest
An advisor's fairness analysis is a regulated act with its own disclosures (FINRA Rule 5150, VP-V-040 to 043; Item 1015, VP-V-050 to 052); this skill does not stand in for it. A seller's or vendor's valuation is a claim, not a benchmark.

## Confidentiality
Data-room material, the discount-rate worksheet and the bid view stay inside the buyer's environment. Do not paste them into public tools.
