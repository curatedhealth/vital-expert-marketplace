# Questions for the reviewers

Written 7 November 2026. Short, answerable in a sentence each. Leave blank what you cannot judge.

## All reviewers
1. Does each register give you enough to challenge it without reading the method files?
2. Where did a "GAP" or "[CHECK]" mark feel like an evasion rather than an honest limit?
3. Is anything stated as a fact that you would want a source for?

## Valuation practice (V1 to V6)
4. V1 and V2 ship no default rate, cost structure or probability. Is that useful to a deal team, or does it leave too much to be supplied?
5. Is the pairing rule (low takes the adverse end of every driver) the right default for a scenario table?
6. Are one-at-a-time swings plus switching values enough, or would you expect a simulation?
7. Does the memo structure (range, what would change the call, decisions with owners) match how a decision committee reads?

## Comparables (V7 to V9)
8. Is a minimum of three deals for a range reasonable? What would you use?
9. Is showing the model range and the benchmark range side by side, never merged, the right stance?
10. Which public sources of deal terms would you accept?

## Boundaries
11. The tool refuses any fairness, solvency, recommendation or single-value statement. Is anything legitimate being refused?
12. Is the list of human-only decisions complete?

## The summary
13. Are the proposed green / yellow / red statuses (an engineering rule from counts) helpful, or would you rather see counts only?
