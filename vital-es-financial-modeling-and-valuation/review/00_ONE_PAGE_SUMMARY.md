# One-page summary for the reviewer

Financial Modeling & Valuation plugin, nine competencies in three missions of three. Draft, 7 November 2026. Nothing in it is customer-facing, and no part has been reviewed or back-tested.

**What it is.** A buyer's valuation kit for a development-stage drug asset: nine skills, each producing one register a person can audit, plus a summary that counts and compares what the registers say. Buyers are pharma business development and life-sciences investors.

**What it will not do.** It gives no single value, no recommended price, no fairness or solvency view and no adopted valuation; the discount rate, the final probability, the scales, the weights, the choice of comparables and deal terms stay with people.

**How it stays honest.** Every method step, constant and check traces to a registered verbatim passage from a primary or official source, or is labelled as the authors' own rule with a stated gap (checklist 03). Untraced items are [CHECK], disabled and never scored. The WIPO text is a guide, not a standard.

**State of proof.** Unit tests, lint and fixtures pass. Known-answer sets are frozen and not run; the retired back-test was never run; the filing-fidelity set is frozen and not run. This review is the first practitioner input.

**What we ask of you.** Pick the files for your profile (06), read the method summary (02) and the walkthrough (01), go through your rows of the checklist (03) marking confirm, change or reject, answer the questions (05).
