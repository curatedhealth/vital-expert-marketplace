# Output format: traceability and design control check

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** two or three sentences: the scope checked (release, files given), how many items and hazards were read, and the count of findings by kind, hazard-trace findings first.
2. **Hazard trace findings:** Hazard · Broken link (quoted) · Code.
3. **Other findings:** at most five: Item or document · Finding · Criterion and row.
4. **Not checked:** one line naming files not provided.
5. **Record:** "Findings, not a score or a compliance verdict. Uncleared AI-assisted draft." or the clearer's name.

End with one offer: the full version, or the review and repair plan (B1).
