Rows marked [WF] were read through a fetch tool that returns a model-written reading of the page, not the raw text; their quotes are as returned and should be re-checked against the page before being relied on as verbatim. All other rows are quoted from raw downloads saved under `research_q/raw/`. Not owner-reviewed.

Evidence class for every row: P (published primary source), read on 19 October 2026, except rows marked M (a count or absence measured in this pass from a primary file, method stated in the row).

## Method

- Downloaded with curl through the configured proxy, TLS verification on. HTML converted to text with a short Python script (tags stripped, whitespace collapsed); PDFs with `pdftotext` (reading order, not `-layout`, because the Federal Register and the compliance program print in columns). The Federal Register rule was read from the govinfo HTML edition. Excel data was exported to CSV with openpyxl.
- EUR-Lex returned an empty 202 challenge to scripted download. The same legal texts were fetched from the EU Publications Office cellar (`publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX>`, XHTML). ## Changes and conflicts

- **QS regulation numbering vs QMSR.** The FY 2025 483 data (QS52 to QS54) cite the retired QS regulation sections (820.100, 820.198, 820.30). FY 2025 ended 30 September 2025, before the QMSR took effect on 2 February 2026. Future spreadsheets will cite QMSR and ISO 13485 clauses; trends across the boundary need a crosswalk (FDA points to AAMI TIR102:2019 on its QMSR page; not read).
- **Effective date.** Proposed rule: 1 year; final: 2 years (QS1, QS2). Before 2 February 2026 the QS regulation applied; after, the QMSR. No phase-in.
- **Rule correction.** The eCFR source note for 820.3 cites a correction at 89 FR 82945 (15 October 2024); its content was not read.
- **Management review records.** QS regulation 820.180(c) shielded management review, audit and supplier audit reports from inspection; the QMSR does not (QS15). FDA FAQ "content current as of" 10/02/2026.
- **ISO 14971 in the US vs EU.** Not incorporated into the QMSR (QS21) but FDA-recognized in full (ST5). In the EU, EN ISO 14971:2019/A11:2021 is harmonised (EU29).
- **IEC 62304 in the US vs EU.** FDA recognizes IEC 62304 edition 1.1 in full (ST9). In the EU, no 62304 entry appears in Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/1182 or its ten amendments through 11 June 2026 (EU30, measured), so conformity with EN 62304 does not give a presumption of conformity under the MDR. Edition 2.0 is forecast for 26 October 2028 (ST8), after the 2028 stability date of edition 1.1.
- **ISO/TR 24971.** Stage "to be revised" since 6 July 2026 [WF] (ST3); content may change.
- **MDR text version.** Read in the consolidation of 10 January 2025 (amendments up to Regulation (EU) 2024/1860). Article 120 dates (CH1, CH2) are from Regulation (EU) 2023/607. Any later consolidation was not checked.
- **MDCG versions.** MDCG 2023-3 is Rev.2 dated January 2025 on its cover; MDCG 2020-3 is Rev.1 dated May 2023 on its cover. The MDCG listing page shows other dates next to entries (layout ambiguity); the cover dates are used.
- **US vs EU vs GB clocks.** EU and GB: 15 days (general), 10 days (death or unanticipated serious deterioration), 2 days (serious public health threat), calendar days from awareness (VG4 to VG6, VG12, VG28). US: 30 calendar days, or 5 work days for remedial-action events (VG17, VG18). US corrections and removals: 10 working days from initiation (VG22).
- **MDSAP documents.** fda.gov copies show P0002 revision 2024-08-06, P0027 effective 2022-11-21, P0037 effective 2021-09-08; the authoritative copies have moved to the TGA-managed site (QS39), which was not reachable.
