# Output format: AI/ML pathway map

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

**Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.** Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. The calls list is not counted.

1. **Answer:** two or three sentences that answer the question asked, with the one date or caveat that matters most (for the AI Act, the date and the act that set it).
2. **One table, by jurisdiction:** Jurisdiction · Candidate routes (owner decides) · Change plan mechanism (final, draft or announced, with date) · AI-specific obligations and date · Row.
3. **Precedents** (only if step 4 ran): up to three lines, each by its record: name, manufacturer, route, decision date, source.
4. **Gaps:** at most three lines, one per class.
5. **Record:** "Sourced references read 19 October 2026 and library records retrieved DATE. Class and route are the regulatory owner's decision. Uncleared draft."
