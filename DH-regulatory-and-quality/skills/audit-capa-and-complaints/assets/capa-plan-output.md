# Output format: CAPA plan

Short mode is the default for a direct question: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column. Cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections. Offer the full version in one line.

## Short mode

Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **The plan in three lines:** correction; cause (or "not yet established" and the next step); main corrective action.
2. **Risk level and depth:** the level you gave and what your procedure requires at that level, or "level not given".
3. **Effectiveness criteria:** one line per action in your words, or `[GAP]` asking for them.
4. **Linked obligations:** reporting check (A2), change control, risk file, each with its owner.
5. **Decisions for you:** at most three (level, cause sign-off, approval).
6. **Record:** "Draft CAPA plan. Actions, criteria and owners are yours. Uncleared."

## Full mode

### Problem statement
What, where, which versions, since when, record ids.

### Risk level
Level under your procedure, risk file entry, review triggered (QS22, EU15).

### Plan
| Part | Content | Evidence or basis | Owner | Status (yours / proposed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
Correction · Cause · Corrective action · Preventive action · Linked changes.

### Effectiveness criteria
| Action | Record read | Measure | Window | Pass condition (your words) | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Depth check against your procedure
Where the plan meets, exceeds or falls short of the level's requirements.

### Linked obligations
| Obligation | Rule | Routed to | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|

### Record
Draft. Approval and closure stop for the owner. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
