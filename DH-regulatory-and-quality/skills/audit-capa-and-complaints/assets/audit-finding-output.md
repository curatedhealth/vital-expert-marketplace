# Output format: audit finding write-up

Short mode is the default for a direct question: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column. Cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections. Offer the full version in one line.

## Short mode

Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Finding:** one sentence: requirement not met, where, on what evidence.
2. **Proposed grade:** "Proposed grade N under [MDSAP scale / your procedure X]" with the arithmetic (for example "direct 3 + repeat 1 = 4"), or "No grade: your grading rule is not given."
3. **Requirement · Evidence · Gap:** one row each.
4. **Notice rule:** one line (MDSAP 5-working-day notice applies or not; internal audit: not applicable).
5. **Owner and next step:** the owner you named, or "owner not named"; next step B1 CAPA plan or your recorded decision.
6. **Record:** "Draft. Grade and owner are yours. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary
The findings in the set, their proposed grades, and any notice rule triggered.

### Findings
| ID | Requirement (source) | Evidence (record ids, dates) | Gap | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Grading
| ID | QMS impact (clause, QS46) | Repeat (R3) | No procedure + not implemented (R4) | Released nonconforming (R5) | Sum | Proposed grade (cap 5, R1) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Or the person's own rule, with the condition that decided each grade.

### Notice and report timing (MDSAP only)
Count of grade 5 and grade 4; 5-working-day notice yes or no; package due before day 45 or day 90 (QS48, QS49). Auditing organization's obligation.

### FDA inspection mapping (inspection prep only)
| ID | QMS area or other requirement (R6) | Minimum element in model 2 (QS33) |
|---|---|---|

### Gaps
Facts missing, rules not given.

### Record
Draft. Grades are proposed; the auditor or your procedure decides. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
