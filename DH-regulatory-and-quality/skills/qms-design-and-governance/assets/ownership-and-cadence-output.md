# Output format: process ownership and governance cadence (design draft)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections, then table rows that the person's facts do not touch (list them by P number in one line); count every table cell except the Rows column; offer the full version in one line. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** processes with no owner named, records with no reader, and the records FDA can now inspect (QS15).
2. **Owner, record, reader (compact):** Process · Owner you named · Record · Forum that reads it.
3. **Forums:** each forum's inputs, decisions it may take, attendees and cadence as you set them; blanks marked "not set".
4. **External clocks for your markets:** one line each, labeled facts, not targets.
5. **Open questions:** owners not named, cadence not set.
6. **Record:** "Design draft. Owners and cadence are yours. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.
7. One line offering the full version.
