# Multi-region regulatory alignment and cross-region compliance monitoring (medical device software)

Research pass of 19 October 2026. Primary sources only: statutes and regulations, regulators' own pages and guidance, IMDRF and MDSAP official documents. Every quote was downloaded with curl and checked by script against the raw text in `raw/`. No row in this file relies on WebFetch; the marker [WF] (a quote taken through the WebFetch summarizer rather than from a raw download) is therefore not used. This file has not been reviewed by the owner.

## Method

- Downloads with curl through the configured proxy into `raw/` (HTML stripped to text; PDFs converted with `pdftotext -layout`). eCFR text from the eCFR versioner API (title 21 as of 14 October 2026). EU MDR from the Publications Office Cellar (CELEX 32017R0745, as originally published) and Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 (CELEX 32024R1860). Swiss MedDO from the Fedlex filestore (consolidated version in force 1 July 2026, English translation, which Fedlex states has no legal force). Australian instruments from the Federal Register of Legislation API (latest compilations).
- Quotes are reproduced in their original language. Table rows from layout PDFs (IMDRF N84) are quoted as they read after whitespace normalization; the column assignment (Implemented, Partly implemented, Not implemented) was read from the column positions in the layout text, and the Fact column states the reading.
- Quote check: every quote in column 5 was normalized (whitespace collapsed, ligatures and curly quotes straightened) and searched for in the normalized raw files. Result is reported at the end of this file.
- "Read on" is the date this pass read the source: 19 October 2026 for every row.

## Changes and conflicts

- Health Canada MDSAP: the Medical Devices Regulations (s. 32(2)(f), 32.1) require a QMS certificate from a registrar recognized by the Minister and do not name MDSAP. The MDSAP requirement is a Health Canada recognition policy, stated on the MDSAP site (MR-05) and in the 2018–2019 transition notice (MR-06). The notice's canada.ca "page details" date (2016-04-22) is older than the 2018–2019 deadlines it contains.
- UK international recognition: the 2023 statement of policy intent excluded SaMD and AIaMD (MR-28) and named the EU as a comparable regulator country. The July 2025 government response limits reliance to TGA, Health Canada and FDA and includes "specific software" (MR-25), with EU devices handled through CE recognition (MR-23, MR-24). GOV.UK marks the 2023 statement as superseded. The scheme is still in a draft statutory instrument (MR-26, MR-27).
- UK CE acceptance end dates: 30 June 2028 (MDD/AIMDD) and 30 June 2030 (IVDD, EU MDR, EU IVDR) are current law. The February 2026 consultation proposes indefinite recognition for MDR/IVDR devices; that is not yet adopted.
- Switzerland: FDA-approved devices are not yet recognized. Federal Council decision of 30 April 2025 sets direction only; no in-force date (MR-21).
- Australia software rules: apply to new applications from 25 February 2021 and to devices already on the register from 1 November 2024, with exceptions in regulation 11.45(3) to (5) (MR-17, MR-18).
- Australia overseas regulators: UK approved bodies were added by the 2025 amendment (compilation in force 1 December 2025); the TGA guidance was updated 11 May 2026 to match (MR-07, MR-13).
- EUDAMED: Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 replaced the "all modules ready" trigger with module-by-module mandatory use. First four modules mandatory from 28 May 2026; devices already on the market need registration by 28 November 2026 per the Commission timeline (JX-36 to JX-41). The EUDAMED Q&A states it is not formally endorsed by the Commission.
- EU MDR Annex XIV quote is from the MDR as originally published (CELEX 32017R0745). Later amendments (2023/607, 2024/1860) were not found to change Annex XIV Part A section 3 in this pass, but the consolidated text was not compared line by line.
- MDSAP administration moved: FDA states MDSAP documents moved to a TGA-managed site from 18 December 2025 (MR-34).
- IMDRF N84 is self-reported by members as of 1 September 2025. It notes regulators may need at least a year to implement a document, so later IMDRF documents (for example N81 of 2025) show lower implementation.
