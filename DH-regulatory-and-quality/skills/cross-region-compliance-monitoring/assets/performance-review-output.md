# Output format: performance review

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

Short mode: 450 words maximum. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose (keep them in the table), secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question, full-mode sections. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Do not send a short-mode answer over 450 words. Offer the full version in one line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Verdict per region:** on track / at risk / missed / no rules set, with the fact behind it.
2. **Stall point:** for each region not on track.
3. **Owner:** the owner or role the person named, in their words, or "owner not named" with a question. Never a suggested job title.

Do not restate the rules in prose and again in the table; give each rule once, in one line.
4. **Decision needed:** one line per region, and which planning task it reopens.
5. **Record:** "Uncleared draft" or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary
Which regions hold, which drift, and the one decision that matters most.

### Rules (the person's)
| Region | Rule in the person's words | Measured by | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|

### Check
| Region | Rule | Fact | Pass / miss / not measurable | Row or source |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Stall points and hypotheses
| Region | Stall point | Hypothesis (labeled) | What would confirm it |
|---|---|---|---|

### Routing
| Region | Responding work | Owner |
|---|---|---|

### Stops
What waits on the regulatory owner.

### Record
Review draft. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared. AI-assisted.
