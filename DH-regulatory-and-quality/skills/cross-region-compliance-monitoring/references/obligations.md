## How to state an obligation

"Health Canada: annual license statement due before 1 November 2026 (JX-33), 13 days from 19 October 2026; if not filed the Minister may cancel the license (JX-34). Confirm with the owner whether it has been filed." Never "you are late", never "urgent".
