# Output format: dossier assembly and agency plan

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column. Cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections. Offer the full version in one line. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Answer:** counts by status (ready, draft, missing, not in the inventory), sections with no owner, and the one blocking item.
2. **Open sections:** Section · Status (person's word) · Owner or "owner not named" · Row. Sections not in the inventory: a count and at most five names.
3. **Agency plan:** at most four lines, each a channel with its sourced clock and basis; the decision line gives the goal and the average total days (RP US-58 or RP US-61).
4. **Decisions for the regulatory owner:** the open decisions, as a list.
5. **Record:** "Draft for the regulatory owner. Uncleared" or the clearer's name.
6. One line offering the full version.

## Full mode
