# Output format: complaint and recurrence trends

Short mode is the default for a direct question: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column. Cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections. Offer the full version in one line.

## Short mode

Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **What needs attention:** candidate recurrences after CAPA closure; clocks passed without a decision or report; complaints over your ageing limit. One line each, with ids and dates.
2. **Timeliness:** counts of on time, late, undecided past due, decided not reportable after the due date, then one line per late or undecided row only; GB counting `[GAP]`; holidays excluded or not. Rows decided on time are counted, not listed.
3. **Context:** one line of FY 2025 FDA 483 data, labeled pre-QMSR, not a benchmark (only if useful).
4. **Where it goes:** undecided complaints to DH-03.02.4.1 A2; candidate recurrences to A1 or B1 here.
5. **Record:** "Draft from your log. Limits and verdicts are yours. Uncleared."
