# US Federal holidays, 2026 and 2027, as observed for most Federal employees.
# Source: U.S. Office of Personnel Management, "Federal Holidays",
#   https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/pay-leave/federal-holidays/ (read 19 October 2026).
# Where a holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, OPM lists the observed weekday. OPM's note:
#   "If a holiday falls on a Saturday, for most Federal employees, the preceding Friday will be treated
#   as a holiday for pay and leave purposes. (See 5 U.S.C. 6103(b).)"
# 21 CFR 803.3 defines "Work day means Monday through Friday, except Federal holidays." It does not say
# whether the observed weekday or the calendar date counts; this file uses OPM's observed dates
# (proposed reading). Rows R9 and R14 in references/primary-text-additions.md (same file as in the audit-capa-and-complaints skill).
