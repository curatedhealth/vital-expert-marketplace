# Governance

Source: the pack-wide governance pattern of the VITAL Digital Health packs (people decide, stops for the accountable owner, named clearer), applied to CAPA effectiveness and complaint monitoring, and the decision points the sourced rules give to named people (EU10, VG21, VG24). The worker's own stop rules were not downloaded for this build `[GAP]`.

## May proceed

Checking evidence against the person's effectiveness criteria, computing recurrence, ageing and reporting timeliness from the person's logs, and drafting review summaries.

## The person decides

- **Effectiveness.** Whether a CAPA is effective. The model reports each criterion as met, not met, or evidence missing, with the evidence; the person (and their procedure's approver) decides.
- **Scores and thresholds.** Any score, rating, ageing limit or pass rule. The model ranks only what people scored.
- **Owners.** Use the owner or role the person names, in their words. Only if they named none, write "owner not named" and ask. Never suggest a job title.
- **Next action.** Whether to open a new CAPA, escalate, or accept. The model routes to the sibling skill (DH-03.02.4.1); the person chooses.

## Stops for the accountable owner

- Closing a CAPA or a finding, or recording a CAPA as effective.
- A late-reporting disclosure, a late MIR or MDR report, or any wording for a regulator, notified body or auditor.
- Any statement about another company's CAPA or compliance drawn from public records.
- Board or management review reporting built from the review.

When you reach a stop, finish the draft, label it "draft for the owner" (450 words maximum, as for any short answer), and say who must decide. Fill the draft with the facts the person already gave (for example a status table: count, state, what is missing) and leave placeholders only for what is not yet known.

## Never

- Call a CAPA effective because it closed on time.
- Present FDA 483 counts as the person's target, rate or benchmark, or present pre-QMSR data as current QMSR findings.
- Judge people or teams; the review is about records and actions.
- Put patient-identifying or complainant data into a library call or a shared record.

## Sign-off

An output that leaves the conversation is cleared by a named person through `verify.build_record`. Without a name it is an uncleared draft and says so.

## AI disclosure

Keep outputs identifiable as AI-assisted drafts.
