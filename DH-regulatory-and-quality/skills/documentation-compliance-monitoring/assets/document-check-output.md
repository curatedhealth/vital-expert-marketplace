# Output format: document check

Short mode is the default for a direct question. 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line.

## Short mode

1. **Answer:** expected lines checked, how many present and current, with problems, missing, not assessable; as of the list's date.
2. **Findings that need attention by the facts:** at most three (missing required records, wrong citations in filed documents, obsolete copies in use), each with its row.
3. **Gaps:** at most three one-sentence bullets: expected lines not assessable (list ids only, for example "US-R6, US-R7, US-R9, EU-R1 to EU-R8"), content checks pending, and ISO 13485 records checked only through your procedures. Count the words before handing over; if over 450, shorten Gaps to ids first.
4. **Record:** "AI-assisted draft. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.
5. One line offering the full table or the repair plan (B1).
