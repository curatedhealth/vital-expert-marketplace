# Output format: review and repair plan

Short mode is the default for a direct question. 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line.

## Short mode

1. **Top findings by your scores:** Finding · Score (who scored) · Proposed repair · Owner you named · Date you set.
2. **Unscored findings:** count, and that they stay unranked.
2a. **If nothing is scored:** one sentence naming the scoring options (MDSAP grading inputs, QS45 and QS46, or your own scale) and offering the full criteria; the clause split and repeat definition go in full mode only. Count the words before handing over.
3. **The decision needed:** one or two sentences (for example whether a CAPA is warranted for changes released before approval).
4. **Record:** "Repair plan draft. Scores, owners and dates are yours. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary
Findings in scope, how they were scored and by whom, the one decision the plan asks for.

### Scoring criteria (yours)
| Criterion | Scale | Source if borrowed (for example QS45) |
|---|---|---|

### Ranked findings
| Rank | Finding | Facts | Scores (who) | Total | Row |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Ties shown as ties. Weight swap points, if weights were used.

### Repair plan
| Finding | Proposed repair | Basis | Owner you named | Date you set | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Unscored findings
Listed, unranked.

### Stops
CAPA decisions, regulator responses, corrections to filed documents: for the owner.

### Record
AI-assisted draft. No past record edited or backdated. Cleared by NAME via verify.build_record, or uncleared.
