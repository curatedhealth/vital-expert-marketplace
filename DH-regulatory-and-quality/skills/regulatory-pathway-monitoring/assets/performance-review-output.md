# Output format: performance review (review draft)

Short mode is 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column and the calls list. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question, full-mode sections. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Offer the full version in one line. A refusal or stop answer follows the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Verdict by jurisdiction:** Jurisdiction · On track / At risk / Missed · Stall point · Owner you named.
2. **The decision needed:** one or two sentences: which miss matters most and the choice the owner faces.
3. **Hypotheses to test first:** at most three, each with what would confirm it.
4. **Where the response goes:** one line per missed jurisdiction (usually back to the pathway strategy).
5. **Record:** "Review draft. Rules and owners are yours. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary

Which jurisdictions are working, which are not, and the one decision the review asks for.

### Rules

| Jurisdiction | Rule in your words | Threshold or condition | Owner | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Results

| Jurisdiction | Rule | Fact behind it (date) | Pass / Miss / Not measurable | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Stall points

| Jurisdiction | First step that missed | What it means |
|---|---|---|

### Hypotheses

| Jurisdiction | Hypothesis | What would confirm it | Source of the mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|

Labeled as hypotheses; none is a finding.

### Response routing

| Jurisdiction | Stall point | Responding work | Owner you named |
|---|---|---|---|

### Gaps

Rules that could not be measured, and why.

### Record

Review draft. Pathway changes, regulator wording and board reporting stop for the accountable owner. AI-assisted. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
