# Pathway facts by route

Interpretive reference for tasks A2 and B1. One section per route with the five facts B1 lays out per option: what the route is, the evidence bar, the review clock, the fee, and capacity. Row prefixes as in `classification-rules.md` (RP = `reg-pathways-sources.md`, MR and JX = `multi-region-sources.md`, AI = `ai-ml-regulation-sources.md`). All read 19 October 2026. A cell that no row supports is `[GAP]`.

Read the clocks carefully. There are three different numbers for the same FDA route: the clock in the regulation, the MDUFA goal in "FDA days" (which excludes time the file sits with the sponsor), and the average total days actually observed. Never present one as another (RP "Changes and conflicts").

## European Union (MDR)

| Fact | Class I | Class IIa | Class IIb | Class III |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Route | Self-declaration after Annex II and III documentation (RP EU-11). | Annex IX Chapters I and III, technical documentation assessed for at least one representative device per category (RP EU-10). | Annex IX Chapters I and III, at least one representative device per generic device group (RP EU-09). | Annex IX, or Annex X with Annex XI (RP EU-08). |
| Notified body | No (except sterile, measuring, reusable surgical aspects) (RP EU-11). | Yes | Yes | Yes; may consult an expert panel on the clinical development strategy first (RP EU-19). |

Evidence bar for all classes: clinical evaluation under Article 61 and Annex XIV Part A, with the level of evidence specified and justified by the manufacturer (RP EU-17, RP EU-18, RP EU-20). For software, MDCG 2020-1 (March 2020) frames clinical evidence as valid clinical association, technical performance and clinical performance (RP EU-32, RP EU-33). Equivalence to another device is possible but needs similar technical characteristics including software algorithms and sufficient access to its data (JX-21 to JX-24).

Notified body facts (20th Commission-commissioned contractor survey, data as of 28 February 2026, published 2 July 2026; RP EU-40):
- 52 notified bodies designated for MDR (RP EU-41, RP EU-43).
- 31,902 MDR applications lodged, 20,698 written agreements signed, 989 applications refused since designation (RP EU-41, RP EU-42).
- 62% of notified bodies report 13 to 18 months from written agreement to a new QMS certificate; for QMS plus product certificates, 51% report 13 to 18 months and 31% report 19 to 24 months (RP EU-44).
- No software-specific capacity breakdown was found `[GAP]` (RP "Not sourced").
- The manufacturer may choose any notified body designated for the device type, but may not apply to two in parallel (RP EU-12). The official list is in the Single Market Compliance Space (RP EU-45).

Fees: notified body fees are set by each body and are not in the research rows `[GAP]`.

Registration: EUDAMED actor and UDI/device registration have been mandatory since 28 May 2026; a device first placed on the market on or after that date must be registered before the first unit is placed (RP EU-36; JX-36, JX-40).

AI-enabled software in class IIa or higher is also high-risk under the AI Act; obligations apply from 2 August 2028 (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744); conformity is checked inside the MDR assessment, one technical file (AI EU-04, AI EU-19, AI EU-27, AI EU-29). Pre-determined changes documented at initial conformity assessment are not a substantial modification (AI EU-05, AI EU-30).
