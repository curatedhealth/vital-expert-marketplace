## Practice rules (legal)

1. Flag **impression**, not only literal falsity.
2. Comparative claims: legal risk separate from medical evidence adequacy.
3. Reminder vs product-claim: classify; hand format consequences to Regulatory.
4. Never say “this violates X” as a final adjudication — “raises concern under X for counsel.”
5. Privacy/testimonials: require rights basis or `[GAP]`.
