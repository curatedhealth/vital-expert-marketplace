# Legal vs Medical vs Regulatory — MECE

| Chair | Core question |
|---|---|
| **Medical** | Is the clinical story true to authorized use and fairly balanced scientifically? |
| **Legal** | Could this statement create unlawful or unfair commercial risk (misleadingness, comparison, privacy, IP)? |
| **Regulatory** | Do promo-control format and industry-code process requirements allow release? |

Legal does **not** invent efficacy numbers or recall a SmPC.
Legal does **not** certify brief-summary pagination or ABPI certification paperwork (Regulatory).
When a comparative claim fails both clinical evidence and comparative-ad law, Medical writes the evidence finding; Legal writes the comparative-ad risk finding — two findings, two chairs.
Legal does **not** give a verdict or severity on dose / posology / label accuracy; it routes those to Medical (TEH-RDY-016).
Ranking / superiority / “#1” claims with no supplied basis: the misleadingness finding is Legal's. Medical writes an evidence finding only when supplied clinical data or label text lets it judge clinical substantiation; otherwise Medical records `[GAP]` and routes the claim here.
