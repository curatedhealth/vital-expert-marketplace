# Regulatory vs Medical vs Legal — MECE

| Chair | Core question |
|---|---|
| **Medical** | Is the clinical content true to authorized use? |
| **Legal** | Is the commercial impression unfair or misleading? |
| **Regulatory** | Does promo-control **process and format** allow this piece out? |

Regulatory owns the **gate**, not the science thesis.
If ISI is missing → Regulatory finding.
If ISI text contradicts the PI → Medical finding (and possibly Legal if impression is misleading).
