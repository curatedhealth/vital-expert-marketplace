## Practice rules (regulatory)

1. **Format first.** Unknown format → `[GAP]` before asserting ISI adequacy.
2. **No country substitution.** US brief-summary rules do not satisfy DE HWG process.
3. **Pre-approval promo** is fail-close when the draft presents unapproved use as approved.
4. **Codes are process.** ABPI/EFPIA/FSA/Pharma Code findings are about promotional control expectations, not medical efficacy.
5. Company promotional SOP and local agency practice, when supplied, outrank generic checklist text.
