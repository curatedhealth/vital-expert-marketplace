# Access monitoring reference: does coverage turn into patient access?

Germany (DiGA) · France (PECAN, LPPR, LATM) · England (NICE early-use / EVA, NHS Talking Therapies, MedTech Funding Mandate)

**Header note.** Research pass of 19 October 2026. Primary sources only (statute, regulator, payer, HTA body, official agency). Rows marked **[WF]** were read through a web-fetch summarizer because the site blocked direct download; the quote passed through a model and must be re-read on the live page before it is relied on. All other quotes were taken from raw downloads saved under `research42/raw/`. This file has not yet been reviewed by the owner.

Evidence class for every row is P (published by the named primary body) unless the row says otherwise. Where a figure is a calculation made in this research pass from a published dataset, the row says "derived" and gives the arithmetic.

## Method

Pages and PDFs were downloaded with curl through the session proxy and converted to text locally (`pdftotext -layout`, a small HTML-to-text script). Quotes were copied from the converted text. HAS (has-sante.fr) serves an incomplete certificate chain; the missing Sectigo intermediate was fetched from the certificate's own AIA address and added to a local CA file, so TLS verification stayed on. Legifrance, ameli.fr, sante.gouv.fr, esante.gouv.fr news pages, digital.nhs.uk HTML pages and england.nhs.uk HTML pages returned bot-protection pages (Cloudflare, Incapsula, F5 or "Vérification de sécurité"); where a fact mattered, WebFetch was used and the row is marked [WF]. NHS Talking Therapies figures were taken from the published CSV and XLSX files on files.digital.nhs.uk, which downloaded directly. Topics already sourced elsewhere (listed in the brief) were not redone, except where a row here adds a new field from the same document.

## Changes and conflicts

1. **sinCephalea status depends on the date.** GKV25 Table 5 lists it as "noch in Erprobung" at 31 December 2025. GKV25 footnote 6 records its deletion on 22 January 2026 by court decision. Any count "still on trial" taken from GKV25 is a 31 December 2025 snapshot.
2. **Repeated listing after deletion.** GKV25 (executive summary) says that in individual cases DiGA were re-listed after deletion ("Auch wurden in Einzelfällen DiGA nach bereits erfolgter Streichung aus dem GKV-Leistungskatalog wieder in das DiGA-Verzeichnis aufgenommen"). The statute bars a second trial listing (§ 139e Abs. 4 last sentence). The sinCephalea re-listing was by interim court order. A monitor should not treat deletion as final until the court route is exhausted.
3. **AbEM legal status vs the Leitfaden caveat.** LF36 (10 December 2025) prefaces its AbEM chapter with "basieren auf dem aktuellen Referentenentwurf der 2. DiGAV-ÄndV ... vorbehaltlich weiterer Anpassungen". The consolidated DiGAV on gesetze-im-internet.de (downloaded 19 October 2026) already contains § 20 Abs. 4 and § 23a (first report 15 April 2027). GKV25 describes AbEM as planned in a 2025 draft. The in-force text should be read as current. LF36 details such as the 1 July 2026 start and the stages should be checked against a later Leitfaden version.
4. **Scope of AbEM differs by source.** DiGAV § 23a covers DiGA with a permanent-listing decision, with PROM data waived for structure-and-process-only benefit. LF36 says data collection covers permanently listed DiGA with at least one proven medical benefit. These may not be the same set (DE-17).
5. **When the French filing clock starts.** HAS says 6 or 9 months "à compter de la décision de PECAN" (FR-2). The G_NIUS English page says "from the start of early coverage" (G_NIUS raw/fr/gnius_pecan_en.txt). HAS also says the 12 months run "à partir de la demande" (FR-1). These three anchors (decision, start of coverage, application) are not reconciled in the sources read. The arrêtés and CSS articles are needed.
6. **France counts are old.** The only official count (FR-4) is from April 2024. HAS file numbering reaches PECAN-18 by June 2026 (FR-6), so the April 2024 figure should not be presented as current.
7. **England: NICE vs NHS England status of Minddistrict and Deprexis.** NICE HTG675 places Minddistrict research-only for depression (EN-6). NHS England lists Minddistrict (step 3 GAD) and Deprexis as not compliant with DET criteria ([WF], EN-10). The 2024-25 NHS Talking Therapies data still records 843 Minddistrict and 43 Deprexis IET sessions (EN-7). The data does not show the indication or setting of those sessions, so no inference about compliance should be drawn without more evidence.
8. **England: two different "digital" measures in the same publication.** EN-8 counts referrals whose last therapy type was IET (37,112). EN-9 counts courses of "Guided Self Help (Computer)" (14,316) on a different unit. They must not be added together or compared directly. EN-7 counts sessions, not patients.
9. **MTFM exit dates in the policy page are internally inconsistent** according to the [WF] summary: the Annex 4 table and paragraph 31 give different timelines for the 2022/23 technologies. This needs to be re-read on the live page.
10. **GKV25 is the payer's view.** It argues for abolishing the trial and free-pricing rules (for example "Ein Festhalten an den bestehenden Erprobungs- und Preismechanismen für DiGA kann kein gangbarer Weg sein."). Its figures are official statutory-report data, but its causal readings (for example on marketing-driven approvals) are the GKV-Spitzenverband's interpretation.
