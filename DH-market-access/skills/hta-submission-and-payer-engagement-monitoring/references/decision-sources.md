## Official outcome figures (context, never targets)

### Germany: applications (BfArM, snapshot of 9 September 2026)

254 applications since 27 May 2020: 196 for trial listing, 58 for permanent listing (`DE33`). Outcomes: 62 listed, 29 refused, 131 withdrawn, 14 in review, 18 delisted, 7 of them at the manufacturer's request (`DE34`).

How to read them:
- Withdrawals (131) far exceed refusals (29); a refusal-only rate understates failed applications (conflict note 9).
- The outcome categories are a snapshot of current state, not a partition you can sum to 254: a delisted DiGA was once listed. Report the categories as given and do not compute a "success rate" from them unless the person asks, and then label it derived, state the arithmetic, and say the categories may overlap.
- A withdrawal costs up to 75 percent of the fee (`DE26`) and starts the 12-month re-application bar (`DE23`).

### Germany: trial outcomes (GKV-Spitzenverband, to 31 December 2025)

Of 60 DiGA first listed on trial, 34 became permanent, 16 were removed, 10 were still on trial (`DE-1`). About one in five that became permanent did so with a narrower scope (`DE-2`). The payer states the 16 were removed because they could not show a benefit for care (`DE-3`). Trials averaged 20 months against the 12 the rules provide (`DE-6`). Litigation can extend a trial (`DE-7`).

### France: CNEDiMTS 2025

387 evaluation requests registered; for LPPR and intra-GHS, 297 opinions on 234 products; reimbursement recommended for 214 products (91 percent), progress recognized for 41 (19 percent); for new products 87 (83 percent) and 26 (25 percent) (`FR21`). LATM: 10 opinions on 8 dossiers, 8 favorable, 2 unfavorable (`FR22`). PECAN: 5 requests, 1 favorable, 4 unfavorable, in 57, 23, 29, 34 and 18 days (`FR23`). Incompleteness: 69 percent of requests (204 of 297) suspended at least once; 51 percent (153 of 297) for administrative reasons only; mean 49 days to administrative completeness (`FR24`). A separate count of digital-therapeutic opinions is not broken out `[GAP]`.

Named French cases from the research: Joe (Ludocare), first favorable LPPR opinion for a digital therapeutic on 2 April 2026, after an unfavorable PECAN opinion in 2025 (`FR-5`); POPPINS CLINICAL, favorable PECAN opinion of 23 June 2026 (`FR-6`).

### England: NICE early use and re-evaluation

Early-use uncertainties should be resolvable within 3 years (`EN-1`, `UK7`). NICE may withdraw an early-use recommendation on listed grounds, including a company not engaging in evidence generation (`EN-2`, `UK8`). Technologies that complete evidence generation are re-evaluated for routine use; companies may submit evidence early at monitoring touchpoints (`EN-3`). Examples: HTG712 low back pain, about 3 years (`EN-4`); HTG768 eating disorders, 2 years (`EN-5`); HTG675 depression, review within 4 years, no re-evaluation outcome yet (`EN-6`).

### United States

No official outcome statistics for digital devices were sourced `[GAP]`. TCET accepts up to five candidates a year (`US5`).

## Why a national figure is never the person's rate

A body's rate pools products, indications, routes and years. The person's file is one case. Use the figures to show where files commonly stall (incompleteness, withdrawal, unproven claimed effects), never as a probability or a target.
