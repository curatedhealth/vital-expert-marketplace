# Output format: performance review

## Short mode (250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.

1. **Answer:** verdict per body (on track, at risk, missed) against the person's rules, each with the fact behind it.
2. **Stall point and owner:** one line per missed or at-risk body: stage, owner as named (or "owner not named").
3. **Hypotheses:** at most three, each labeled, with what would confirm it.
4. **Responding work:** one line per stall point, naming the task in the preparation skill or elsewhere.
5. **Decision needed:** one.
6. **Record:** "Rules and owners are yours. Uncleared draft."
