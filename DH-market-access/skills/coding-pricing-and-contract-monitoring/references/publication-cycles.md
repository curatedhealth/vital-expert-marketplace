### What is proposed for 2027 (not law)

US23 RTM established patient; US24 face-to-face initiation; US25 practice-employed staff only; US26 revaluation of device codes judged overvalued; US27 comment solicitation on four monthly G-codes; US28 "Software as a Medical Service". Each is "proposed 16 July 2026" until the final rule is read.

OIG finding cited by CMS as background to the 2027 proposals: about 43 percent of enrollees who received RPM did not receive all 3 components (MO4). Useful as a denial-risk signal, not as a finding about the person's claims.

## What the library can and cannot show

The library holds a day-zero snapshot of CMS codes (15 October 2026) and of DiGA listings, with no price history (`tools.md`). Change detection is therefore: the person's earlier run or contract value versus today's row, plus the publication events above.
