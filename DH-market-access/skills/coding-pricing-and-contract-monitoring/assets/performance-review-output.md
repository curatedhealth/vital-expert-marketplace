# Output format: performance review (review draft)

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 300 to 450 words, hard cap. If an A1 check is run, report it in one line: "Rule changes: none bearing on these contracts" or the one change and its row.)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.

1. **Verdict by contract:** Contract · On track / At risk / Missed · Stall point · Owner you named.
2. **The decision needed:** one or two sentences.
3. **Hypotheses to test first:** at most three, each with what would confirm it.
4. **Where the response sits:** one line per missed contract (coding map, price evidence, negotiation prep, outcomes contract, revenue cycle).
5. **Record:** "Review draft. Rules and owners are yours. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary
Which contracts perform, which do not, and the one decision the review asks for.

### Rules
| Contract | Rule in your words | Threshold | Owner | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Results
| Contract | Rule | Number behind it | Pass / Miss / Not measurable | Source (A1 or A2, date) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Stall points
| Contract | First missed stage | What it means |
|---|---|---|

### Hypotheses
| Contract | Hypothesis | What would confirm it | Source of the mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
Labeled as hypotheses; none is a finding.

### Response routing
| Contract | Stall point | Responding work | Owner you named |
|---|---|---|---|

### Gaps

### Record
Review draft. Price, contract, billing changes and external wording stop for the accountable owner. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
