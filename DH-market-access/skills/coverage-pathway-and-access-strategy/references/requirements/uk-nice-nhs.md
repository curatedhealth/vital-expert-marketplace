## Changes and conflicts

1. **The ESF is stale on CE marking.** It still says "CE marking (until June 2023)" (how-to-meet-the-standards, standard 1). The MHRA guidance now allows CE-marked devices until 30 June 2028 or 2030. Use the MHRA dates. The ESF itself was last updated 9 August 2022.
2. **The ESF tier names differ across NICE pages.** The update log names the tiers "Tier A: system impact", "Tier B: understanding and communicating" and "Tier C: interventions" (the 2021 names). Section B, as updated in 2022, uses "System service", the three tier B groups and four tier C groups. Use the section B classification.
3. **EVA has been renamed.** "Early value assessment" is now "early-use HealthTech guidance" under PMG48 (from 14 July 2025). The EVA interim statement still governs topics that started before that date.
4. **NHTAP naming varies.** Pages use "National HealthTech Access Programme" and "National Healthtech Access Programme", and the news item uses "NHAP". The programme was previously the "Rules Based Pathway". The about-healthtech page and the "backing the healthtech" URL served identical content.
5. **NHTAP coverage.** The news item says "People across England and Wales". The task scope is England.
6. **CE recognition end date is in flux.** In law the end dates are still 30 June 2028 and 30 June 2030. The indefinite-recognition consultation closed on 10 April 2026, and the outcome was unpublished as read. The current position must be re-checked before use.
7. **DCB0129 and DCB0160 are under revision.** The consultation closed on 11 September 2026, so revised standards may follow. DTAC also says "Further reviews are still ongoing to some of the standards within DTAC, such as the DCB0129".
8. **DTAC form version.** The DTAC form from February 2026 (file name "dtac_form_2.0_february_2026.docx") replaces the earlier form from 6 April 2026. Any material citing the older DTAC question set is out of date.
9. **NICE pages are mostly undated.** The about-healthtech page and the NHS England clinical safety assurance page carry no date, so treat their currency as unverified.
