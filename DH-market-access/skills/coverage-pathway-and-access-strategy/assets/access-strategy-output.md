# Output format: access strategy (decision draft)

## Short mode (default for a direct question; aim for 300 to 500 words)

1. **Recommendation as ranked by your scores:** one or two sentences naming the order and the one condition that matters most.
2. **Ranking:** Route · Value estimate · Biggest contributing criterion.
3. **How robust:** the weight at which the top two would swap, and the same for second and third; name the close call.
4. **Sequence and gates:** a numbered list; each step names its gate.
5. **Facts that could change a score:** at most three, each with its source.
6. **Open inputs:** one line.
5. **Record:** "Decision draft. Scores and weights are yours. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### What this ranks

One paragraph: the options considered, the options left out and why, and that weights and scores are the person's.

### Criteria, anchors and weights

| Criterion | 0 means | 2 means | 4 means | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Facts behind the scores

| Route | Criterion 1 | Criterion 2 | … |
|---|---|---|---|

Each cell: the fact with its source, then the person's score in brackets. A `[GAP]` cell says so.

### Ranking

| Route | Value estimate | Contributions by criterion |
|---|---|---|

Method: the tool's MCDA method and citation, as returned.

### How robust

For each adjacent pair in the ranking, the weight at which they would swap, with the other weights kept in proportion. Name the close call.

### Sequence and gates

| Step | Route or action | Why here (what it unlocks or waits for) | Gate to proceed |
|---|---|---|---|

### Risks and open inputs

Facts that are `[GAP]`, conflicts between sources, and assumptions the ranking depends on.

### Record

Decision draft. The choice, pricing and any external wording stop for the accountable owner. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
