# Output format: reimbursement status check

Two modes. Pick by the request (see the task guide). Both keep the same order; the short mode compresses.

## Short mode (default for quick questions)

Aim for 150 to 300 words.

1. **Answer:** two or three sentences that answer the question asked, with the as-of date and the one caveat that matters most.
2. **Table:** only the schemes that answer the question, with columns Scheme · Status and what it means · Since · Source.
3. **Comparables** (only if step 4 ran): up to three lines.
4. **Gaps:** at most three lines, one per class.
5. **Sources and record:** one line ("Library records, retrieved DATE; confirm on the authority's page. Uncleared draft.").

End with one offer: the full version, or the next task (for example, coverage pathway mapping).

## Full mode (memo, board, document to share)

### Answer

Two or three sentences. Start with the finding, give the as-of date, name the most important caveat.

### Status by scheme

| Scheme | Country | Kind | Status (source wording) | What it means | Since | Clock or next event | Price stated | Source, retrieved | Library coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

If other schemes were checked, add one line below: "Also checked: N other schemes; see gaps."

### Comparable products

Only if step 4 ran: product, scheme, status, since, price as stated, source. Labeled as comparables, not as evidence that this product would qualify.

### What the gaps mean

Grouped by class from `references/gap-rules.md`, one line each, with the next step.

### What this implies

One or two advisory lines that follow from the statuses. Labeled advisory. No promise of coverage or price.

### Sources and record

- Numbered source list with URL and retrieval date.
- A line saying the facts rest on the library's copy of each register.
- Record: cleared by NAME, or "uncleared draft".
