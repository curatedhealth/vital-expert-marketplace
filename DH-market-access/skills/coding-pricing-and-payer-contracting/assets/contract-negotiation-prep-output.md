# Output format: contract negotiation preparation

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 300 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.


1. **Counterparty and clock:** who, which process, the dates that bound it (counted from the anchor you gave).
2. **Inputs they will require:** at most five, each marked have / missing / [GAP].
3. **Fixed terms:** the terms the law or the model sets, with row.
4. **Open terms:** the terms the parties define, position left blank.
5. **Your objectives and walk-away:** in your words, or "not set".
6. **Decisions you owe before the meeting:** at most three.
7. **Record:** "Preparation draft. Positions are yours. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary
The negotiation in three sentences: counterparty, clock, the decisive open term.

### Process and clocks
| Step | Rule | Anchor date | Date | Row |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Counterparty requirements
| Input | Required by | You have it? | Note |
|---|---|---|---|

### Terms
| Term | Fixed or open | Rule or floor | Evidence in hand | Your position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
"Your position" is filled by you only.

### Objectives and walk-away (yours)
| Rank | Objective in your words | Constraint | Walk-away |
|---|---|---|---|

### Risks the facts show
| Risk | Rule | Row | Conditional consequence |
|---|---|---|---|

### Decisions for the owner
Numbered list, each with the owner you named.

### Record
Preparation draft. Price, terms and any wording for the counterparty stop for the accountable owner. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
