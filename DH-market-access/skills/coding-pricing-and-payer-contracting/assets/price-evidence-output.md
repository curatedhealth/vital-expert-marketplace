# Output format: price evidence

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.


1. **Answer:** the rule that bounds price on this route, and the comparator range as stored, in two or three sentences. No price for the product.
2. **Rules in force:** at most six lines, only those that bound price on this route (year-one rule, month-13 rule, arbitration, threshold, the product's group maximum and its mechanics, outcome share and volume discount), each with value, validity window and row.
3. **Registered prices:** comparators or codes, with status, months listed, stored amount, source and retrieval date; national context labeled.
4. **Model outputs (only if asked):** inputs, result, and "hypothesis on your inputs; not a price".
5. **Gaps:** at most three.
6. **Record:** "Uncleared draft. The pricing position is the owner's." or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary
What binds price on each route, what comparators are registered at, and what is still unknown.

| Market | Rule | Value | Valid from / to | Row |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Layer 3: model outputs (hypotheses)
| Tool | Your inputs | Result | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
Every input is yours. These are not prices.

### What the rules constrain
Conditional consequences only.

### Gaps
Each with its class and where to check.

### Record
Advisory draft. Any pricing position stops for the accountable owner. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
