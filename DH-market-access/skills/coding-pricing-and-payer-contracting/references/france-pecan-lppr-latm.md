## Changes and conflicts

1. **Two versions of the HAS PECAN filing guide.** The 2023-03 URL holds the "Version du 19 décembre 2023". A March 2026 version exists at a separate URL but could not be read. Content may have changed; re-check before relying on the December 2023 wording.
2. **One year versus 12 months from the decision.** The law reads "un an, non renouvelable"; HAS reads "12 mois à compter de la décision de prise en charge". The G_NIUS FAQ says the start date is the entry-into-force date of the published arrêté, not the decision date. The anchor date is ambiguous across sources.
3. **LATM review clock.** The HAS LATM principles give CNEDiMTS 90 days, cut to 70 days for an activity under PECAN. A WebFetch summary of the PECAN principles described a "9 months for LATM / 6 months for LPPR" opinion deadline; no verbatim quote supported that reading, and it likely confuses the company filing deadlines. Not used.
4. **ANS referential version labels.** The file named "Référentiel_DMN_TLS_V1_1_2.pdf" reported its content as V1.2.2 dated 09/02/2023, the same as REF_IS_DMN_FR_V1.2.2_0.pdf. The ANS PECAN guide names "REF_IS_DMN_V1.2.2" and the Pro Santé Connect referential v1.8.4. The referential PDF itself does not mention PECAN; the ANS PECAN guide does. A newer referential version may exist on ANS HTML pages that could not be read.
5. **ANS PECAN guide cover.** The extraction tool reported both "Version 1.2.2 / Validé" and a "Version 0.1 / En cours / Restreinte" label on the cover, with no publication date. Confirm the current version with the ANS.
6. **Telemonitoring compensation may be superseded.** G_NIUS (updated 12 December 2024) gives "from 50 € TTC" monthly, degressive. An arrêté of 31 March 2026 on telemonitoring and PECAN compensation is listed on Légifrance but could not be read.
7. **LATM evaluation vocabulary.** The HAS LATM principles use "intérêt attendu" (clinical improvement, organizational gain, public-health interest) rather than the LPPR terms SA and ASA. A skill should not apply the LPPR SA/ASA scale to LATM.
8. **HDS referential revision.** The arrêté of 26 April 2024 (JO 16 May 2024) revised the HDS certification referential, adding EEA-only storage (requirement 28) and applying six months after publication to new and renewal applications. Its effect on certificates already issued is not stated in the text read.
