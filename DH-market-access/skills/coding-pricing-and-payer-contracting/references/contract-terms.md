# Counterparties, process and terms by market

Interpretive reference for task B1. Rows cited from `coding-pricing-sources.md`, `outcomes-contracting-sources.md` (by topic) and `france-pecan-lppr-latm.md` (by topic). Read on 19 October 2026; not owner-reviewed.

## Germany: GKV-Spitzenverband (DiGA price agreement)

| Item | Fact | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Counterparty | GKV-Spitzenverband, for all sickness funds | DE1 |
| When the agreed amount applies | After the first year from listing, permanent or provisional (from month 13) | DE1, DE9 |
| Fallback | Arbitration board if no agreement within nine months of listing; it decides within three months; the difference is trued up; a lawsuit does not suspend | DE5 |
| Arbitration board | Neutral chair, two neutral members, two from funds, two from manufacturers; chair has the casting vote | DE6 |
| What the manufacturer must submit | Evidence and trial results, self-pay and other-European-country prices, results of in-use outcome measurement | DE4 |
| Confidentiality | Negotiations and their preparation are confidential; agreement can be terminated after one year at the earliest | DE4 |
| Outcome clauses visible to funds | GKV-Spitzenverband may tell funds about performance-dependent payment rules relevant to billing | Outcomes file, "Framework agreement: disclosure of outcome clauses" |
| Price criteria | Extent of proven medical benefit and/or structural and procedural improvements | DE12 |

Mandatory terms (statute):

| Term | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome-linked share | At least 20 percent of the amount from 1 January 2026 | DE2 |
| Volume discount tiers | From 1 January 2027: at least 2 percent above 3,000 units a year; at least 30 percent above 100,000 | DE3 |
| Group maximum and threshold (context, not a term of the agreement) | Bound the manufacturer's year-one price (temporary remuneration), not the negotiated amount; quote only the group the product falls in by indication and care effect (DE11); if the indication is not given, [GAP] | DE7, DE11, DE14, DE15 |

Open terms (statute sets a floor or nothing; the parties define): which outcomes count and how attainment is measured (framework section 9 leaves it to the parties; outcomes file, "Framework agreement: how outcome components are defined"); the size of the outcome share above 20 percent; discount levels above the statutory minimums; tiers between 3,000 and 100,000 units. The payer and industry associations both said the 2026 pricing deadline does not match AbEM data starting 15 April 2027 (outcomes file, "Payer and industry position"). No implementing framework or arbitration award was found (coding file Not sourced 9 and 10).

## France: CEPS (LPP brand-name listing)

| Item | Fact | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Counterparty | CEPS, interministerial, 10 members; its secretariat's opinion does not bind it | FR1, FR3 |
| Process | HAS (CNEDiMTS) evaluation then price negotiation inside a 180-day clock; publication in the Journal officiel | FR4 |
| Price vs tariff | Can differ; no predefined range | FR5 |
| Prerequisite | Interoperability and security certificate | FR6 |

Not sourced: the CSS articles on price criteria, conventions and rebates, and the CEPS framework agreement for devices (coding file Not sourced 6). Any price-volume or rebate clause is `[GAP]`. PECAN and LATM amounts are set by order (arrêté), not negotiated in the sources read (France file, "PECAN process: decision and amount"; "LATM evaluation: tariffs").

## What the person sets, never the model

Objectives, opening position, concessions, walk-away point, price, discount offers, outcome measures proposed, and who signs. The model lists inputs the counterparty will require and the terms that are fixed or open; the pricing position stops for its owner (`governance.md`).
