# Output format: engagement plan and value narrative

## Short mode (250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.

Hard cap: count the words before sending. Over 450, cut in this order: comparables to one line, the consultation line, rows already marked "[GAP]" merged into one row ("Not in the record: ..."), the closing offer. Never cut the answer, row ids, the claim status or the record line.

1. **Answer:** the sequence in one sentence (the person's, or "proposed" with the deciding constraint), and how many claims are usable.
2. **Sequence:** one table: Step · Body and route · Depends on · Lead time or clock (row) · Owner (as named, or "owner not named").
3. **Usable claims:** three to five lines: claim · requirement (row) · source and locator · checked.
4. **Excluded claims:** one line each with the reason (not checked, inadmissible endpoint, no organizational endpoint).
5. **Decisions for the person:** at most four.
6. **Record:** "Draft for [owner]: not for submission. Price and contract terms: coding, pricing and contracting skill. Uncleared draft. AI-assisted."
