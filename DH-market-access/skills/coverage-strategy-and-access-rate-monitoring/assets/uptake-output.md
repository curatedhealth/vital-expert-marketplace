# Output format: uptake measurement

## Short mode (default for a direct question; aim for 250 to 450 words)

1. **Where patients are lost:** one sentence naming the market, the stage and the number (for example "Germany, Q2: 1,200 prescriptions, 900 activated (75 percent); the largest drop is between activation and week 4").
2. **Funnel by market:** Stage · Count · Share of previous stage · National figure for context (period, base).
3. **What each drop means:** one line per stage, from the access rules (for example unpaid prescriptions, outcome-linked price).
4. **Measure next:** at most three measurements that would locate the loss.
5. **Data gaps:** stages not measured and who would hold them.
6. **Record:** "Uncleared draft. Counts are yours." or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary
Where coverage is and is not turning into use, in two or three sentences.

### Stage definitions
| Stage | Definition agreed with you | Data source |
|---|---|---|

### Funnel by market and period
| Market | Period | Stage | Count | Share of previous | Share of first stage | National figure (context, with period and base) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### What each drop means
One line per stage, citing the rule behind it.

### Next measurements
What to measure to locate the loss, and who holds it.

### Trend
Change per stage across periods, if more than one period was given. No extrapolation.

### Gaps and caveats
Stages not measured, units that differ, bases that differ from the national figure.

### Record
Advisory draft. Uptake is use, not clinical effect. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
