# Contract terms: the term sheet structure and the design test

Interpretive reference for task B1. Rows cited as in `risk-share-models.md`. Read on 19 October 2026. Not owner-reviewed.

No published model clause for DiGA outcome components was found (OC "Not sourced"). The structure below is drawn from the sourced design rules (ACCESS, the OECD design questions, ISPOR, the DiGA framework agreement). Every value in it is the parties' to set.

## The design test (run before drafting any term)

From OC Method guidance "OECD: design questions for measurable outcomes" and "ISPOR: when worth doing":

| Test | Question for the person | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Name the uncertainty | Which uncertainty about the product does this contract resolve or share? (effect size in routine care, persistence, cost offset, population fit) | No uncertainty is named, or it is one the measure cannot speak to |
| 2 Confirm the data source exists | For each measure, is there a source that holds it for the target population, and can the design estimate performance accurately enough? | The data does not exist yet, or no party can reach it (for example AbEM PROMs before Stage II, DE-18) |
| 3 Value of information exceeds administration cost | Is the cost of collecting, verifying and reconciling the data justified by the better decision it allows? | Burden is high and the decision would not change |

If any test fails, the honest output is "a financial term may fit better", laid out for the owner, not a contract.

## Term sheet sections

| # | Section | What it fixes | Sourced anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Parties and scope | Who pays whom, for which product, population and indication; the legal route | Germany: GKV-Spitzenverband negotiates for all funds (OC Germany rows); § 140a selective contracts for fund-level deals. US: ACCESS pays the care organization (OC "ACCESS: who is paid") |
| 2 | Measures | Each measure's definition, instrument, unit, time window (from A1) | ACCESS measures (OC "ACCESS: outcome measures"); AbEM fields (DE-18) |
| 3 | Baselines | When the baseline is taken and what happens without one | ACCESS: baseline within 60 days or unaligned (OC "ACCESS: verification data") |
| 4 | Attainment rule | Patient level (all required measures met) or population level (average change, share meeting target); the threshold | ACCESS: all-or-nothing per patient; 50 percent threshold (OC "ACCESS: all-or-nothing", "attainment threshold") |
| 5 | Payment link | Share at risk, timing (upfront, withheld, rebate), formula below threshold, cap or floor | ACCESS 50 percent withhold of the Medicare portion (80 percent of the allowed amount), proportional formula, 50 percent cap (OC "ACCESS: withhold", "attainment threshold", "ACCESS: Medicare portion and payment basis"); Germany at least 20 percent outcome-linked (OC "DiGA outcome-linked price share") |
| 6 | Data flow and verification | Who collects, who submits, how, how often, how values are checked; validity windows | ACCESS API submission, 15-day validity for BP, weight and PROMs, self-report limits (OC "ACCESS: verification data"); AbEM cadence (OC "AbEM reporting cadence") |
| 7 | Lost to follow-up | Whether patients who stop or vanish stay in the denominator and count as non-attainment | ACCESS: they stay in the denominator (OC "ACCESS: all-or-nothing"); AbEM PROMs voluntary (OC "AbEM: voluntary patient participation") |
| 8 | Dispute and audit | Who can audit the data, how disputes are settled | Germany: arbitration board sets amounts if no agreement within nine months of listing (OC "Arbitration route"); audit clause wording `[GAP]` |
| 9 | Confidentiality and disclosure | What is confidential, what may be shared | DiGA negotiations confidential; outcome clauses may be shared with funds for billing (OC "disclosure of outcome clauses"); ACCESS publishes risk-adjusted outcomes (OC "ACCESS: public reporting") |
| 10 | Exit and review | Term length, renegotiation triggers, what happens when rules change | DiGA agreement can be terminated after one year at the earliest (DE4, coding and pricing pass); OECD: recouping and delisting are hard, so state exit up front (OC "OECD: known problems") |

## Rules for drafting

- Every value in a term (share at risk, threshold, window, cap) is the person's or the counterparty's. If the person asks for a proposal, mark each value "proposed" with the sourced anchor it borrows from, and say that borrowing a public model's number is a design choice, not a norm.
- Where a term depends on a German framework method that does not exist yet, write `[GAP]: framework method not set` and keep the term conditional.
- Never state that a term will be accepted.
