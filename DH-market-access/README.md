# DH Market Access & Reimbursement

Install **DH Market Access & Reimbursement** from `curatedhealth/vital-expert-plugins`, then paste the Digital Health Library key. The key is not in this pack.

This pack is only DH-03.01 Market Access & Reimbursement. Clinical Validation, Health Economics, and Regulatory Strategy are separate packs.

The connector is `https://mcp.vital.expert/api/mcp/library`. Do not add a second connector.

Choose one heading, then one job. The job holds its tasks as sections. A missing fact stays a gap.
