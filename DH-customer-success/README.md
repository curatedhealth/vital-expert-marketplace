# DH Customer Success & Implementation

Install **DH Customer Success & Implementation** from `curatedhealth/vital-expert-plugins`, then paste the Digital Health Library key. The key is not in this pack.

This pack is only DH-02.02 Customer Success & Implementation. Other Digital Health cores are separate packs.

The connector is `https://mcp.vital.expert/api/mcp/library`. Do not add a second connector.

Choose one heading, then one job. The job holds its tasks as sections. Ask which one task, then follow that section. A missing fact stays a gap.

## Skills

- `account-growth-and-renewal`. Run renewals and account growth for a digital health product sold to health systems, practices, employers or payers: which renewals fall inside your horizon and which accounts are ready to expand, what a renewal or expansion offer may contain under US fraud-and-abuse rules (discounts, free modules or services, anything reaching patients), what renewal decks and account materials may claim, and a risk note with actions for accounts that may not renew.
- `adoption-monitoring`. Measure whether a digital health product is actually adopted after training and go-live at a customer (health system, clinic, payer, employer or pharma organization), and decide what to do where it is not: an adoption rule stated as the trained task done in production, adoption and penetration computed over time from the person's own usage file with every denominator shown (Proctor, RE-AIM), the old step still in use, activity after training, and a follow-up that names whether the task, the training or the workflow is wrong.
- `customer-implementation-planning`. Plan how a digital health product (SaMD, DTx, remote monitoring, clinical software) is deployed at a customer site: the implementation scope and blueprint (sites, modules, users, day-one workflow, integrations now and later, milestones, measures), the deployment sequence with its dependencies and the contract and data steps that gate it (BAA, GDPR Article 28, minimum necessary access, DTAC and DCB0160 for the NHS), training placed after configuration, and the go-live bar the site signs.
- `customer-success-capability-map`. The map of the VITAL Customer Success & Implementation pack for digital health products sold to health systems, clinics, payers, employers or pharma.
- `digital-health-expert`. Find and download a VITAL Digital Health expert (a ready-made role such as the SaMD Regulatory Affairs Director, the Quality & Software Lifecycle Director or the Regulatory Submission Manager) to run on the person's own assistant.
- `expansion-and-renewal-monitoring`. Measure renewals and expansion for a digital health product sold to health systems, practices, employers or payers: write down the renewal and retention definitions the team will report (cohort, what counts as renewed, exclusions, value basis), compute the renewal rate, value retained with and without expansion, and losses by reason from the account file with the formula shown, and check whether growth offers turned into signed expansions.
- `implementation-quality-monitoring`. Check whether customer deployments of a digital health product (SaMD, DTx, remote monitoring, clinical software) met their go-live bar and how they perform: evidence per bar item per site, implementation outcomes computed from the person's own site file (penetration of eligible patients and of providers, setting adoption, usage against the profile agreed at commissioning, fidelity to the milestone plan, milestone and go-live slippage), and a site review with open items, owners and repairs.
- `outcome-tracking-and-customer-health`. Track the outcomes a digital health customer agreed to see and read each account's health against targets the person sets: choose usage and outcome signals with sourced definitions (RE-AIM, Proctor penetration, NICE ESF usage reporting, PHQ-9, GAD-7 and PAM with what is and is not sourced), compute attainment and a health view per account from the person's account file, and draft the value note or next action per account.
- `training-and-change-enablement`. Plan and run customer training and change enablement for a digital health product (SaMD, DTx, remote monitoring, EHR-integrated tools) at a health system, clinic, payer, employer or pharma customer: task cards per role built from the day-one workflow, a training plan that matches what NICE ESF and FDA human factors guidance expect, session records that keep attendance apart from task completion, train-the-trainer and champion rosters, and the plan that makes the old step stop.
- `value-realization-monitoring`. Check over time whether a digital health company's customer health thresholds still fit and whether the value it reports to customers is backed: targets no account has met, signals no longer stored, shares that are not reach, targets eased after results turned accounts green, value notes matched to signal rows (supported, value differs, no row), effect or cost wording no usage figure can carry, and a correction plan with owners the person names.
