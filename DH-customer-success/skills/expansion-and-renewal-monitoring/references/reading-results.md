# Reading losses and refusals, and where each reason routes

Interpretive reference for B1 (and the reasons section of A2). Reasons are as the account record states them. This file says what a recorded reason can and cannot tell, which sourced facts sit beside it, and which skill or owner it routes to. It never turns a reason into a cause.

| Recorded reason (the person's words vary) | Sourced facts beside it | Can tell | Cannot tell | Routes to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Implementation open, go-live missed | Implementation outcomes are preconditions for clinical or service outcomes (CS32 [PAPER], Proctor 2011, DOI 10.1007/s10488-010-0319-7); quality of execution covers fidelity to plan, intensity, timeliness and engagement of key people (CS9); the implementation blueprint has aim, scope, timeframe and milestones, and progress measures (CS42) | The account never reached the state where value could be seen | Whether the product would have delivered value | Implementation skills (`customer-implementation-planning`, `implementation-quality-monitoring`) |
| Value unseen, result not yet seen | NICE asks for a usage report against an expected usage profile agreed at commissioning (CS61); effectiveness includes negative effects, quality of life and economic outcomes (CS23) | Whether an agreed yardstick existed and was reported | Whether value existed; a missing report is not missing value | Customer Health skills (`outcome-tracking-and-customer-health`, `value-realization-monitoring`); assign an owner (SR21, if adopted) |
| Price | None in the sources defines price-driven churn | That price was the stated reason | Whether a different price would have kept the account | Pricing owner (`pricing-and-value-proposition`); payer prices are Market Access |
| Champion or sponsor left | Champions drive implementation and overcome resistance (CS8, CS46) | That a named role changed | That the change caused the loss | The person's account owner |
| Budget or funding ended | Outer-setting financing, for example grants or reimbursement, is a published determinant (CS16); a funding decision is complex when several unlinked organizations must agree (CS53) | That external funding was the stated reason | Whether coverage could have been secured | Market Access (`coverage-pathway-and-access-strategy`) where reimbursement is involved |
| Payment depends on outcomes not met | Outcomes-based payment terms (CP-US-11 to CP-US-14) | That a contract term was triggered | Whether the outcome measure was right | Market Access (`outcomes-contract-monitoring`) |
| Pilot ended, never annual | None | That the contract type ended as written | Anything about renewal, if the person excludes pilots (SR14) | The person's definition (A1) |
| No reason recorded | None | Nothing | Anything | Ask; write "reason not recorded `[GAP]`" |

## Proposed repairs that become offer elements

A repair such as a credit, a free module, an extended trial, a rebate for volume or a benefit to patients is an offer element. Before it is listed as anything but "proposed", run it through `offer-rules.md` and stop for the compliance or legal owner. The model never says the repair is protected.

## Hypotheses

Where the person asks why, write at most three hypotheses per reason, each labeled "hypothesis", with the record that would confirm it (for example the go-live date in the implementation plan, the commissioning file for an agreed usage profile, the written price objection).
