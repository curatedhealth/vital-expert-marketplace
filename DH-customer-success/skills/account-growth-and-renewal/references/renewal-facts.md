# Facts that bear on a renewal or a non-renewal

Interpretive reference for A1 and B1. It lists the sourced facts that change what a renewal conversation has to cover. It sets no renewal rule: horizon, readiness, risk and owners are the person's. Rows are in `customer-success-research.md` (CS), `channels-partners-launch-research.md` (CP-) and `promotion-and-claims-research.md` (PC-).

## 1. What the account agreed to see

| Fact | Rows | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| NICE's evidence framework asks company and evaluator to agree a plan to report ongoing usage against an expected usage profile agreed at commissioning, plus anonymized aggregate outcomes. | CS61 | If the account is an NHS commissioner and a usage profile was agreed, ask for it: it is the account's own yardstick for "value seen". If none was agreed, write "usage profile not agreed `[GAP]`". |
| For products that change over time, the same standard asks for an agreement on how and when performance changes are reported and to whom. | CS62 | Ask whether that agreement exists before a renewal that adds a new version or module. |
| Implementation outcomes (acceptability, adoption, appropriateness, feasibility, fidelity, cost, penetration, sustainability) are preconditions for clinical or service outcomes. | CS31, CS32 [PAPER] (Proctor 2011, DOI 10.1007/s10488-010-0319-7) | A renewal risk reason "value unseen" may sit on an implementation that has not happened yet. Keep the two apart in the note. |
| Penetration has two published calculations: eligible users who use the service over all eligible, and providers delivering over providers trained or expected to deliver. Reach is counted on a valid denominator, not on volunteers. | CS39, CS27 | When the account's usage figure is quoted, ask which denominator it uses. |
| At setting level, maintenance means the program is still running 6 or more months after study funding or support ends; sustainability means it is part of ongoing, stable operations. | CS30, CS26, CS40 | Definitions, not thresholds. A person may choose one as a readiness signal; label it theirs. |

Outcomes the account agreed to see are tracked in the Customer Health skill (`outcome-tracking-and-customer-health`, DH-02.02.3.1). Where payment depends on outcomes, the contract is Market Access's (`outcomes-based-contracting`, `outcomes-contract-monitoring`).

## 2. Data duties when a contract renews, changes or ends

| Fact | Rows | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| A covered entity may let a vendor handle PHI only under a written contract meeting 164.504(e). | CS96, CP-US-44 | A renewal that adds a module, site or data flow is a point to check that the BAA covers the new use. |
| The BAA sets permitted uses; requires safeguards, reporting of non-permitted uses and breaches, flow-down to subcontractors, support for patient rights; lets the covered entity terminate for material breach; and at termination the vendor returns or destroys PHI where feasible. | CS97 to CS101, CP-US-45 to CP-US-48 | For a non-renewal, the return or destroy duty (CS100) is the step the plan must name, with the owner the person gives. |
| A business associate notifies a breach without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 calendar days after discovery. | CS102, CP-US-49 | A fact for the contract file, not a renewal lever. |
| Minimum necessary and role-based access apply; recurring feeds need standard protocols. | CS103 to CS105 | A renewal that adds roles or feeds changes these settings. |
| GDPR Article 28: a written processor contract (electronic allowed) with set clauses, including delete or return of all personal data at the end of the service, at the controller's choice; sub-processor changes need authorization; a processor that decides purposes itself (for example reusing hospital data to train a product) is a controller for that processing. | CS106 to CS110, CP-EU-01 to CP-EU-07 | For an EU account that does not renew, the delete-or-return choice is the controller's. A growth offer that reuses account data for product training raises CP-EU-07: stop for the data protection owner. |

## 3. Market-specific facts

| Market | Fact | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| Germany (DiGA) | Before use, users get information on functions, intended purpose and contract terms; the DiGAV checklist bars opaque offers such as auto-renewing subscriptions or time-limited special offers. | PC-DE-17 |
| Germany (DiGA) | After the first year of listing, the amount negotiated with the GKV-Spitzenverband applies. That price is Market Access's subject, not a renewal term. | CP-DE-08, CP-DE-09 |
| Germany (DiGA) | Contract physicians may not steer prescriptions to particular providers or make allocation deals with DiGA manufacturers; DiGA manufacturers may not make agreements with drug or aid makers that restrict patient choice or therapeutic freedom. | CP-DE-06, CP-DE-07 |
| England (NHS) | Buyers review the supplier's completed DTAC during procurement. NHS England refreshed the form in 2026; the old form was retired with full transition by 6 April 2026. | CP-UK-01, CP-UK-04 (CS69 [WF]) |
| England (NHS) | Procurement frameworks usually last up to four years and new suppliers cannot join an agreed framework, except a Dynamic Purchasing System. | CP-UK-12 |
| United States | Where the customer bills Medicare for digital mental health treatment devices, G0552 requires that the billing practitioner incurs the device cost; it is not payable if the practitioner bears no cost. A free device at renewal can therefore remove the customer's billing basis. | CP-US-59 |

## Not sourced

- Renewal notice periods, auto-renewal terms and price escalators in B2B contracts: no row. They are the contract's and the person's.
- Whether an NHS renewal must run through a new procurement or a framework call-off: no row beyond CP-UK-12 `[GAP]`.
- Any figure for typical renewal or churn: none exists in the sources (`gap-rules.md`).
