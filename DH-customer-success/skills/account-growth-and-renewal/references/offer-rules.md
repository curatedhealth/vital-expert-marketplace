# What a renewal or expansion offer is tested against

Interpretive reference for A2 and B1. A renewal or growth offer often carries more than a price: a discount, a free module or site, free services, a device or app for patients, a fee to a champion or reseller, a guarantee tied to results. Each of these is tested against a different rule. This file lays out the elements by offer element, with rows. It never says an arrangement is protected: protection needs every condition met precisely (CP-US-07), and that judgment belongs to the person's compliance or legal owner. Rows: `channels-partners-launch-research.md` (CP-), `discount-rule-additions.md` (FA), `promotion-and-claims-research.md` (PC-), `customer-success-research.md` (CS).

## Step 0. Does the US statute reach this offer?

| Fact | Rows |
|---|---|
| It is a felony to knowingly and willfully offer or pay anything of value, in cash or in kind, to induce a purchase, order or recommendation of an item or service payable by a Federal health care program; the receiving side commits the same offense. | CP-US-01, CP-US-02 |
| No knowledge of the statute or specific intent to violate it is needed; OIG applies it where one purpose of the payment is to induce referrals. | CP-US-03, CP-US-08 |
| A claim that includes items or services resulting from a kickback is a false claim; civil money penalties and exclusion also apply. | CP-US-04, CP-US-06 |
| Safe harbor protection applies only when every condition is met; other arrangements are judged case by case. | CP-US-07 |

When stating CP-US-01 in an answer, keep 'knowingly and willfully'; never shorten to 'is a felony'.

Whether the product, or the customer's service built on it, is payable in whole or in part by Medicare, Medicaid or another Federal program is a fact about the customer's billing. The rows do not settle it for any product: write "Federal program payment: as the person's compliance owner determines `[GAP]`". `commercial-dh.map_money_flow` can record user, buyer and payer as the person states them (`tools.md`); it does not decide the question.

## Offer elements

| Offer element | What the rows say | Rows | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price reduction or rebate on the renewal | The statute exempts a properly disclosed discount reflected in costs claimed or charges made. The regulatory safe harbor sets standards per party: a claims-submitting buyer gets the discount at sale or fixed rebate terms in writing at the initial sale; a cost-reporting buyer earns it on the same good within one fiscal year and reports it; the seller reports it on the invoice and tells the buyer of its reporting duties; where the value is unknown at sale, the seller reports the program and documents the calculation later. A reduction for one payer but not Federal programs is not a discount. | CP-US-05, FA2 to FA8, FA11 | Compliance or legal owner; the price itself is the pricing owner's |
| Free or reduced module, site, seat or service to win a renewal or a different purchase | Supplying one good or service free or at a reduced charge to induce the purchase of a different one is outside the discount definition unless both are reimbursed by the same Federal program by the same methodology and fully disclosed. Warranties, services under a services contract and other remuneration are also outside it. [INDUSTRY] AdvaMed says reimbursement and economics help must not be an unlawful inducement, for example free services that remove an HCP overhead cost. | FA10, FA12, PC-US-93 [INDUSTRY] | Compliance or legal owner |
| Free evaluation or trial period of a new module | [INDUSTRY] AdvaMed allows reasonable quantities for evaluation with documented no-charge status, and multi-use products on written terms for a reasonable period with title retained and prompt removal. MedTech Europe: samples and demonstration products may be free for evaluation, must not improperly induce purchase, with the no-charge basis recorded in writing. | PC-US-94 [INDUSTRY], PC-EU-13, PC-EU-15 [INDUSTRY] | Compliance or legal owner; say these are codes, not law |
| Donated EHR software or cybersecurity technology | Donated EHR software must be interoperable when provided (ONC certification is deemed interoperable), with the recipient paying 15 percent of the donor's cost in advance under a signed agreement. Donated cybersecurity technology needs no contribution but must not be tied to referrals or made a condition of doing business, with a signed written description. | CP-US-29 to CP-US-31 | Compliance or legal owner |
| Anything that reaches patients (devices, phones, gift cards, waived fees, engagement tools) | Offering anything of value to a Medicare or state-program beneficiary likely to influence their choice of provider, practitioner or supplier carries civil money penalties; exceptions exist for items that promote access to care at low risk, for retail rewards offered to the general public on equal terms, and for need-based items under set conditions. A device maker may give patient engagement tools only if they are digital health technology, in kind, recommended by the patient's licensed professional, not used for marketing or recruitment, and within the annual cap, which OIG states is $623 for calendar year 2026. OIG approved one loaned limited-function smartphone program under need-based limits; an advisory opinion protects only its requestor. | CP-US-34 to CP-US-38, CP-US-25 to CP-US-28, CP-US-39 to CP-US-41, CP-US-43 | Compliance or legal owner |
| Device given free where the customer bills DMHT codes | G0552 is not payable if the billing practitioner bears no device cost or the patient gets the device independently. | CP-US-59 | Compliance owner and the customer's billing owner; Market Access for coding |
| Fee, credit or reward to a champion, clinician, reseller or referral partner | Payments to an agent are protected only under a signed written agreement covering all services, for at least one year, with compensation set in advance at fair market value and not tied to the volume or value of referrals, and services not beyond commercial need. [INDUSTRY] AdvaMed and MedTech Europe: consultants only for a legitimate need set in advance, at fair market value, never as a reward for past or inducement for future purchases. OIG found fees paid to receive referrals through referral software would be prohibited remuneration if intent were present. | CP-US-09, CP-US-10, PC-US-89, PC-US-90, PC-EU-16, PC-EU-17 [INDUSTRY], CP-US-33 | Compliance or legal owner |
| Price or payment tied to outcomes, or a results guarantee | Outcomes-based payments to an agent are protected only with outcome measures chosen on clinical evidence, written terms, at least one year, and monitoring; payments by device or medical supply manufacturers are excluded from that protection, as are payments based only on internal cost savings or patient satisfaction. Device makers also cannot use the substantial-downside-risk or full-risk safe harbors. FDA does not regulate the terms of value-based contracts with payors; only the economic information communicated is in scope. | CP-US-11 to CP-US-14, CP-US-23, PC-US-42 | Compliance or legal owner; the contract design is Market Access's (`outcomes-based-contracting`) |
| Germany: gifts and benefits with an offer | Offering or granting gifts and promotional benefits in device advertising is prohibited except listed cases (low-value marked items, customary accessories, information or advice). DiGA: no prescription steering deals, no restrictive agreements with drug or aid makers, no opaque or auto-renewing user offers. | PC-DE-06, CP-DE-06, CP-DE-07, PC-DE-17 | Compliance or legal owner |
| England: NHS buyers | No sourced rule on commercial discounts to NHS buyers. Frameworks and DTAC are procurement facts (CP-UK-01, CP-UK-04, CP-UK-12). | `[GAP]` | Compliance or legal owner |

## How to write it up

For each offer element the person names: the element in their words, the rows that apply, the elements the arrangement would need to show (as the row states them), the facts the person has given against each element, and the facts still missing. End with "Stops for: [the compliance or legal owner the person named, or 'owner not named']". Never write "compliant", "protected", "safe harbor met" or "low risk".
