# Output format: implementation scope and blueprint

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the scope in three lines: sites; modules and user groups in the first release; the day-one workflow. Then what is missing to finish the blueprint.
2. **Not in scope:** modules or integrations sold but not agreed by the site, and those planned for later.
3. **Measures:** the ones the person picked, each with its named definition and row; targets only if the person gave them.
4. **What the site needs from us:** the NICE ESF deployment items marked in hand, to produce, or [GAP] (one line each).
5. **Gaps and open questions:** at most three.
6. **Record:** "Scope draft on your facts. Cleared by NAME; record built with verify.build_record." Until a named person clears it: "Scope draft on your facts. Uncleared draft." AI-assisted. Refusals carry the same line.
7. **Papers:** one line, each [PAPER] paper used once with its DOI (not counted toward the 450 words); omit if none was used.
8. One line offering the full version.
