# Output format: go-live bar and rollout decision

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the bar for the site in one table (item, evidence that shows it, source or "yours"), the pass rule, the site approver; or what is missing to set them.
2. **Market items:** the items a row requires for this market (data agreement, NHS deploying-organization assessment).
3. **On a miss:** owner per item in the person's words, and what happens (in the person's words).
4. **Rollout ranking (if scored):** the ranking of the person's scores, values, and the weight change that flips the top two.
5. **Stops:** issuing the go-live plan to the customer; any statement that the site is live or ready.
6. **Record:** "Go-live bar draft. Cleared by NAME; record built with verify.build_record." Until a named person clears it: "Go-live bar draft. Uncleared draft." AI-assisted. Refusals carry the same line.
7. **Papers:** one line, each [PAPER] paper used once with its DOI (not counted toward the 450 words); omit if none was used.
8. One line offering the full version.
