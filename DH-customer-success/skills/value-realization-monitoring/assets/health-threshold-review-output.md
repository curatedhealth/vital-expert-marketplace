# Output format: health threshold review

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** targets no account has met, signals missing data (overall or by account), shares not comparable, and target changes that turned accounts to met, with the numbers.
2. **Frame:** periods, current and previous target versions with dates and who set them.
3. **Signals (compact):** Signal · Your target · Met per period (of measured) · Flag.
4. **Target changes:** Signal · Old · New · Eased or tightened · Accounts turned to met. "Whether the change stands is your decision." When a change turned accounts to met, give each turned account's last value before the change (for example Northfield activation 41.0% at 30 June 2026 against the new 40%) and cite, once, the register rule (V, not law): "Do not lower a threshold only to turn accounts green".
5. **Open questions (at most three):** reasons recorded for changes, stored fields, missing reports.
6. **Record:** "Threshold review on data you supplied." Name who clears it: "Cleared by NAME; record built with `verify.build_record`." Until a named person clears it: "Uncleared draft." This line also closes every refusal.
7. One line offering the full version.
