## Changes and conflicts

1. **EU AI Act Article 10(5) moved.** The Digital Omnibus deletes Article 10(5) and inserts Article 4a with the same six conditions, extended to deployers of high-risk systems and to other AI systems (DA rows in section 3). Older guidance citing "Article 10(5)" now points to Article 4a(1). The omnibus recital says the Article 4a legal basis "should apply from the date of entry into application of that Regulation"; which regulation "that" refers to is not stated in the operative text read here, so the exact start date of Article 4a is an open reading point.
2. **Article 72(3) template.** Original: implementing act with a template by 2 February 2026. Amended: Commission guidance with a template by 2 September 2027. The omnibus recital calls the template "voluntary".
3. **Articles 10 to 15 dates.** As adopted, 2 August 2026 (Annex III) and 2 August 2027 (Annex I). As amended, 2 December 2027 and 2 August 2028 (research_reg2 EU-19).
4. **FDA AI lifecycle guidance is still a draft** (January 2025). Its footnote anticipates the QMSR from 2 February 2026, replacing "risk analysis" with "risk management", while the body still cites QSR section numbers (820.30, 820.100). Section numbers in the final version may change.
5. **"Validation" means different things.** EMA uses AI/ML "validation" for tuning data and the hold-out "test" set for final performance; CLAIM 2024 discourages "validation" in favor of "internal testing" and "external testing"; FDA uses "tuning" and "test" for development and "validation" for performance validation; NIST uses the ISO 9000 sense. Skills should name the data subset, not rely on the word.
6. **PROBAST-2019 superseded** by PROBAST+AI (2025). Reviews using the 2019 tool should say so.
7. **Fairness criteria conflict.** Calibration and equal error rates cannot both hold across groups with different outcome rates (Paulus and Kent). A skill must ask which criterion fits the decision rather than report all as pass or fail.
8. **WHO LMM guidance date.** The news release is dated 18 January 2024; the WHO publication page now shows 25 March 2025. Whether the 2025 version changed content was not checked.
9. **OMOP CDM version.** OHDSI names v5.5 as current; the field-level rows here come from the v5.4 specification page.
10. **USCDI.** v7 is published (23 July 2026), but 45 CFR 170.213 as read adopts only v1 (expired 1 January 2026) and v3. Published versions are not certification requirements until adopted by rule. ONC also applies enforcement discretion to some v3 elements.
11. **FDA Bayesian.** Devices: final 2010 guidance. Drugs and biologics: separate draft of January 2026 (FDA-2025-D-3217). The two should not be cross-applied.
