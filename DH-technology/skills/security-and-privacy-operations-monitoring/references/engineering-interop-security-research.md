## Method

Downloaded with curl through the configured proxy into `research_en/raw/`; PDFs converted with pdftotext (Federal Register PDFs in reading order, FDA and NIST PDFs in layout mode); HTML converted with a local tag stripper. EUR-Lex texts fetched as XHTML from publications.europa.eu (CRA, NIS2); GDPR text reused from research_dl raw; EHDS text reused from research_sp raw; 45 CFR 170.315 and all other eCFR sections re-downloaded on the read date. HL7 version facts come from each specification's official `package-list.json`. Every non-[WF] quote was checked by script against the raw text after normalizing whitespace, curly quotes and ligatures (result at the end of this file). Existing research files were read first and are cited, not repeated (section "Already sourced elsewhere").

## Changes and conflicts

- CSA: issued final 24 September 2025, reissued 3 February 2026 under the title "Production and Quality Management System Software". The GPSV cover note still names the September 2025 title. The FDA TIR45 recognition entry still lists CSA as a "Draft ... September 2022". Use the 3 February 2026 version.
- Premarket cybersecurity guidance: September 2023 final, then 27 June 2025, now 3 February 2026 ("Quality Management System Considerations"). FDA recognition entries for TIR57, TIR97 and IEC 81001-5-1 already cite the February 2026 issue.
- The postmarket cybersecurity guidance (2016) predates section 524B (2022). Its 30 and 60 day timelines are an FDA enforcement policy for 21 CFR 806 reporting, not statutory deadlines. The 524B text says "reasonable time" and "as soon as possible out of cycle".
- GPSV, OTS and the postmarket guidance carry QMSR cover notes: the guidances predate the QMSR (effective 2 February 2026), and their 21 CFR 820 citations refer to the old QS regulation.
- FHIR: R5 is HL7's current release, but US regulation (ONC 170.215, CMS-0057-F) is pinned to R4.0.1. ONC also pins older versions of US Core (6.1.0 against current 9.0.0), SMART (2.0.0 against 2.2.0) and Bulk Data (1.0.0 against 3.0.0). Certification follows the regulation, not the latest HL7 publication.
- USCDI: CMS-0057-F ties payer API content to 170.213, so the content moves when ONC adopts new versions. As read, 170.213 holds only v3 (v1 expired 1 January 2026). HTI-2's proposed new baseline was withdrawn on 29 December 2025.
- HIPAA Security Rule NPRM: proposed 6 January 2025. The 2026 Unified Agenda moved it to Long-Term Actions with final action 07/00/2027. The current rule (with addressable specifications) remains in force.
- HTI-5 proposes removing parts of (b)(11), (g)(3) and (g)(5) (research_ux). It was still at the proposed stage on the read date.
- 42 CFR Part 2: effective 16 April 2024, compliance by 16 February 2026. Both dates have passed, so the new consent and breach rules apply now.
- CRA vs MDR: devices under the MDR or IVDR are excluded from the CRA. Health wearables outside the MDR are CRA Annex III Class I important products. Article 14 reporting has applied since 11 September 2026. Products placed on the market before 11 December 2027 are also subject to Article 14.
- NIS2 Annex II covers MDR and IVDR manufacturers as "other critical sectors", and Annex I covers only devices on the public health emergency critical list. Whether an entity is in scope also depends on size thresholds and national transposition, which were not read here.
- EHDS (Regulation 2025/327): research_sp holds the phased dates. The exchange-format implementing act is due by 26 March 2027 (EN-165).
