# Co-design method: what the sources say

Interpretive reference for A1 and A2. Every rule cites a row in `patient-engagement-research.md` (PE-) or `ux-research.md` (UX-). Rows marked [PAPER] are published methods, not law. R- rows are the register's starting rules from the earlier skill version; they are method choices the person may adopt, not sourced requirements.

## 1. The research question

| Element | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Decision served | Write the decision the question serves before anything else. | R-1 |
| Participants | Name who takes part (patient, carer, or both) and why. | R-2 |
| Non-goal | Name what the session will not decide. | R-3 |
| Order | Do not recruit before the question is written. | R-4 |
| Experts | FDA recommends engaging early with patients and with qualitative researchers, survey methodologists, statisticians, psychometricians and preference researchers. | PE-08 (FDA PFDD Guidance 2, final February 2022, PE-07) |
| Meaningful aspect of health | Where the question is about what matters to patients in daily life, FDA's frame is a meaningful aspect of health: some aspect of feeling or functioning in daily life that is important to patients. | PE-14 (PFDD Guidance 3, final October 2025, PE-10) |

The PFDD guidances are written for drug and biologic development; Guidance 3 is also issued by CDRH (PE-10). Use them as FDA's stated methods for hearing patients, not as a requirement for a co-design workshop. Say so when citing them.

## 2. What counts as patient experience data

- Data collected by anyone (patients, caregivers, advocacy groups, researchers, manufacturers) about patients' experience of a disease, including impacts and treatment preferences (PE-04).
- FDA lists patient focus group or meeting reports and patient surveys among its sources (PE-05). A co-design session report can therefore be patient experience data in FDA's sense; whether it is used in a submission is the regulatory owner's decision.

## 3. Who takes part, and how many

| Point | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Representativeness | Probability sampling makes results more likely to reflect the target population; non-probability samples may not be representative, and the choice must be weighed when interpreting the data. Co-design recruitment is almost always non-probability: say so in the plan. | PE-06 |
| Saturation | Saturation is the point when no new relevant information emerges; FDA says there are no set criteria for evaluating it. Do not promise a sample size "for saturation". | PE-09 |
| Inclusive opportunities | UK Standard: involvement partnerships are accessible and include a range of people and groups, as informed by community and research needs. A framework, not a mandated rule. | PE-65, PE-66 |
| Carers and observers | An observer report comes from someone other than the patient or a health professional, such as a parent or caregiver. Keep carer words apart from patient words. | PE-13 |
| Staff | Do not treat staff standing in for patients as patient co-design. | R-5 |
| Sample size | No source in this skill sets a number of participants for co-design `[GAP]`. FDA's human factors sample figures (UX-M-01) are for usability problem discovery, not co-design; do not borrow them. | UX-M-01 to UX-M-04 |

## 4. When the question is about preferences

- FDA's final guidance "Incorporating Voluntary Patient Preference Information over the Total Product Life Cycle" was issued 30 March 2026 and supersedes the 2016 guidance (PE-22).
- PPI is a qualitative or quantitative assessment of how desirable or acceptable patients find alternatives or outcomes (PE-23). It is voluntary (PE-24) and most useful when decisions are preference sensitive (PE-25). It differs from a PRO (PE-26).
- FDA's listed study qualities include a representative study population, heterogeneity of preferences, and comprehension with minimal cognitive bias (PE-27).
- Consequence: a co-design session that asks "which would you rather" can inform design, but calling it patient preference information for FDA is a regulatory decision. It stops for the person's regulatory owner.

## 5. When an insight may become a measure

- FDA recognizes four clinical outcome assessment types: PRO, ObsRO, ClinRO, PerfO (PE-11). A COA proposal should name the meaningful aspect of health, the concept of interest and the context of use (PE-15).
- Guidance 4 (endpoints) is a draft dated April 2023, for comment only (PE-17).
- Consequence: if a co-design point turns into "we should measure X", hand it to the person's clinical or evidence owner. Co-design capture is not instrument development. COSMIN content validity (PE-93, [PAPER]) applies to instruments, not to session notes.

## 6. Patient input to EU HTA and NICE

| Point | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| EU HTA Regulation | Applies from 12 January 2025. Joint clinical assessment scoping takes into account input from patients (Article 8); patients may comment on draft reports (Article 11) and on joint scientific consultations (Article 18). | PE-52 to PE-55 |
| Conflicts | Patients in EU joint work must have no financial or other interests in the developer's industrial sector that could affect impartiality and must declare interests (Article 5); they act in an individual capacity (recital). | PE-56, PE-57 |
| Consequence | A patient who joins your co-design panel and is paid by you may later be unable to serve as an independent patient expert in EU joint work on your product. State this when planning; the decision is the person's. | PE-56 (reading of the article, not an official statement) |
| NICE | NICE committees have at least two lay members; patients can comment through their organizations on draft scopes and recommendations. | PE-61, PE-64 |
| NICE patient expert submissions | Rules in the NICE technology evaluation manual were not fetched `[GAP]`. | Not sourced |

## 7. Capture and reporting

| Point | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Words | Record claims in participants' words. | R-6 |
| Request vs observation | Separate a request from an observation. | R-7 |
| Count | Note how many participants raised each point. | R-8 |
| No product requirement in capture | Do not replace their words with a product requirement in the capture. | R-9 |
| Reporting | GRIPP2 is international guidance for reporting patient and public involvement: GRIPP2-SF has five items (aims, methods, results, outcomes, critical perspective) for work where involvement is secondary; GRIPP2-LF has 34 items where it is the main focus. [PAPER] doi:10.1136/bmj.j3453 | PE-89 to PE-91 |
| Impact | UK Standard: identify and share the difference involvement makes. | PE-68 |

## 8. Engagement data brought into a session

If the team brings usage data to a session (for example, people stop using the app), use the sourced definitions: dropout attrition versus nonusage attrition (PE-102, [PAPER] doi:10.2196/jmir.7.1.e11); engagement as extent of use plus subjective experience (PE-105, [PAPER] doi:10.1007/s13142-016-0453-1). Do not compute engagement KPIs here; that is the engagement KPI skill.
