# Output format: session capture and insight log

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the points that meet your repeated-point rule, each with distinct patient-side participants and sessions; if no rule, the counts with no label and the offer of R-10.
2. **Kept apart:** staff or clinician points, carer points, captures outside the question.
3. **How far it reaches:** one line: these participants, non-probability recruitment (PE-06).
4. **Identifiers:** "None found by the check" or the stop.
5. **Record:** "Capture draft on your notes. AI-assisted." Then: "Cleared by [name]; record built with verify.build_record." or "Uncleared draft; a named person clears it, then the record is built with verify.build_record."
6. One line offering the full version (the capture table).
