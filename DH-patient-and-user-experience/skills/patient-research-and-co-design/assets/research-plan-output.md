# Output format: research question and plan

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the decision served, the question, who takes part and why, the non-goal (three to four lines).
2. **Recruitment and what it means:** one or two lines, with PE-06; no sample size promised (PE-09).
3. **Settle before recruiting:** the ethics, consent, conflict and payment questions that apply, each with its row and the owner as the person named them, or "owner not named". For interviews, workshops or surveys, name both exemptions the owner will weigh: (d)(2) (UX-M-14) and (d)(3) (UX-M-15, UX-M-16), with recording noted. Never say either applies.
4. **Comparables (if searched):** up to three published co-design studies by title, year and DOI, design as titled.
5. **Gaps:** what the sources do not answer.
6. **Record:** "Plan draft. The question and participants are yours. AI-assisted." Then: "Cleared by [name]; record built with verify.build_record." or "Uncleared draft; a named person clears it, then the record is built with verify.build_record."
7. One line offering the full version.
