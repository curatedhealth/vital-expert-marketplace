# Output format: equity evidence definition

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the definition in one sentence ("Reached = RECORD among eligible DENOMINATOR in PERIOD"), and the fields not yet recorded.
2. **Definition (compact):** Change and version · Group and field · Record of use · Denominator · Period · Outcome (optional). Each: recorded / in part / not recorded `[GAP]`.
3. **Counting rules:** mid-period entry, "not recorded" reported as its own row, your small-cell rule (or `[GAP]`), your reference group.
4. **Open questions (at most three):** denominator first, then period, then the small-cell rule.
5. **Record:** "Evidence definition draft. Cleared by [the person you name]; the record is built with verify.build_record once that person clears it." Without a name: "uncleared draft."
6. One line offering the full version.
