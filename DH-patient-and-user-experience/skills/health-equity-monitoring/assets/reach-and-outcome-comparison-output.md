# Output format: reach and outcome comparison

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the subgroups with the largest gaps against your reference, in numbers with intervals, and what is hidden or not recorded.
2. **Basis:** period, evidence definition (one line), your reference per dimension, your small-cell rule.
3. **Results (compact, per dimension):** Subgroup · Reached / eligible · Reach (95% interval) · Difference (points) and ratio vs reference · Outcome among reached · Suppressed.
4. **Not recorded and hidden:** share not recorded per dimension; suppressed cells (primary or complementary). No hidden count in prose.
5. **Open questions (at most three):** your rules for B1 first.
6. **Record:** "Comparison on your aggregate counts. No verdict. Cleared by [the person you name]; the record is built with verify.build_record once that person clears it." Without a name: "uncleared draft."
7. One line offering the full version.
