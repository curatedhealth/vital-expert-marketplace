# Output format: equity review and follow-up

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** which subgroups miss your rules (with the numbers), the leading hypothesis to test for each, the owner and the next period.
2. **Rules (yours) and results (compact):** Rule · Number · Met / missed / not measurable `[GAP]`.
3. **Hypotheses:** per miss, change or measurement, each with what would confirm it. Labeled hypotheses.
4. **Follow-up:** option (proposed or yours) · Owner (yours, or "owner not named") · Next period (yours, or "not set").
5. **Stops:** what stops for the accountable owner.
6. **Record:** "Equity review draft. Follow-up open. Cleared by [the person you name]; the record is built with verify.build_record once that person clears it." Without a name: "uncleared draft."
7. One line offering the full version.
