# Output format: advocacy partnership terms (boundary note and term sheet draft)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap and end with the same record line: who clears it, and that the record is built with `verify.build_record` once a named person clears it; otherwise "uncleared draft".

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the boundary note (role, payment, approver of patient-facing words), what is not stated, and the rule set that applies ([INDUSTRY] or law).
2. **Agreement must hold:** the elements under the code that binds you, each with its row.
3. **Independence and disclosure:** at most four lines, each with its row.
4. **Stops:** agreement and amounts to your legal owner; payments that could relate to referrals and any support to patients to your compliance owner; owners as you named them, or "owner not named".
5. **Record:** "Partnership term sheet draft. Terms and amounts are yours. Clears: [person or role as you named them, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft."
6. One line offering the full version.
