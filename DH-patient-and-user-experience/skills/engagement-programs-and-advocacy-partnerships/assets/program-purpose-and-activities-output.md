# Output format: program purpose and activities (charter draft)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap and end with the same record line: who clears it, and that the record is built with `verify.build_record` once a named person clears it; otherwise "uncleared draft".

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the purpose in one line (audience, action asked, action not asked, partner role), activities kept and dropped, and how many sentences of the material are flagged for your review owner.
2. **Activities (compact):** Activity · Serves the purpose? · Content it may use · Approver. Support items marked "B1".
3. **Flags for the owner:** at most five, each: sentence, rule, row.
4. **Data rule:** one line, conditional on your status.
5. **Stops:** patient-facing wording to your review owner; support to B1; owner as you named them, or "owner not named".
6. **Record:** "Program charter draft. Purpose, activities and claims list are yours. Clears: [person or role as you named them, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft."
7. One line offering the full version.
