# Output format: release decision

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

1. **Answer:** what your rule says on these facts (for example "your rule says block: four open in-target issues on day-one tasks"), and the facts that drive it. Never "ship" or "block" as decided.
2. **Rule:** yours, quoted, or the register starter rule as you adopted it.
3. **Clause by clause (compact):** Clause · Facts it reads · What it says.
4. **Exclusions:** each with your basis and row, or "no sourced basis: your choice".
5. **Ranked repairs (only if asked, from your scores):** order, RICE score, day-one status.
6. **Owner and stops:** the owner you named or "owner not named"; the decision, any published claim and any burden determination stop for them.
7. **Record:** "Release decision draft. Decision: ______ (by the owner). AI-assisted. To be cleared by NAME; the record is built with verify.build_record once NAME clears it." Until a named person clears it: "Uncleared draft." Who clears it: the owner the person named, in their words, or "owner not named". The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then the line reads "uncleared draft".
8. One line offering the full version.
