# WCAG criteria table: additions from the same raw downloads

Rows WX1 to WX9, added in the build of 19 October 2026 from files the research pass already saved (`research_ux/raw/wcag22.txt`, `wcag21.txt`, `nhs_acctest.txt`, `uswds_access.txt`). No new source was fetched. Quotes were checked by script against the normalized raw text (9 of 9 rows, 10 of 10 quote parts found). They back `references/data/wcag_criteria.csv`, which lists every success criterion with its level in WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2. Not owner-reviewed.

How the table was built: the success-criterion headings and their "(Level ...)" markers were extracted from the W3C Recommendation pages for WCAG 2.2 (https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) and WCAG 2.1 (the page saved in `wcag21.html` is the republished Recommendation of 6 May 2025, "This version: https://www.w3.org/TR/2025/REC-WCAG21-20250506/"; ADA Title II and HHS 504 incorporate the 5 June 2018 text, UX-US-10, so any difference between those two dated texts is `[GAP]`). The WCAG 2.0 column is derived, not read: it is the 2.1 list minus the 17 criteria W3C lists as new in 2.1, at their 2.1 levels. The WCAG 2.0 text itself was not downloaded.
