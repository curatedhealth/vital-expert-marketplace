# Output format: design decision and evaluation plan

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the order your scores produce, how close the top two are, the weight change that would flip them, and the first formative test.
2. **Options and facts (compact):** Option · Key facts per criterion (rows) · Consequence in one line.
3. **Your scores and the ranking:** Option · Value · Largest contributions. "Scores and weights are yours; this is not a recommendation."
4. **First formative test or service pilot:** tasks or service steps, populations, what it decides. A service pilot is labeled "formative; method, not a sourced rule"; deployment pilots go to customer-implementation-planning.
5. **For the regulatory owner:** the decision-point facts (UI change, critical tasks new or impacted, history of use), no category chosen.
6. **Stops:** decision owner, regulatory owner, risk owner, in the person's words or "owner not named".
7. **Record:** "Design decision draft. Uncleared until a named person clears it through verify.build_record. AI-assisted."
8. One line offering the full version.
