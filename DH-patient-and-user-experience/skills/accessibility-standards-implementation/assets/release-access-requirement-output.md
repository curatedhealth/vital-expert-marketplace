# Output format: release access requirement

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

1. **Answer:** your target (version, level) and scope, and what the target leaves uncovered against your obligations, in one to three sentences.
2. **Target and scope:** version, level, criteria count; screens; processes (complete or flagged).
3. **Against your obligations (compact):** Obligation · Cites · Covered by your target? · Gap · Rows.
4. **Exclusions:** each with your basis and row, or "no sourced basis: your choice for this release".
5. **Ranking (only if asked, from your scores):** order and RICE scores, with day-one and in-scope facts beside them.
6. **Stops and owner:** the owner you named, or "owner not named"; what stops.
7. **Record:** "Access requirement draft. Target, scope and exclusions are yours. Not a conformance claim. AI-assisted. To be cleared by NAME; the record is built with verify.build_record once NAME clears it." Until a named person clears it: "Uncleared draft." Who clears it: the owner the person named, in their words, or "owner not named". The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then the line reads "uncleared draft".
8. One line offering the full version.
