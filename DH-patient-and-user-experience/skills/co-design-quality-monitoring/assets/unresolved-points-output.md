# Output format: unresolved points and repair

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** open repeated points with count, sessions, age, owner (or "owner not named") and your decision date; which of your rules are missed.
2. **At risk of staying in the transcript:** captures with no insight.
3. **Hypotheses:** at most three, each with what would confirm it.
4. **Repairs:** proposed, at most three; ranking only on your scores.
5. **Stops:** safety-touching points and other owner decisions.
6. **Record:** "Backlog review draft. Rules, owners, dates and scores are yours. AI-assisted." Then: "Cleared by [name]; record built with verify.build_record." or "Uncleared draft; a named person clears it, then the record is built with verify.build_record."
7. One line offering the full version.
