# Output format: participant fit and reporting

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** sessions that missed a planned participant type or included staff, and the decisions that rest on them.
2. **Staff-only or carer-only points:** insight ids.
3. **How far results reach:** one line (PE-06); any saturation claim and its basis (PE-09).
4. **Consent recorded:** per session, as you stated it.
5. **Reporting (if a report is shared):** GRIPP2-SF items present, partial or [GAP].
6. **Record:** "Fit review draft on your records. Whether each decision stands is your owner's call. AI-assisted." Then: "Cleared by [name]; record built with verify.build_record." or "Uncleared draft; a named person clears it, then the record is built with verify.build_record."
7. One line offering the full version.
