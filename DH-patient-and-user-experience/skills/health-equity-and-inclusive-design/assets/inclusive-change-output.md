# Output format: inclusive change

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the change you chose (or the top option on your scores), the group, the version, the owner, and the one decision still open.
2. **Group, step, barrier:** one line, from A1.
3. **Options (compact):** Option · Basis (row or "your evidence") · Your score total (value from the tool) · Note.
4. **Ranking note:** which criterion separates close options; "a different weight could change the order" where it could.
5. **Evidence definition for monitoring:** change, group, record of use, denominator, first period.
6. **Stops:** what stops for the accountable owner.
7. **Record:** "Inclusive change draft on your scores. Cleared by [the person you name]; the record is built with verify.build_record once that person clears it." Without a name: "uncleared draft."
8. One line offering the full version.
