# Output format: partner for the gap

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** each unseen group with its partner (or "partner not named") and the one question to ask.
2. **Requests (compact):** Group · Kind of holder · Partner (yours) · Question · Aggregate data requested (period, your subgroup definitions, your small-cell rule).
3. **Relationship rules:** only those that apply to the relationship type, with rows; [INDUSTRY] labeled; any payment or free item written as a stop for your compliance or legal owner.
4. **Open questions (at most three):** partner names first, then relationship type, then the small-cell rule.
5. **Record:** "Partner draft. Aggregate requests only. Cleared by [the person you name]; the record is built with verify.build_record once that person clears it." Without a name: "uncleared draft."
6. One line offering the full version.
