# Output format: component contract

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the component, the target it is written to (yours, or "target not chosen"), the criteria with no evidence yet, and the content it must never invent.
2. **States:** the states you named, and any state a criterion needs that you have not confirmed.
3. **Criteria (compact):** Criterion · Level · Row · Evidence: seen / described / none.
4. **Never invents:** content the component must not generate or alter; who owns any clinical content it shows.
5. **Allowed surfaces:** in your words, clinical and device surfaces flagged.
6. **Open questions (at most three):** target, states, device status first.
7. **Record:** "Contract draft. Component tests are evidence toward page conformance, not a conformance claim. Clears: the person you name. Record built with verify.build_record once they clear it; until then, uncleared draft. AI-assisted."
8. One line offering the full version.
