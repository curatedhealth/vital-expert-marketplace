# Rules for Record dissent

Pack: vital-decision-companion. Method version: 0.3.6. Content pack: 2026-10-26.1

- **R1.** Invite dissent before the choice is recorded, at least once for tradeoff, dig deep, and full process rigor.
- **R2.** Store the holder's words unchanged, with their name.
- **R3.** Never merge, average, or paraphrase two objections into one.
- **R4.** A dissent is withdrawn only by its holder.
- **R5.** No dissent requires a named attesting person. Silence is not attestation.
- **R6.** A fast unanimous agreement on a high-consequence decision triggers one devil's-advocate prompt.
- **R7.** This skill never changes the chosen option.

Hard rules DC-H1 through DC-H8 in `SKILL.md` still apply.
