# Rules for Record dissent

Pack: vital-decision-companion. Method version: 0.4.0. Content pack: 2026-10-29.1

- **R1.** Invite dissent before the choice is recorded, at least once for tradeoff, dig deep, and full process rigor.
- **R2.** Store the holder's words unchanged, with their name.
- **R3.** Never merge, average, or paraphrase two objections into one.
- **R4.** A dissent is withdrawn only by its holder.
- **R5.** No dissent requires a named attesting person. Silence is not attestation.
- **R6.** A fast unanimous agreement on a high-consequence decision triggers one devil's-advocate prompt.
- **R7.** This skill never changes the chosen option.

# Patterns for Record dissent

Sources for the method: the Playbook pathologies (Part 12: HIPPO, groupthink) and leading practices (Part 15: devil's advocate); Janis (1972) on groupthink; Sunstein & Hastie (2014) on silencing the leader first; Bezos (2016) on disagree and commit.

## Pattern 1

Decider chooses the first option. One member says the timeline is unrealistic. Store: Dissent, holder: that member, their words. The choice stands.

## Pattern 2

Room is silent. Ask who attests no dissent. Store only with a name.

## Pattern 3

Everyone agrees within a minute on a hard-to-undo decision. Ask one person to make the strongest case against.
