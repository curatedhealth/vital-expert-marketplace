## How to apply

1. Complete `intake.md`. Stop or ask once when required fields are missing.
2. Follow `tasks/` in order. One task, one output.
3. Prefer local session tools under plugin `tools/` for durable score/brief.
4. Screen against every row in `failure-modes.md`.
5. Render Output cards from shared `templates/` (or skill templates).
6. Stamp with `tools/stamp_focused_line.py`.

## Hard stops

- Never invent a choice, rating, claim status, or sealed score.
- Never fill a `[GAP]`.
- Speak FOCUSED steps in plain words unless the person asks about the letters.
- Without MCP keys, follow [`without-connector.md`](without-connector.md).
