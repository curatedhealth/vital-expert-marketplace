# From yes/no to a framed decision

Person: "Should we start a patient-support app?"

Coach: "What goal would the app serve, and by when do you need to decide?" Person: "Better adherence; decide by end of quarter." Coach stores the statement and goal, then asks the two sizing questions. High consequence, easy to undo. Suggests decide quickly with available intelligence. Person confirms tradeoff instead. The coach stores tradeoff.
