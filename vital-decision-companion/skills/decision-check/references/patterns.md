# Patterns for Decision check

Sources for the method: the Playbook Decision Quality Checklist (Part 20) and Decision Quality Framework (Part 15); Decision Quality's six elements and weakest-link rule (Spetzler, Winter & Meyer 2016).

## Pattern 1

Two options stored, no do-nothing. Options section: 1 of 4 met. Weakest element: alternatives. Point to widen-options.

## Pattern 2

No review trigger stored. Action section notes it as not met. Point to implement-plan.

## Pattern 3

Person asks for a quality score out of 100. Explain there is no validated single score; show the section counts and the weakest element.
