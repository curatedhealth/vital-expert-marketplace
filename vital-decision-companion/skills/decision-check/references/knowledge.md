# Rules for Decision check

Pack: vital-decision-companion. Method version: 0.4.0. Content pack: 2026-10-29.1

- **R1.** Framing: decision stated clearly; scope and constraints defined; deadline set; decider is a named person.
- **R2.** Information: key facts from reliable sources; uncertainties named; stakeholder views collected; disconfirming evidence sought.
- **R3.** Options: at least three; pros and cons for each; trade-offs clear; do nothing considered.
- **R4.** Reasoning: criteria defined before rating; options evaluated against criteria; assumptions named and tested; a devil's-advocate view considered.
- **R5.** Action: the person's choice with their rationale; next steps; owner and timeline; review trigger; communication plan.
- **R6.** An item is met only with stored evidence or the person's answer. Otherwise not known. Never assume met.
- **R7.** Report counts per section. Do not add them into one quality score; there is no validated aggregate.
- **R8.** Name the weakest of the six decision quality elements (frame, alternatives, information, values and trade-offs, reasoning, commitment to action). Overall quality is never an average of them.

Hard rules DC-H1 through DC-H8 in `SKILL.md` still apply.
