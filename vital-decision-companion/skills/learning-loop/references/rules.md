# Rules for Learning loop

Pack: vital-decision-companion. Method version: 0.3.0. Content pack: 2026-10-24.1.

- **R1.** The stage completes only when review_date, success_signal, and authored_by are all present.
- **R2.** authored_by is the person recording the plan. Never ai, model, assistant, or a role title.
- **R3.** Record the expected outcome and the person's probability before the outcome is known. This is the decision-journal rule: a forecast written later is hindsight.
- **R4.** A relative date such as next quarter is stored as said. Do not convert it to a day they did not choose.
- **R5.** At review, show what was expected before asking what happened.
- **R6.** Judge the process and the outcome separately. A bad outcome is not proof of a bad decision, and a good outcome is not proof of a good one. The person places the cell.
- **R7.** Repeated surprises across decisions are a reason to review the process, not to blame luck again.

Hard rules DC-H1 through DC-H8 in `SKILL.md` still apply.
