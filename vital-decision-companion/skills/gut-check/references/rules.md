# Rules for Gut check

Pack: vital-decision-companion. Method version: 0.3.17. Content pack: 2026-10-28.1

- **R1.** The gut check is a turn of its own, before any lean or choice question.
- **R2.** With the connector, the computed view is shown first; without it, the person weighs their own grid.
- **R3.** A mismatch is a question, never a verdict.
- **R4.** A sound instinct that agrees with the person's priorities is not argued with.
- **R5.** The person's reaction and reasons are stored only in their words.
- **R6.** This skill never sets the chosen option.

Hard rules DC-H1 through DC-H8 in `SKILL.md` still apply.
