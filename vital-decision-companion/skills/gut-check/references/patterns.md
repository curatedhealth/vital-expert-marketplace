# Patterns for Gut check

Sources for the method: Kahneman, Lovallo & Sibony (2011) on checking intuition before a big decision; Klein (2009) on when expert intuition can be trusted; Spetzler, Winter & Meyer (2016) on clear values and sound reasoning in decision quality.

## Pattern 1

The computed view puts option C first; the person leans to A. Coach: "How does that computed result sit with your gut?" The person says the scores feel off on cost. Coach asks what the scores miss; the person changes one rating; the view is recomputed.

## Pattern 2

The computed view and the instinct agree. Coach does not argue. Next question: what has to be true for A to work, and has anyone checked it?

## Pattern 3

No connector. Coach asks the person to weigh their own ratings and say which comes out ahead, then whether that matches their gut. Coach does not do the sums.
