# Worked example: a leadership update full of buzzwords

Invented case for teaching. "Tavrelo" is a fictional product; the affiliate, people and
figures are made up.

## Context card (from trusted-intake, already in the conversation)

```
TRUSTED context: quarterly market access update for the affiliate leadership team

Reader          affiliate leadership team, deciding Q4 priorities          (stated)
Market          not needed for this work                                   (assumed)
Product status  not needed for this work                                   (assumed)
Use             internal only                                              (stated)
Deliverable     one-page update, prose                                     (stated)
Material        market access notes, 9 October 2026
Stakes          leadership
Next process    none apparent
```

The card already holds the reader and their job, so nothing is asked again.

## Request

"Tighten this before it goes to the leadership team. It feels long and says nothing."

## Notes supplied

> Tavrelo access, Q3: listed by 4 of 6 cantonal hospital groups (2 new in Q3).
> Canton X payer review still open, concern = year-2 budget impact; asked for updated
> budget impact model. Canton Y decision meeting 12 November.
> Field: 1 of 2 open KAM roles filled.
> Ask for leadership: approve budget for an external health-economics update of the
> model (quote received, CHF 40,000).

## Draft

> In today's rapidly evolving healthcare landscape, market access remains a key
> strategic priority. We continue to leverage synergies across the stakeholder ecosystem
> to drive holistic engagement with payers. Key stakeholders have shown strong interest
> in Tavrelo, and we expect further listings soon. Robust progress has been made on our
> field capabilities. Going forward, we will continue to optimize our approach to payer
> engagement and drive alignment with stakeholders. Overall, market access momentum
> remains strong, and we remain focused on leveraging our strengths.

## Revised text

> Tavrelo is now listed by 4 of 6 cantonal hospital groups, 2 of them new in Q3. The
> main open risk is canton X, where the payer review is still open: the payer is
> concerned about budget impact in year 2 and has asked for an updated budget impact
> model. Canton Y decides at its meeting on 12 November.
>
> One of the two open key account manager roles is filled [GAP: needs a decision —
> plan for the second role — say whether it is still being recruited].
>
> **For decision:** approve CHF 40,000 for an external health-economics update of the
> budget impact model, to answer canton X [GAP: needs a decision — owner and target
> date for sending the model to canton X — name who sends it, by when].

## Short note to the person

Cut the opening and closing paragraphs and five buzzword phrases ("leverage synergies
across the stakeholder ecosystem", "holistic engagement", "robust progress"); none
carried information your notes did not state more plainly. Made four statements
specific from your notes (listings, canton X concern, canton Y date, hiring). Added for
you to confirm: canton X as "the main open risk", the ask placed at the end as a
decision, and the link between the model update and canton X's request (your notes list
them separately). Still needed from you: who sends the model to canton X and by when, and the
plan for the second role.

## Why it is written this way

- Leadership depth (TR-H12): every task that applies ran, with a short note.
- "We expect further listings soon" became the canton Y date from the notes, not a
  forecast of how many listings will follow.
- The ask was in the notes but missing from the draft; actionability moved it into the
  update. The owner and date were not supplied, so they are gaps, not guesses.
- "The main open risk" is the assistant's reading, so it is listed for the person to
  confirm.
- Tone and length of sentences were not judged (TR-H9).

## Lines returned to trusted-check (not shown to the person)

