# Worked example: "make it more concrete" with no data

Invented case for teaching. "Tavrelo" is a fictional product.

## Request

"Make this slide more concrete. Add numbers so it lands with the GM."

> **Brand performance**
> - Tavrelo continues to perform well in a competitive market
> - Uptake is strong among key prescribers
> - Several new accounts expected to come on board soon

No context card in the conversation. No figures, notes or data supplied.

## What the skill does

The reader (the general manager) and the job (a performance slide) are in the request,
so nothing is asked about the reader. The material holds no figures, so none are added.
The request for numbers is met with the places where numbers go and the list of what
to send.

## Reply as the person sees it

> I've marked where the numbers go rather than adding any, because none came with the
> slide and the GM would read them as real.
>
> **Brand performance** [heading: once the figures are in, this can state the point,
> for example how performance compares with plan]
> - Tavrelo net sales [GAP: not supplied — net sales and plan for the period — from
>   your sales report]
> - Uptake among [GAP: not supplied — which prescriber group — name it] [GAP: not
>   supplied — uptake figure and comparison period — from your market data]
> - [GAP: not supplied — number of new accounts expected and by when — from your
>   account plan]
>
> To make it concrete, send me:
> 1. Net sales for the period and the plan figure.
> 2. Which prescribers you mean by "key prescribers", and their uptake now and before.
> 3. How many new accounts you expect, and by when.
>
> I took out "in a competitive market"; it adds nothing unless you want a line on a
> specific competitor.

## Why it is written this way

- TR-H2: the request for numbers is the moment an assistant is most likely to invent
  them. The skill asks, and shows where each would go.
- "Performs well", "strong" and "soon" each hide a missing figure or date; each became
  a gap with the place the figure would come from.
- The heading suggestion does not state a conclusion; it says what the heading could
  say once there is data.
- Internal leadership slide: a short reply, no full repair note, no check line.
