# Worked example: a board update on a competitor launch

Invented case for teaching. "Competitor Y" is fictional. Prices and dates are made up.

## Context card (from trusted-intake, already in the conversation)

```
TRUSTED context: board update on a competitor launch

Reader          the board, deciding how much to invest in Germany   (stated)
Market          not needed for this work                            (assumed)
Product status  not needed for this work                            (assumed)
Use             internal only                                       (stated)
Deliverable     short update, prose                                 (stated)
Material        market tracker, 1 November 2026
Stakes          leadership
Next process    none apparent
```

## Request

"Tighten this competitor update for the board. It reads like filler."

## Material supplied

> (Market tracker, 1 November 2026) Competitor Y launched in Germany only, not in France
> or the UK. Their list price is EUR 1,240 per month; ours is EUR 1,180 per month. Payer
> feedback, 3 interviews, not representative: two cited the convenience of monthly
> dosing; none committed to a formulary change. Our Phase 3 readout is expected in Q1
> 2027, per the program lead's email.

## Draft supplied

> Strategic landscape remains dynamic. Competitor Y has launched and we will leverage
> synergies across the competitive landscape to respond. We will dominate share and
> expect to reach 35% by year-end. Payers are excited. Going forward we will continue to
> monitor.

## Revised text

> As of 1 November 2026, Competitor Y is on the market in Germany only, not in France or
> the UK. Their list price is EUR 1,240 per month; ours is EUR 1,180 per month.
>
> Three payer interviews (not a representative sample) found that two payers cited the
> convenience of monthly dosing. None has committed to a formulary change.
>
> Our Phase 3 readout is expected in Q1 2027, per the program lead.
>
> **So what:** Competitor Y's reach is limited to Germany so far, and none of the three
> payers interviewed has committed to a formulary change.
>
> **For decision:** [GAP: needs a decision; what the board is asked to decide on this
> update, and who owns the follow-up; say the decision and the owner]

## Short note to the person

Vague statements: 2. Filler passages: 3. Sections with no "so what": 1.
Cut 3 passages of filler ("strategic landscape remains dynamic", "leverage synergies
across the competitive landscape", "going forward we will continue to monitor") and one
claim your notes do not support (that we will dominate share and reach 35% by
year-end). Made 2 statements specific from your tracker: where Competitor Y launched,
and what the payer interviews said. Added for you to confirm: the "so what" line,
which rests on the Germany-only launch and the three interviews. Still needed from you:
what the board is asked to decide, and who owns the follow-up. If you want a share
line, send the figure and its source.

## Why it is written this way

- Leadership depth (TR-H12): every task that applies ran, with a short note.
- "Payers are excited" became what two of three payers said, with the sample size and
  the caveat from the tracker. Nobody's enthusiasm was stated that the notes do not state.
- Both list prices are shown and nothing is derived from them. A price gap in
  percent would be a new figure, and it is not needed to make the point.
- The "so what" is a reading of the tracker, so it is listed for the person to
  confirm. It makes no recommendation about investment.
- No next step, owner or date was invented. The decision and owner are a gap the
  person fills.
- The note reports the cut of the 35% claim and does not judge it; whether any share
  figure can be stated belongs to the True letter.
