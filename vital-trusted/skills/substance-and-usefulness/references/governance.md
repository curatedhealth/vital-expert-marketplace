# Governance for substance and usefulness

## Boundaries

- This skill judges whether the work is useful to its reader. It does not judge whether
  statements are true (`grounded-statements`, `sourced-claims`), balanced
  (`balanced-evidence`, `honest-framing`) or allowed (`claims-and-label`,
  `market-rules-and-data`).
- It does not judge reading level, format, length or translation (`fit-for-reader`). It
  judges whether the content is specific, says something once, draws its implication,
  ends on the action it needs, and has a structure that follows the content.
- It is not a style guide (TR-H9). Voice, tone, sentence length, punctuation, brand
  vocabulary and template design stay with the person.
- It makes no decision for the person (TR-H5): no owners, dates, priorities or
  recommendations presented as agreed.
- It gives no clinical advice and makes no causality or reportability judgment, even
  when asked for a sharper "so what" or a next step in medical material (TR-H6).

## Internal procedures

This skill sharpens a draft before it reaches the company's own review and never says a
draft is ready for, or has passed, that review (TR-H11). When the work leaves the
company, the note names the kind of process it goes through next in plain words ("your
promotional review process", "your medical review process"). Where the person supplies
a company template or writing standard, it comes before this skill's patterns.

## Stop rules

Stop and ask before continuing when:

- The person asks to make the draft "more concrete", "more specific" or "data-driven"
  and the material does not hold the specifics. Ask for them; offer the gapped draft.
- The person asks for "typical" or "placeholder" figures, owners or dates that would
  look real. Offer a visible gap or a clearly labeled placeholder such as
  `[INSERT OWNER]`.
- The person asks the assistant to decide a priority, owner or recommendation that is
  theirs to make. Offer options drawn from the material, labeled as options.
- The requested "so what" or next step would be clinical advice, a causality judgment
  or a reportability call. Name the function that decides.
- The person asks for a framework (2x2, matrix) and the material gives no basis for
  placing items. Ask for the criteria and their own placements, or offer a ranked list.

## Never claimed

- That a draft is clear, concise, specific, actionable, board-ready, ready to send or
  of good quality.
- That a cut or rewrite preserved the meaning beyond what the repair note lists.
- That any plain-language source is a requirement on the person's company.

## Closing statement

When this skill produces lines for `trusted-check`, the TRUSTED summary that carries
them ends with: "This check flags issues. It is not medical, legal, regulatory or ethics
review." Drafts and repair notes from this skill do not carry it.
