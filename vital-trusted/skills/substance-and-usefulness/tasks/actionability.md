# Task TR-1.1.4 Actionability

## Formula

| Slot | For this task |
|---|---|
| Worker | Senior editor and engagement lead |
| Workflow | Decide whether the work calls for a decision, recommendation, next step or owner; check each is present and specific; propose from the material or ask |
| Skill | TR-1.1.4 |
| Knowledge | `references/patterns.md` (A1–A5) |
| Tool | None. Companion slot: none yet |
| Prompt | This guide |
| Template | `assets/check-line.md`, `assets/repair-note.md` |
| Input | The draft, the supplied material, the context card (reader, deliverable, stakes) |
| Governance | TR-H1, TR-H2, TR-H5, TR-H7 |

## When the work calls for an action

| Work | Usually calls for |
|---|---|
| Board or leadership update | The decision or ask for the reader, or an explicit "for information, no decision needed" |
| Business review, brief, proposal | A recommendation and next steps with owners |
| Meeting notes, follow-up email | Agreed actions, each with an owner and, where agreed, a date |
| Competitor summary, literature summary | What to watch or do next, if the reader has a decision coming |
| Information note, newsletter item, reply to a question | Often nothing; do not force one |

Use the context card and the request. When unsure whether the reader is meant to act,
ask once.

## What a usable action has

- **What:** a specific action or decision, not "align on next steps" or "explore
  options".
- **Who:** a named person or function, as the person supplied it.
- **By when:** a date, quarter or milestone, where the work needs one and the person
  supplied it.
- **What the reader is asked to do:** approve, choose, note, fund, escalate.

## Guard (while drafting)

1. **Decide whether the work calls for an action** (table above).
2. **Write the action from the material.** Meeting notes that say "agreed: we send the
   updated model" become "We will send the updated budget impact model [GAP: not
   supplied — owner and date — who sends it, by when]".
3. **A recommendation may be proposed, labeled as a proposal**, when it follows from the
   material and the person asked for one: "Proposed for your decision: …". It is never
   presented as already agreed.
4. **Never assign an owner or a date.** Not even an obvious one. Ask, or leave
   `[GAP: needs a decision — owner — name the person or function]`.
5. **Close on the ask** for leadership material: one line that says what the reader is
   asked to do.

## Repair (existing draft)

1. **Find the action, if any.** Mark whether a decision or recommendation is present,
   missing, or not needed for this work.
2. **Check each action** for what, who and by when. Mark vague actions ("we will drive
   alignment") and actions with no owner.
3. **Fix** from the material, or with a gap. Turn "next steps: align with stakeholders"
   into the specific step the notes record, or into a question for the person.
4. **List each added or changed action** in the repair note; mark proposals as "for you
   to confirm".

## Check line

Feeds the next step line in `assets/check-line.md`: decision or recommendation present,
missing or not needed; owner named, missing or not needed.

## Boundaries

- The decision is the person's (TR-H5). The skill makes room for it and words it; it
  does not make it.
- Recommendations on clinical care, safety reporting, legal or privacy matters are not
  proposed here (TR-H6); the next step is "raise with <the function>".
- A recommendation in external material may be a claim; it goes through the internal
  review process with the rest of the work (TR-H11).
