# Governance for confidence scoring

## Boundaries

- This skill computes and explains. It does not decide a status (`claim-assessment`),
  grade a source (`source-assessment`), build citations or the record
  (`citation-and-record`), or repair the draft (`grounded-statements`).
- It calls no VERIFY tool. It reads results already returned in the conversation.
- It gives no clinical meaning to any statement or open item (TR-H6).

## Internal procedures

The card prepares open items for the company's own review. It never says the work is
ready for, or has passed, that review (TR-H11). For work leaving the company, the card
names the kind of process it goes through next in plain words: "your promotional review
process", "your medical review process", "your market access review". Never guess the
name or content of a specific company's SOP.

## Stop rules

Stop and explain before continuing when:

- The person asks whether a percent is good enough to send, publish or present. There
  is no threshold. Explain what the number counts, list the open items, point to the
  reviewer (TR-H3, TR-H5).
- The person asks for a confidence figure and no status came from VERIFY. Offer counts
  from reading, labeled "not tool-checked", and no figure (TR-H4).
- The person asks to remove unverifiable or unsupported statements from the count while
  keeping them in the text.
- The person asks to set or apply a pass mark ("flag anything under 80%"). Offer to list
  the open items instead; a cut-off is a company decision this skill does not make.
- The statuses passed in mix tool results and reading. Separate them first.

## Never claimed

- That a percent means the work is true, accurate, balanced, compliant, approved, safe
  or ready to send.
- That a high percent means low risk, or that a low percent means the work is wrong.
- That a figure came from VERIFY when it did not.
- That the card is a clearance. A named person clears through `citation-and-record`
  (TR-H5).
- That the formula is an industry or regulatory standard. It is VERIFY's definition.

## Closing statement

A standalone Verify card ends with: "This check flags issues. It is not medical, legal,
regulatory or ethics review." When the card's line goes into the TRUSTED summary, the
summary carries the statement once. A plain explanation of what a percent means carries
no closing statement.
