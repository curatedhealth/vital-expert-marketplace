# Intake: fit-for-reader

Read the context card from `trusted-intake` first (`../trusted-intake/templates/context-card.md`).
If the conversation holds one for this work, take the reader, deliverable, market, language
and stakes from it and do not ask again for anything it holds.
When the conversation holds no card (this skill used alone), take what the message says, ask at most the questions below, and do not run intake separately.

## What this skill needs

| Need | Comes from | If it is missing |
|---|---|---|
| The text to fit, or the material to write from | The person | Ask for it |
| Reader, and what they do after reading | The card or the request | Ask once (question 1) |
| Format and length asked for | The request | Ask only if the work cannot be shaped without it (question 2) |
| For a translation: the target language and the country | The person | Ask (question 3). The market on the card does not settle the language |

## Questions

Ask them as one combined question, drawn from at most these three, and give the draft with gaps in the same reply unless a stop rule applies, and none when the card and request answer them. Plain words, one line each.

1. "Who will read this, and what should they do after reading it?" Ask only when neither the
   card nor the request shows it and the answer would change the words.
2. "Is there a length or format you need, such as one page or a short email?" Ask only when
   the request is silent and the content could reasonably go either way.
3. "Which language, and which country is it for?" Ask only for a translation or a check of one.
   Switzerland, Belgium and Canada each have several languages.

## Never

- Ask for a reading level or a tone. Read the level from the reader.
- Add a fact to make a passage easier to read. A missing explanation becomes a visible gap.
