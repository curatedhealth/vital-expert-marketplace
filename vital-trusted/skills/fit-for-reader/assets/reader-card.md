# Output format: reader card

Steers the writing. Filled from the context card, the request and
`references/readers.md`. Not shown to the person unless they ask; if shown, it is
already in plain words.

```
Reader:       <who will read it, as stated>                       (<stated | from the card | assumed>)
They decide:  <what they must decide or do after reading>
Their words:  <vocabulary to use; terms to avoid or explain>
Depth:        <how much method, number and detail this reader can use>
Format:       <format and length as asked | not stated>
```

If a field is assumed, say so in one line to the person ("I've written this for the
payer's medical adviser; tell me if it's the budget holder"). An assumption never lowers
the depth for material that leaves the company.

## Two readers

```
Primary:      <reader>  decides <…>
Secondary:    <reader>  needs to find <…>
Conflict:     <none | what conflicts — ask which leads>
```
