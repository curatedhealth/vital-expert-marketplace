# Intake: balanced-evidence

Read the context card from `trusted-intake` first (`../trusted-intake/templates/context-card.md`).
If the conversation holds one for this work, take the reader, use, stakes and material from
it and do not ask again for anything it holds.
When the conversation holds no card (this skill used alone), take what the message says, ask at most the questions below, and do not run intake separately.

## What this skill needs

| Need | Comes from | If it is missing |
|---|---|---|
| The draft, or the material to draft from | The person | Ask for it |
| Reader, and whether it leaves the company | The card or the request | Ask once (question 1). If it stays unknown, assume it may leave the company |
| The safety and tolerability results, and every trial or endpoint the material holds | The material supplied | Ask (question 2) when only benefit data came in |
| Which result should lead when the studies disagree | The person | Ask (question 3) |

## Questions

Ask them as one combined question, drawn from at most these three, and give the draft with gaps in the same reply unless a stop rule applies, and none for a quick internal note unless an omission would mislead a
colleague. Plain words, one line each, with the reason.

1. "Who will read this, and could it go outside the company?" Ask only when the card and
   request do not show it.
2. "Do you have the safety results and the other trials or endpoints? A benefit shown
   alone gives a one-sided picture." Ask only when the material supplied is benefit only.
3. "Two of the results point in different directions. Which should lead, or should both
   appear together?" Ask only when the material shows that conflict.

Whatever the reader, the rule is the same: ask for the missing safety or limitation
data, or leave a visible gap where it will go. Never draft a benefits-only piece for a
reader outside the company.

## Never

- Draft a benefits-only piece for an outside reader because the person asked to keep it
  positive.
- Supply a safety figure that the material does not hold.
