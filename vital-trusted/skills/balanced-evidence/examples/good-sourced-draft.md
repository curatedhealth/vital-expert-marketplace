## Request

"Fix the balance in this value story before it goes to the payer."

## Material supplied

> (Registry report, internal, data cut-off 30 June 2026) 1,850 patients followed for 12
> months. Hospital admissions: 12.0% on Brand A versus 20.0% on standard care (adjusted
> hazard ratio 0.60, 95% CI 0.48 to 0.75). Stopped treatment for any reason: 22% versus
> 14%. Stopped because of side effects: 7% versus 3%. Most common side effects: nausea
> 11%, headache 6%. The authors state that the registry is observational, that
> confounding is possible and that it covers one country. No cost analysis was done.

## Draft supplied

> - Brand A cuts hospital admissions from 20.0% to 12.0%.
> - Brand A is well tolerated, with a favorable safety profile.
> - Patients stay on therapy, so the savings are sustained.

## Revised bullets

> - In a registry of 1,850 patients followed for 12 months, hospital admissions were
>   12.0% on Brand A versus 20.0% on standard care (adjusted hazard ratio 0.60, 95% CI
>   0.48 to 0.75). The registry is observational and covers one country, and the authors
>   state that confounding is possible.
> - Within 12 months, 22% of patients stopped Brand A, versus 14% on standard care; 7%
>   stopped because of side effects, versus 3%. The most common side effects were nausea
>   (11%) and headache (6%).
> - [GAP: not supplied; cost analysis; supply it before any statement about savings]

## Why it is written this way

- Both sentences that pointed away from the data were replaced with the data, not
  softened. The registry reports more stopping on Brand A, so "stay on therapy" was the
  reverse of the material.
- Every figure is the registry's own. Nothing was calculated.
- The note does not call the result balanced or fair.
- The market was not stated, so it is asked and not guessed.
