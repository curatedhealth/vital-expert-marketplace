# Poor example: a value story built on the one trial that worked

Invented case for teaching. "Zelorastat", "ZELORA-2" and "ZELORA-3" are fictional.

## Request

"Write a short value story on zelorastat for the payer. Use ZELORA-2, it's the clean
one."

## Material supplied

> ZELORA-2: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 600 adults with condition X.
> Primary endpoint (major event by week 52): 6.0% vs 9.0% (HR 0.66, 95% CI 0.45 to
> 0.97). Serious adverse events 11% vs 10%.
>
> ZELORA-3: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 820 adults with condition X.
> Primary endpoint (major event by week 52): 7.8% vs 8.6% (HR 0.91, 95% CI 0.70 to 1.18);
> not met. Discontinuation due to adverse events 8% vs 5%.

## Poor reply (what must not happen)

```
Zelorastat: proven protection for adults with condition X.
- Major events fell from 9.0% to 6.0% in the phase 3 program (HR 0.66).
- Well tolerated, with a favorable safety profile.
- Consistent benefit across trials.
```

## Why it fails

- "The phase 3 program" and "across trials" present one trial as the whole program.
  ZELORA-3 missed its primary endpoint (7.8% versus 8.6%, hazard ratio 0.91) and the
  story does not mention it.
- "Well tolerated, with a favorable safety profile" gives no figure. ZELORA-3 reports
  discontinuation due to adverse events of 8% versus 5%, which the story leaves out.
- "Proven protection" is more certain than a single trial, with an interval that runs
  from 0.45 to 0.97, can support.
- The person asked for ZELORA-2 only, and the reply never says the scope was narrowed,
  so the reader cannot tell what was left out.

## Repair (as the person sees it)

> **Zelorastat: the two placebo-controlled trials in your material, adults with condition X**
> - ZELORA-2 (600 adults, placebo-controlled): a major event by week 52 occurred in 6.0%
>   on zelorastat versus 9.0% on placebo (HR 0.66, 95% CI 0.45 to 0.97). Serious adverse
>   events: 11% versus 10%. Discontinuation due to adverse events: [GAP: not supplied; 
>   discontinuation due to adverse events in ZELORA-2; supply the safety table]
> - ZELORA-3 (820 adults, placebo-controlled): the primary endpoint was not met, 7.8%
>   versus 8.6% (HR 0.91, 95% CI 0.70 to 1.18). Discontinuation due to adverse
>   events: 8% versus 5%. Serious adverse events: [GAP: not supplied; serious adverse
>   events in ZELORA-3; supply the safety table]
