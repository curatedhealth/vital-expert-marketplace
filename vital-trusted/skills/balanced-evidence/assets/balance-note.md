# Output format: balance note

Follows the fixed text. Plain language, no rule codes (TR-H13). Size it to the stakes
(TR-H12): for an internal note, one or two lines; for anything leaving the company, the
full note with the evidence inventory.

## Full note

```
Balance: what I added, moved and left open (draft, not yet reviewed)

Based on: <one line per thing you supplied>

Added or moved
1. "<before>" → "<after>". <Why, in one plain sentence, e.g. "The study reports that 9%
   stopped because of side effects; a payer will look for it next to the benefit.">
2. ...

Scope now stated
1. <e.g. "Slide 2 now says it covers the primary endpoint only; secondary endpoints are
   on slide 3.">

Left out on purpose, and why
1. <item from your material> — <why it is outside the stated scope>

Left as gaps for you to fill
1. <what is missing> — <where it would come from>

Questions for you
1. <a choice only you or your medical reviewer can make, e.g. which side effects to show>

<If the request asked to remove or hide risks:> I kept the main risks in the piece, in
short wording. Leaving them out would make the piece read as one-sided and your review
would ask for them.

<If the work leaves the company:> Next step: this goes through your <kind of internal
process, e.g. promotional review> before it is used.

I worked only from the material you gave me. I did not look for other studies, and I did
not judge whether any side effect matters clinically.

<Evidence inventory, for anything leaving the company: assets/evidence-inventory.md>
```

## Short note (internal, low stakes)

```
Added: <one line, e.g. "the second trial's result">. Gap left: <one line, if any>.
```
