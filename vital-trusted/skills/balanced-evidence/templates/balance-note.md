# Output format: balance note

Follows the fixed text. Plain language, no rule codes (TR-H13). Size it to the stakes
(TR-H12): for an internal note, one or two lines; for anything leaving the company, the
full note with the evidence inventory.

The counts line is required in both forms whenever something was changed or found, and
shows only the counts that apply: leave out a count that would read 0. It is the only
counts line in the reply (the evidence inventory has none). Count each numbered change
in the note once. When nothing was changed or found, give no note and no counts line;
use the "Nothing to change" line below. Drafting from material with no draft of the
person's, nothing counts as added or moved: give "Studies in your material: <n>." and
the gaps.
"Other changes: <n> (<short kinds>)" counts any change the named counts do not cover (a
causal claim softened, a disclosure added, an unsupported claim removed), so the main
fix is never uncounted.

## Full note

```
Balance: what I added, moved and left open (Uncleared draft)

Based on: <one line per thing you supplied>

Studies in your material: <n>. Results added or moved: <n>.
Risks or limitations added or moved: <n>.
Gaps left: <n>. Other changes: <n> (<short kinds, e.g. causal claim softened, disclosure added,
unsupported claim removed>).

Added or moved
1. "<before>" → "<after>". <Why, in one plain sentence, e.g. "The study reports that 9%
   stopped because of adverse events; the draft gave the benefit without it.">
2. ...

Scope now stated
1. <e.g. "Slide 2 now says it covers the primary endpoint only; secondary endpoints are
   on slide 3.">

Left out on purpose, and why
1. <item from your material>: <why it is outside the stated scope>

Left as gaps for you to fill
1. <what is missing>: <where it would come from>

Question for you
<a choice only you or your medical reviewer can make, e.g. which side effects to show; one combined question at most, covering at most two related points (TR-H7)>

<If the request asked to remove or hide risks:> I kept the main risks in the piece, in
short wording. Leaving them out would make the piece read as one-sided; your review
process decides what the piece carries.

<If the work leaves the company:> Next step: this goes through your <kind of internal
process, e.g. promotional review> before it is used.

I worked only from the material you gave me. I did not look for other studies, and I did
not judge whether any side effect matters clinically.

<Evidence inventory, for anything leaving the company: templates/evidence-inventory.md>
```

## Short note (internal or team-level work)

For a team email, a team call summary or another piece that stays inside the team. It
holds the reason for each change and the gaps left, and nothing else.

```
<Only the counts that apply, none at 0.>
Changed: 1. "<before>" → "<after>": <why, in one plain sentence>. 2. ...
Gaps left: <what is missing, and where it would come from, if any>.
```

## Nothing to change

No note and no counts line. One sentence, no verdict (never "balanced", "fair", "fine"):

```
Nothing needed changing for balance: <what the text already does, e.g. it gives both
trials' primary results and the stopping rates in both arms>.
```
