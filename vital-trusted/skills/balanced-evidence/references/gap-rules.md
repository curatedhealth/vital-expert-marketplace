# Gap rules applied to balanced evidence

> **Where the marker belongs.** Use the `[GAP: …]` marker only inside a drafted deliverable. In notes, open-item lists and explanations, write the gap as a plain sentence ("Still missing: …, from …"). See `trusted-capability-map/references/gap-rules.md`.

Same format and classes as every TRUSTED skill:
`[GAP: <class> — <what is missing> — <what would close it>]`

| Class | Typical case in this skill | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `not supplied` | Side-effect, discontinuation or limitation data the draft needs and the material lacks; the second trial named but not given; results at the pre-specified time point not given | `[GAP: not supplied — side effects and discontinuations in LUMI-1 — supply the safety table or the label]` |
| `needs a decision` | Which risks are material for this reader; which study or time point should lead; whether a stated scope is enough for this use | `[GAP: needs a decision — which side effects to show on the payer slide — ask your medical reviewer]` |
| `conflicting` | Two supplied sources give different safety or discontinuation figures | `[GAP: conflicting — abstract gives 4% discontinuation, full paper 5% — confirm which is final]` |
| `not checkable here` | Whether other studies of the product for this use exist, or whether all registered outcomes were reported, with no companion connected | `[GAP: not checkable here — whether other trials of zelorastat in condition X exist — ask your medical information or literature team]` |
| `out of date or unclear date` | Safety data with no data cut-off date; "long-term safety" with no follow-up period | `[GAP: out of date or unclear date — safety data cut-off not stated — add the date from the study report]` |

Wording rules:

- Name the exact item: "discontinuation due to side effects in each group at week 52",
  not "safety data".
- Never hint at a value or a study inside a gap ("probably around 5%", "the other phase 3
  trial").
- "The study reported no serious side effects" is a finding only when the full safety
  section was supplied and read. Otherwise use `not supplied`.
- Never write a gap that implies nothing else exists. "No other trials" is never a
  finding of this skill.
