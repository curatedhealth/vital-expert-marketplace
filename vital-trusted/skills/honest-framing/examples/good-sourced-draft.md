# Worked example: slides that say a trial proves superiority

Invented case for teaching. "Velostra", "Corvane", "KELNA-1" and "Example Pharma" are
fictional; no real product, trial, company or person.

## Context card (from trusted-intake, short form)

Reader: sales force now, doctors later through an agency build. Use: promotional.
Stakes: leaving the company. Market: not stated. Next process: your promotional review
process.

## Request

"Tighten these five slides. Keep the punch."

## Material supplied (abstract and disclosures of the KELNA-1 paper)

> KELNA-1 was a randomized, open-label, active-controlled phase 3 trial in 640 adults,
> 320 per arm. Primary endpoint, at least one flare by week 24: 22% with Velostra versus
> 28% with Corvane (relative risk 0.79, 95% CI 0.64 to 0.97). Secondary endpoint, at
> least 75% improvement at week 16: 88% versus 84% (difference 4 percentage points, 95%
> CI -1 to 9). Injection-site reactions: 3% versus 1%. Authors name the open-label
> design and the 24-week follow-up as limitations. Funding: Example Pharma, the maker of
> Velostra, which designed the study and analyzed the data. Three of eight authors are
> Example Pharma employees.

## Draft supplied

> 1. KELNA-1 proves Velostra is superior to Corvane.
> 2. Velostra cuts flares by 21%, a dramatic improvement.
> 3. Independent head-to-head data.
> 4. Chart: bar chart of 75% improvement at week 16, vertical axis from 80% to 90%,
>    Velostra 88%, Corvane 84%, no n.
> 5. Injection-site reactions: only 3%.

## Revised slides

> 1. In KELNA-1, an open-label trial of 640 adults, fewer patients had a flare by week 24
>    with Velostra than with Corvane.
> 2. Flares by week 24: 22% on Velostra versus 28% on Corvane (relative risk 0.79, 95%
>    CI 0.64 to 0.97). Follow-up was 24 weeks.
> 3. KELNA-1 was funded by Example Pharma, the maker of Velostra, which also designed the
>    study and analyzed the data.
> 4. See the chart description in the note.
> 5. Injection-site reactions: 3% on Velostra versus 1% on Corvane.

## Note to the person

How I changed the framing (Uncleared draft)

Based on: the KELNA-1 abstract and disclosures you supplied.

   trial cannot prove superiority, and the authors name the open-label design.

   limitations the authors name.

## Why it is written this way

- Every figure is in the abstract. The difference between the two flare rates is
  offered, not added, because the person did not ask for it.
- The chart is described, not redrawn; the person or the agency redraws it.
- The interest is stated from the paper's own disclosure. The note does not call the
  result neutral or fair.
