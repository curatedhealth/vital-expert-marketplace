# Output format: claims note

Follows the fixed text. Plain language, no rule codes or clause numbers (TR-H13). Size
it to the stakes (TR-H12): one or two lines for internal work, the full note for
anything leaving the company or used with people outside it.

## Full note

```
Label, promotion and comparisons: what I changed (Uncleared draft)

Working from: <label or intended-purpose statement as supplied, with market and
version or date as given | no label supplied>; for <reader>, as <use, as you said>.

Changed
1. "<before>" → "<after>". <Why, in one plain sentence, e.g. "Your label covers adults
   only; this line reached teenagers.">
2. ...

Kept out
1. <message or claim not written>: <why, plainly>; <what could be said instead, or
   where the question belongs, e.g. "questions doctors ask about teenagers go to your
   medical information team">

Comparisons
1. "<claim>": <compared directly in <study as supplied> | no study compared the two
   directly | two separate trials>. <What was done: kept with comparator and measure,
   split into two statements, or left as a gap.>

Left as gaps for you to fill
1. <what is missing>: <where it would come from>

Question for you
<e.g. which market this is for; whether the piece is promotional or a reply; one combined question at most, covering at most two related points (TR-H7)>

Next step: this goes through your <kind of process, e.g. promotional review process>
before it is used. Whether it can be used is decided there. Questions about the label
itself (what it covers, whether it is current) go to your regulatory team.

<When a label was supplied:> I compared the draft with the label you gave me. I did not
check that the label is the current approved version, and none of this was tool-checked.
<When no label was supplied:> No label came with your request, so product statements are
left as gaps and nothing was compared with a label. None of this was tool-checked.
```

## Short note (internal, low stakes)

```
<With a label:> Outside the label you gave me: <one line>. <Without one:> Needs the
label: <one line>. Comparison without a direct study: <one line, if any>.
```
