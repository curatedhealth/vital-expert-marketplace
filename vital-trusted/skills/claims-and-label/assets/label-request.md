# Output format: asking for the label or intended purpose

Used when the work makes product statements and no label or intended-purpose statement
was supplied (TR-H7). One short message. Ask only for what changes the answer.

## For a medicine

```
Before I write the product lines, could you share the approved product information for
<market, or "the market this is for">? The summary of product characteristics or
prescribing information is best (the patient leaflet if this is for patients).

I'll keep every product statement inside it: what it is approved for, which patients,
dose and wording. Without it I'd have to guess, and a reviewer will check against the
real one.

If you'd like me to start now, I'll mark each product statement as a gap to fill.
```

## For a device or app

```
Could you share the intended-purpose statement for <product>, for <market>? It is
usually in the instructions for use or the technical documentation, and your
regulatory team will have the current wording.

I'll describe what the app does in those terms: what it records or measures, who reads
the result, and when to see a doctor. Copy that says it does more than that is the
first thing a reviewer will question.
```

## When the market is also missing

Add one line:

```
Which market is this for? What the product is approved for can differ between
countries.
```
