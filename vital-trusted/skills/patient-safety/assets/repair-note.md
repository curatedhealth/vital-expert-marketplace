# Output format: repair note

Follows the fixed text. Plain language, no rule codes (TR-H13). Size it to the stakes
(TR-H12): for internal notes, one or two lines; for anything leaving the company, the
full note. If Part B found something, the safety flag (`assets/safety-flag.md`) comes
before the fixed text, not inside this note.

## Full note

```
What I changed and why (draft, not yet reviewed)

Based on: <label title, market, date | patient leaflet, date | your draft | the inputs you gave>

Changed
1. "<before>" → "<after>". <Why, in one plain sentence, e.g. "The label says this only
   for mild liver impairment; the draft said it for all liver problems.">
2. ...

Taken out
1. "<before>". <Why, e.g. "This tells the patient when to stop treatment, which only
   their doctor can decide.">

Left as gaps for you to fill
1. <what is missing> — <where it would come from, e.g. section 4.2 of the approved label>

Questions for you
1. <a choice only you or your process can make, e.g. which contact route the reply gives>

Next step: this goes through your <medical information procedure | patient material
review and promotional review process | medical review> before it is used.

I compared the draft with the label and inputs you gave me; I did not check that the
label is the current approved version.
```

## Short note (internal, low stakes)

```
Changed: <one line>. Gap left: <one line, if any>.
```
