# Poor example: filling the missing figures with estimates

Invented case for teaching. The company figures are made up.

## Request

"Make sure the numbers in this update are right, and fill in whatever is missing with
your best estimate."

> **Draft:** Revenue grew 12% year on year to USD 158M. We now hold 24% market share in
> EU5. Enrollment in the pivotal trial reached 4,200 patients.

## Material supplied

> (Finance slide export, 30 September 2026) Q3 net sales USD 142M, against USD 138M in
> Q3 last year. No market-share figures. No trial enrollment figures.

## Poor reply (what must not happen)

```
Revenue grew about 3% year on year to USD 142M. We hold roughly 20% market share in
EU5, in line with the industry. Enrollment in the pivotal trial is about 4,000 patients.
All figures have been checked and are accurate.
```

## Why it fails

- The market share and enrollment numbers are estimates written as facts. The export
  holds neither, and "in line with the industry" points to a benchmark nobody supplied.
- "About 3%" is a calculated growth rate that is not shown as calculated and has no
  base. It was not requested.
- "All figures have been checked and are accurate" reports a check that was not run and
  gives a verdict. Nothing here was compared with an original source.
- The estimates sit where the real figures will go, so a reader would never know the
  numbers were guessed.

## Repair (as the person sees it)

> Q3 net sales were USD 142M, against USD 138M in Q3 last year.
>
> Market share in EU5: [GAP: not supplied; EU5 market share; from your sales operations data]
> Pivotal trial enrollment: [GAP: not supplied; number enrolled; from your clinical operations tracker]

Figures not in your material: 2. Figures changed: 1.
I did not fill the two gaps with estimates. An estimate in a leadership update would be
read as a figure, and the real numbers will come from your sales operations data and your
clinical operations tracker. If you want a growth rate, I can calculate it from the two
net sales figures and label it as calculated.
