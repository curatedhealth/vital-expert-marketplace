# Output format: repair note

Follows the fixed text. Plain language, no rule codes (TR-H13). Size it to the stakes
(TR-H12): for an internal email, one or two lines; for anything leaving the company, the
full note.

The counts line opens both forms whenever something was changed or found, and shows
only the counts that apply: leave out a count that would read 0 ("Figures changed: 1.
Statements left as gaps: 2."). When nothing was changed or found, give no note and no
counts line, and say in one sentence that nothing needed changing against your
material, with no verdict. Drafting from notes with no draft of the person's, nothing
counts as changed: give only "Statements left as gaps: <n>." when there are gaps, or no
counts line at all.
"Other changes: <n> (<short kinds>)" counts any change the named counts do not cover (a
causal claim softened, a disclosure added, an unsupported claim removed), so the main
fix is never uncounted.

## Full note

```
What I changed to match your material (Uncleared draft)

Based on: <one line per thing you supplied>

<Only the counts that apply:> Figures not in your material: <n>. Figures changed: <n>.
Statements left as gaps: <n>. Other changes: <n> (<short kinds, e.g. causal claim softened, disclosure added,
unsupported claim removed>).

Changed
1. "<before>" → "<after>". <Why, in one plain sentence, e.g. "Your notes give a forecast,
   not an actual figure.">
2. ...

Kept out, and why
1. "<wording asked for and not written>": <why, in one plain sentence>

Left as gaps for you to fill
1. <what is missing>: <where it would come from>

Question for you
<a conflict or a choice only you can make; one combined question at most, covering at most two related points (TR-H7)>

<If the work leaves the company:> Next step: this goes through your <kind of internal
process, e.g. promotional review> before it is used.

I compared the draft with what you gave me; I did not check it against the original
sources with a tool.
```

## Short note (internal, low stakes)

```
<Only the counts that apply, none at 0.>
Changed: <one line>. Gap left: <one line, if any>.
```
