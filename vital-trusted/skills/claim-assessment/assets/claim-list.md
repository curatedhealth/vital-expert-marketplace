# Output format: claim list (Part A only)

Used when the person asks what in a text can be checked, or before Part B when the
pairing needs their input. Plain language; no rule or pattern ids (TR-H13). No support
words yet.

```
What in this text can be checked (draft)

Split: <by VERIFY | manual, not tool-checked>
Checked against: <one line per source you supplied, or "nothing supplied yet">

Check first
1. "<claim as written>" — <why it matters, e.g. "a figure", "a quote from a named
   person", "a safety statement">
   Passage: "<exact words>" (<source, locator>) | no passage found in what you supplied

Check next
1. ...

Not something a passage can settle
1. "<recommendation, opinion or definition>" — for your judgment and your review process

Needed from you
1. <a passage or source for a claim with none, or which of two passages governs>
```

At light depth, give only "Check first" and "Needed from you".
