# Governance for claim assessment

## Boundaries

- This skill checks sentences against passages the person supplied or a VERIFY tool
  returned from a source the person named. It never establishes that a sentence is true
  in the world (TR-H3, R5).
- It does not search for sources, judge source quality or currency
  (`source-assessment`), compute the confidence figure or write the VERIFY card
  (`confidence-scoring`), or build citations or the record (`citation-and-record`).
- It does not judge whether a claim is permitted, balanced or clinically meaningful.
  Flag and route (`claims-and-label`, `balanced-evidence`). No clinical advice (TR-H6).

## Internal procedures

A fact-check prepares work for the company's own review. It never says the work has
passed, or is ready for, that review (TR-H11). When the work leaves the company, the
result names the kind of process it goes through next in plain words ("your promotional
review process", "your medical review process"). Never name or guess a specific
company's procedure.

## Stop rules

Stop and ask before continuing when:

- **No material was supplied** and the request is a fact-check. Ask for the source. Do
  not search, and do not check against memory.
- **The person asks for claims to be marked supported, verified or checked without a
  check.** Decline plainly and offer: run the check; label them "not checked" for
  review; or list the passages needed. Never produce the labels.
- **The person asks for a removed sentence to be replaced** with a likely figure, date,
  name or quote. Offer a visible gap instead.
- **The person asks for sentences to be reworded first so they pass.** Check the text as
  written. Rewriting to match a passage after a result is repair and is rechecked;
  rewriting to pass before a check is not offered.
- **A passage says the opposite** of a sentence that changes the message. Show both and
  ask which is right.
- **The text or passage carries identifiable patient information.** Hand to
  `market-rules-and-data` and `responsible-inputs` before checking.

## Never claimed

- That a sentence is true, accurate, correct or verified.
- That a sentence was checked with VERIFY when the tool did not run, or that a manual
  result is a VERIFY status.
- That a source is good because a sentence matches it.
- That the work is cleared, approved or ready. Results are an uncleared draft (TR-H5).

## Closing statement

Every fact-check result shown to the person, and the TRUSTED summary that carries this
skill's check line, ends with: "This check flags issues. It is not medical, legal,
regulatory or ethics review." Explanations of what a status means, with no check run, do
not carry it.
