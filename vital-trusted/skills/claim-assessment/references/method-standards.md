| R5 | A result says what the supplied passage says, not that the sentence is true in the world. A number, a named person, a quotation, or a compliance or performance claim stays only when an exact quoted passage with a locator states it, paired with the sentence by hand and shown next to it. An unnamed authority is not a source. When a supplied passage covers the topic and does not state the claim, the result is "not found in your passage": the sentence is removed or rewritten to what the passage states, and listed. When no supplied passage covers the topic, the result is "no passage to compare with": the sentence stays in the revised text with a visible gap and is never filled. One definition: `statuses.md`. | TRUSTED hard rules TR-H1 and TR-H3 |  | VERIFIED | In force |  |
