# Intake: responsible-use

Read the context card from `trusted-intake` first (`../trusted-intake/templates/context-card.md`).
If the conversation holds one for this work, take the reader, use, stakes and next process
from it and do not ask again for anything it holds.
When the conversation holds no card (this skill used alone), take what the message says, ask at most the questions below, and do not run intake separately.

## What this skill needs

| Need | Comes from | If it is missing |
|---|---|---|
| The work that is about to be used, or the plan for it | The person | Ask for it |
| Who reads it next and whether it leaves the company | The card or the request | Ask once (question 1) |
| Whose name it goes out under, and whether that person wrote or reviewed it | The person | Ask (question 2) for an article, a letter, or any byline |
| Where it will be published or submitted | The request | Ask (question 3) only when a journal, congress, payer or similar body may have its own AI statement rule |

## Questions

Ask them as one combined question, drawn from at most these three, and give the draft with gaps in the same reply unless a stop rule applies. None for a quick internal note; fix a stigmatizing word quietly. Plain
words, one line each, with the reason.

1. "Who will read this next, and does it go outside the company?" Ask only when the card
   and request do not show it.
2. "Whose name will this go out under, and has that person read it?" Ask only when the
   work goes out in someone's name.
3. "Where will it be published or submitted?" Ask only when the destination may set its own
   rule on AI help.

## Never

- Say that a review has happened. Record only what the person states.
- Name a specific company procedure or form. Name the kind of process in plain words.
- Draft a disclosure that reads as a record of a review that did not take place.
