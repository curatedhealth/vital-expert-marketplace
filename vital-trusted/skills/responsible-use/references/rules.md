# Primary rules behind this skill

Each rule names its primary source, the date and how it was confirmed. A rule marked
`[CHECK]` has not been confirmed against the primary document and is applied as good
practice, not cited as a requirement (TR-H8). Jurisdiction or venue is stated for each.

Last reviewed: 20 October 2026.

## Authorship and AI in publications

| Id | Rule | Primary source | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | Chatbots and other AI-assisted tools should not be listed as authors or co-authors, nor cited as an author, because they cannot be responsible for the accuracy, integrity and originality of the work. ## Human oversight

No single public rule sets how much review an everyday work product needs. Review steps
come from the company's own procedures (medical information, promotional review,
market access review, publications), which this skill cannot read (U0). U3, U6 and U7
show the direction public rules take: a person reads AI-assisted work, edits it and is
accountable for it, and formal checks such as spell-checking do not count as review.

## How to use these rules in output

- In what the person reads, give the reason in plain words ("journals that follow the
  editors' authorship recommendations ask you to say how AI was used, in the cover letter
  and the acknowledgments"). Cite the document only if asked.
- Never extend a rule beyond its scope. U6 is EU law for text published to inform the
  public on public-interest matters; it does not, on its own, make a payer dossier or an
  internal email subject to disclosure. U7 is NICE only.
- A `[CHECK]` rule is described as good practice, never as a requirement.
