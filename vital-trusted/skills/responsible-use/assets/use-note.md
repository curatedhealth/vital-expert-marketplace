# Output format: use note

What the person sees after the draft, when work is about to be used. Plain language, no
rule codes or skill names (TR-H13). Size it to the stakes (TR-H12). Include only the
parts that apply; most notes have one or two.

## No note (internal and quick)

Do the work. Say nothing about review, disclosure or authorship. If a stigmatizing word
was changed, at most:

```
(Changed "<before>" to "<after>".)
```

## One line (leadership or cross-team)

```
Worth a read by <owner of the figures or facts, as you named them> before it goes to <reader>.
```

## Full note (leaving the company)

```
Before this is used

Who reads it next: <name and role, as you gave them | to be named by you>, through your
<kind of process, e.g. medical information process>. It stays a draft until then.
<Optional: What to read for: <one line, e.g. the dosing sentence against the label>.>

AI help: <one of:>
- Your AI use policy <says <their words, as you told me> | was not mentioned>. <If not
  known:> Does it say anything about disclosing AI help in <this kind of work>?
- <Venue> asks <plain rule, e.g. how AI was used, in the cover letter and the
  acknowledgments>. <Only for a confirmed rule; otherwise:> Check <venue>'s own
  submission rules.
- Suggested wording, if you disclose: "<disclosure line built from what happened>"

Whose name it goes under: <one or two lines, e.g. "For this to go out under Dr <name>'s
name, she shapes and approves the content, and the writing support and funding are
acknowledged. Your publications process sets the steps.">

Language: changed <n> phrases.
1. "<before>" → "<after>": <a few plain words of why>.
<Optional: Question: <a preference only the community or your team can settle>.>
<Optional: Evidence gap named: <the sentence added, and the supplied source it comes from>.>
```

## Redirect (send without review, or hidden ghostwriting)

```
<One plain sentence on why, e.g. "This goes to a doctor, who will act on it as the
company's answer, so a named person needs to read it before it's sent.">

Here's what I can do now: <the honest route, e.g. "the reply ready for review, with
the two points the reviewer should check">. Who will read it?
```

Never add the TRUSTED closing statement to a use note. It belongs to check outputs only.
