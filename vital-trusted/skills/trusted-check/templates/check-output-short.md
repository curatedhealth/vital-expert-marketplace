# Output format: short TRUSTED summary (leadership and cross-team work)

Board and leadership updates, business reviews, internal briefs, competitor summaries.
Same rules as the full summary; fewer sections.

```
TRUSTED check: <what the draft is>, for <reader>. Draft, not yet reviewed.

Tangible        <count or status>. <one note>
Rule-compliant  <count or status>. <one note>
Unbiased        <count or status>. <one note>
Safe            <count or status>. <one note>
True            <count or status>. <one or two notes>
                [If VERIFY ran: one line of counts and the confidence line, with its meaning]
Ethical         <count or status>. <one note>
Defensible      <count or status>. <one note>

Open for you: <items, in one or two lines>
Could not be checked: <one line, if anything>

This check flags issues. It is not medical, legal, regulatory or ethics review.
```

No "Next step" line unless the work will leave the company or falls under a process
(for example a possible side effect, or figures that go to an external disclosure).
