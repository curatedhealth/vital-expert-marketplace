# Worked example: a board update with a forecast stated as actual (short depth)

Invented case for teaching. "Lumivra" is a fictional product; the affiliate and figures
are made up.

## Request

"Run TRUSTED on this before it goes into the board pack."

Draft:

> Lumivra delivered Q4 net sales of CHF 3.1 million, well ahead of Q3 (CHF 2.4
> million), as the August campaign drove two major hospital listings. Share of new
> patients grew 20%. Reimbursement in canton X is expected shortly.

Notes supplied with it:

> Lumivra Q3: net sales CHF 2.4m (plan 2.9m). Q4 forecast 3.1m.
> Share of new patients 10% → 12% Jul–Sep.
> Two big hospital groups listed it in Sept. Campaign went live August.
> Payer: canton X still reviewing, concerned about budget impact.

All seven letters are on.

## Depth

Board update: leadership. Short summary. If VERIFY is connected, hand off, since the
notes are a supplied source.

## The summary as the person sees it (VERIFY not connected)

```
TRUSTED check: board update on the Lumivra launch, for the board. Draft, not yet reviewed.

Tangible        1 to fix. The update gives no decision or ask for the board, and no next step.
Rule-compliant  nothing flagged in what was checked
Unbiased        2 to fix. Q3 is shown without its plan (CHF 2.4 million actual against CHF 2.9 million plan), so the miss is hidden, and the payer's budget concern is left out.
Safe            nothing flagged in what was checked
True            4 to fix. Q4 CHF 3.1 million is a forecast in your notes, not an actual. "Went up 20%" is 2 percentage points (10% to 12%). The notes do not say the campaign caused the listings, and they do not say reimbursement is expected shortly.
Ethical         nothing flagged in what was checked
Defensible      1 missing. The update does not say what it is based on; add "Source: affiliate sales notes" or similar.

Open for you: whether to state the Q4 figure as a forecast or wait for the actual.
Could not be checked: whether each figure matches your notes word for word, with a tool. VERIFY is not connected; it would test the 4 figures and 2 statements against your notes.

This check flags issues. It is not medical, legal, regulatory or ethics review.
```

## With VERIFY connected

The hand-off packet sent to the Prove skills (no results are shown here, because none
were returned in this example):

```
Text:       the three-sentence draft above
Priority:   "Q4 net sales of CHF 3.1 million"; "grew 20%"; "the August campaign drove two major hospital listings"; "expected shortly"
Sources:    the notes, as a pasted passage
Question:   board update on launch performance, read by the board
Clearer:    none given
```

Their lines would fill the True row (counts by status and the confidence line, with its
meaning) and the Defensible row ("record not built: no reviewer named"). The rest of the
summary stays the same.

## Why it is written this way

- The forecast stated as actual is the first note under True, because it is the error
  a board acts on.
- The missed plan and the dropped payer concern are Unbiased, not True: each statement
  left is correct, but the selection flatters (standard, True and Unbiased boundary).
- Short depth: one or two notes per row, no "Next step" line, because the board pack is
  internal.
- No VERIFY result is described (TR-H4).
