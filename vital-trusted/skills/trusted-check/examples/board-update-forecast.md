# Worked example: a board update with a forecast stated as actual (short depth)

Invented case for teaching. "Lumivra" is a fictional product; the affiliate and figures
are made up.

## Request

"Run TRUSTED on this before it goes into the board pack."

Draft:

> Lumivra delivered Q4 net sales of CHF 3.1 million, well ahead of Q3 (CHF 2.4
> million), as the August campaign drove two major hospital listings. Share of new
> patients grew 20%. Reimbursement in canton X is expected shortly.

Notes supplied with it:

> Lumivra Q3: net sales CHF 2.4m (plan 2.9m). Q4 forecast 3.1m.
> Share of new patients 10% → 12% Jul–Sep.
> Two big hospital groups listed it in Sept. Campaign went live August.
> Payer: canton X still reviewing, concerned about budget impact.

All seven letters are on.

## Depth

Board update: leadership. Short summary. The notes are a supplied source and True is on,
so the Prove skills run by hand at short depth: the priority claims, the counts and the
open items.

## The summary as the person sees it

```
TRUSTED check: board update on the Lumivra launch, for the board. Uncleared draft.

Tangible        1 to fix. The update gives no decision or ask for the board, and no next step.
Rule-compliant  nothing flagged in what was checked
Unbiased        2 to fix. Q3 is shown without its plan (CHF 2.4 million actual against CHF 2.9 million plan), so the miss is hidden, and the payer's budget concern is left out.
Safe            nothing flagged in what was checked
True            4 to fix. Q4 CHF 3.1 million is a forecast in your notes, not an actual, and "grew 20%" is not in your notes, which give 10% to 12%. The notes do not say the campaign caused the listings or that reimbursement is expected shortly.
                Compared by hand with your notes (not tool-checked): 5 factual claims: 1 found in your passage, 2 partly found, 1 not found in your passage, 1 your passage says something different, 0 no passage to compare with.
                No confidence figure: these results were not tool-checked.
Ethical         nothing flagged in what was checked
Defensible      1 missing. The update does not say what it is based on; add "Source: affiliate sales notes" or similar. Record note written by hand (not tool-checked): Uncleared draft.

Open for a person: whether to state the Q4 figure as a forecast or wait for the actual (your notes say "Q4 forecast 3.1m"); whether to keep "drove" and "expected shortly", which your notes do not say; the 20% figure, which your notes do not give.

This check flags issues. It is not medical, legal, regulatory or ethics review.
```

## The by-hand comparison behind the True counts

The packet sent to the Prove skills:

```
Text:       the three-sentence draft above
Priority:   "Q4 net sales of CHF 3.1 million"; "grew 20%"; "the August campaign drove two major hospital listings"; "expected shortly"
Sources:    the notes, as a pasted passage
Question:   board update on launch performance, read by the board
Reviewer:   none given
```

How each claim compared with the notes, by hand (not tool-checked):

| Claim in the draft | Result | Passage in your notes |
|---|---|---|
| "Q3 (CHF 2.4 million)" | Found in your passage | "Q3: net sales CHF 2.4m" |
| "Q4 net sales of CHF 3.1 million" | Your passage says something different | "Q4 forecast 3.1m" |
| "Share of new patients grew 20%" | Partly found: the 20% figure not in your passage | "Share of new patients 10% → 12% Jul–Sep" |
| "the August campaign drove two major hospital listings" | Partly found: that the campaign caused the listings not in your passage | "Two big hospital groups listed it in Sept. Campaign went live August." |
| "Reimbursement in canton X is expected shortly" | Not found in your passage | "canton X still reviewing, concerned about budget impact" |

These lines fill the True row and the Defensible row above. No reviewer was named, so the
record says "Uncleared draft".

## Why it is written this way

- The forecast stated as actual is the first note under True, because it is the error
  a board acts on.
- The missed plan and the dropped payer concern are Unbiased, not True: each statement
  left is correct, but the selection flatters (standard, True and Unbiased boundary).
- Short depth: one or two notes per row, no "Next step" line, because the board pack is
  internal.
- Each result is read by hand and labeled "not tool-checked". No tool result is
  described, and no confidence figure is shown (TR-H4).
- The 20% is partly found: the notes give 10% and 12%, and a figure worked out from them
  is not in the notes. No new figure is added in its place.
