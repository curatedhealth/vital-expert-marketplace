# Intake: trusted-check

Read the context card from `trusted-intake` first (`../trusted-intake/templates/context-card.md`).
If the conversation holds one for this work, take the reader, market, use, product status,
material and stakes from it, and take the active letters from `trusted-capability-map`.
Do not ask again for anything they hold.
When the conversation holds no card (this skill used alone), take what the message says, ask at most the questions below, and do not run intake separately.

## What this skill needs

| Need | Comes from | If it is missing |
|---|---|---|
| The finished draft | The person | Ask for it; there is nothing to check without it |
| Reader, to set the depth | The card, or the greeting and "to" line of the draft | Ask once |
| Market and use | The card | Ask only at full depth, and only if the answer changes what can be said |
| Sources the factual claims rest on | What the person supplied | At short or full depth, when the draft makes factual claims and no sources were supplied, ask once (question 3). Otherwise the letter that needs them reports "not checked" and says what it could not check |
| Label or data the draft rests on | What the person supplied | Do not ask separately; the letter that needs it reports "not checked" and says what it could not check |
| Which letters are on | The person's last choice, or all seven | Do not ask |

## Questions

If any input mentions a possible side effect, pregnancy or breastfeeding exposure, overdose, misuse or off-label use reported for a person, medication error, lack of effect or product quality problem, patient-safety's flag goes out first, before any of
these questions (TR-H14).

Ask at most one combined question, drawn from these three, and give the summary in the same reply unless a stop rule applies; none when the draft and the card answer them. Plain words, one line.

1. "Who will read this?" Ask only when the depth would differ and the draft does not show it; the summary still comes in the same reply, at the stricter depth.
2. "Which country is it for, and was it asked for or is it going out unprompted?" Ask only
   at full depth, when the card lacks both and a rule-compliance finding depends on them.
3. "Do you have the source for the figures and claims, so I can compare them by hand?"
   Ask once, only when the draft makes factual claims at short or full depth and nothing
   was supplied. This is the one rule for asking for sources.

## Never

- Ask for six fields in one message.
- Ask whether a tool is connected.
- Rewrite the draft at this step. This skill reads and counts.
