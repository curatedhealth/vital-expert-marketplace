# Output format: privacy note

Shown once, near the top or bottom of the reply, when the material or the draft held
details about a person. Never repeats them. Size it to the stakes (TR-H12).

## One line (internal, or details simply left out)

```
Your notes include details about an identifiable patient; I've left them out of <the
summary and the email>.
```

## Full note (leaving the company, or a case is needed)

```
People in this <email | deck | summary>

I left out <kinds of detail, e.g. the patient's name, age, village and the month of the
hospital stay> and kept the point: <e.g. "the payer's question about hospital costs during
treatment">. Other details could still point to someone, so I haven't called it
anonymous; your data privacy function decides that, and whether the case can be used
at all.

<If small numbers:> <n> figures are small counts that could point to individuals. What
minimum your company shows is set by your data privacy function; I've marked them.

<If named professionals:> I've referred to <role> instead of naming them, unless you
confirm they agreed to be named for this.

<If something happened to a patient on a product:> The notes mention <kind, e.g. a
hospital stay while on treatment>. It may need to go through your safety reporting
procedure; I haven't judged whether it is related.
```

Never write "anonymized", "de-identified", "safe to share" or "no personal data".
