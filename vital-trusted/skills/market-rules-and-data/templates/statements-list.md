# Output format: statements list

Part of the full market note for promotional or external material. One row per element
on the list for this market (`tasks/required-statements.md`), or per element of the
person's own template. Plain words; no clause numbers unless the person asks.

```
Statements usually expected for <kind of material> in <market>
(<from the rules read for this market | from your template, dated <date>>)

- Prescribing information, or a direct link to it ........ <in the draft | placeholder left | from another market | missing>
- Date of preparation or last revision ................... <placeholder left: set at approval | ...>
- Side-effect reporting statement ........................ <in the draft, link and company to fill | placeholder left | not confirmed for this market: ask your review process>
- Audience statement (who the material is for) .......... <in the draft | placeholder left> (usual practice)
- Job code ............................................... <placeholder left: from your approval system>
- <Other elements for this market, e.g. dispensing category, authorisation holder and address>

Not filled in by me: job code, dates, links and prescribing information text. They come
from your approval system and the approved label.
```

Status words are limited to those above. Never "complete", "compliant" or "OK". An
element marked "usual practice" or "not confirmed for this market" says so in the row.
