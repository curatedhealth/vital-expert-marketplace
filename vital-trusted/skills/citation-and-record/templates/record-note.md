# Output format: record note

What the person sees. Plain words; no status codes, field names or tool names
(TR-H13). No table. Size it to the stakes (TR-H12).

## Full note (work leaving the company)

```
<Status, one of the forms in ../../work-record/templates/status-line.md:
 Uncleared draft
 | Uncleared draft, reviewed by <name>, as you told me (only when the person said it)
 | Uncleared draft (to be cleared by <name>, <role if given>; with no name: a reviewer not named yet)
 | Uncleared draft (you said <name> cleared it; add role and date to record the clearance)
 | Cleared by <name>, <role>, <date> (only when the person named who cleared it, with role and date)>

What this covers: <the deliverable, in one line>
Sources: <n>, listed below the work <or: in the reference list>
Factual claims compared with their passages by hand: <n> (one checkable fact each; not tool-checked; counts only, never estimated)
<If no comparison ran before the record was asked for, this line reads "Factual claims compared with their passages by hand: none yet" and the five rows below are left out.>
  <n> found in your passage
  <n> partly found
  <n> not found in your passage
  <n> your passage says something different
  <n> no passage to compare with
Listed separately, not in the total: <n> opinion, recommendation or definition (left to the person)
Found in your passage means the passage says it. It does not mean the claim is true in the world.

Decisions
1. "<claim as written>": <revised | removed | escalated to <kind of process> | Cleared by <name>, <role>, <date>, who reviewed it: <reason as they gave it>>
2. ...

Still open (no decision yet)
1. "<claim or question>": <one plain line>; goes to <kind of process>.

Next step: this record goes with the work into your <promotional or medical> review, as your process names it. It does not replace it.

Record note written by hand; not tool-checked.
```

Only a person who has cleared the work, with role and date, goes after "Cleared by". A
person who will review it later goes in the placeholder "Uncleared draft (to be cleared by
<name>, <role if given>)", in the note and inside a drafted deliverable alike. A clearance
reported in the past without role or date is "Uncleared draft (you said <name> cleared it;
add role and date to record the clearance)". No other
status form is written (`../../work-record/templates/status-line.md`).

## Results you told me about

When the claim check was done elsewhere and its results are not in this conversation,
the counts block reads as follows. Every other line of the note stays as above. Write a
number wherever the person's own figures fix it (a total that the listed results add up
to, such as "7 of 9 found, 1 partly, 1 not found", or a list the person says is all), and
"not given" wherever nothing they said fixes it. "Four found and one partly found" with
no total and no "that is all" gives found 4, partly found 1, every other result not given
(`../../claim-assessment/references/statuses.md`, counts the person gave you).

```
Factual <claims | statements, in your words> compared with their passages by hand, as you told me: <n | not given> (not tool-checked; I did not see the check)
  found in your passage: <n | not given>
  partly found: <n | not given>
  not found in your passage: <n | not given>
  your passage says something different: <n | not given>
  no passage to compare with: <n | not given>
Listed separately, not in the total: opinion, recommendation or definition: <n | not given>
<If open items must be named:> Open items: send me the sentences and their results to list them here.
```

No percentage and no "% backed" figure goes into the note, even when the person gave one.
The factual total equals the sum of the five rows under it; opinion lines are never added
to it. Recount before sending. Name a source, data on file, attachment or approval only
when the person supplied it or said so; otherwise it is an open item.

## Short note (leadership or cross-team)

```
<Status, as in the full note>. <n> sources, <n> open items (<one-line list>). Not tool-checked.
```

## Never

- "Approved", "validated", "compliant", "verified", "ready to send", "ready to go", "good
  to go", "all set" or "fine to send".
- A clearer the person did not name, or a tool, model or assistant as clearer.
- The evidence table, unless asked.

The record note on its own carries no closing statement, even when it lists counts from a
check: those counts record a check, they do not show results. When the same reply also
shows check results per claim, or a source card given as a check, the statement comes
once, as the last line of the reply
(`../../claim-assessment/references/statuses.md`, the closing statement).

## Explanation only (no record or reference asked for)

A question about what a record, a reference list or a status means ("what is the
difference between a reference list and a record note?") gets two to four plain
sentences and nothing else: no template, no status line, no counts, no open items and no
closing statement. Count the sentences before sending: at least two and at most four,
with no list, heading or follow-up offer added after them.
