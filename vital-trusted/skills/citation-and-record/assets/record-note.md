# Output format: record note

What the person sees. Plain words; no status codes, field names or tool names
(TR-H13). No table. Size it to the stakes (TR-H12).

## Full note (work leaving the company)

```
<Uncleared draft | Record cleared by <name>, <role>, on <date as the person gave it>>

What this covers: <the deliverable, in one line>
Sources: <n>, listed below the work <or: in the reference list>
Claims checked against their passages: <n> <with VERIFY | not tool-checked>
  (the counts below appear only when VERIFY returned them; never estimated)
  <n> supported by the quoted passage
  <n> partly supported
  <n> not supported by the passage
  <n> contradicted by the passage
  <n> could not be checked (no passage available)
Support means the quoted passage says it. It does not mean the claim is true in the world.

Decisions
1. "<claim as written>": <revised | removed | escalated to <kind of process> | cleared by <name>, who reviewed it: <reason as they gave it>>
2. ...

Still open (no decision yet)
1. "<claim or question>": <one plain line>; goes to <kind of process>.

<If uncleared:> To be cleared by: <name the person gave | [name of the person who reviews it]>
Next step: this record goes with the work into your <kind of internal process>. It does not replace it.

<Record written with VERIFY | Record note built by hand; not tool-built>
```

## Short note (leadership or cross-team)

```
<Uncleared draft | Cleared by <name>, <role>, <date>>. <n> sources, <n> open items (<one-line list>). <Record written with VERIFY | not tool-built>.
```

## Never

- "Approved", "validated", "compliant", "verified" or "ready to send".
- A clearer the person did not name, or a tool, model or assistant as clearer.
- The evidence table, unless asked.
