# Open items handed to a person

An open item is a sentence a hand check could not settle. The packet gives a person what
they need to decide without reading the whole work again. It is not a clearance, and this
skill does not decide.

## What goes in each packet

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Claim | The sentence as written, and where it sits in the text |
| Result | One of: partly found, not found in your passage, your passage says something different, no passage to compare with |
| Source | The source as the person named it, or "no source supplied" |
| Passage | The quoted words, or a plain statement that none was available |
| Where in the source | Page, section or table. Empty only when there is no passage |
| Question | One question the person can answer without redoing the check |

Template: `../templates/open-items.md`. Label every packet "not tool-checked: read by
hand".

## Rules

- **One packet per open item.** Do not bundle two sentences in one.
- **Two sources that disagree are one packet**, with both quotes and both locations. Never
  drop one quietly.
- **The question does not carry the answer you prefer.** Write "Does the label allow this
  dose in this group?", not "Can we remove this?".
- **The signer is left blank.** Name the kind of review the item goes to, as your process
  names it, and leave the decision "not yet made" (`../governance.md`).
- **A safety report goes first.** If the packet touches a possible adverse event or product
  complaint, stop and send it to your safety reporting procedure before anything else.

## Which review, by deliverable

This is the one table the Prove skills and `work-record` use to name the next review. Name
it as "your <x> review, as your process names it". Never say which review applies: the
company's process decides. Where two could apply, name both. If the context card or the
person names the process, use their words instead.

| Deliverable | Name it as |
|---|---|
| Payer value story, payer deck, economic or budget material | "your market access or promotional review, and your medical review, as your process names it" |
| Material for doctors or other health professionals (detail aid, email, congress slide) | "your promotional review and your medical review, as your process names it" |
| Reply to a doctor's question (medical information letter) | "your medical information or medical review, as your process names it" |
| Advisory board pre-read, scientific summary for outside readers | "your medical review, as your process names it, and promotional review if your company treats it as promotional" |
| Patient leaflet, patient support or public material | "your medical and regulatory review, as your process names it" |
| Publication, abstract or poster | "your publications review, as your process names it" |
| Training material for nurses, doctors or other health professionals outside the company (counts as external, even when the company runs the training) | "your medical review, as your process names it, and promotional review if your company treats it as promotional" |
| Submission to an ethics committee (protocol pages, patient information, consent form) | "your clinical review for study submissions, as your process names it"; the committee's own review comes after and is not the company's |
| Protocol synopsis or other clinical study document | "your clinical document review, as your process names it" |
| Internal training slides for your own staff, sales-force training, field guide, congress booth panel (external depth: they leave the author's team) | "your <promotional or medical> review, as your process names it": promotional for sales-force material, field guides and booth panels; medical for medical or scientific training. Name both if both could apply |
| Internal board update, business review or brief | "the sign-off your process names"; no outside review is implied |
| Anything that mentions a possible side effect | "your safety reporting procedure" first, then the review above |

## What the person may decide

The person may keep the sentence, rewrite it, remove it, or send it on to another review.
This skill does not choose among these and does not record a choice the person did not
make.
