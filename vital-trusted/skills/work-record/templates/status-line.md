# Output format: status line

One line, used in the record note and, where the person wants it, on the work itself.
Only the person can raise a status above draft; the assistant records what they say
(TR-H5).

| Situation | Status line |
|---|---|
| Nothing stated | `Status: draft, not yet reviewed.` |
| Comments received | `Status: draft. Comments from <name> (<role>) received <date>, as you told me.` |
| Reviewed | `Status: reviewed by <name> (<role>) on <date>, as you told me. Not yet approved.` |
| Approved through a process | `Status: approved by <name> (<role>) through your <process> on <date>, reference <as given>, as you told me.` |
| Final, confirmed | Draft markings removed. Keep: `Approved by <name> (<role>) through your <process> on <date>, as you told me.` |
| Asked for approval, none stated | `Status: draft. [Approval: name, process, date]` |

Rules:

- Every element in angle brackets comes from the person. If one is missing, leave the
  bracket visible or ask; never fill it.
- Dates in full: "16 October 2026".
- If a reference is not given, leave it out rather than invent one.
- "As you told me" stays in the record note. On the work itself the person may drop it,
  since the statement is now theirs.
