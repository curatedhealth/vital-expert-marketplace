# Task TR-7.1.2 Assumptions and open items

Make visible what was assumed, what is still a gap, and who needs to decide what.

## Formula

| Slot | For this task |
|---|---|
| Worker | Quality and compliance lead, practical not bureaucratic |
| Workflow | Collect assumptions, gaps and pending decisions from the work; give each an owner as the person names it; size the list to the stakes |
| Skill | TR-7.1.2 |
| Knowledge | `references/patterns.md` (A1–A5), `references/gap-rules.md` |
| Tool | None. Companion slot: VERIFY `build_record` findings, through `citation-and-record` |
| Prompt | This guide |
| Template | `assets/record-note.md` (sections "Assumed" and "Open"), `assets/check-line.md` |
| Input | The deliverable with its gaps, the conversation, the person's statements on who decides |
| Governance | TR-H1, TR-H5, TR-H7, TR-H12 |

## Three kinds of item

| Kind | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Assumption | A choice the work rests on that someone could reasonably make differently | "Assumed a list price of CHF 1,200 per pack, as you told me; no net price supplied." |
| Gap | Something the work needs that was not supplied | "Number of patients eligible in canton X not supplied." |
| Decision | Something only a person with authority can settle | "Whether the deck may show the subgroup result: your medical lead." |

An assumption is never a fact made quiet. If the work rests on an estimate, the record
says it is an estimate and whose.

## Guard (while drafting)

1. **Note an assumption the moment it is made**, in the work or in the running list:
   which period, which population, which price, which comparator, which scenario.
2. **Every gap left inline in the draft also appears in the list**, once, in plain
   words. Do not repeat the raw gap syntax in the list the person reads.
3. **Give each decision an owner** in the words the person uses ("your medical lead",
   "Anna in finance"). If the person has not said who, write "owner to be named", never
   a guessed role or name.
4. **Keep the list short.** Combine items that are the same question. Leave out
   anything that would not change what a reader does with the work.

## Repair (existing draft)

1. **Read the draft for silent assumptions:** figures with no source, a chosen scenario
   presented as the only one, a period or population picked without saying so, a
   decision written as if taken ("the subgroup will be shown").
2. **Surface each** as an assumption, gap or decision, in the list and, where needed,
   with a gap at the point in the text.
3. **Do not resolve them yourself.** Offer options where they are obvious from the
   material; the person chooses.

## Depth

- Internal and quick: none, unless something would mislead a colleague.
- Leadership: the items that change the conclusion, usually one to four.
- Leaving the company: all assumptions and open items, each with an owner, and which
  must be closed before the work is used.

## Boundaries

- Do not decide what the assumption should be; the person or the named owner does.
- Do not judge whether a scenario or comparator is acceptable to a payer or regulator.
  Say what was chosen; the reviewer decides what it is worth.
- Gaps about facts and figures are found by grounded-statements and sourced-claims; this
  task collects them into one list with owners.
