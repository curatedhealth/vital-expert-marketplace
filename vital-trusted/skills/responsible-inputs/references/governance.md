# Governance for responsible inputs

## Boundaries

- This skill covers what goes into the assistant and other people's work used in it. It
  does not cover what the finished output reveals or claims (`market-rules-and-data`,
  `claims-and-label`), how much review or disclosure the result needs
  (`responsible-use`), or the record of how it was made (`work-record`).
- It names the kind of material and asks the question. It never decides it.
- It does not give legal, privacy, compliance or licensing advice.

## Decisions this skill never makes

| Decision | Who makes it, in plain words |
|---|---|
| Whether a tool is approved for a kind of material | The company's AI use policy and its owner |
| Whether data is anonymous or de-identified | The privacy or data protection function |
| Whether a transfer of personal data abroad is permitted | The privacy or data protection function |
| Whether information is inside information or price-sensitive | Legal or compliance |
| Whether something already pasted is an incident, and what follows | The privacy or data protection function, under the company's procedure |
| Whether a use of copyrighted or licensed work is permitted | The library, licensing or legal team; the contract owner for client material |
| Whether a reported event is a side effect to report | The safety function (`patient-safety`) |

## Internal procedures

TRUSTED supports the company's own procedures and never replaces them (TR-H11). Name the
kind of procedure in plain words: "your AI use policy", "your data protection function",
"your compliance team", "your library or licensing team". Never name or invent a
specific company's SOP, and never say that a use satisfies a policy.

## Stop rules

Stop and ask before continuing when:

- The material appears to contain identifiable patient or personal details and the
  person has not said their policy covers this tool for them. Ask; offer to work from a
  version with identifiers removed.
- The material looks like undisclosed results or deal information of a listed company.
  Name the category and ask; offer the public version.
- The person asks you to confirm a tool is approved, data is anonymous or a use is fair.
  Explain who decides and what you can do instead.
- The person asks to reproduce a whole copyrighted or licensed work for use outside the
  company. Offer a summary with citation and ask about the license.

Do not stop for public material, the person's own non-sensitive work, or a request to
understand or summarize material for one's own use where nothing sensitive appears.

## Never claimed

- That a tool is approved, allowed, compliant or safe for any material.
- That data is anonymous, anonymized or de-identified.
- What a tool does with pasted data (storage, training, deletion), unless the person
  supplied the terms, and then only as the terms say.
- That a use is fair use, permitted or licensed.
- That this skill satisfies the company's AI literacy, data protection or copyright
  obligations.

## Closing statement

When this skill's check line reaches the TRUSTED summary, the summary ends with: "This
check flags issues. It is not medical, legal, regulatory or ethics review." Direct
replies and input notes do not carry it.
