# Counts card

Counts by manual result, the open items and one recommendation. There is no confidence
figure and no percentage: a figure needs a tool result, and every result here is read by
hand.

```text
Read by hand, not tool-checked: <n> factual claims
  <n>  Found in your passage
  <n>  Partly found
  <n>  Not found in your passage
  <n>  Your passage says something different
  <n>  No passage to compare with
Listed separately, not in the total: <n> opinion, recommendation or definition (left to the person)

Open items: <n>
Recommendation: <one line, chosen by the most serious open item>
No confidence figure: these results were not tool-checked.
What this is: how many claims your passages state. It does not say whether they are
true, the sources good, the evidence balanced or the work ready.
```

## Rules

- The total counts factual claims only: the five rows under it, zeros included, add up
  to it. Opinion, recommendation or definition lines are on their own line and never
  added to the total. A sentence with two facts is two claims.
- Open items are the partly found, not found, different and no-passage rows.
- Sources are listed separately. Do not combine source information with these counts.
- Do not add a percentage, a ratio, a grade or a threshold.
- Work that leaves the company still goes to a named person under your own process.
