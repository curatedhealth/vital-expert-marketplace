# DH Quality Management & Compliance

Install **DH Quality Management & Compliance** from `curatedhealth/vital-expert-plugins`, then paste the Digital Health Library key. The key is not in this pack.

This pack is only DH-03.02 Quality Management & Compliance. Other Digital Health cores are separate packs.

The connector is `https://mcp.vital.expert/api/mcp/library`. Do not add a second connector.

Choose one heading, then one job. The job holds its tasks as sections. Ask which one task, then follow that section. A missing fact stays a gap.

## Skills

- `design-controls-and-risk-management`. Set up and run design controls and ISO 14971 risk management for medical device software (SaMD, DTx, connected-device apps): which rules apply under the FDA QMSR and EU MDR, what IEC 62304 gives in each market, a trace matrix from user needs through design inputs to verification and validation with every orphan and gap found, a risk file with hazard-to-harm chains and controls in priority order, hazards seeded from comparable products' software recalls, and a design review pack that leaves risk acceptance to named people.
- `design-control-and-risk-monitoring`. Check whether design controls and ISO 14971 risk management are actually working for a medical device software product (SaMD, DTx, connected-device app): walk every hazard to its control and verification in the person's own files, find broken trace links, read complaints and comparable products' software recalls against the risk file to see whether observed harms were foreseen and whether controls held, and turn the findings into a repair plan ranked only from the team's own scores, with the owners they name.
- `document-and-change-control`. Set up document and record control for a medical device software quality system and work out what a software change means for regulators.
- `documentation-compliance-monitoring`. Check whether a medical device software company's documents, records and change control are in order, and plan the repairs.
- `qms-design-and-governance`. Design the quality management system and its governance for a regulated digital health product (SaMD, DTx, connected device): what the QMS must cover in each market, which requirement each process answers (FDA QMSR and ISO 13485 as FDA restates them, MDR Article 10(9), the PRRC under Article 15, MDSAP), who owns each process, what management review and internal audit read and decide, and which operating model to choose.
- `qms-maturity-monitoring`. Check whether a digital health manufacturer's quality management system and its governance actually work: the evidence each process runs (against the QMS process map), management review coverage and actions, audit finding closure and repeat findings, CAPA ageing and effectiveness checks, reporting timeliness, FDA Form 483 citation patterns as context, and a repair plan with the owners the person names.
- `audit-capa-and-complaints`. Run audit, complaint and CAPA work for regulated digital health and SaMD under the QMSR, the EU MDR and UK rules: write up an audit finding (requirement, evidence, gap) with a proposed MDSAP 1 to 5 grade, decide what a complaint needs (is it a complaint, is it an EU serious incident, an FDA MDR 30-day or 5-day report, an 806 correction), compute reporting due dates from the awareness date, find comparable public recalls, and draft a CAPA plan whose depth matches the risk.
- `capa-effectiveness-monitoring`. Check whether CAPAs actually worked and whether complaint handling is under control for regulated digital health and SaMD: test each closed CAPA against the effectiveness criteria the person set (evidence present or not), find complaints that recur after a CAPA closed, age open complaints, check reporting decisions against the EU MDR, UK and FDA clocks, and run a CAPA and complaint review with people's scores and named owners.
- `quality-management-capability-map`. The map of the VITAL Quality Management & Compliance pack for digital health and SaMD makers.
- `digital-health-expert`. Find and download a VITAL Digital Health expert (a ready-made role such as the SaMD Regulatory Affairs Director, the Quality & Software Lifecycle Director or the Regulatory Submission Manager) to run on the person's own assistant.
