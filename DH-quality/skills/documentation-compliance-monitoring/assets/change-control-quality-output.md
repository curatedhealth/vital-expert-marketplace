# Output format: change control quality

Short mode is the default for a direct question. 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line.

## Short mode

1. **Answer:** changes in the log, period, findings by check (count of changes and rate, base stated).
2. **Findings on hazard-touching changes:** listed first, with dates.
3. **Your rules:** met or not met with the count behind each, and the owner you named; or "no rules set, no verdict".
4. **Not measurable:** checks the log's columns could not support.
5. **Record:** "AI-assisted draft. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.
6. One line offering the full table or the repair plan (B1).

## Full mode

### Summary
Log, export date, period, counts by check.

### Findings
| Change | Check | Detail (dates, fields) | Row |
|---|---|---|---|

### Counts and your rules
| Check | Changes | Rate (base) | Your rule | Result | Owner you named | Row |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Not measurable
Columns missing and the checks they block.

### Public context (only if asked)
| Firm, product, version range | Event date | Class | What the record states | Source, retrieved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
Context only; not evidence about your log.

### Record
AI-assisted draft. Entries are never edited or backdated; repairs are new dated entries. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
