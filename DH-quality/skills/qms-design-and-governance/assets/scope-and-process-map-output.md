# Output format: QMS scope and process map (design draft)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections, then table rows that the person's facts do not touch (list them by P number in one line); count every table cell except the Rows column; offer the full version in one line. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the requirement set per market in one line each (with row ids: US QS1, QS3, QS7 plus additions QS11, QS13, design controls QS9; EU EU1, BX1, BX2, EU8), and the processes that have no procedure yet.
2. **Scope facts:** products, sites, outsourced parties, markets, class per market as the person stated it. `[GAP]` for anything not given.
3. **Process map (compact):** one row per process: Process · US basis · EU basis · Rows. MDSAP column only if multi-region. The vigilance row (P12) always carries the clocks as facts: US 30 calendar days (VG17); EU 15, 10 and 2 days from awareness (VG4 to VG6). Where the person outsources complaint intake, the complaints row carries VG11.
4. **What it means for you:** two or three consequences from the rows.
5. **Open questions:** what the person or their regulatory owner must supply.
6. **Record:** "Design draft. Classes and scope are yours. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.
7. One line offering the full version.
