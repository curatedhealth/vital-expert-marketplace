# Output format: governance effectiveness indicators

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line. Before handing over, count the words of the answer (excluding the calls list and table source columns). If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the two or three indicators that stand out, with numbers, as of the date used.
2. **Indicators (compact):** Indicator · Number · Your rule · Met / missed. If the person has set no rules, skip the table: give the numbers once in the Answer and ask for the rules once.
3. **Context, labeled:** FDA FY 2025 device 483 categories, pre-QMSR, one or two lines (for a CAPA question: 820.100(a) 279 and 820.100(b) 63, QS53, QS54); safety events for your product if requested.
4. **What it means for you:** two lines from the rows.
5. **Next measurements:** the data that would sharpen the picture.
6. **Record:** "Indicator draft on your data. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.
7. One line offering the full version.
