# Maturity criteria: what evidence each process can be checked against

Interpretive reference for A1. It lists criteria the evidence can be checked against, process by process, each with its row. It is not a maturity scale and assigns no levels or scores. Row ids point to `quality-systems-research.md` (QS, EU, VG, CH), `qms-requirements-additions.md` (BX) and `process-map-requirements.md` (P1 to P16).

## Whose scale

1. **The person's scale, if they have one.** Use it as written: its levels, its definitions, its evidence rules. Apply it only where the person's definitions decide the level; where a definition is unclear for a process, say so and ask.
2. **No scale.** Present the criteria below with the evidence found against each, and no level, score, color or average. Say "no maturity scale given; criteria and evidence only".
3. **The register's starter rule.** The VITAL framework register (CF-12.03.02.3.2.1, 19 October 2026 download) carries a starter rule: a process passes when it has an owner, a record from the last period, and a review of that record; a process missing any one fails; processes are never averaged into one score. Offer it as an option, labeled "register starter rule, yours to adopt or replace". Apply it only if the person adopts it.
4. **MDSAP grading is not a maturity scale.** It grades individual nonconformities 1 to 5 from QMS impact, repeat, missing procedure with failure to implement, and release of nonconforming devices (QS44 to QS46). Use it only to read graded findings the person already holds.

## Evidence rules (all processes)

- A procedure is evidence that a process is designed, not that it runs. "Do not claim maturity from a handbook" (register starter rule). Look for records from the period the person names.
- Evidence is what the person supplies or describes. The model reads it; it does not audit the site.
- Each criterion gets one of: evidence seen (what, dated), evidence described but not seen, no evidence, not applicable per the person.
- No criterion is marked met or failed unless the person's scale or the adopted starter rule says what met means.

## Criteria by process

| Process (A1 map) | Criteria the evidence can be checked against | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| P1 Documents, records, quality manual, medical device file | QMS documented to comply with ISO 13485 as incorporated; document and record control elements present; technical documentation kept at least 10 years after the last device (15 for implantables) for the EU | QS7, BX7, EU6 |
| P2 Management responsibility and review | Management review records exist for the period and can be produced to FDA; reviews show risk-based and resource decisions; quality problems, complaints and CAPA reach the review | QS15, QS16, QS19, BX7, QS35 |
| P3 Regulatory compliance strategy, PRRC | Strategy covers conformity assessment and modification management; at least one PRRC with the Article 15 qualification, or one at the disposal of a micro or small enterprise; shared PRRC responsibilities in writing | EU3, EU8, EU9, EU11 |
| P5 Risk management | Risk file reviewed and updated when new information or unforeseen risks emerge; production and PMS information evaluated against hazards and benefit-risk | QS22, EU15 |
| P6 Design and development | Design controls applied where required (class II, III, software-automated class I); design and development file holds or references all records for design requirements | QS9, QS24, BX10 |
| P8 Purchasing and outsourcing | Outsourced processes and purchased product monitored and controlled; suppliers selected and controlled | BX11, BX1 |
| P10 Change control | Changes evaluated for risk and impact before implementation, including software changes; software modifications treated as design changes; substantial QMS changes notified to the notified body | QS34, CH9, EU24 |
| P11 Complaint handling | Investigated or reportable complaints record the seven US items; a justification is kept when a similar complaint is not reinvestigated | QS11, QS12 |
| P12 Vigilance and reporting | Written MDR procedures cover identification, a standardized reportability review and timely transmission; event files record the deliberation; EU reports within 15, 10 or 2 days of awareness, counted in calendar days from day 0; non-reported corrections carry a justification reviewed by a designated person | VG20, VG21, VG4 to VG6, VG12, VG24 |
| P13 Post-market surveillance | PMS system in place; trend-reporting method and observation period in the PMS plan | BX2, VG9 |
| P14 Internal audit | Internal and supplier audit reports exist for the period and can be produced to FDA; audits run against aligned requirements | QS15, BX15 |
| P15 Nonconforming product and CAPA | CAPA depth matches the size of the problem and the risk; CAPA effectiveness is verified; a repeat nonconformity within three years signals a correction not adequately taken | QS18, EU4, QS47 |

Processes P4, P7, P9 and P16 have no record-level criteria in the rows beyond their presence in the A1 map; check them against the person's own procedures and say so.

## What a maturity check never does

- Never assigns a level, score, color or average without the person's scale or the adopted starter rule.
- Never states that the QMS is effective, compliant or ready for inspection or audit (`governance.md`).
- Never infers that a process works from the absence of findings, recalls or 483s.
