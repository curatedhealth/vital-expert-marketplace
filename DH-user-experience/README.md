# DH User Experience Excellence

Install **DH User Experience Excellence** from `curatedhealth/vital-expert-marketplace`, then paste the Digital Health Library key. The key is not in this pack.

This pack is only DH-02.04 User Experience Excellence. Other Digital Health cores are separate packs.

The connector is `https://mcp.vital.expert/api/mcp/library`. Do not add a second connector.

Choose one heading, then one job. The job holds its tasks as sections. Ask which one task, then follow that section. A missing fact stays a gap.

## Skills

- `accessibility-compliance-monitoring`. Check a digital health release against the accessibility requirement it set: sample screens and complete processes against the claimed WCAG version and level (2.0, 2.1 or 2.2) or EN 301 549 clause, summarize and age the accessibility issue log (criterion, level, the severity the team assigned, status) against that target, separate day-one tasks from later screens, and lay out the release decision under the team's own rule. Says plainly that automated scans cover only part of WCAG.
- `accessibility-standards-implementation`. Work out which accessibility rules apply to a digital health product and turn them into a release requirement. Who the organization is (US federal, state or local, HHS-funded, contractor, private seller, EU or UK public body) and where it sells decides whether Section 508, ADA Title II, HHS Section 504, Section 1557, the EAA, the Web Accessibility Directive, UK PSBAR or the Equality Act apply, and which WCAG version and EN 301 549 edition each cites, with dates as moved by the 2026 interim rules. Maps barriers to WCAG criteria and records assistive checks.
- `clinical-workflow-ux-design`. Design how a digital health screen and its service fit real clinical work: the task, role and moment; data shown and withheld; the step it replaces or adds; a service blueprint on request; use errors and critical tasks per FDA human factors guidance (reissued 3 August 2026) and MDR Annex I; controls; what CDS Criterion 4 and ONC decision support rules ask the interface to show; options scored by the person; the formative plan or service pilot.
- `design-system-adoption-monitoring`. Measure whether a digital health design system is used as built: component adoption across products, version drift (which products run old or forked components, by owner, with clinical and medical device surfaces shown separately), accessibility status of each component version in use, checks of a live surface against the component contract (states, WCAG 2.2 criteria such as target size, content it must never invent), and a review naming the fix owner and the version that carries each fix.
- `design-system-development`. Build and govern a design system for digital health products: write a component contract (states, WCAG 2.2 criteria it must meet such as 24 by 24 CSS pixel targets, test evidence, content it must never invent, allowed surfaces), assess a component change (which products and clinical surfaces to recheck, and the change-control questions when it sits in a medical device user interface under MDR Annex I or FDA human factors guidance), and plan adoption and contribution (lifecycle status, build, promote or deprecate, owners).
- `digital-health-expert`. Find and download a VITAL Digital Health expert (a ready-made role such as the SaMD Regulatory Affairs Director, the Quality & Software Lifecycle Director or the Regulatory Submission Manager) to run on the person's own assistant.
- `patient-and-clinician-user-research`. Plan and run formative user research with patients, caregivers and clinicians for a digital health product: the decision the study serves, user groups and how many per group, methods (interviews, contextual inquiry, task sessions, remote sessions), the ethics screen, session records with stops and participants' words, SUS scoring from the person's file, and turning repeated stops into actions or refusals with owners.
- `usability-monitoring`. Read usability evidence for a digital health product against the person's own acceptance rules: use errors and task success by task and by the severity the person assigned; whether a test meets FDA validation conditions (15 per user group, no employees, no think-aloud, final UI); SUS with published benchmarks as context; whether each risk control is in the shipped build; field signals and comparable recalls; and a release review with repairs and owners.
- `user-experience-capability-map`. The map of the VITAL User Experience Excellence pack for digital health products.
- `user-research-quality-monitoring`. Check whether patient and clinician user research for a digital health product was sound and acted on: whether participants matched the question and user groups, whether the method supports the claim made from it (formative vs human factors validation, think-aloud, employees), protocol completeness, GRIPP2 reporting of patient involvement, insights traced to decisions and shipped changes, open and unowned findings, SUS across rounds, and a review of unactioned stops with the owners the person names.
