# Output format: adoption and governance plan

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** products behind current on clinical or device surfaces with their owners, the governance decisions still open, and the top of the backlog if you scored it.
2. **Version map (compact):** Component · Current (yours, or highest in inventory, labeled) · Products behind · Owner · Clinical / device.
3. **Moves:** for each product behind, the date or reason its owner gave, or "not given".
4. **Governance:** your choices in your words; open elements with the public options and rows in one line each.
5. **Backlog:** ranked only on your scores, with the tool and inputs; or "not ranked: scores needed".
6. **Open questions (at most three).**
7. **Record:** "Plan draft. Criteria, statuses, rules, scores, owners and dates are yours. Clears: the person you name. Record built with verify.build_record once they clear it; until then, uncleared draft. AI-assisted."
8. One line offering the full version.
