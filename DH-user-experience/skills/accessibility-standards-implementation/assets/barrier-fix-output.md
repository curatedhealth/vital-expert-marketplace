# Output format: barrier fix and assistive check

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for one barrier; 250 to 450 words)

1. **Answer:** criterion number and name, its level in your target version, whether it is in your target, and the barrier's state: open, change proposed, or change made and checked with the named method.
2. **Barrier:** screen, task, what was observed and how.
3. **Requirement:** as sourced, with row; or "criterion text not quoted here `[GAP]`".
4. **Change:** yours, or labeled "proposed".
5. **Path:** the steps a person using the named method takes to complete the task after the change.
6. **Check:** method, build, date, who, where it breaks; "assistive check not yet run" if so.
7. **Record:** "Barrier record draft. Not a conformance statement. AI-assisted. To be cleared by NAME; the record is built with verify.build_record once NAME clears it." Until a named person clears it: "Uncleared draft." Who clears it: the owner the person named, in their words, or "owner not named". The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then the line reads "uncleared draft".
8. One line offering the full version.
