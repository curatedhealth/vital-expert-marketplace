# Output format: equity question

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the one or two steps where the chosen groups are most likely left out on the evidence given, and the groups no data can see.
2. **Frame:** product, steps, market, subgroups ("chosen by you" or "offered, not yet chosen"), covered-entity status as your legal owner sees it (or `[GAP]`).
3. **Groups by step (compact):** Subgroup · Step · Barrier · Rows and dates · Your counts (rate, interval, difference from your reference) or "no counts".
4. **Unseen groups:** each `[GAP]` with the record that would show it; goes to A2.
5. **Open questions (at most three):** the subgroups if not chosen, then covered-entity status, then the counts that would decide most.
6. **Record:** "Equity question draft on facts you supplied. Not a legal opinion. Cleared by [the person you name]; the record is built with verify.build_record once that person clears it." Without a name: "uncleared draft."
7. One line offering the full version.
