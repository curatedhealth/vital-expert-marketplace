# Output format: co-design trace

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** traced credited changes out of all credited, and which changes are not supported (no capture link, planned before capture, staff-only).
2. **Verdict (only with your rule):** pass or miss against your trace rule; otherwise "no rule set".
3. **Accepted but not shipped or without version:** insight ids.
4. **What it means:** two or three conditional consequences with rows; any "co-designed" wording as a stop.
5. **Record:** "Trace draft on your files. AI-assisted." Then: "Cleared by [name]; record built with verify.build_record." or "Uncleared draft; a named person clears it, then the record is built with verify.build_record."
6. One line offering the full version.
