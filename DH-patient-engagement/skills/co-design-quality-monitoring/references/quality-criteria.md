# Co-design quality criteria: what the sources say

Interpretive reference for every task. Each criterion cites a row in `patient-engagement-research.md` (PE-), `ux-research.md` (UX-) or the register starting rules (R-, below). None of these sources sets a pass mark for co-design; the person's rules turn a number into a verdict.

## 1. Trace: does a change come from what participants said?

| Criterion | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Change to capture | List changes claimed to come from co-design; point each at a capture row. | R-16, R-17 |
| No capture | Mark a change with no capture as not from this research. | R-18 |
| Already planned | Do not credit co-design for a change the team already planned. Where the change was in the backlog before the first linked capture, report it as "planned before capture"; the capture may still support it. | R-14 |
| Accepted but not shipped | An accepted insight should name the version that will show it. | R-12 |
| Difference made | UK Standard "Impact": identify and share the difference involvement makes. A framework, not a mandated rule. | PE-65, PE-68 |
| GRIPP2 outcomes | GRIPP2-SF asks for results and outcomes of involvement. [PAPER] doi:10.1136/bmj.j3453 | PE-91 |

What the trace does not show: that a change worked, that patients benefit, or that the product is "patient-centered". A trace shows provenance only.

## 2. Participant fit: were the right people in the room?

| Criterion | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Planned vs recruited | Compare recruited roles with the participant type on the question; note a session that did not include that type; state whether the decision still stands. | R-19, R-20, R-21 |
| Staff standing in | Do not treat staff standing in for patients as patient co-design. | R-5 |
| Carers | A carer's report is an observer report, from someone other than the patient or a health professional. Count it as carer evidence. | PE-13 |
| Representativeness | Non-probability samples may not be representative; the choice must be weighed when interpreting the data. | PE-06 |
| Saturation | No set criteria for evaluating saturation. A claim of saturation needs the team's own stated basis. | PE-09 |
| Inclusive opportunities | Involvement partnerships accessible and including a range of people, informed by community and research needs (framework). | PE-66 |
| Consent | MHRA guidance (advisory, device human factors) expects informed consent where practicable and documented risks and benefits to participants, even in formative work. Ask whether consent was recorded; do not judge its adequacy. | UX-UK-14, UX-UK-19 |
| Conflicts | For EU joint clinical assessment, patient experts must declare interests and have none in the developer's sector that could affect impartiality. If panel members are paid by the developer, note it for the regulatory owner. | PE-56 |
| Lay membership benchmark | NICE committees have at least two lay members. This is NICE's rule for its own committees, context only, never a target for a company panel. | PE-61 |

## 3. Reporting quality

| Criterion | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Short form | GRIPP2-SF: five items (aims, methods, results, outcomes, critical perspective), for work where involvement is secondary. | PE-91 |
| Long form | GRIPP2-LF: 34 items, for work where involvement is the main focus. The item list beyond the categories quoted in PE-90 is not in the research rows `[GAP]`: check against the paper itself. | PE-90 |
| How built | Three-round Delphi plus a consensus meeting. | PE-92 |
| Six UK Standards | Inclusive Opportunities, Working Together, Support and Learning, Governance, Communications, Impact. A framework adaptable to situations. | PE-65 |
| Governance standard | Involve the public in research management, regulation, leadership and decision making. | PE-67 |

Report each item as present, partial or `[GAP]` with the location in the person's report. No score and no overall grade unless the person sets a scale.

## 4. Unresolved points

| Criterion | Rule | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Name it | Name the point and the count. | R-22 |
| Owner | Name the owner. | R-23 |
| Date | Name the date the accept, defer or refuse will be written. The date is the person's. | R-15 |
| Not only in the transcript | Do not leave a repeated point only in the transcript. | R-24 |
| Safety | A point that touches a safety constraint is decided by the person's clinical safety or risk owner. | R-13 |
