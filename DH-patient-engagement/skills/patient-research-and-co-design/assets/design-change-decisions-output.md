# Output format: design change decisions

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** decided points (accept, defer, refuse) with change id and version for accepted ones; open points; stops.
2. **Ranking (only if you scored):** the order from your scores, "not a recommendation".
3. **Log checks:** decisions missing a reason, owner, change or version.
4. **Stops:** safety-touching changes, patient-facing copy, regulatory use; owner as you named them, or "owner not named".
5. **Record:** "Decision log draft. Decisions and scores are yours. AI-assisted." Then: "Cleared by [name]; record built with verify.build_record." or "Uncleared draft; a named person clears it, then the record is built with verify.build_record."
6. One line offering the full version.
