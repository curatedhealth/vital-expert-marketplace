# Output format: results and partnership indicators

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap and end with the same record line: who clears it, and that the record is built with `verify.build_record` once a named person clears it; otherwise "uncleared draft".

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** the two or three indicators that stand out, with numbers, denominators and the as-of date.
2. **Activities (compact):** Activity · Result · Your target · Met, missed or unscored. Off-list claims listed for your review owner.
3. **Attrition:** usage half-life and dropout attrition, with the number observable.
4. **Support and partners:** patients above the year's figure (2026: $623, CP-US-27), cash-equivalent items, partnership flags with rows ([INDUSTRY]); then the stop for your compliance owner.
5. **Record:** "Indicator draft on your data. Clears: [person or role as you named them, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft."
6. One line offering the full version.
