# Output format: result definitions (measurement plan draft)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap and end with the same record line: who clears it, and that the record is built with `verify.build_record` once a named person clears it; otherwise "uncleared draft".

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** one line per activity: the action that counts, the period, the record, the denominator. Activities with no record named.
2. **Attrition (digital programs):** how usage is read, and that dropout and nonusage attrition are reported separately with a curve and half-life (PE-102, PE-103).
3. **Questionnaire (if used):** the properties to check, labeled instrument criteria, not targets (PE-93, PE-95, PE-96).
4. **What it cannot show:** at most three lines (sample, clinical benefit, who was not reached).
5. **Targets:** yours, or "no target set".
6. **Record:** "Measurement plan draft. Definitions and targets are yours. Clears: [person or role as you named them, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft."
7. One line offering the full version.
