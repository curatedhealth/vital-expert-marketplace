# Advocacy partnership terms: what the sources require

Interpretive reference for task A2. Every rule cites its row in `patient-engagement-research.md` (PE-), `channels-partners-research.md` (CP-) or `promotion-and-claims-research.md` (PC-). Rows marked [INDUSTRY] are self-regulatory codes, not law: say so every time one is applied. Read on 19 October 2026 by the research pass; not owner-reviewed.

## 2. The boundary note: three facts the person must state

From the old "Partner boundary" task, kept as the spine of A2. The person states each; the model never fills them.

1. **Role.** Co-author, reviewer or distributor only.
2. **Payment.** Paid (amount, purpose, for what service), supported in kind, or not paid.
3. **Approval of patient-facing words.** Who approves, by role or name as the person gives it.

## 4. Independence and editorial control

- The organization's independence in political judgment, policies and activities must be assured; companies must not ask the organization to promote a prescription medicine (PE-70) [INDUSTRY].
- No influence on the text of sponsored material in the company's commercial favor; factual corrections allowed (EFPIA PE-72; ABPI PE-81) [INDUSTRY].
- No company may require to be the sole funder of the organization or its programs (PE-73) [INDUSTRY].
- Public use of the organization's logo or material needs written permission stating purpose and manner (PE-74) [INDUSTRY].
- If the organization is to be an EMA eligible organization: it must follow a code governing independence from sponsors and publish statutes, funding sources and activities (PE-51), and disclose each funder's contribution in absolute terms and as a percentage of budget, annually (PE-50).
- US independent charity patient assistance: donors exert no direct or indirect influence or control over the charity or its program (PE-88); disease funds defined by widely recognized clinical standards and covering a broad spectrum of products (PE-86); donor reports limited to aggregates (PE-87). A manufacturer-controlled foundation is not independent (PE-85).
- Germany, consumer-facing material: publications whose advertising purpose is unclear are banned for device advertising to the public (PC-DE-11); EU: paid editorial content not identified as paid is always unfair (PC-EU-12).

## 6. When patients from the partner give regulatory or HTA input

- EMA: individual patient experts sign a declaration of interest and confidentiality undertaking (PE-45); organizational eligibility is re-assessed every year (PE-46); an organization can qualify with a European focus and offices or members covering at least 50 percent of EU/EEA Member States (PE-49).
- EU HTA: patients in joint work must have no financial or other interests in the developers' industrial sector that could affect impartiality, and must declare interests (PE-56); they act in an individual capacity, not for an organization (PE-57). A company-funded advocate may therefore be unable to take part; that judgment is the person's compliance owner's.
- NICE: organizations can comment on draft scopes and recommendations and submit evidence (PE-64).

## 7. US payments to a partner

Any payment to an advocacy partner that could induce referrals, purchases or recommendations of a product payable by a Federal health care program sits under the Anti-Kickback Statute (CP-US-01, CP-US-02), where one purpose is enough (CP-US-08) and knowledge of the statute is not needed (CP-US-03). The personal services safe harbor requires, among other conditions, a signed writing of at least one year with compensation set in advance at fair market value and not tied to referrals (CP-US-09, CP-US-10); every condition must be met (CP-US-07). Laying these out is the model's job; whether an arrangement meets them stops for the person's compliance or legal owner.
