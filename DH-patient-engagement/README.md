# DH Patient Engagement & Advocacy

Install **DH Patient Engagement & Advocacy** from `curatedhealth/vital-expert-plugins`, then paste the Digital Health Library key. The key is not in this pack.

This pack is only DH-02.03 Patient Engagement & Advocacy. Other Digital Health cores are separate packs.

The connector is `https://mcp.vital.expert/api/mcp/library`. Do not add a second connector.

Choose one heading, then one job. The job holds its tasks as sections. Ask which one task, then follow that section. A missing fact stays a gap.

## Skills

- `co-design-quality-monitoring`. Check whether patient and carer co-design was done well and actually used in a digital health product or program: trace each design change credited to co-design back to what participants said, flag changes the team had already planned, check that participants matched the research question (patients, not staff standing in), check reporting against GRIPP2 and the UK Standards for Public Involvement, and track repeated points left undecided to an owner and a decision date the person sets.
- `digital-health-expert`. Find and download a VITAL Digital Health expert (a ready-made role such as the SaMD Regulatory Affairs Director, the Quality & Software Lifecycle Director or the Regulatory Submission Manager) to run on the person's own assistant.
- `engagement-and-advocacy-impact-monitoring`. Check whether a patient engagement program and its advocacy partnerships deliver, for a digital health product (SaMD, DTx, connected device, health app): result definitions tied to the action the program asked for (not attendance or followers), results per activity from the person's records, unscored activities and off-list claims, dropout and nonusage attrition with a usage half-life, support per patient per year against the 2026 safe-harbor figure, partnership agreement and disclosure gaps (EFPIA, ABPI), and one decision per missed activity with the owner the person names.
- `engagement-kpi-dashboards`. Define, compute and place patient engagement KPIs for a digital health program or product (DTx, SaMD app, patient support program): KPI cards with numerator, valid denominator, period, grain and source; reach, adoption, penetration, effective engagement and attrition computed from the person's own event file; nonusage and dropout attrition curves with usage half-life; and one dashboard per audience with its refresh job, owner and visible timestamp.
- `engagement-programs-and-advocacy-partnerships`. Design a patient engagement program and its advocacy partnerships for a digital health product (SaMD, DTx, connected device, health app): purpose, the action it asks and refuses, activities and the content each may use, what program material may say, the boundary and written terms with a patient organization (role, payment, editorial independence, EFPIA and ABPI disclosure, EMA and EU HTA conflict rules), and a check of any support to patients against the US patient engagement safe harbor (2026 cap), beneficiary inducement rules and OIG opinion 19-02.
- `health-equity-and-inclusive-design`. Find who a digital health product or patient program may leave out and shape the change that reaches them: subgroups the person defines (language, disability, age, connectivity, deprivation, site type), the step where exclusion shows, groups the data cannot see, the partner who could see them, and an inclusive change ranked only on the person's scores, with an owner and a version. Sourced frames: Section 1557 language access (92.201) and decision-support tool duties (92.210) with dates, diversity action plan status, CMS equity priorities, CDC/ATSDR SVI, NIH disparity populations, NHS Core20PLUS5. Use it for "who are we leaving out", "equity assessment", "inclusive design", "language access", "do we need a diversity action plan", "which community partner", even if the word equity is not used. Not for WCAG or accessibility conformance (dh-user-experience), measuring reach after launch (health-equity-monitoring), payer coverage (dh-market-access), or any decision about one person's eligibility.
- `health-equity-monitoring`. Check whether an inclusive change or a digital health program actually reached the groups it was meant for, from aggregate counts only: the evidence definition (change, group, record of use, denominator, period), reach and outcome rates per subgroup the person defines with intervals, gaps against the reference group they name, small-cell suppression under their rule, the share not recorded, and a follow-up with the owner they name and the next period to score. Brings sourced measures: NIH disparity definition, dropout and nonusage attrition, effective engagement, SVI area bands, Section 1557 dates. Use it for "did the change reach Spanish speakers", "reach by subgroup", "equity dashboard", "are older adults dropping off", "compare enrollment by SVI", "equity follow-up", even if the word equity is not used. Not for WCAG or accessibility conformance monitoring (dh-user-experience), designing the change (health-equity-and-inclusive-design), outcomes a payer pays on (dh-market-access), or patient-level data.
- `kpi-reliability-monitoring`. Check whether patient engagement KPIs for a digital health program or product can be trusted: each KPI card's quality (decision, numerator, valid denominator, period, grain, source, missing-data rule), drift between the card and the formula the dashboard actually uses, missing and inconsistent event data, recomputation of a dashboard number from the source, the measurement properties of questionnaire-based KPIs (COSMIN), and a repair plan with the owners the person names.
- `patient-engagement-capability-map`. The map of the VITAL Patient Engagement & Advocacy pack for digital health and life sciences teams.
- `patient-research-and-co-design`. Plan, capture and act on patient and carer co-design for a digital health product or program: the research question and the decision it serves, who takes part and why, the ethics and consent questions (Common Rule exemptions, MHRA consent), patient experience and patient preference rules (FDA PFDD, PPI guidance final 30 March 2026, EU HTA patient input, NICE, UK Standards for Public Involvement), session capture in participants' own words, an insight log from capture to decision, and the accept, defer or refuse decision per point with a linked design change.
