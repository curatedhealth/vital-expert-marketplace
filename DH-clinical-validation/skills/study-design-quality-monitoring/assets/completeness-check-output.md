# Output format: protocol completeness check

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Answer:** protocol version checked; counts of addressed, partly addressed, stated not relevant, missing and not checkable (over the 53 SPIRIT 2025 rows plus triggered duties); the missing items that block the most. No score, no "complete".
2. **Missing and partly addressed, by dependency:** item · what is missing · row.
3. **Triggered digital duties:** one line each, status and row.
4. **Superseded citations:** one line each with the current version and row. Diversity action plan line cites PE-34, PE-36, PE-41 together.
5. **Calls made:** list (usually none).
6. **Record:** "Completeness check of protocol VERSION, draft for OWNER. AI-assisted." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
