# SPIRIT 2025 checklist items: sourced reference

[PAPER] Chan A-W, Boutron I, Hopewell S, et al. SPIRIT 2025 statement: updated guideline for protocols of randomised trials. BMJ 2025. DOI 10.1136/bmj-2024-081477. Read in full text through the Europe PMC REST service on 19 October 2026. Not owner-reviewed. No row is [WF].

Why this file exists: the shared research file holds SPIRIT 2025 structure rows (CV115 to CV117) but not the item wording. Protocol drafting and completeness checks need the wording, so it was extracted from the raw file already downloaded, without new web reads.

Method: `cv1/bin/make_sp.py` cut Table 1 at each item number; the section and topic headings that follow each item in the extracted text were removed from the quote and placed in the Topic column. Every quote was then checked against the normalized raw text (53 of 53 found). British spellings in quotes (randomised, enrolment, authorised) are the source's own.

Rules that apply to every row (from the shared research file):
- SPIRIT 2025 has 34 minimum protocol items plus a schedule diagram and supersedes SPIRIT 2013 (CV115).
- All items should be addressed before ethics submission; an item that does not apply is stated as not relevant, explicitly (CV117).
- New in 2025: the open science section (items 4 to 8), patient and public involvement (item 11) and trial monitoring (item 29) (CV116).
- SPIRIT-AI adds 15 items for AI interventions but extends SPIRIT 2013, so its item numbers refer to the superseded checklist (CV122; research file "Changes and conflicts").
