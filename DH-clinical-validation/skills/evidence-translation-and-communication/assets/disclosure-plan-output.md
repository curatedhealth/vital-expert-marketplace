# Output format: disclosure and publication plan

## Short mode (default for direct questions; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Answer:** the next due date with its rule and arithmetic, or the regime decision that blocks it.
2. **Clock table:** Study · Regime (confirmed by the person) · Rule (row) · Input date · Due date · Status as of today.
3. **Checklist:** the reporting checklist for the design, [PAPER] row and DOI, and the items the draft does not yet address. When the person asks about publication: data sharing plan in the registration for trials enrolling from 1 January 2019 (CV135) and the data sharing statement content (CV136).
4. **Gaps:** regimes not confirmed, dates not given.
5. **Record:** "AI-assisted draft." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
