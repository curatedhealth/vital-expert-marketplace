# Gap rules

A gap is information, not an apology. Each reason leads to a different next step. When a source is open access, read the full text before marking a field `[GAP]` from an abstract.

| Class | When | How to word it | Next step for the person |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passage missing | An outcome, number or comparison with no results passage read | "No results passage for the 24-week outcome: not stated `[GAP]`." | Ask for the paper's results section, the clinical study report or the registry results. |
| Certainty not rated | No rating from a person, a review or an HTA body | "Certainty not rated `[GAP]`; the library's grade is a design-based starting level." | Ask who rates it, or point to dh-clinical-validation (literature-and-real-world-evidence-review). |
| Person's decision not made | Applicable clinical trial status, EU CTR scope, critical outcomes, authors, audiences, owners | "Applicable clinical trial status not stated: US clocks not computed `[GAP]`." | Ask once; route to the owner the person names for regulatory status, or owner not named. |
| Date not supplied | First consent, first enrollment, primary completion, end of trial, certification, approval | "Primary completion date not given: results due date not computed `[GAP]`." | Ask for the registry record or the date. |
| Library cannot answer | No registration found, design label unchecked, table field empty, tool error | "The library found no ClinicalTrials.gov link for this paper; registry id from the paper: DRKS..." | Use the paper and the person's registry record. |
| Intended use not stated | No label or authorization text for the market | "Cleared indication for the US not stated: payer and clinician narratives are provisional `[GAP]`." | Point to the owner the person names for regulatory status, or owner not named, or to dh-regulatory-strategy (regulatory-pathway-and-classification) for device status. |
| Not sourced | A rule the research files do not hold (EU CTR Annex V content, STROBE item wording, press-release rules) | "Not sourced here `[GAP]`." | Name the text to read; never fill from memory. |

## Never

- Never turn "no study found" in the library into "no evidence exists".
- Never turn a missing rating into a rating, or the library's starting level into certainty.
- Never estimate a missing date or a missing effect.
