# Output format: review protocol and search

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Answer:** the rule set that applies (body and rows), the required sources the library cannot reach, and the one decision the person must make next.
2. **PICOs:** Population · Intervention · Comparator · Outcomes · Source (person or "proposed").
3. **Sources:** Source · Required by (row) · Library can query? · Who runs it.
4. **Strategy note:** the concept blocks and any limit with its justification or `[GAP]`; PRESS reviewer (person named or "owner not named").
5. **Cut-off and reporting:** the body's currency rule with its row (no schedule), registration step.
6. **AI use:** steps planned with AI and the human responsible.
7. **Calls made:** list.
8. **Record:** "AI-assisted. Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft." This line appears in every answer, refusals included; never drop the verify.build_record clause.
9. One line offering the full version.
