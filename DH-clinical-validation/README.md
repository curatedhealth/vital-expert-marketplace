# DH Clinical Validation & Evidence

Install **DH Clinical Validation & Evidence** from `curatedhealth/vital-expert-marketplace`, then paste the Digital Health Library key. The key is not in this pack.

This pack is only DH-01.02 Clinical Validation & Evidence. Other Digital Health cores are separate packs.

The connector is `https://mcp.vital.expert/api/mcp/library`. Do not add a second connector.

Choose one heading, then one job. The job holds its tasks as sections. Ask which one task, then follow that section. A missing fact stays a gap.

## Skills

- `clinical-validation-capability-map`. The map of the VITAL Clinical Validation & Evidence pack for digital health products (SaMD, AI/ML devices, DTx, DiGA-type apps).
- `decentralized-trial-operations`. Set up and run the operations of a clinical trial with decentralized elements for a digital health product or a trial that uses digital health technologies (DHTs): which visits are remote, at site or the participant's choice, remote consent, local health care providers, wearables and apps as data acquisition tools, source data, data flow, verification, validation and usability evidence, same method across arms, audit trails and system validation.
- `digital-health-expert`. Find and download a VITAL Digital Health expert (a ready-made role such as the SaMD Regulatory Affairs Director, the Quality & Software Lifecycle Director or the Regulatory Submission Manager) to run on the person's own assistant.
- `evidence-communication-monitoring`. Check whether the evidence a regulated digital health product (DTx, SaMD, DiGA) communicates stayed true and was disclosed on time: registration, results posting and publication per trial against 42 CFR Part 11, EU CTR Article 37, ICMJE and the WHO pledge; a fidelity audit of abstracts, lay summaries, payer narratives, slides and reviews against their passages and certainty ratings; and a review against the person's own pass rules, with owners and responses.
- `evidence-quality-and-signal-monitoring`. Check whether a literature review or evidence base for a digital health product (DTx, SaMD, DiGA, app or sensor data) is still current and how far it can be trusted, then frame the update decision. Covers search currency (Cochrane 12 months, EU JCA 3-month cut-off, IQWiG up-to-dateness), new-study, retraction and correction signals, appraising a published review with AMSTAR 2 (critical domains, no summed score), real-world data quality (FDA reliability and relevance, device RWE guidance), digital signal validation (FDA DHT, V3), and whether to rely, update or rerun. Use it when someone asks "is this review still current", "can we cite this meta-analysis in our dossier", "has anything been retracted", "how good is this systematic review", "is this RWD good enough" or "do we need to update our search", even without the word "AMSTAR". Not for running a new review (use literature-and-real-world-evidence-review), device adverse-event signals (dh-regulatory-strategy), or claims wording (dh-commercial).
- `evidence-translation-and-communication`. Turn the clinical evidence for a regulated digital health product (DTx, SaMD, DiGA, remote monitoring, digital mental health) into statements people can rely on: a per-outcome evidence statement with passages checked and certainty worded as the person or HTA body rated it, a registration, results-posting and publication plan (42 CFR Part 11, EU CTR Article 37, ICMJE, CONSORT 2025, CONSORT-AI, TRIPOD+AI), and evidence narratives per audience (payer, clinician, lay) inside the sourced communication rules.
- `literature-and-real-world-evidence-review`. Run a literature and real-world evidence review for a digital health product (DTx, SaMD, DiGA, app or sensor endpoints) the way Cochrane, NICE, IQWiG and the EU JCA expect: PICO, protocol, search strings, PRESS, PRISMA-S and registration; screening, deduplication, extraction and risk of bias (RoB 2, RoB 1 for a JCA, ROBINS-I) with library design labels checked against the paper; GRADE, Summary of Findings, PRISMA 2020 flow and evidence table. Covers real-world data (FDA reliability and relevance, device RWE guidance) and digital endpoints (FDA DHT, V3). Use it when someone asks "build our search strategy", "screen these records", "which risk of bias tool", "grade this evidence", "make the evidence table", "can we pool these" or "literature review for our JCA", even without the words "systematic review". Not for trial design, a published review's currency or quality (evidence-quality-and-signal-monitoring), economic models (dh-health-economics), payer routes (dh-market-access) or claims (dh-commercial).
- `study-design-quality-monitoring`. Check whether a digital health study protocol (DTx, DiGA, SaMD, AI tool, wearable or app-captured endpoint) will hold up, and keep it holding up: every SPIRIT 2025 item addressed, the estimand and digital-measure duties present, the registry record matching the protocol and the paper, registration and results clocks, amendments tracked, and a robustness review against the pass rules the team sets, with an owner for every miss.
- `study-protocol-and-endpoint-design`. Design the clinical study for a digital health product (DTx, DiGA, SaMD, AI tool, remote monitoring, wearable or app-captured endpoint): pick endpoints and clinical outcome assessments from what comparable registered trials used, check whether a digital measure is fit for purpose (verification and validation), draft a protocol that covers every SPIRIT 2025 item, write the estimand, and lay out the statistical analysis plan decisions for the statistician.
- `trial-operations-monitoring`. Check how a running clinical trial of a digital health product, or a trial with decentralized elements, is executing: enrollment, retention, protocol deviations and data completeness by site and by arm, computed from the person's own export; registration, update and results-reporting clocks under 42 CFR Part 11, EU CTR Article 37, ICMJE and the WHO joint statement; and a review of which sites miss the person's rules, where execution stalls, who owns it and what responds.
