# Output format: channel measure sheet (A1)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

1. **Answer:** the measures per partner type in your words, the period, and which miss forces a hold. If you have not set thresholds: "No thresholds yet" and the starting list.
2. **How each is counted:** one line per measure: from which record, and what does not count (partner reports not tied to a buyer).
3. **Duties to check:** only those your terms trigger, one line each with its row.
4. **Gaps:** measures without a record; owners not named.
5. **Record:** "Measure sheet draft. Measures, thresholds and hold rules are yours. AI-assisted." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
6. One line offering the full version.
