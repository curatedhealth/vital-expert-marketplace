# Output format: positioning line (draft for the owner)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

1. **Recommended line per segment (proposed):** For · What changes · What it is not.
2. **Flags:** word or phrase · rule and row · safer wording (draft for the owner).
3. **Claims the line relies on:** on your cleared list, or `[GAP]`.
4. **Other drafts:** one line each.
5. **Stop:** "Claims audit (DH-02.01.3.2), then OWNER for regulatory or legal review." Owner as you named, or "owner not named".
6. **Record:** "Draft for the owner. AI-assisted." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
7. One line offering the full version.
