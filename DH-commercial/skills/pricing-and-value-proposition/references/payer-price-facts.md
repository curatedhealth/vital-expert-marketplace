# Payer price facts used as context for commercial pricing

These rows are context for a commercial price position. They are never a target and never a price for the person's product. Rows marked [WF] were read through a summarizing fetch tool in the Market Access research pass; re-check the quote on the page before relying on it. Row ids are those of the source files.
