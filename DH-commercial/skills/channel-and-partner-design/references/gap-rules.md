# Gap rules

A gap is information. The person needs to know why something is missing, because each reason leads to a different next step.

## Rules and requirements

| Class | When | How to word it | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sourced | A CP row states the rule. | The rule in plain words with its row id. | None. |
| Sourced from guidance, not the standard | The row rests on an NHS guidance page or blog summarizing a standard (CP-UK-06 to CP-UK-11). | "NHS England guidance states...; the standard's own text was not read `[GAP]`." | Confirm against the standard. |
| Advisory opinion | The row is an OIG advisory opinion. | "In AO 26-15, OIG reasoned... The opinion binds only its requestor (CP-US-43)." | Compliance owner. |
| Not sourced | No row covers it (see "Not sourced" in each reference). | "Not in the sourced rules `[GAP]`; this needs your legal owner." | Legal or compliance owner. |

## Partner facts from the library

| Class | When | How to word it |
|---|---|---|
| Found | The record states it. | The fact, its source URL and retrieval date. |
| Field missing | The record exists but the field is empty (counterparty, 8-K item, intended purpose). | "The record does not state X `[GAP]`; open the filing at its URL." |
| Source not loaded | The source is listed as not loaded (Companies House, Zefix, private rounds, NICE). | "The library holds no data from SOURCE `[GAP]`." |
| Search miss | A lookup by legal name returns nothing while another block shows records. | "Not found by this lookup; the screen shows N authorizations." Never "no authorizations". |

## The person's facts

| Situation | How to word it |
|---|---|
| No score for an option or criterion | "Not scored `[GAP]`; no ranking for this option until you score it." |
| No owner named | "owner not named"; ask. |
| Term status not given | "Status not stated `[GAP]`." Never mark a term agreed. |

## Never

- Never turn a gap into "not allowed", "protected", "no risk" or "no authorizations".
- Never fill a gap from memory or from a passage without a source URL.
