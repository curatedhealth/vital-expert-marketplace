# Output format: partner terms and compliance check (A2)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words)

1. **Answer:** what blocks this partner from starting (absent terms, holds) and what stops for legal or compliance.
2. **Terms that are absent or in draft:** at most five lines, each "Term: required elements (rows); status as you stated it".
3. **Fraud-and-abuse stop (if any):** the trigger and its row; the elements of the safe harbor or exception, one line each, marked "stated in your terms", "not stated" or "not applicable"; exclusions that apply on the facts (for a pharma partner, P1 first: CP-US-13). "Stops for OWNER." Never "protected".
4. **Disclosure (if a party is public):** the 8-K rule in one line with rows.
5. **Record:** "Term checklist draft for the owner. Not legal advice. AI-assisted." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. One line offering the full version.
