# Launch prerequisites by market

What the sourced rules require before, or at the moment of, selling a regulated digital health product. Interpretation of rows in `channels-partners-launch-research.md` (CP rows) and `promotion-and-claims-research.md` (PC rows), all read on 19 October 2026. Each line names its row; the row holds the URL and the verbatim quote. These are prerequisites the rows state. They are not legal advice, and they are not the full list for any market: where the rows are silent, the answer is `[GAP]`, not "nothing required".

Use in A1: for each market in scope, list the prerequisites below, then record the person's status for each (shown, stated, not stated). The model never marks a prerequisite as met on its own reading; it reports what the person showed or stated.

## United States

| # | Prerequisite | When | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|
| US1 | Establishment registration and device listing, including firms that develop specifications for a device made by a second party | Initial registration within 30 days after starting the operation, listing at that time; annual registration each 1 October to 31 December | CP-US-71, CP-US-72 |
| US2 | New listing when a non-exempt device with a premarket number not yet listed enters commercial distribution; foreign establishments list before import | At launch | CP-US-73 |
| US3 | No marketing statement that registration or a registration number means FDA approval | Always | CP-US-74 |
| US4 | UDI on label and package; software as a device shows its UDI as plain text at start-up or through a menu, with the version in the production identifier | At launch, unless an exception applies (GMP-exempt class I) | CP-US-75, CP-US-76, CP-US-77 |
| US5 | GUDID data submitted no later than the date the label must bear the UDI; changes not on the label within 10 business days | At launch, then on change | CP-US-78 |
| US6 | Business associate agreement with each covered entity when the product creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI for it; required clauses; breach notice to the covered entity within 60 calendar days | Before PHI flows | CP-US-44 to CP-US-49 |
| US7 | Direct-to-consumer app outside HIPAA: FTC Health Breach Notification Rule applies (amended rule effective 29 July 2024); an unauthorized disclosure is a breach; notice within 60 calendar days | Before launch to consumers | CP-US-54 to CP-US-56; PC-US-79 to PC-US-84 |
| US8 | If the customer will bill Medicare digital mental health treatment codes: G0552 requires a 510(k) or De Novo device classified at 21 CFR 882.5801 (and, for CY 2026, 882.5803 ADHD devices), the billing practitioner must bear the device cost, and use must match the FDA classification | Before positioning the channel | CP-US-57 to CP-US-64 |
| US9 | If the customer will bill remote monitoring: the device must meet FDA's device definition and upload data automatically; RPM needs an established patient; one practitioner per patient per 30 days; CY 2026 adds 2 to 15 day codes that do not stack with 16 to 30 day codes | Before positioning the channel | CP-US-65 to CP-US-70 |
| US10 | If the firm holds certified health IT modules: information blocking rules apply to it as a developer | From the date it holds a certified module | CP-US-50 to CP-US-53 |
| US11 | If a party is an SEC reporting company and the launch partnership is a material definitive agreement: Form 8-K Item 1.01 within four business days | On signing | CP-US-79 to CP-US-83 |
| US12 | Wellness positioning only holds if labeling, advertising, user interface and functionality stay within the general wellness policy (revised final 6 January 2026) | Before any wellness launch | PC-US-57 to PC-US-66 |

Payment amounts for US8 and US9 are not launch prerequisites and are Market Access's subject.

## Germany (DiGA route)

| # | Prerequisite or rule | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| DE1 | Patients get a listed DiGA on prescription by the treating physician or psychotherapist, or with sickness fund approval; fund approval needs proof of the indication | CP-DE-01, CP-DE-02 |
| DE2 | Excluded from the route: products controlling active therapeutic devices, DiGA for use with a specific aid or drug, general everyday items | CP-DE-04 |
| DE3 | Delivery electronically or on data carriers; public app stores only where that is not possible; needed hardware usually lent | CP-DE-05 |
| DE4 | Contract physicians may not make deals with DiGA makers about allocating or transmitting prescriptions | CP-DE-06 |
| DE5 | No agreements with drug or aid makers that could restrict patient choice or prescribing freedom | CP-DE-07 |
| DE6 | BfArM decides within three months of complete documents, extendable by up to three months; provisional listing for trial up to twelve months | CP-DE-12, CP-DE-13 |
| DE7 | Manufacturer's actual price applies in year one; the GKV-Spitzenverband agrees the amount after year one; arbitration sets it within three months if no agreement within nine months of listing | CP-DE-08, CP-DE-09, CP-DE-11 |
| DE8 | For negotiation the maker hands over self-pay and other European prices, so a cash-pay launch price becomes a negotiation input | CP-DE-10 |
| DE9 | A DiGA must be free of advertising; DiGA data may not be processed for advertising; users get pre-use information; no opaque offers such as auto-renewing subscriptions | PC-DE-15 to PC-DE-17 |
| DE10 | HWG applies to device advertising; public advertising limits apply outside professional circles | PC-DE-01 to PC-DE-13 |

Price negotiation itself is Market Access's subject; this skill names DE7 and DE8 only because they set what a launch price commits the maker to.

## United Kingdom (England)

| # | Prerequisite | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| UK1 | NHS England guidance: technology used in the NHS needs to meet DTAC; buyers review the completed DTAC in procurement; the page calls DTAC best practice, not a legal requirement | CP-UK-01, CP-UK-02 |
| UK2 | DTAC asks for proof of UKCA marking (or recognized equivalent) and the risk management system | CP-UK-03 |
| UK3 | Refreshed DTAC form, old form retired with full transition by 6 April 2026 | CP-UK-04 |
| UK4 | DCB0129 (manufacturer clinical risk management) is an information standard under section 250 of the Health and Social Care Act 2012; NHS England guidance says a product that cannot meet it cannot be used in the NHS | CP-UK-05 to CP-UK-08 |
| UK5 | DCB0160: the deploying organization checks DCB0129 compliance and does its own clinical risk assessment, and should not adopt a non-compliant product | CP-UK-09, CP-UK-10 |
| UK6 | Clinical safety case report shared with the deployer and a Clinical Safety Officer: stated in an official service blog, not the standard; confirm against DCB0129 | CP-UK-11 |
| UK7 | Procurement frameworks usually last up to four years; new suppliers cannot join an agreed framework except a Dynamic Purchasing System | CP-UK-12 to CP-UK-14 |
| UK8 | Medical claims in UK advertising need the applicable conformity marking ([INDUSTRY] CAP Code) | PC-UK-10 |

The DTAC criteria document and the DCB0129 and DCB0160 texts themselves were not readable in the research pass: specific clauses are `[GAP]`.

## Switzerland and other markets

No research row covers Swiss launch prerequisites. The connector accepts a Swiss route only as the caller's hypothesis (`self_pay`, `vvg_supplementary`, `okp_compulsory`) and states that a German listing does not imply a Swiss phase. The library's CH_BAG scheme record (live, 19 October 2026) states MiGeL position 40.01.01.00.1, valid from 1 July 2026, is a class position for interactive CBT digital applications in depression and names no product; it is a library fact, not a sourced rule. Every other market: `[GAP]`.

## The connector's readiness checklist (g1-commercial-v1)

`get_launch_readiness` returns seven criteria (roles, note, authorization, claims, route, peers, price) as met, attested or `[GAP]`, with `met_count`. It is a count of what the call could fill, not a readiness judgment, and it does not cover the prerequisites above. Observed on 19 October 2026 (`references/tools.md`): "claims" is met as soon as claims are labeled, whatever the labels say; "peers" is met with a peer count of 0; "price" is met from a public register price. Report it as the connector's count and lay the prerequisites beside it.
