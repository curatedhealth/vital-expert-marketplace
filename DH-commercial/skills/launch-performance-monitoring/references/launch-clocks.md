# Clocks and moving rules after go-live

Dated rules and recurring duties that a launch keeps running into after the first sale. Interpretation of CP rows (`channels-partners-launch-research.md`) and PC rows (`promotion-and-claims-research.md`), read 19 October 2026, plus library facts where named. Clocks are facts: state them with the instrument that sets or moved them, never as pressure.

## Recurring and event-driven duties

| Area | Clock | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| US registration | Annual registration each 1 October to 31 December | CP-US-72 |
| US listing | New listing when a non-exempt device with a premarket number not yet listed enters distribution | CP-US-73 |
| US GUDID | Changes not on the label submitted within 10 business days | CP-US-78 |
| EU EUDAMED | Actor data changes updated within one week; data confirmed within a year and every second year after | CP-EU-11 |
| EU EUDAMED backfill | Article 29 data for devices already on the market within 12 months of the notice; the research file computes about 27 November 2026 by arithmetic and notes the Commission page does not state it | CP-EU-16 |
| US business associate breach | Notice to the covered entity without unreasonable delay, no later than 60 calendar days after discovery | CP-US-49 |
| US consumer app breach | Notice to individuals within 60 calendar days; FTC notice at the same time for 500 or more people | CP-US-56, PC-US-83 |
| EU processor breach | Notice to the controller without undue delay (no hour limit set for the processor) | CP-EU-08 |
| SEC | Form 8-K within four business days of a material definitive agreement | CP-US-81 |
| UK framework entry | Frameworks usually last up to four years; new suppliers join only a Dynamic Purchasing System | CP-UK-12 |

## Germany (DiGA)

| Clock | Rows |
|---|---|
| Provisional listing for trial up to twelve months | CP-DE-13 |
| Manufacturer's actual price in year one; negotiated amount after the first year | CP-DE-08, CP-DE-09 |
| Arbitration if no agreement within nine months of listing; it sets the amount within three months | CP-DE-11 |
| Library: DE_DIGA rule "provisional trial up to 12 months (exceptionally 24)"; `ends_on` empty on 82 of 82 listings, so trial end dates are `[GAP]` in the library | live `list_commercial_dh_sources`, 19 October 2026 |

Price negotiation is Market Access's subject; these clocks are named here because they change what the field may say about price and status.
