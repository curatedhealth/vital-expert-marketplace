# DH Commercial & GTM Strategy

Install **DH Commercial & GTM Strategy** from `curatedhealth/vital-expert-marketplace`, then paste the Digital Health Library key. The key is not in this pack.

This pack is only DH-02.01 Commercial & GTM Strategy. Other Digital Health cores are separate packs.

The connector is `https://mcp.vital.expert/api/mcp/library`. Do not add a second connector.

Choose one heading, then one job. The job holds its tasks as sections. Ask which one task, then follow that section. A missing fact stays a gap.

## Skills

- `channel-and-partner-design`. Design the channels and partners that carry a regulated digital health product (DTx, SaMD, DiGA, remote monitoring) to its buyers: which routes reach the buyer in each market and the rules each route sets, what each partner may do, the handoff to your team, the terms that must be true before a partner starts (BAA, GDPR Article 28, claim use, SEC disclosure), and which partners or initiatives to pursue first from the scores you set.
- `channel-and-partnership-monitoring`. Check whether the channels and partners that carry a regulated digital health product (DTx, SaMD, DiGA, remote monitoring) are working: define what a healthy partner looks like in your own measures, score each partner for the period from your handoff record rather than the partner's own report, catch claim breaches and data-term duties (breach notices, sub-processors, patient engagement caps), read public signals on a partner, and decide hold, continue or change terms against your own rules.
- `commercial-gtm-capability-map`. The map of the VITAL Commercial & GTM Strategy pack for digital health products (SaMD, DTx, DiGA-type apps, remote monitoring, clinical software).
- `digital-health-expert`. Find and download a VITAL Digital Health expert (a ready-made role such as the SaMD Regulatory Affairs Director, the Quality & Software Lifecycle Director or the Regulatory Submission Manager) to run on the person's own assistant.
- `gtm-positioning-monitoring`. Check whether a digital health product's go-to-market positioning is used in market and still holds: which artifacts must carry the line (website, app store page, leave-behind), whether each in-market copy has it present, altered or absent, which words cross the sourced promotion rules, what changed in peers' public records, payer routes and wording rules, and which artifact must change, owned by whom.
- `launch-performance-monitoring`. Monitor a regulated digital health product's launch after go-live (DTx, DiGA, SaMD, remote monitoring, digital mental health): public signals for the product and peers (listings, authorizations, status changes, clocks after launch), launch measures from the team's own field records (accounts reached, meetings, briefings, claims used, unapproved claims in the field), and a launch review against the team's thresholds with an owner and next step for every miss.
- `launch-planning-and-sales-enablement`. Plan the commercial launch of a regulated digital health product (DTx, DiGA, SaMD, remote monitoring, digital mental health) and equip the field: the launch sequence against peers, the prerequisites each market sets before selling (FDA registration and UDI, EUDAMED, DTAC and DCB0129, DiGA routes, Medicare billing conditions), the claim list each launch may use, the sales leave-behind and the enablement roster.
- `market-segmentation-and-gtm-strategy`. Lay out the segments a regulated digital health product (DTx, SaMD, DiGA, remote monitoring, digital mental health, software sold with a drug) could serve: who buys, uses and pays, which payer route and channel rules exist per market, which peers are on record; then rank the segments the person scored and draft a GTM motion and a positioning line per chosen segment.
- `pricing-and-value-message-monitoring`. Check whether a regulated digital health product's pricing and value messages hold up: which deals landed inside, below or above the corridor and at what realized price, which live claims in ads, app store text, payer decks or websites cross the sourced promotion rules (FD&C Act 502(a) and 502(gg), FDA CFL and general wellness, FTC, MDR Article 7, UCPD, HWG and DiGAV, DMCCA, CAP Code), with safer wording drafted for the regulatory or legal owner, and whether the corridor or the deals need to change.
- `pricing-and-value-proposition`. Build the commercial price position and value proposition for a regulated digital health product (DiGA, DTx, SaMD, remote monitoring, digital mental health): who uses it, who buys it and who pays, what unit the price attaches to, what registered comparator prices and value-based anchors say about a corridor, and value messages per audience that stay inside the sourced promotion rules (FD&C Act 502(a) and 502(gg) for payors, FDA CFL, FTC, MDR Article 7, HWG, CAP Code).
