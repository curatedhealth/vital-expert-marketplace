# Output format: data completeness check

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

**Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; aim for at least 250. Four parts only: the answer with its number, one table, open items (at most three), the record line. No rules list and no context paragraph in short mode: they belong to the full mode; offer the full version in one line.** The calls list is not counted.

1. **Answer:** how many failures are missing data rather than targets not met; the rate under the contract's rule and the other way; the stage where most data is lost.
2. **One table, completeness by measure:** Measure · Baseline present · Final present · Out of window · Self-reported final.
3. **Open items (at most three):** the next fields to measure or the questions for the person, each `[GAP]` with its reason.
4. **Record:** "Measurement on your data. No values imputed. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.
