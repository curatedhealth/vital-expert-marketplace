# Output format: performance review (review draft)

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

**Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; aim for at least 250. Four parts only: the answer with its number, one table, open items (at most three), the record line. No rules list and no context paragraph in short mode: they belong to the full mode; offer the full version in one line.** The calls list is not counted.

1. **Answer:** the verdict per rule (On track / At risk / Missed) with its number, the stall point (data or outcome, with A2's two-way rate), the decision needed and where the response sits (contract design (DH-03.01.1.1), data operations, product and clinical work, or rules change), in at most four sentences.
2. **One table, results:** Rule · Number behind it · Verdict · Owner you named.
3. **Open items (at most three):** hypotheses to test first, each labeled a hypothesis with what would confirm it.
4. **Record:** "Review draft. Rules and owners are yours. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.

The worth-keeping (ISPOR) context appears in full mode only.

## Full mode

### Summary
Is the contract working against your rules, and the one decision the review asks for.

### Review rules
| Rule in your words | Threshold | Owner | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|

### Results
| Rule | Number behind it | Pass / Miss / Not measurable | Source (A1 or A2, date) |
|---|---|---|---|

### Stall point
| First loss | Patients | Data or outcome | What it means under the contract |
|---|---|---|---|

### Hypotheses
| Hypothesis | What would confirm it | Source of the mechanism |
|---|---|---|
Labeled as hypotheses; none is a finding.

### Response routing
| Stall point | Responding work | Owner you named |
|---|---|---|

### Worth keeping (full mode only)
The ISPOR test, with the facts from this review, for the person to judge.

### Gaps and record
Rules that could not be measured, and why. Review draft; contract, pricing and payer wording stop for the accountable owner. Attainment is not clinical evidence. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared. AI-assisted draft.
