# Gap rules

A gap is information, not an apology. Each kind of gap leads to a different next step.

| Class | When | How to word it | Next step for the person |
|---|---|---|---|
| Person's decision not yet made | Response codes, the measure, the flag rule, which lines failed, an owner, a certainty rating | "Reception measure: not chosen `[GAP]`; counts only." | Ask once. Never fill it; if asked for a proposal, label it "proposed". |
| Log incomplete | A response with no line or claim id, no date, or no code; too few responses for the person's minimum | "L3: 2 responses, below your minimum of 3; no share reported." | Complete the log or accept the smaller base. |
| Passage no longer holds | Retraction, correction, or a re-read that no longer states the claim in the same direction | "C1: source corrected on DATE; passage re-read required." | Re-read; hold the line until then. |
| Candidate new evidence | A search hit since the last check | "Two 2026 trials with economic outcomes found; not read here." | Read them, or commission a review update (dh-clinical-development, literature-and-real-world-evidence-review). |
| Guidance moved | A row in `currency-signals.md` dated after the narrative | "Cites the 2018 payor guidance only; June 2026 revised draft not named (PC-US-47)." | Owner decides whether to update. |
| Not sourced | Any reception benchmark, publication rule, payer policy change | "No sourced benchmark for payer acceptance `[GAP]`." | The person's rule applies; do not fill from memory. |
| Tool failed | HTTP 422 or 503 | "Retraction check unavailable (HTTP 503, DATE)." | Retry once, or check the publisher's page. |

Open access: When a source is open access, read the full text before marking any field `[GAP]` from the abstract or the library excerpt.

## Never

- Never read a stakeholder objection as evidence that the product does not work, or an acceptance as evidence that it does.
- Never turn a search hit into "the evidence has changed".
- Never fill a missing measure, threshold or owner.
