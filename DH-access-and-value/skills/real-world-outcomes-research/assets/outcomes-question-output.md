# Output format: outcomes question (question brief)

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap. Aim for at least 250. Any [PAPER] row id carries its DOI beside it, in prose and in tables (for example HE-026, DOI 10.1002/pds.4297).

1. **Answer:** the question as PICO in the person's words, the two study types open to them (HETE or exploratory), the one condition that decides between them, and that the declaration is theirs (write "proposed" if you point to one) (for example "outcome data not yet seen"), in at most four sentences.
2. **One table, open choices:** Choice · Options with rows · Status (yours, not made yet / made).
3. **Data fitness gaps:** at most three, each with the framework named and its row.
4. **Prior record:** at most three lines, each with design read from the paper, data source and DOI.
5. **Record:** "Question brief. Choices are yours. Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. AI-assisted draft." The record line appears in every answer, including refusals.

Offer the full version in one line.
