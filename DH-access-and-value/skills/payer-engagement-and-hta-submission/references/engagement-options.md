## Order constraints that are facts, not choices

These follow from the rules and bound any sequence the person chooses.

- **BfArM consultation before filing takes months.** The meeting usually comes three to four months after the request, and the package is due three weeks before it (`DE28`). A plan that wants consultation feedback in the application must start there.
- **One trial listing only.** A refused or withdrawn DiGA cannot get a second trial listing and waits 12 months with new evidence for a permanent application (`DE23`). The evaluation concept is therefore the one chance on that route.
- **PECAN starts a filing clock.** From the PECAN decision: 6 months to file for LPPR, 9 months for LATM (`FR14`). The LPPR or LATM dossier has to be in preparation before PECAN is granted.
- **ANS runs in parallel with CNEDiMTS for PECAN** (`FR15`), and its certificate is a prerequisite for LATM (`FR16`).
- **NICE comes to the company.** Requests for information arrive at scoping (`UK2`); an early-use recommendation then binds the company to evidence generation, and NICE can withdraw it if the company does not engage (`UK8`).
- **TCET nomination is tied to FDA timing** (`US5`).

## Payers and price

Price and contract negotiation (GKV-Spitzenverband after DiGA listing, CEPS after a CNEDiMTS opinion, US payers) is not engagement for evidence. It belongs to the coding, pricing and contracting competency (`coding-pricing-and-payer-contracting`, DH-03.01.2.1). Note only that the CEPS economic file is filed jointly with the LPPR medico-technical file (`FR2`), so its owner has to be in the plan from the start.
