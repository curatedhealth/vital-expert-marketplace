# Output format: submission readiness

## Short mode (250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.

Hard cap: count the words before sending. Over 450, cut in this order: comparables to one line, the consultation line, rows already marked "[GAP]" merged into one row ("Not in the record: ..."), the closing offer. Never cut the answer, row ids, the claim status or the record line.

1. **Answer:** complete or not against the sourced checklist, the blocking items, the fee range and the review clock from a complete file, with the read date.
2. **Checklist:** one table: Item · Status (present, missing, `[GAP]`) · Row. Formal-completeness items first.
3. **Fees and clocks:** two or three lines.
4. **Consultation:** one line on the route, cost and lead time.
5. **Context:** the body's own failure figures in one or two lines, with period, labeled context.
6. **Record:** "Checklist read 19 October 2026. Uncleared draft. Filing stops for the owner."
