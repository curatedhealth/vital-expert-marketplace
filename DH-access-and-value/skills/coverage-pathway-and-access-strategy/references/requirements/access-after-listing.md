Research file for the market-access "benefit design" skill. Read on 19 October 2026.

**Method.** Every row below comes from a page opened on 19 October 2026. Pages on gesetze-im-internet.de, gkv-spitzenverband.de, kbv.de, bfarm.de, gnius.esante.gouv.fr and nice.org.uk were downloaded in full and the quotes were copied from the raw text (for PDFs, from the extracted text, with words rejoined where the PDF splits them with a hyphen at a line end). All non-[WF] quotes were checked by script against the downloaded text. Pages on ameli.fr and england.nhs.uk refused direct download (403 or empty 202 responses), so they were opened with WebFetch. WebFetch passes the page through a summarizing model and caps quotes at 125 characters. Rows marked **[WF]** depend on that extraction: the quote is what the tool returned as verbatim, and it was not checked against raw HTML. legifrance.gouv.fr (403 and fetch error) and has-sante.fr (TLS failure and robots block) could not be opened at all. ## Changes and conflicts

1. **When German outcome data appear.** §139e(13) SGB V says BfArM publishes outcome-measurement results from 1 January 2026. DiGAV §23a(3) sets the first manufacturer report for 15 April 2027 (use data), 15 April 2028 (satisfaction and health status) and 15 April 2029 (indication-specific questionnaires). In practice, published results cannot exist before the 2027 report. Read the law date as the start of the legal basis, not of available data.
2. **Who reports outcomes.** §139e(13) applies to "a DiGA". DiGAV §23a(1) limits reporting to permanently listed DiGA. Trial-listed products do not report under §23a.
3. **Prescription vs approval share.** The report summary gives 87 percent prescribed over the whole period. The body applies 87 / 13 to the majority of DiGA, and elsewhere says the average approval share is "slightly over 10 percent". These are different bases (all activations vs average per DiGA) and should not be mixed.
4. **German e-prescription status.** The KBV DiGA page (updated 2 July 2026) says DiGA "können seit Januar elektronisch verordnet werden". The KBV news item of 12 March 2026 says the start is voluntary and has no mandatory date. Both agree that paper form 16 remains valid. The approval-route activation process in the GKV report is described as paper-based up to 31 December 2025.
5. **Trial listing length.** §139e(4) allows 12 months plus one extension of up to 12 months. KBV paraphrases this as "zunächst für 12, jedoch längstens 24 Monate". These agree.
6. **PECAN length wording.** G_NIUS says one year. ameli adds "non renouvelable" **[WF]**. These agree, but confirm against L162-1-23 when legifrance can be reached.
7. **Report file name.** The GKV report covering to 31 December 2025 (published 1 April 2026) sits at a URL named "2025_DiGA_Bericht_GKV_SV.pdf". The title page reads "DiGA-Bericht des GKV-Spitzenverbandes 2025". Cite it by its period and publication date, not by the file name.
8. **Payer framing.** The GKV-Spitzenverband sections on "Rezeptservices" and marketing are the payer's own view. They cite one appeal-court judgment (OLG Brandenburg, 18 November 2025, Az. 6 U 130/24) that the report itself marks as not final ("nicht rechtskräftig").
