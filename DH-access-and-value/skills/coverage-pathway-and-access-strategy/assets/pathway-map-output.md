# Output format: coverage pathway map

## Short mode

Aim for 200 to 350 words.

1. **Answer:** two or three sentences: which routes fit this product in the target countries, the one fact most likely to drive the choice, and the as-of date.
2. **Map:** Country · Scheme · Fits? · Clock · Precedents (n) · Main gap.
3. **Inputs still missing:** one line (for example the regulatory class).
4. **Sources and record:** one line.

End with one offer: the full map, or task B1 (access strategy) to choose and sequence.

## Full mode

### Answer

Two or three sentences, as above.

### Pathway map

| Country | Scheme | Kind | Fits this product? (why) | Prerequisite stated | Evidence bar stated | Clock | Precedents (n, examples) | Library coverage | Gaps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Schemes that do not fit stay as rows with the reason.

### What will drive the choice

Two to four facts from the map that Part B must weigh (a clock, a missing prerequisite, no precedent, a scheme that pays a service rather than the product). Facts, not a recommendation.

### Inputs still missing

What the map assumed or left as `[GAP]`, and where each comes from (for example: regulatory class, from a regulatory classification).

### Sources and record

- Numbered sources with URL and retrieval date.
- A line saying the facts rest on the library's copy of each register.
- Record: cleared by NAME, or "uncleared draft".
