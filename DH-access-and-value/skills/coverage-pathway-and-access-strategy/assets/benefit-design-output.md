# Output format: benefit design

## Short mode (default for a direct question; aim for 300 to 500 words)

1. **Answer:** two or three sentences: where patients are most likely lost between coverage and use, and the one or two levers that address it.
2. **Journey:** Stage · Rule (source) · Known drop-off (source or unknown) · Lever options.
3. **What to measure:** one line per stage.
4. **Open inputs and record:** one line each.

## Full mode

### Answer

Two or three sentences, as above.

### Access journey, per country

| Stage | Who acts | Rule (source) | Known drop-off (source, grade) | Lever options | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Evidence on uptake and adherence

The official figures and the graded studies used, each with its source and what it does and does not show.

### Post-listing obligations that shape the design

What must be reported or collected, by when (as the source states), and what that means for the product's data design.

### Open inputs

What was assumed or left `[GAP]`, and where each comes from.

### Sources and record

Numbered sources with URL or DOI and date read; Verify result for literature claims; cleared by NAME, or "uncleared draft". Any prescriber- or patient-facing wording stops for regulatory review.
