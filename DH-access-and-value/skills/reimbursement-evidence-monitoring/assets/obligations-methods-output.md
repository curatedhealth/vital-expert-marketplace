# Output format: evidence obligations and methods currency

## Short mode (default; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Answer:** the obligation most exposed on the facts and the methods change that touches the model most directly.
2. **Obligations table:** Decision · Outcome asked (verbatim) · Collected / not collected / `[GAP]` · Window (source, dates, as of).
3. **Methods table:** Change (date, instrument) · Applies to this topic? (why) · What it touches · Row.
4. **Studies:** one line each: DOI · currency result.
5. **Calls and record:** calls made. Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named: ask "Who clears this record?"]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.

End with one offer: the full tables, or B1 (outcome review).
