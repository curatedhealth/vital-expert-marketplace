# Health economics methods for digital health: sourced reference (HE)

Research pass of 19 October 2026. Primary sources only: HTA bodies and regulators (NICE, EU, HAS, IQWiG), the methods bodies' own publications (CHEERS 2022, ISPOR task force reports, EuroQol), ICER, and AMCP. Every quote was copied from a raw download saved under `raw/` and machine-checked against it. No row was read through WebFetch, so no row carries the [WF] mark ([WF] would mean the page was read through the WebFetch tool rather than a saved raw file). [PAPER] marks a peer-reviewed or task-force paper; "abstract only" means the full text was paywalled and only the abstract on the publisher's or society's official page was read. Not owner-reviewed.

## Method

- Downloaded with curl through the configured proxy. HTML pages were converted to text by stripping tags; PDFs were converted with `pdftotext -layout`. The raw files and text conversions are in `raw/`.
- HAS (has-sante.fr) serves an incomplete certificate chain. The missing Sectigo intermediate was fetched from the certificate's AIA URL into a local CA file. TLS verification was never disabled.
- EU legal texts were fetched from publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/<CELEX>.
- Value in Health, Medical Decision Making (SAGE) and JMCP block automated full-text download (HTTP 403 or a captcha). ISPOR task force reports were therefore read as the abstracts on ispor.org. The AMCP Format 5.0 and 6.0 full texts could not be read. AMCP rows rest on amcp.org pages and on AMCP's own March 2026 public-comment draft of Format 6.0.
- Quote check: `he_scripts/check_quotes.py` normalizes whitespace, ligatures, non-breaking hyphens and curly quotes, then tests every quote as a substring of its raw text. The result is at the end of this file.
- Spelling: Facts use American spelling. Quotes keep their source's spelling.

## Changes and conflicts

1. NICE threshold: PMG36 and PMG48 disagree. PMG36 (TA and HST manual, updated March 2026) uses £25,000 to £35,000 per QALY (HE-050 to HE-052). PMG48 (HealthTech manual, last updated 17 December 2025) still states £20,000 and £20,000 to £30,000 (HE-074), and uses £20,000 and £30,000 in its switching-value example (HE-077). For a digital product assessed under the HealthTech programme, the written threshold on 19 October 2026 is £20,000 to £30,000. Treat this as an open conflict until NICE updates PMG48. Do not assume the PMG36 range carries over.
2. NICE EQ-5D: PMG36 section 4.3.16 (online text) still requires the 3L value set and 5L-to-3L mapping (HE-046). PMG51, published 27 August 2026, replaces this for new topics: UK 5L value set, 3L mapped to 5L, and no 3L sensitivity analyses (HE-057 to HE-063). NICE says the updated manuals will follow. Topics already under way keep 5L-to-3L mapping (HE-063).
3. NICE manual names: the ESF (2022) cites "NICE health technology evaluations: the manual" (section 4.2.14) for cost-utility analysis. That manual is now titled "NICE technology appraisal and highly specialised technologies guidance: the manual" (HE-033), and HealthTech methods sit in PMG48.
4. NICE EVA: EVA is now "early-use HealthTech guidance" in PMG48 section 3. The EVA interim statement applies only to early-use topics started before 14 July 2025 (HE-066, HE-067).
5. NICE budget impact test: raised from £20 million to £40 million a year in June 2025 (PMG36 update information; HE-055).
6. Discount rates differ by jurisdiction: NICE 3.5% (1.5% by exception); HAS 2.5%, falling to 1.5% after 30 years; IQWiG 3% (sensitivity analyses at 0%, 5% and a differential 3%/1.5%); ICER 3%; AMCP draft 3%.
7. Perspective differs: NICE uses NHS and PSS costs with all health effects; HAS a collective perspective; IQWiG the SHI-insured community; ICER the health care system with a modified societal co-base; ICER-PHTI the payer and the DHT user; AMCP draft the payer (HCDM).
8. EU HTA: JCA is clinical only. Economic evaluation stays with Member States (HE-093 to HE-095). The first device selection (6 October 2026) covered two cardiac valve systems; no standalone software was among them (HE-101).
9. Eddy et al. 2012: ISPOR's page cites Value Health 15(5):843-850. Europe PMC indexes the Value in Health version with DOI 10.1016/j.jval.2012.04.012. The issue number was not verified at the publisher.
10. ICER file naming: the VAF PDF is named "2023_2026" and sits under a December 2025 upload path (filename suffix 070126), but its cover reads "Updated September 25, 2023". The reference case PDF says "Current as of October 23, 2025" despite a 2024 upload path.
11. AMCP: Format 6.0 was released in October 2026, but only the March 2026 public-comment draft could be read. Rows HE-133 to HE-139 that rest on the draft are labeled as such.
