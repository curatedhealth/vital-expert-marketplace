## Changes and conflicts

1. Device class. Current rule: classes I, IIa (low risk) and IIb (higher risk); class III excluded (§ 33a Abs. 2 SGB V). Source of change: Digital-Gesetz (DigiG) of 26 March 2024, per the BfArM Leitfaden ("mit Inkrafttreten des DigiG am 26.03.2024 wurde die Definition von DiGA erweitert") and the BMG DigiG page ("Mit der Ausweitung der DiGA auf digitale Medizinprodukte der Risikoklasse IIb"). Class IIb: medical benefit only, prospective study required (DiGAV § 11a), no trial listing.
2. Who negotiates after year one. Statute: GKV-Spitzenverband with the individual manufacturer (§ 134 Abs. 1). The Leitfaden v3.6 hint box says GKV-SV agrees "mit den maßgeblichen Spitzenorganisationen der Hersteller", which describes the framework agreement parties, not the price negotiation. Statute governs; the GKV-SV page and Rahmenvereinbarung § 2 also name the individual manufacturer.
3. Performance-based price. Rahmenvereinbarung (February 2024) § 9: performance components are optional ("können erfolgsabhängige Preisbestandteile enthalten"). Current statute: mandatory, at least 20 percent from 1 January 2026, with existing agreements to be re-agreed by that date. No updated framework agreement found.
4. Volume discount. New in § 134 Abs. 1 from 1 January 2027 (at least 2 percent above 3,000 units, at least 30 percent above 100,000). Not reflected in the Leitfaden v3.6 or the February 2024 framework agreement.
5. Trial length. 12 months, plus one extension of up to 12 months (24 months maximum). § 139e Abs. 4 frames the extension as "um bis zu zwölf Monate einschließlich des Zeitraums nach Satz 6" (including BfArM's three-month decision window); DiGAV § 17 Abs. 3 requires the extension request at least three months before the trial ends. During the decision window after the trial, the DiGA stays listed (Leitfaden).
6. Data protection certificate. Statute: mandatory GDPR Art. 42 certificate from 1 August 2024. BfArM Leitfaden (10 December 2025): no accredited certifiers yet; DiGAV § 4 and Annex 1 continue to apply until BfArM sets a deadline. The statute itself allows that deferral (§ 139e Abs. 11 Satz 3). Current position after December 2025 not confirmed.
7. Data security certificate. Statute: BSI certificate from 1 January 2025. BfArM adds: since 1 July 2025 the certificate is a condition of formal completeness, and listing without it is impossible.
8. In-use outcome measurement (AbEM). Statute says BfArM publishes results from 1 January 2026. DiGAV (amended 27 January 2026) sets the first report by 15 April 2027 and publication only after 200 users in a quarter; the Leitfaden says data collection starts 1 July 2026. Effective publication is therefore later than the statutory date.
9. Study design. DiGAV § 10 sets a retrospective comparative study as the minimum, with prospective required where suitable data do not exist (§ 11) or for class IIb (§ 11a). The Leitfaden agrees ("mindestens die Vorlage einer retrospektiven vergleichenden Studie").
10. Currency of BfArM guidance. The Leitfaden v3.6 (10 December 2025) predates the DiGAV amendment of 27 January 2026. Where they differ, the DiGAV text is used here.
