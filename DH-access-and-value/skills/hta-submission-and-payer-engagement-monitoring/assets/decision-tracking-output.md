# Output format: decision tracking

## Short mode (250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.

1. **Answer:** the person's open decisions and the clock running on each, with dates; one line on how comparable products fared.
2. **Comparables:** one table: Product · Scheme · Status (source wording) · Since · Stated end · Flag · Source, retrieved.
3. **Official context:** two or three lines, each figure with period and row, labeled "context, not odds".
4. **Not published:** one or two lines.
5. **Record:** "Library records retrieved DATE; the person's own documents for own submissions. Uncleared draft."
