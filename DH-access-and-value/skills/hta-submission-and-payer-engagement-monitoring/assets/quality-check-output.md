# Output format: submission quality check

Findings, never scores. Two modes, same order.

## Short mode (250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.

1. **Answer:** complete or not against the sourced checklist (read DATE), counts of items present, missing and `[GAP]`, and the formal-completeness blockers by name.
2. **Missing and `[GAP]` items:** one table: Item · Row · Status · Where it should sit. Formal-completeness blockers get one row each. Missing D6 content items go in one row, comma-separated. `[GAP]` items go in one row. Count words before sending and trim the table and findings, not the answer, to stay at or under 450.
3. **Findings for the owner:** at most five lines, each "fact; rule (row)".
4. **Claims:** one line per claim checked: checked or not checked, with locator.
5. **Record:** "Completeness is factual; quality judgments are yours. Uncleared draft. Any change to the draft for the body is a draft for the owner."
