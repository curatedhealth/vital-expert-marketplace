# Coding, pricing and contracting: primary-source reference file

Research pass of 19 October 2026. Primary sources only (statutes, regulators, payers, official agencies). Rows marked [WF] were read through the WebFetch tool, which returns a model-summarized rendering of the page; their "verbatim" quotes are as returned by that tool and should be re-checked against the live page before being relied on. All other rows were downloaded with curl through the configured proxy and quoted from the saved raw text in `research_ma2/raw/`. This file has not been reviewed by the owner. Evidence class for every row: P (published primary document), except where a row says otherwise.

## Method

- United States: the Federal Register API was used to locate the CY 2026 PFS final rule (FR Doc 2025-19787, 90 FR 49266, published 5 November 2025), its corrections, and the CY 2027 PFS proposed rule (FR Doc 2026-14327, 91 FR 43842, published 16 July 2026). federalregister.gov then blocked the session's IP ("Your IP address has been blocked"), so the full texts were taken from the official GPO edition on govinfo.gov (same FR documents). The CY 2024 PFS final rule (FR Doc 2023-24184) was pulled for the standing RPM/RTM billing conditions. CMS pages and PDFs (ACCESS model page, ACCESS payment amounts PDF, ACCESS OAP billing guidelines PDF, MLN901705 booklet, NCCI page, PFS overview page) were downloaded from cms.gov.
- Germany: SGB V sections 87, 134, 139e and DiGAV section 20 from gesetze-im-internet.de; GKV-Spitzenverband DiGA page, the framework agreement (Rahmenvereinbarung, reading version valid from 28 February 2024) and the maximum-amount page with the threshold/group maximum PDF valid from 1 October 2026; KBV DiGA billing page (updated 12 March 2026).
- France: legifrance.gouv.fr returned HTTP 403 (bot challenge) to curl and an error to WebFetch; sante.gouv.fr returned empty replies to curl and a captcha to WebFetch. Only the G_NIUS pages (Délégation au numérique en santé) could be downloaded.
- England: england.nhs.uk returned HTTP 202 with empty bodies to curl (bot protection). NHS England pages were therefore read with WebFetch [WF]. One NHS Payment Scheme PDF (Annex B, currencies) downloaded by curl.
- Quotes are given in the source language. Paraphrases in the "Fact" column use American spelling.

## Changes and conflicts

1. **16-day rule vs new 2-to-15-day codes (US).** The CY 2024 rule (US11) ties billing to at least 16 days of data. From 1 January 2026 the 2-to-15-day codes 99445/98984/98985/98986 (US2 to US4) allow billing below 16 days. The "one practitioner per 30 days" and "RPM or RTM, not both" rules are unchanged (MLN booklet, US15). Older secondary material citing a hard 16-day minimum is out of date.
2. **RTM established-patient requirement.** The MLN booklet (December 2025) says RTM does not require an established patient; the CY 2027 proposed rule (US23) would add it from 2027. Proposed, not final.
3. **Third-party RPM vendors.** US25 would bar payment where monitoring staff are contracted rather than employed, starting 1 January 2027 if finalized. Any vendor contract built on outsourced clinical staff is exposed. Final rule expected in the CY 2027 PFS final rule, not yet published.
4. **Possible collapse of 17 codes into 4 G-codes** (US27). If finalized, the code mapping in any coding strategy changes for 2027.
5. **Rule text inconsistency.** CY 2026 final rule says "four new RTM codes" but the sentence lists three (98984, 98985, 98979); 98986 is named elsewhere in the same section (US3).
6. **Germany: statute ahead of framework agreement.** Section 134 SGB V requires at least 20% performance-based pricing from 1 January 2026 and a volume discount from 1 January 2027 (DE2, DE3); the published framework agreement (valid from 28 February 2024) only says performance components "can" be included (DE13). Treat the statute as controlling; the implementing detail is not sourced (Not sourced 9).
7. **Germany: group maximums published for only two groups** in the 1 October 2026 file (DE14), although the framework defines more indication groups; groups need at least two DiGA (DE11). The file footer reads "Stand 07.05.2026" while its validity date is 1 October 2026; noted, not resolved.
8. **Germany: maximum-amount reductions.** The trial-listing maximum is 80% of group maximum; above 10,000 codes it falls to 75% (permanent) and 60% (trial) (DE15). The 2027 statutory volume discount (DE3) is a separate mechanism on negotiated amounts; how the two interact is not sourced.
9. **ACCESS overlaps with PFS digital codes.** DMHT (G0552/G0553) and RTM setup (98975) billed by other providers count as substitute spend against ACCESS BH participants (US34), and participants may not bill FFS for aligned beneficiaries (US36). A DTx sold into both channels faces a channel conflict.
10. **England sources are [WF] only.** All england.nhs.uk quotes came through the WebFetch summarizer; re-verify verbatim before reuse.
