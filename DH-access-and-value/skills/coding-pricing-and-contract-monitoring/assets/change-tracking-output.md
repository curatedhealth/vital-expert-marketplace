# Output format: code and price change tracking

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words, hard cap. Use only the six sections below. Do not add Changes in force, Gaps, comparator price lists or proposals that do not touch the person's codes unless they ask for a review pack; put open gaps as one line under What it means for you.)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.

1. **What moved or is about to:** one to three sentences, each with a date and row or source.
2. **Tracked items:** Code, route or product · Today's value (setting) · Baseline value and date · Changed? · Source, retrieved.
3. **Proposed, not law:** at most three, each "proposed DATE; final rule not published".
4. **Clocks:** dates that move a price (month 13, arbitration point, end of the maximum-amount window).
5. **What it means for you:** one to three conditional lines.
6. **Record:** "Uncleared draft" or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary
What changed since the baseline, what is proposed, and what to check.

### Baseline
What this run compares against, or "none: current snapshot".

### Tracked items
| Item | Today's value | Setting or unit | Baseline (date) | Change | Source, retrieved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Changes in force
| Change | Effective | Affects | Row |
|---|---|---|---|

### Proposed changes
| Proposal | Proposed on | Status | Affects | Row |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Clocks
| Item | Rule | Anchor | Date | Row |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### What it means for you
Conditional consequences; anything needing a strategy or contract change is routed to DH-03.01.2.1.

### Gaps
Each with its class from `gap-rules.md`.

### Record
Advisory draft. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
