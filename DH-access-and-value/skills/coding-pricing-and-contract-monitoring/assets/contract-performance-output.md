# Output format: contract performance

## Short mode (default for a direct question; 250 to 450 words, hard cap. One measures table, one line per rule, no separate formula column for figures the person can check by eye.)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.

1. **Where money is lost:** one sentence naming the contract, the measure and the amount on your numbers.
2. **Measures by contract:** Contract · Period · Denial rate · Realization vs agreed · Volume tier (units to next) · Outcome component at stake and earned.
3. **Rule behind each measure:** one line each (for example "at least 20 percent outcome-linked, section 134 SGB V, DE2").
4. **Measure next:** at most three.
5. **Data gaps:** measures not given and who holds them.
6. **Record:** "Uncleared draft. Figures are yours." or the clearer's name.
