# Output format: economic question and reference case

## Short mode (default for direct questions; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Answer:** two or three sentences answering the question asked, naming the body and the version of its text (for example PMG48 of 14 July 2025, last updated 17 December 2025).
2. **Rules that bind:** a table, Element · Rule as written · Row. Only the elements the question needs.
3. **Conflicts or changes:** one line per open conflict that touches the question (NICE thresholds, PMG51), never resolved.
4. **Gaps:** at most three lines, by class (`references/gap-rules.md`).
5. **Record:** "AI-assisted draft. Thresholds and conclusions are the decision-maker's." Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.
