# Output format: risk-share options

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

**Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without tables' source column; cut in this order: context paragraphs, secondary rules, full-mode sections; offer the full version in one line.** The calls list is not counted; aim for at least 250 words.

1. **Answer:** the options that fit, the fixed rule that constrains them, and what the choice turns on.
2. **Options:** Design · How it pays · Share at risk · Attainment rule · Main known problem.
3. **Fixed rules for this market:** at most three lines, each with its source.
4. **Scenario (if numbers given):** one line per design at the person's central attainment rate, labeled "hypothesis on your inputs".
5. **Record:** "Draft for the owner. Design, shares and thresholds are NAME's to decide. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.
