# Uptake measures: what to count, and what the official figures say

How coverage turns into patients using a product, stage by stage, with the official definitions and national figures that exist. Sources are the rows in `access-monitoring-sources.md` (DE-, FR-, EN-) and `access-after-listing.md`. Read on 19 October 2026; not yet owner-reviewed.

## The funnel

Use the stages that fit the market. The first stages come from how access works in that country (`access-after-listing.md`); the later ones from what the product itself can see.

| Stage | Germany (DiGA) | France (PECAN, LATM) | England (NHS) | Who holds the data |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Eligible patients | People with the listed indication and statutory insurance (about 73 million insured are entitled to DiGA) | Patients in the decree's indication | Population in the guidance scope, within commissioning | Epidemiology; not the library |
| 2 Prescribed or approved | Prescription (form 16 or e-prescription) or sickness-fund approval | Prescription by the operator (remote monitoring) or a doctor's referral (PECAN) | Commissioned and offered by the service; self-referral in Talking Therapies | Payer statistics; the buyer's partners |
| 3 Code issued | Fund issues a one-time activation code | Not applicable | Not applicable | Payer |
| 4 Activated or started | Code redeemed in the app | First billed month or first session | First session | The buyer |
| 5 Still using | Minutes per week, sessions per week; dropout is three weeks without use for products used 90 days or more (DiGAV § 23a) | "Effective use": no official indicator yet; companies propose their own | Sessions; finished courses | The buyer |
| 6 Completed or renewed | Repeat prescription | Renewal within the coverage period | Course finished | Payer and buyer |

Name the stage where conversion drops most. That stage, not the overall rate, is what the performance review acts on.

## Rules for using these figures

- A national average describes the whole market, not what this product should achieve. Show it beside the buyer's number as context, labeled with its period and base. The pass rule is always the person's (see `tasks/performance-review.md`).
- Do not add units that differ: sessions, referrals, courses and patients are different counts (EN-7, EN-8, EN-9).
- The GKV-Spitzenverband report is the payer's statutory report. Its figures are official; its explanations (for example about marketing-driven approvals) are the payer's view. Label them so.
- Before 15 April 2027 there are no published per-DiGA outcome-measurement results. Do not imply a benchmark for minutes of use or dropout exists.
- Where a market has no official figure, say so. Do not borrow another country's figure as a proxy.
