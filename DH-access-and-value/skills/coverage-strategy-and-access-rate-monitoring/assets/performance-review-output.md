# Output format: performance review (review draft)

## Short mode (default for a direct question; aim for 300 to 500 words)

1. **Verdict by market:** Market · On track / At risk / Missed · Stall point · Owner you named.
2. **The decision needed:** one or two sentences: which miss matters most and what choice the person faces.
3. **Hypotheses to test first:** at most three, each with what would confirm it.
4. **Where the response sits:** one line per missed market (strategy, benefit design, product, evidence).
5. **Record:** "Review draft. Rules and owners are yours. Uncleared." or the clearer's name.

## Full mode

### Summary
Which markets are working, which are not, and the one decision the review asks for.

### Pass rules
| Market | Rule in your words | Threshold | Owner | Measured by |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Results
| Market | Rule | Number behind it | Pass / Miss / Not measurable | Source (A1 or A2, date) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Stall points
| Market | First missed stage | What it means |
|---|---|---|

### Hypotheses
| Market | Hypothesis | What would confirm it | Source of the mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
Labeled as hypotheses; none is a finding.

### Response routing
| Market | Stall point | Responding work | Owner you named |
|---|---|---|---|

### Gaps
Rules that could not be measured, and why.

### Record
Review draft. Rule changes, pricing or contract changes, and external wording stop for the accountable owner. Cleared by NAME, or uncleared.
