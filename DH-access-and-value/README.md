# Access & Value (DH)

Replaces two packs: dh-market-access (DH-03.01) and dh-health-economics (DH-01.07).

## Pack map and shared skills

- `access-and-value-capability-map` (DH-03.01; DH-01.07). The map of the VITAL Access & Value pack for digital health products (SaMD, DTx, DiGA, remote monitoring): market access and reimbursement plus health economics and value demonstration.
- `digital-health-expert` (shared). Find and download a VITAL Digital Health expert (a ready-made role such as the SaMD Regulatory Affairs Director, the Quality & Software Lifecycle Director or the Regulatory Submission Manager) to run on the person's own assistant.

## DH-03.01 Market Access & Reimbursement (from dh-market-access)

- `outcomes-based-contracting` (DH-03.01.1.1). Design an outcomes-based or risk-share contract for a regulated digital health product (DiGA, DTx, SaMD, remote monitoring, digital mental health): which outcome measures could carry payment, which risk-share designs fit the market, and what the term sheet has to say.
- `outcomes-contract-monitoring` (DH-03.01.1.2). Check whether a running outcomes-based or risk-share contract for a digital health product (DiGA, DTx, SaMD, remote monitoring, digital mental health) is delivering: attainment per measure and per patient against the contract's own rules, what missing data and dropouts do to the result and the payment, and whether the contract is on track against the review rules the person sets.
- `coding-pricing-and-payer-contracting` (DH-03.01.2.1). Work out which codes or payment routes could pay for a regulated digital health product, what the price rules and comparator prices say, and how to prepare a payer or provider negotiation.
- `coding-pricing-and-contract-monitoring` (DH-03.01.2.2). Check whether a digital health product's codes, prices and payer contracts are still working: what changed or is proposed in the codes and price rules it depends on (fee schedule rules, new RPM and RTM codes, DiGA thresholds and group maximums, the month-13 price switch), how the contract performs on the person's own data (claims paid and denied, realized versus agreed price, volume against discount tiers, outcome-component attainment), and which contracts are off track against the person's rules.
- `payer-engagement-and-hta-submission` (DH-03.01.3.1). Prepare a digital health product (DiGA, DTx, SaMD, remote monitoring) for an HTA or reimbursement submission: what BfArM, the HAS CNEDiMTS (LPPR, PECAN, LATM) or NICE actually require, which of the person's own studies meet it, what a complete file needs, what it costs and how long review takes, and who to engage in what order with a value narrative built only from checked claims.
- `hta-submission-and-payer-engagement-monitoring` (DH-03.01.3.2). Check a draft HTA or reimbursement dossier for a digital health product (DiGA, DTx, SaMD, remote monitoring) against what BfArM, the HAS CNEDiMTS (LPPR, PECAN, LATM) or NICE asks for, track the decisions on the person's submissions and on comparable products, and review whether payer and HTA engagement is working against the person's own rules.
- `coverage-pathway-and-access-strategy` (DH-03.01.4.1). Work out where a regulated digital health product (DiGA, DTx, SaMD, remote monitoring, digital mental health) is reimbursed today, where it could be paid, and how to turn coverage into real patient access.
- `coverage-strategy-and-access-rate-monitoring` (DH-03.01.4.2). Check whether a digital health product's coverage strategy is working: what changed in its reimbursement status, which clocks are running (DiGA trial periods, PECAN year, NICE evidence generation), whether coverage is turning into patients who activate and keep using it, and which markets have stalled and why.

## DH-01.07 Health Economics & Value Demonstration (from dh-health-economics)

- `reimbursement-evidence-support` (DH-01.07.1.1). Build the evidence and economic case a payer or HTA body asks for, for a digital health product (DTx, DiGA, SaMD): which economic standards apply at NICE (reference case, PMG48 early use, ESF tiers and standards 13, 17 and 18, EQ-5D-5L rule of 27 August 2026), HAS, IQWiG, ICER-PHTI and the EU JCA (clinical only), where the person's model, budget impact analysis and studies fall short, and which gaps to close first.
- `reimbursement-evidence-monitoring` (DH-01.07.1.2). Track how reimbursement and HTA decisions treated the evidence and economic case for a digital health product (DTx, DiGA, SaMD, remote monitoring): what NICE committees accepted, questioned or asked to be generated, which early-use evidence windows are running, which methods changed since the model was built (NICE EQ-5D-5L rule of 27 August 2026, PMG36 and PMG48 thresholds, IQWiG 8.0, AMCP 6.0, EU JCA device rules), and whether the evidence outcomes the person set were met.
- `economic-model-development` (DH-01.07.2.1). Frame, build and run an economic model for a digital health technology (DTx, SaMD, DiGA, remote monitoring) the way the deciding HTA body or payer expects: the decision problem, the reference case (NICE PMG36, PMG48, PMG51, ESF; HAS; IQWiG; ICER and ICER-PHTI; AMCP draft), an input register with sources, budget impact, cost-utility or cost-comparison runs, one-way sensitivity, switching values and scenarios, all on the person's own numbers.
- `economic-model-validity-monitoring` (DH-01.07.2.2). Check whether an economic model of a digital health technology (DTx, SaMD, DiGA, remote monitoring) is complete, current, valid and still holding: CHEERS 2022 reporting item by item (never a score), the deciding body's reference case (NICE PMG36, PMG48, PMG51; HAS; IQWiG; ICER; ICER-PHTI), retired rules and threshold conflicts, evidence for each validation type, recomputation of reported numbers, and re-runs on real-world values the person observed.
- `real-world-outcomes-research` (DH-01.07.3.1). Plan, run and report a real-world outcomes study for a digital health product (DiGA, DTx, SaMD, remote monitoring): frame the outcomes question as a hypothesis-testing or exploratory study with an estimand, check whether the claims, EHR, registry or app data are fit for it, keep a study execution record that proves the protocol and analysis plan were fixed before anyone saw outcomes, and report the result against that plan.
- `outcomes-evidence-quality-monitoring` (DH-01.07.3.2). Check how much weight a real-world or outcomes study for a digital health product (DiGA, DTx, SaMD, remote monitoring) can bear: lay out the facts per ROBINS-I or RoB 2 domain for each result, whether the data are fit for purpose, whether the plan was fixed before outcomes were seen, retractions and currency; tally and score the reviewers' judgments only under the person's own rule; and plan the repair of weak evidence with named owners.
- `value-narrative-and-dossier` (DH-01.07.4.1). Turn the evidence for a regulated digital health product (DTx, DiGA, SaMD, remote monitoring, digital mental health) into a value narrative and a value dossier for the stakeholders who decide on it: payers and formulary committees (AMCP Format, HCEI under FD&C Act 502(a), pre-clearance information under 502(gg)), NHS commissioners (NICE Evidence Standards Framework), HTA bodies and health-system buyers.
- `value-communication-monitoring` (DH-01.07.4.2). Check whether a regulated digital health product's value narrative and value dossier (DTx, DiGA, SaMD, remote monitoring) are landing and still hold: how payers, formulary committees, NHS commissioners and HTA bodies responded, line by line, on the person's own measure; whether every claim is still supported, current and not retracted, and whether the guidance it leans on (FDA payor guidance, AMCP Format 6.0, NICE methods) has moved; and which lines need repair, drafted for the owner.
