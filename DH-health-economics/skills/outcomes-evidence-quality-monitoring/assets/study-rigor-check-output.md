# Output format: study rigor check (fact sheet)

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap. Aim for at least 250. Any [PAPER] row id carries its DOI beside it, in prose and in tables (for example ES-75, DOI 10.1136/bmj.i4919).

1. **Answer:** design as read from the paper (and the library label if different), the tool that applies and why, the two or three domains where the facts raise the most questions, prespecification status, in at most four sentences. No verdict.
2. **One table, domains:** Domain · Facts from the paper (with locator) · Reviewer level (empty, or the reviewers' own).
3. **Open items:** at most three (a fact not reported, a registration not confirmed, abstract only).
4. **Record:** "Fact sheet for your reviewers. No judgment made. Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. AI-assisted draft." The record line appears in every answer, including refusals.

Offer the full version in one line.

## Full mode

### Study
Title, venue, year, DOI; design as read (with locator); library label; tool and why (ES-75, ES-71, ES-163, ES-164).

### Facts per domain
| Domain | Facts and locators | Questions the sources raise | Reviewer level |
|---|---|---|---|

### Data fitness (framework: NAME)
| Element | Fact | Row |
|---|---|---|

### Prespecification and registration
| Item | Fact | Row |
|---|---|---|

### Currency, funding, conflicts
Retraction and currency results with the source checked; funding and every conflict line as the paper states it (employees, shareholders, paid consultants), quoted with its locator.

### Reporting gaps (not a score)
STROBE item numbers or CHEERS items not found (CV123, HE-005).

### Certainty context
GRADE start for the design (ES-90, ES-100); the library's draft level, labeled as a draft. No certainty verb until the person rates; then Table 15.6.b wording (ES-203 to ES-207).

### Calls made
Each call with arguments and retrieval date.

### Record
Fact sheet; judgments are the reviewers'. Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. AI-assisted draft.
