# Output format: study score (portfolio tally)

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap. Aim for at least 250. Any [PAPER] row id carries its DOI beside it, in prose and in tables (for example ES-75, DOI 10.1136/bmj.i4919).

1. **Answer:** counts per overall level, the results excluded from any synthesis (ES-77), the most common weak domain, in at most four sentences.
2. **One table, tally:** Study · Result · Tool · Overall · Score (your rule).
3. **Open items:** at most three (an unjudged domain, a rule element not set, a "No information" domain).
4. **Record:** "Tally of your judgments under your rule; not a validated score. Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. AI-assisted draft." The record line appears in every answer, including refusals.

Offer the full version in one line.
