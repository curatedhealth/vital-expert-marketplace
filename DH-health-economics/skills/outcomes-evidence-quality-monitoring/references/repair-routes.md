# Repair routes: interpretive reference

What a weakness found in A1 or A2 can and cannot be repaired by, and where the work sits. Every route names its row (read 19 October 2026). Routes are options; the person chooses and names the owner.

## What cannot be repaired after the fact

- **Prespecification.** A plan written or changed after outcome access cannot be made prespecified. Registering now does not turn the study into HETE (HE-026, HE-027). The repair is disclosure: a conformance or deviation statement (HE-028), and, for a device submission, a statement of whether the plan was final before outcome review (CV94).
- **Critical risk of bias.** A result at critical risk in any ROBINS-I domain should not be included in any synthesis (ES-77). Re-analysis of the same data rarely changes a design flaw such as an uncontrolled before-after comparison (ES-78, which covers uncontrolled before-after studies only, not a historical comparison group); the repair is a new study.

## Repair options by weakness

| Weakness | Repair option | Row | Where the work sits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hypothesis generated and tested in the same data | Test in a different data source and population | HE-029 | New study: `real-world-outcomes-research` (DH-01.07.3.1) |
| Changes after outcome access | Deviation statement; sensitivity analyses exploring the change (review method applied by analogy) | HE-028, ES-40 | The study team, through DH-01.07.3.1 A2 |
| Post hoc subgroup used as a finding | Report as exploratory; test in a new prespecified study | ES-87, HE-026 | DH-01.07.3.1 |
| Confounding not addressed, or concurrent comparator missing | Target trial emulation design for a new study; feasibility assessment first | CV107, CV106 | DH-01.07.3.1 A1 |
| Outcome definition not validated | Validate the operational definition against a reference standard (sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV) | CV80, CV81 | The data partner and study team |
| Claims continuity not handled | Re-analysis restricted to continuous coverage, if the data allow | CV83 | Study team |
| Linkage not prespecified | Document linkage method and inconsistency rule; disclose as a deviation | CV24, HE-028 | Study team |
| No public protocol | Post protocol and plan; say when they were posted relative to analysis | HE-027, CV77 | The owner the person names for registrations, or owner not named |
| Doubtful trustworthiness of a study in the portfolio | Sensitivity analysis without it | ES-144 | dh-clinical-validation `literature-and-real-world-evidence-review`; owner as the person names, or owner not named |
| Published criticism of the methods | Address it publicly | HE-030 (rec 6) | Authors |
| Retraction, correction or expression of concern | Re-check status at every update; remove or flag | ES-119, ES-120 | The owner the person names for the evidence register, or owner not named |
| Randomized evidence needed | A trial | CV133 | dh-clinical-validation `study-protocol-and-endpoint-design` (DH-01.02.1.1) |

## Routing outside this competency

- Systematic review method and evidence tables: dh-clinical-validation `literature-and-real-world-evidence-review`.
- Model inputs and economic validity: dh-health-economics 2.x.
- HTA and payer submissions: dh-market-access `payer-engagement-and-hta-submission`.
- One customer's outcomes: dh-customer-success `outcome-tracking-and-customer-health`. Outcome-linked payment: dh-market-access `outcomes-contract-monitoring`.
- Device status and regulatory submissions: dh-regulatory-strategy `regulatory-pathway-and-classification`.
- Promotional claims: dh-commercial `pricing-and-value-message-monitoring`, with the owner the person names for claims, or owner not named.
