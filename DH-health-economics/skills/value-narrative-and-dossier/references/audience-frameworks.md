# Audience frameworks: what each decision maker expects

Interpretive reference. Every rule cites its row in `health-economics-research.md` (HE), `promotion-and-claims-research.md` (PC) or `evidence-synthesis-research.md` (ES), all read on 19 October 2026. Where a row is a draft, the line says so. Anything not listed here is `[GAP]`.

Use the audiences the person names, in their words. This file tells you which sourced framework applies to each; it does not choose the audience.

## United States: payers, formulary and technology assessment committees

| What applies | Rule | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| Who counts as a payor audience | Public and private payors, formulary and technology assessment committees, PBMs, TPAs and similar entities deciding on a population basis. | PC-US-34 |
| Economic content is HCEI | Any analysis of the economic consequences of using the device, including its clinical inputs and assumptions, comparative or not. | PC-US-06 |
| Safe harbor conditions (cleared use) | Relates to a cleared, approved or De Novo indication; based on competent and reliable scientific evidence; conspicuous statement of material differences from labeling. Devices are covered since Pub. L. 117-328 section 3630. | PC-US-04, PC-US-11 |
| Evidence standard for HCEI | CARSE: generally accepted scientific standards; FDA points to good research practices from bodies such as ISPOR. Applies to clinical inputs too. | PC-US-38, PC-US-39 |
| Required context | Study design and methodology, generalizability, limitations, sensitivity analyses, balanced presentation. | PC-US-40 |
| Forms | An evidence dossier, a reprint, a model with user manual, a budget-impact model, slides or a payor brochure. | PC-US-36 |
| Not for prescribers or the public | HCEI policy does not cover HCPs making individual decisions or consumers, for example on a public website. | PC-US-35 |
| Before clearance, or an uncleared use (502(gg)) | Truthful, non-misleading product information with a clear "not cleared, safety and effectiveness not established" statement, development stage, study design and limitations; no claim that it is cleared or found safe or effective; factual results without conclusions. | PC-US-08, PC-US-09, PC-US-10, PC-US-44, PC-US-45 |
| Uncleared use of a cleared product | Prominent statement of the cleared indication and a copy of current labeling; follow up if materially outdated. | PC-US-46 |
| Guidance status | The 2018 final payor guidance is the final text; a June 2026 revised draft implements section 3630 and will replace it when finalized. Name both whenever US payor material is drafted. | PC-US-47 |
| Contracts | FDA does not regulate value-based contract terms; only the HCEI communicated is in scope. Contract terms belong to dh-market-access. | PC-US-42 |
| AMCP Format | Advisory, not mandatory. Format 6.0 released October 2026; full text not read. | HE-130, HE-131 |
| AMCP dossier types (draft) | A unapproved product, B approved product, C unapproved use. AMCP 6.0 draft. | HE-133 |
| AMCP economic value (draft) | Economic value assessments apply only to approved-product dossiers. | HE-132 |
| ICER for digital health | ICER uses the ICER-PHTI framework for DHTs, with budget impact as the primary measure of comparative value; a 1-year horizon with 2-to-5-year scenarios; user and payer perspectives; a threshold price for 15 percent savings, a 3:1 ROI price for very high-cost populations, or the cost-neutral price. | HE-115, HE-121 to HE-124 |
| ICER-PHTI evidence tiers | Tiers 1, 2, 3a and 3b; tier 1 is self-directed management without clinician involvement. | HE-125 |

## European Union, France, Germany

| What applies | Rule | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| EU joint clinical assessment | Clinical only; economic evaluation and conclusions on value stay national; reports contain no value judgment. | HE-093 to HE-095, ES-149 |
| Devices in JCA scope | Class IIb or III with an expert-panel opinion, by selection; first selection 6 October 2026 had no digital-only product. | HE-096, HE-097, HE-101 |
| France (HAS) | Collective perspective; CUA when quality of life matters; 2.5 percent discount at publication; French EQ-5D-5L value set. No threshold in the passages read. When HAS requires an economic opinion for a device is not sourced: `[GAP]`. | HE-102 to HE-107 |
| Germany (IQWiG) | HEE supports price negotiation as an information synthesis; SHI-insured community perspective; 3 percent discount; BIA of at least 3 years. DiGA economics are not in IQWiG General Methods: `[GAP]`, point to dh-market-access. | HE-108 to HE-113 |
| EU and German promotion | MDR Article 7 bars misleading claims about intended purpose, safety and performance; HWG applies to device advertising, with stricter rules outside professional circles; a cited study must say whether it concerns the advertised product. | PC-EU-01, PC-EU-02, PC-DE-01 to PC-DE-05 |

## Any audience

- Economic evaluations are reported to CHEERS 2022 (28 items; not a score). [PAPER] Husereau et al. 2022, DOI 10.1177/23814683211061097. HE-001 to HE-014.
- Budget impact: ISPOR BIA II elements, decision maker's own population, scenarios chosen from the decision maker's perspective. [PAPER] Sullivan et al. 2014, DOI 10.1016/j.jval.2013.08.2291, abstract only. HE-018 to HE-023.
- Clinicians in the US: communications must be consistent with labeling (PC-US-22 for 510(k) and exempt devices; PC-US-23 and PC-US-24 for De Novo and PMA; PC-US-25 to PC-US-31 for both). Cite only the rows for the product's route.
- Patients and consumers: out of scope for this skill except to say the rules differ; consumer and marketing messages belong to dh-commercial.
