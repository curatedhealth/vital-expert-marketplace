# Output format: study execution record

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap. Aim for at least 250. Any [PAPER] row id carries its DOI beside it, in prose and in tables (for example HE-026, DOI 10.1002/pds.4297).

1. **Answer:** can prespecification be shown (yes / no / not yet) and the one date that decides it, plus the number of deviations the conformance statement must carry, in at most four sentences.
2. **One table, checks:** Check · Result · Row (the script's table).
3. **Open items:** at most three (an undated document, a registry id, a missing deviation reason).
4. **Record:** "Execution record draft. Dates are yours, from your documents. Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. AI-assisted draft." The record line appears in every answer, including refusals.

Offer the full version in one line.

## Full mode

### Summary
What can be shown, under which framework (CV94 devices, CV75 drugs, HE-027 HETE, CV108 EU medicines).

### Timeline
| Date | Event | Document and version | Source (your document) |
|---|---|---|---|

### Checks
| Check | Result | Row |
|---|---|---|

### Revisions and deviations
| Date | What changed | Reason | Before or after first outcome access | Reported as |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Provenance and data handling
| Question | Your answer | Row |
|---|---|---|

### Trial test
Prospective assignment or not (CV133); if a trial, the registration and results clocks with rows, and the pointer to dh-clinical-validation `decentralized-trial-operations`.

### Draft conformance statement (draft for the owner)
One paragraph per deviation: date, change, reason (HE-028).

### Gaps and record
Undated items and why they matter. Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. AI-assisted draft.
