# Output format: outcome result (result draft)

## Short mode (default for a direct question)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap. Aim for at least 250. Any [PAPER] row id carries its DOI beside it, in prose and in tables (for example HE-026, DOI 10.1002/pds.4297).

1. **Answer:** the result in the report's own words with its status (prespecified, changed after outcome access, or post hoc), and whether it meets the person's rule, in at most four sentences.
2. **One table, rules:** Rule in your words · Number behind it · Met / Not met / Not measurable · Owner you named.
3. **Open items:** at most three gaps, each with the work that would close it.
4. **Record:** "Result draft. Rules and owners are yours. Not evidence of efficacy or cost-effectiveness. Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with verify.build_record once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft. AI-assisted draft." The record line appears in every answer, including refusals.

Offer the full version in one line.
