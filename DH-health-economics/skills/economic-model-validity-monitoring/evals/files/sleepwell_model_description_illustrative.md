# SleepWell cost-utility model: methods summary (ILLUSTRATIVE)

ILLUSTRATIVE document written for the build test. SleepWell is fictional; nothing here describes a real product or model.

## Decision problem

The model compares SleepWell, a digital CBT for insomnia app, plus usual care with usual care alone for adults with chronic insomnia in England, for a NICE HealthTech topic expected to launch in 2027.

## Model

A three-state Markov model (insomnia, remission, relapse) with yearly cycles. The perspective is the NHS. The time horizon is three years because the trial followed patients for one year. Costs and QALYs are discounted at 3.5% a year.

## Inputs

Costs are in GBP. Usual care costs come from a published costing study. The utility gain of 0.02 comes from the trial's EQ-5D-3L data, valued with the Dolan 1997 UK 3L tariff. Productivity gains from fewer sick days are included in the base case.

## Validation

The model was validated by two sleep physicians who reviewed the structure and the inputs.

## Results

At 20,000 GBP per QALY the model shows that SleepWell is cost-effective, which proves its value to the NHS. One-way sensitivity analysis varied every input by plus or minus 20 percent; a scenario with a 1.5% discount rate is also shown.
