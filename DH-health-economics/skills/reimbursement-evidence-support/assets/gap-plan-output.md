# Output format: evidence gap plan and economic section

## Short mode (default; 250 to 450 words)

Short mode: 450 words maximum, counted without the calls list and tables' source column. Before handing over, count the words. If over 450, cut in this order until under: context paragraphs, repeated row ids in prose, secondary rules, rows not tied to the person's question. Never cut a gap, a stop or the record line. Refusals follow the same 450-word cap.

1. **Answer:** the blocking gap, what it blocks, and the one decision the person faces.
2. **Dependency table:** Level · Open item (A2 status) · Blocks · Options (one line, rows) · Owner you named.
3. **Order:** "Order within a level is yours" or the person's order; a ranking only on the person's scores, with the swap point.
4. **Draft economic section (opening lines):** at most three sentences, only from shown items and checked claims, labeled "Draft for [owner]: not for submission".
5. **Calls and record:** calls made. Clears it: [the person named, in their words, or owner not named]. The record is built with `verify.build_record` once that named person clears it; until then, uncleared draft.

End with one offer: the full plan and section, or the monitoring skill once the body has decided.
