# DH Health Economics & Value Demonstration

Install **DH Health Economics & Value Demonstration** from `curatedhealth/vital-expert-plugins`, then paste the Digital Health Library key. The key is not in this pack.

This pack is only DH-01.07 Health Economics & Value Demonstration. Other Digital Health cores are separate packs.

The connector is `https://mcp.vital.expert/api/mcp/library`. Do not add a second connector.

Choose one heading, then one job. The job holds its tasks as sections. Ask which one task, then follow that section. A missing fact stays a gap.

## Skills

- `digital-health-expert`. Find and download a VITAL Digital Health expert (a ready-made role such as the SaMD Regulatory Affairs Director, the Quality & Software Lifecycle Director or the Regulatory Submission Manager) to run on the person's own assistant.
- `economic-model-development`. Frame, build and run an economic model for a digital health technology (DTx, SaMD, DiGA, remote monitoring) the way the deciding HTA body or payer expects: the decision problem, the reference case (NICE PMG36, PMG48, PMG51, ESF; HAS; IQWiG; ICER and ICER-PHTI; AMCP draft), an input register with sources, budget impact, cost-utility or cost-comparison runs, one-way sensitivity, switching values and scenarios, all on the person's own numbers.
- `economic-model-validity-monitoring`. Check whether an economic model of a digital health technology (DTx, SaMD, DiGA, remote monitoring) is complete, current, valid and still holding: CHEERS 2022 reporting item by item (never a score), the deciding body's reference case (NICE PMG36, PMG48, PMG51; HAS; IQWiG; ICER; ICER-PHTI), retired rules and threshold conflicts, evidence for each validation type, recomputation of reported numbers, and re-runs on real-world values the person observed.
- `health-economics-capability-map`. The map of the VITAL Health Economics & Value Demonstration pack for digital health products (SaMD, DTx, DiGA-type apps).
- `outcomes-evidence-quality-monitoring`. Check how much weight a real-world or outcomes study for a digital health product (DiGA, DTx, SaMD, remote monitoring) can bear: lay out the facts per ROBINS-I or RoB 2 domain for each result, whether the data are fit for purpose, whether the plan was fixed before outcomes were seen, retractions and currency; tally and score the reviewers' judgments only under the person's own rule; and plan the repair of weak evidence with named owners.
- `real-world-outcomes-research`. Plan, run and report a real-world outcomes study for a digital health product (DiGA, DTx, SaMD, remote monitoring): frame the outcomes question as a hypothesis-testing or exploratory study with an estimand, check whether the claims, EHR, registry or app data are fit for it, keep a study execution record that proves the protocol and analysis plan were fixed before anyone saw outcomes, and report the result against that plan.
- `reimbursement-evidence-monitoring`. Track how reimbursement and HTA decisions treated the evidence and economic case for a digital health product (DTx, DiGA, SaMD, remote monitoring): what NICE committees accepted, questioned or asked to be generated, which early-use evidence windows are running, which methods changed since the model was built (NICE EQ-5D-5L rule of 27 August 2026, PMG36 and PMG48 thresholds, IQWiG 8.0, AMCP 6.0, EU JCA device rules), and whether the evidence outcomes the person set were met.
- `reimbursement-evidence-support`. Build the evidence and economic case a payer or HTA body asks for, for a digital health product (DTx, DiGA, SaMD): which economic standards apply at NICE (reference case, PMG48 early use, ESF tiers and standards 13, 17 and 18, EQ-5D-5L rule of 27 August 2026), HAS, IQWiG, ICER-PHTI and the EU JCA (clinical only), where the person's model, budget impact analysis and studies fall short, and which gaps to close first.
- `value-communication-monitoring`. Check whether a regulated digital health product's value narrative and value dossier (DTx, DiGA, SaMD, remote monitoring) are landing and still hold: how payers, formulary committees, NHS commissioners and HTA bodies responded, line by line, on the person's own measure; whether every claim is still supported, current and not retracted, and whether the guidance it leans on (FDA payor guidance, AMCP Format 6.0, NICE methods) has moved; and which lines need repair, drafted for the owner.
- `value-narrative-and-dossier`. Turn the evidence for a regulated digital health product (DTx, DiGA, SaMD, remote monitoring, digital mental health) into a value narrative and a value dossier for the stakeholders who decide on it: payers and formulary committees (AMCP Format, HCEI under FD&C Act 502(a), pre-clearance information under 502(gg)), NHS commissioners (NICE Evidence Standards Framework), HTA bodies and health-system buyers. Every value claim is checked against a passage read in the same direction, and certainty is stated as the person or the review rated it.
